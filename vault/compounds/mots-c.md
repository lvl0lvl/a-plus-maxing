# MOTS-c

## Metadata
- class: peptide (mitochondrial-derived peptide, MDP; 16-aa sequence MRWQEMGYIFYPRKLR; MW ~2174.6 Da; encoded by a 51-bp sORF within MT-RNR1, the mitochondrial 12S rRNA gene)
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]] (efficacy evidence ~entirely male-mouse IP-injection studies, n≤10; human data exclusively observational biomarker associations; foundational literature ~100% single-group, Cohen/Lee USC, with disclosed CohBar commercial COI; only human clinical program used a different analog, CB4211, which failed on formulation and the company dissolved 2023; no human RCT of native MOTS-c)
- risk_tier: experimental — not FDA-approved; not lawfully compoundable (PCAC review scheduled July 2026, no category determination as of this entry); WADA-prohibited in- and out-of-competition (S4.4, AMPK-activator non-specified substance); native-peptide PK in humans uncharacterized; no chronic human safety data; grey-market product with documented formulation issues in the analog class
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-20

> **Read this first.** MOTS-c is a biologically plausible mitochondrial-derived peptide with an interesting mechanism (AMPK activation via folate-cycle disruption and AICAR accumulation in skeletal muscle), but its therapeutic efficacy evidence is **entirely preclinical** — mouse studies from a single lab (Cohen/Lee USC) with disclosed financial conflicts (CohBar Inc.). No human RCT of exogenous native MOTS-c has ever been published. The one clinical program (CB4211, an engineered analog, not the native peptide) met safety endpoints in a company-press-release-only Phase 1b (n=11), then failed on formulation; CohBar dissolved in 2023. Human evidence is exclusively observational: endogenous circulating MOTS-c correlates with metabolic states but these associations do not establish what happens when you inject the peptide. It is **WADA-prohibited (S4.4)** and **not currently compoundable**. Full depth + every citation: [[library/peptides/mots-c/research-report]].

## Mechanism
MOTS-c (Mitochondrial Open Reading Frame of the 12S rRNA Type-C) is translated from a cytoplasmic mitochondria-exported mRNA transcript. Its primary mechanistic axis operates in skeletal muscle: the peptide depletes intracellular 5-methyltetrahydrofolate (5Me-THF), disrupting the folate one-carbon pool, which blocks de-novo purine biosynthesis and drives accumulation of AICAR (>20-fold in treated cells). AICAR is a well-characterized endogenous AMPK activator; the resulting AMPK phosphorylation occurs independently of changes in the canonical AMP/ATP ratio. Downstream of AMPK, MOTS-c promotes GLUT4 translocation to muscle cell membranes and enhances insulin-stimulated glucose disposal. Under metabolic stress (glucose restriction, oxidative stress), MOTS-c also undergoes AMPK-dependent nuclear translocation within 30 minutes, directly regulating antioxidant response element (ARE) genes in concert with NRF2/Nrf2 stress-response pathways — a retrograde mitochondrial-to-nuclear signaling role not described for any prior MDP. The folate/AICAR/AMPK mechanistic chain is established in cell lines (HEK293, L6 myotubes, HeLa, C2C12) and mouse skeletal muscle; it has **not** been demonstrated in humans. See report §1.4.

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (weight order):

- Lee et al. 2015 (Cell Metabolism, PMID 25738459) — male C57BL/6 and CD-1 mice, n=6–10/group; hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamp: exogenous MOTS-c 5 mg/kg/day IP × 7 days → ~30% increase in glucose infusion rate, elevated Akt/GLUT4 in skeletal muscle; obesity-prevention: 0.5 mg/kg/day × 8 weeks → lower fat mass, AMPK activated; **foundational single-lab, no independent replication as of 2021**. `animal` — Cohen/Lee USC, CohBar COI disclosed.
- Reynolds et al. 2021 (Nature Communications, PMID 33473109) — mouse: C57BL/6N males at 2/12/22 months, 15 mg/kg/day IP × 2 weeks → significantly improved treadmill performance across all ages; aged mice outperformed untreated middle-aged mice; human observational sub-study n=10 healthy young men: skeletal muscle MOTS-c ~11.9-fold increase post-exercise, plasma ~1.6-fold. `animal` (mouse treatment); human component observational only — Cohen/Lee USC, CohBar COI disclosed.
- Zhou et al. 2024 (Diabetol Metab Syndr, PMID 39160573) — meta-analysis, 7 studies, 602 participants: endogenous MOTS-c lower in T2DM vs. controls (SMD=−0.89; 95% CI −1.12 to −0.65); biphasic in obesity (elevated at BMI >28, null when overweight included). `meta_analysis` — associative, not causal.
- Kim et al. 2018 (Cell Metabolism, PMID 29983246) — HEK293, C2C12 cell lines: MOTS-c nuclear translocation under metabolic stress; AMPK-dependent; regulates ARE genes. `in_vitro` — Cohen/Lee USC, CohBar COI disclosed.

Counter-evidence and limits:
- No independent first-author replication of core insulin-sensitization claim (specific dose, strain, route) from any lab other than Cohen/Lee USC through 2021.
- Zempo et al. 2021 (Aging NY, PMID 33468709) — n=27,527 (J-MICC, MEC, TMM cohorts), n=736 centenarians: m.1382A>C variant **not** associated with exceptional longevity (refuting Fuku 2015 hypothesis); in sedentary males the C-allele associated with **65% higher T2DM prevalence**. `cohort`
- Yoon et al. 2025 (PMID 41551324) — n=54 adults plus n=10 bariatric surgery follow-up: circulating MOTS-c paradoxically **higher** in obese vs. lean individuals; MOTS-c unchanged after significant weight loss from bariatric surgery — directly inconsistent with a simple protective-biomarker narrative. `cohort`
- CB4211 (engineered analog, Phase 1b, n=11) — met safety endpoints per company press release only (no peer-reviewed publication); formulation deemed unsuitable for further development; company dissolved 2023. Not a safety or efficacy result for the native peptide.
- Assay non-standardization: MOTS-c immunoassay values span four orders of magnitude across studies (14 pg/mL to 584 ng/mL); directional findings more robust than absolute concentrations.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): no published safety data for native MOTS-c in humans; CB4211 Phase 1b (n=11, company press release only): mild-to-moderate injection-site reactions >10% incidence; 2018 trial suspension due to persistent SC nodules (non-tender, freely mobile, undissolved drug); no serious adverse events reported for CB4211 at one dose level. CB4211 ≠ native peptide.
- adverse effects (anecdotal): injection-site reactions (forum-level; `anecdote_aggregate`; qualitative only; no validated prevalence)
- contraindications:    insulin or insulin secretagogue users (additive glucose-lowering risk — AMPK activation promotes insulin-independent GLUT4 translocation; CB4211 showed −6% fasting glucose vs placebo in n=11 per press release); WADA-tested athletes (prohibited S4.4, non-specified substance, no contamination-defense reduction available); pregnancy/lactation (no data; metabolic/mitogenic activity; theoretical risk)
- monitoring:           [[biomarkers/fasting-glucose]] (pre-treatment baseline + periodic — hypoglycemia risk is the primary pharmacodynamic concern); [[biomarkers/hba1c]] (baseline; at 8–12 weeks of sustained use); ALT/AST (detect unexpected hepatotoxicity; CB4211 showed ALT/AST reductions in NASH model — bidirectional monitoring appropriate); injection-site palpation for nodules; body weight and lipids (AMPK effects on fat oxidation)
- known interactions:   no characterized interactions; theoretical: additive glucose-lowering with insulin, sulfonylureas, GLP-1 agonists, metformin (AMPK overlap); folate supplementation may modulate the folate/AICAR axis at the mechanism level (uncharacterized in vivo)

## Decision Notes
Per [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]], MOTS-c at evidence_tier=C / risk_tier=experimental → **doctor-required / avoid for performance or metabolic use**: efficacy evidence is entirely preclinical (male mice, single lab, pharmacological IP doses), the only human clinical molecule was a different compound that failed in Phase 1b and whose program dissolved, no human PK exists for the native peptide, and it is WADA-prohibited as a non-specified substance (maximum sanction, no contamination defense). The biological mechanism (AICAR-mediated AMPK activation in skeletal muscle) is coherent and independently corroborated in rodents by three non-USC labs post-2023 — the compound warrants watching as NCT07505745 results emerge, but no current evidence supports human use.
