# AOD-9604

## Metadata
- class: peptide — a synthetic C-terminal fragment of human growth hormone (hGH), the historically-designated hGH "lipolytic domain." Most accurately written **Tyr-hGH(177-191)** (the hGH(177-191) sequence with an N-terminal tyrosine added for stability), commonly referred to as **hGH(176-191)**; a 16-residue ("hexadecapeptide") peptide cyclized by an intramolecular disulphide bond. Originating science: Prof. F.M. Ng and co-workers, Monash University (Clayton, Australia); developed as an anti-obesity candidate by Metabolic Pharmaceuticals Ltd (Australia). ("AOD-9604" = "anti-obesity drug 9604.")
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. AOD-9604's pivotal human efficacy **FAILED**: the Phase-2b obesity RCT (~536 patients, 24 weeks, oral) did **not** beat placebo, and obesity development was discontinued (~2007). The remaining efficacy evidence is **preclinical/in-vitro lipolysis** (rodent + adipocyte) plus a clean-but-efficacy-negative human safety record; the osteoarthritis/cartilage "pivot" is preclinical-only (one rabbit study + a sponsor patent; zero registered human OA trials).
- risk_tier: experimental — never approved as a drug by any health authority; sold gray-market as an injectable for which **no published human SC-route data exist**, with an FDA-flagged immunogenicity concern for uncharacterized product. No long-term human safety (longest exposure 24 weeks). The *characterized* clinical peptide was GH-axis-sparing and well-tolerated short-term — the framing is "unapproved, uncharacterized injectable, no long-term data," not "known to be harmful."

> **Read this first.** AOD-9604 is a synthetic **C-terminal fragment of human growth hormone (hGH 176-191 / Tyr-hGH 177-191)** marketed for fat loss on a "lipolysis WITHOUT the GH side effects" premise. That mechanistic premise is, unusually, **verified at the biomarker level**: AOD-9604 does **not** activate the GH receptor and does **not** raise IGF-1 (and did not impair glucose/insulin handling). **BUT its pivotal human efficacy FAILED** — the defining ~536-patient, 24-week, placebo-controlled oral Phase-2b RCT did **not** beat placebo on weight loss, and Metabolic Pharmaceuticals discontinued the obesity program (~2007). The lipolysis evidence is **preclinical/in-vitro** (obese rodents + adipocytes); it is **NOT proven human fat loss**. AOD-9604 was generally **well-tolerated** — it failed on **efficacy, not safety** — but there is **no long-term human safety, no published human subcutaneous-route data** (the gray-market route), and an FDA-flagged **immunogenicity/aggregation/impurity** concern for an uncharacterized injectable. The osteoarthritis/cartilage "pivot" is **preclinical-only** (one rabbit study + a sponsor patent); the circulating "Paradigm Phase-II knee-OA" claim is a **molecule conflation** — that program is **pentosan polysulfate (iPPS/Zilosul), NOT AOD-9604** — and is excluded. AOD-9604 was **never approved as a drug**; a **self-affirmed GRAS** determination for a narrow food-ingredient use exists but **is NOT FDA approval and does NOT make AOD-9604 a lawful US dietary ingredient** (DSHEA drug-exclusion). It is **WADA PROHIBITED** (S0 catch-all confirmed 2013; also within S2 GH-fragment scope; the substance at the centre of the Essendon AFL saga). ## Mechanism
AOD-9604 is a synthetic 16-residue C-terminal fragment of hGH (Tyr-hGH 177-191, disulphide-cyclized) corresponding to the historically-designated "lipolytic domain." Its defining claim — lipolysis WITHOUT engaging the GH receptor or raising IGF-1 — is **verified** at the level of receptor-binding assays, IGF-1 measurements, and glucose/insulin endpoints in animals and ~893 pooled humans: it does not bind/activate the GHR, does not stimulate IGF-1 production, and did not impair carbohydrate metabolism. The lipolytic action itself is described as a dual lipid effect (stimulation of lipolysis/fat oxidation + inhibition of lipogenesis via acetyl-CoA carboxylase), with β3-adrenergic signalling a permissive/downstream contributor to the chronic effect rather than the direct receptor target. The sponsor-affiliated review is candid that the precise mechanism "is not understood." All mechanism data are preclinical/biomarker; native peptide PK is very short (serum half-life ~4 min). No human SC-route PK exists. See report §1.

## Evidence Summary
- **FAILED Phase-2b obesity RCT** — Metabolic Pharmaceuticals "OPTIONS" study, 536 obese adults, 24 weeks, oral 0.25/0.5/1 mg vs placebo; **negative primary weight-loss endpoint**, obesity program discontinued ~2007. `rct` (sponsor disclosure + sponsor-affiliated peer-reviewed account). [[library/peptides/aod-9604/research-report]]
- Herd 2005 — ~300 obese adults, oral 1–30 mg/day × 12 wk; modest signal (1 mg ~2.6–2.8 kg vs ~0.8 kg placebo) with biologically-odd inverse dose-response. **Conference abstract, NOT PubMed-indexed; not confirmed by the definitive trial.** `rct (abstract)`
- Preclinical lipolysis (animal/in-vitro, clearly NOT human fat loss): Ng 2000 (PMID 11146367, obese Zucker rats); Heffernan 2001 (PMID 11673763, obese ob/ob mice). `animal`

Counter-evidence / caveats:
- **No proven human fat-loss efficacy.** The one large well-powered human test (Phase-2b) was negative; safety was good but **safety is not efficacy**.
- OA/cartilage is preclinical-only: Kwon & Park 2015 (PMID 26275694, rabbit OA — chief benefit required HA co-administration) + sponsor patent (US 10,111,933 B2, in-vitro). Zero registered human OA trials.
- The "Paradigm Phase-II knee-OA" claim is a **molecule conflation** (pentosan polysulfate, not AOD-9604) and is **excluded**.
- ~100% single-lineage concentration (Monash/Ng → Metabolic Pharmaceuticals); no sponsor-independent replication using independently sourced material.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): **well-tolerated / GH-axis-sparing** in the characterized clinical peptide across six sponsor RCTs (~893 humans) — AE profile indistinguishable from placebo, **no IGF-1 rise, no glucose impairment**, no treatment-related SAEs. Failed on **efficacy, not safety**. (Most frequent AE: headache, similar in placebo.)
- adverse effects (anecdotal): not characterized for the gray-market SC product; injection-site reactions possible (precautionary).
- contraindications:    no established evidence-based contraindications (unapproved, never labeled). Precautionary: **pregnancy/lactation** (no human data; avoid); general **uncharacterized-injectable caution** (no long-term human data, no human SC-route data, FDA-flagged immunogenicity/aggregation/impurity for uncharacterized product). These are regulatory/practical cautions, not a known toxicity signal.
- monitoring:           no validated AOD-9604-specific activity biomarker. Conservative: [[biomarkers/igf-1]] baseline/periodic (to confirm the *absence* of any rise — a rise would signal off-target/impurity), fasting glucose/HbA1c (document absence of metabolic harm), injection-site assessment.
- known interactions:   none characterized.
