# MK-677

## Metadata
- class: other — an **oral, non-peptidic small molecule** acting as an agonist of the ghrelin receptor / growth-hormone-secretagogue receptor type 1a (GHS-R1a); Merck development codes **MK-0677** and **L-163,191** (handled/dosed as the mesylate salt). It is **not a peptide** and **not a GHRH analogue** — filed under `class: other` precisely because it is a small-molecule peptidomimetic, not a peptide drug.
- evidence_tier: B — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. By a wide margin the **most-studied GH secretagogue in long-term human RCTs** (2-year Nass 2008; 12-month n=563 Sevigny 2008; 18-month n=292 Murphy 2001; Phase IIb hip-fracture Adunsky 2011; plus shorter dose-finding trials). Held **below S/A** because (a) the efficacy that replicates is **biomarker/lean-mass only** (GH↑, IGF-1↑, FFM ≈+1.1 kg) and does NOT translate into strength, function, or any disease endpoint; (b) its **two major disease programs FAILED** (Alzheimer's, hip-fracture); (c) ~**75%** of human primaries carry a Merck (originator) author/sponsor footprint.
- risk_tier: medium — well-characterized from a rich RCT safety record, but **unapproved** (Merck discontinued; never approved anywhere) and carrying a **dominant metabolic harm signal** (raised fasting glucose, reduced insulin sensitivity, raised HbA1c — the predictable consequence of sustained GH/IGF-1 elevation) plus a **congestive-heart-failure (CHF) signal** that terminated a frail-elderly trial early (~6.5% vs ~1.7%). Not lowest-risk (not a wholly uncharacterized research chemical), not highest-risk (no acute-lethality signal in the trial record) — hence **medium**.
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-20

> **Read this first.** MK-677 (ibutamoren) is an **ORALLY-ACTIVE, NON-PEPTIDIC SMALL MOLECULE** ghrelin-receptor (GHS-R1a) agonist — it is **NOT a peptide** and **NOT a GHRH analogue**, a distinction that is load-bearing for both its pharmacology and its regulation. It is taken **orally, once daily**; do not conflate its two time constants — the **molecular elimination half-life is ~4–6 h** (≈4.7 h in the reviewed pharmacology), while the **pharmacodynamic GH/IGF-1 effect persists ~24 h**, which is what permits once-daily dosing despite the short molecular half-life. MK-677 is the **most-studied GH secretagogue in long-term human RCTs**, and the honest verdict is sobering: it **durably raises GH/IGF-1 and lean body mass** (Nass 2008: fat-free mass **+1.1 kg**, IGF-1 restored to the young-adult range) **but does NOT improve strength or physical function**, and its **two major disease programs FAILED** — the Alzheimer's Phase 2b/3 trial (Sevigny 2008, **n=563**: IGF-1 rose +72.9% at 12 months yet no cognitive or functional benefit) and the hip-fracture-recovery trial (Adunsky 2011 — efficacy endpoint missed **and** terminated early for a **CHF signal, ~6.5% vs ~1.7%**). A rise in IGF-1 ("target engagement") is a **biomarker, not a clinical benefit**; lean-mass ↑ ≠ functional benefit. The **dominant safety issue is METABOLIC** — raised fasting glucose, reduced insulin sensitivity, raised HbA1c — alongside the CHF/fluid-retention signal. About **75%** of the human primaries are Merck-lineage. Regulatory state: **never approved** (Merck discontinued; successor LUM-201 still investigational); **NOT a lawful dietary ingredient** (FDA Dec-2025 warning letters to Prime Sports Nutrition and Agebox Inc.); as a **small molecule it is NOT part of the §503A peptide compounding action / FR Doc 2026-07361** that captures BPC-157/TB-500 etc. — that framework simply does not reach it; **WADA-prohibited at all times under S2** (growth-hormone-secretagogue / ghrelin-mimetic subgroup; ibutamoren named). Full depth + citations: [[library/peptides/mk-677/research-report]]. Operator-specific fields (status, baseline biomarkers, goals, stack) intentionally blank — goal-agnostic library entry.

## Mechanism
MK-677 mimics endogenous ghrelin at the **ghrelin receptor / GHS-R1a** (a class-A seven-transmembrane GPCR), a receptor distinct from the GHRH receptor. Agonist binding signals through **Gαq/11 → phospholipase C → IP3/DAG → intracellular Ca²⁺ release**, which in pituitary somatotrophs drives exocytotic **GH release** — mechanistically separate from GHRH-class drugs (sermorelin/CJC-1295/tesamorelin), which act via Gs/cAMP/PKA. It **amplifies the physiological pulsatile pattern** of GH (raising pulse height and trough concentrations without changing pulse number) and, downstream, sustains a rise in hepatic **IGF-1** across the 24-h dosing interval. Its defining differentiator is **oral once-daily activity** (oral bioavailability >60%): every comparable secretagogue (ipamorelin/GHRP on the same receptor; GHRH analogues on the separate receptor) is an injectable peptide, whereas MK-677's non-peptide spiroindane structure confers oral bioavailability and metabolic stability. See report §1.

## Evidence Summary
- Nass 2008 (PMID 18981485) — n=65 healthy older adults (60–81), 2-year modified-crossover RCT, 25 mg/day; **fat-free mass +1.1 kg (95% CI 0.7–1.5; P<0.001)** and IGF-1 restored to young-adult range, but **NO change in strength or physical function**; fasting glucose +0.3 mmol/L (P=0.015). The pivotal long-term trial. `rct`
- Chapman 1996 (PMID 8954023) — n=32 healthy elderly (64–81), double-blind dose-finding (2/10/25 mg); 24-h GH +97±23%, IGF-1 restored to young-adult range; fasting glucose rose 5.4 → 6.8 mmol/L at 4 wk. Established the 25 mg dose (biomarker result, not function). `rct`
- Sevigny 2008 (PMID 19015485) — **n=563 mild-moderate Alzheimer disease**, 12-month RCT; IGF-1 +60.1% (6 wk) / +72.9% (12 mo) yet no benefit on ADAS-Cog/CIBIC-plus/ADCS-ADL/CDR-sob → **FAILED**. The canonical "biomarker↑ ≠ benefit" trial. `rct`
- Adunsky 2011 (PMID 21067829) — **n=123 hip-fracture recovery**, Phase IIb RCT; IGF-1 +51.4 ng/mL (p<0.001) but functional endpoint NOT met and **terminated early for a CHF signal (~6.5% vs ~1.7%)**; "unfavorable safety profile" → **FAILED + safety-halted**. `rct`

Counter-evidence / caveats:
- **Bodybuilding / athletic-performance efficacy is ABSENT** — no admissible randomized or controlled human trial demonstrating MK-677 improves athletic performance, strength, hypertrophy, or recomposition was located. The closest controlled data (Nass, Svensson) measured body composition in older/obese non-athletes and showed **lean-mass gain WITHOUT strength or functional gain**. Performance/physique claims rest on vendor copy/anecdote (excluded as grounding).
- ~75% of human primaries carry a Merck author/sponsor footprint; **no Merck-independent disease-endpoint trial exists**, and the two independent-of-physique disease programs (AD, hip-fracture) are precisely the two that FAILED (report §4).

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): **METABOLIC is dominant** — raised fasting glucose, reduced insulin sensitivity, raised HbA1c (replicated across trials; intrinsic to chronic GH excess). Increased appetite (on-target ghrelin agonism); transient mild lower-extremity edema (fluid retention); arthralgia/myalgia (GH-excess-like); minor/inconsistent cortisol rise; **CHF/cardiac signal** that halted the frail-elderly hip-fracture trial (~6.5% vs ~1.7%, population-specific).
- adverse effects (anecdotal): lethargy / fatigue / somnolence (probable class effect, not rigorously quantified in the controlled trials).
- contraindications:    diabetes / impaired fasting glucose / impaired glucose tolerance (trial-replicated glycemic worsening); CHF or significant cardiac risk / reduced cardiac reserve (fluid-retention CHF signal); active or recent malignancy (theoretical IGF-1 proliferation risk); pregnancy and lactation (no human data).
- monitoring:           **fasting glucose + HbA1c** (primary surveillance, baseline + on-treatment; add OGTT if pre-diabetic — defects can appear only on challenge); **IGF-1** to bound target engagement/exposure; clinical surveillance for **edema and cardiac decompensation** (weight gain, dyspnea, peripheral edema). See [[biomarkers/fasting-glucose]], [[biomarkers/hba1c]], [[biomarkers/igf-1]].
- known interactions:   none specifically characterized; additive metabolic burden expected with anything raising GH/IGF-1 or worsening glycemia.

## Trial Status
- linked experiment:    none
- baseline biomarkers:  # fasting glucose, HbA1c, IGF-1 if an N=1 were attempted
- expected response window: GH/IGF-1 within ~2–4 weeks; lean-mass changes over months (per report §2)
- stopping criteria:    discontinue for new/worsening edema with weight gain or dyspnea (possible CHF), for a clinically meaningful rise in fasting glucose/HbA1c into or toward the diabetic range, or for any sign of cardiac decompensation. The hip-fracture trial's early termination is the precedent.
