## Mechanism
Single acylated peptide agonizing THREE receptors — GIP + GLP-1 + **glucagon** (GIP-dominant potency). The glucagon arm
is the differentiator: it raises **energy expenditure + hepatic fat oxidation** on top of GIP/GLP-1 appetite/glycemic
effects — the leading explanation for the class-leading weight loss. Glucagon agonism also raises hepatic glucose
output, so net glucose-lowering depends on the incretin arms dominating (they did in trials). Acylation → once-weekly. `[in_vitro]/[animal]/[mechanism_review]`

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (Phase 2; Phase 3 topline not peer-reviewed):
- Jastreboff, NEJM 2023 (PMID 37366315), n=338 — obesity **−24.2% at 12 mg/48 wk** (not plateaued). `[rct]`
- Rosenstock, Lancet 2023 (PMID 37385280), n=281 — T2D HbA1c −2.02%, weight −16.94%. `[rct]`
- Sanyal, Nat Med 2024 (PMID 38858523), n=98 — hepatic fat **−81 to −82%**. `[rct]`
- TRIUMPH-1 (NCT05929066), Phase 3 topline ~−28.3% at 80 wk — **press/ADA-2026 stage, NOT peer-reviewed.** `[regulatory]`

Counter-evidence / the honest state (SOURCING is the binding caveat):
- **INVESTIGATIONAL — not approved anywhere; NO legitimate prescription/compounding pathway.** Any "retatrutide" for
  sale is gray-market/research-chemical (unverified purity/dose, no pharmacovigilance). **Posture: wait for approval.**
- Same incretin **goal mismatch** (reduces lean mass −6.5 kg; NO muscle-building/recovery RCT). Thin long-term safety
  (~48-wk Phase-2 horizon); distinctive dose-dependent HR rise (~+6.7 bpm peak at wk 24).

## Risk Profile
- **risk_tier: moderate.** Phase-2 safety only — NO approved label, NO long-term data.
- Common AEs: dose-dependent GI (nausea up to ~60% at 12 mg). **Distinctive HR increase** (~+6.7 bpm peak at wk 24, then
  attenuates). **Cutaneous hyperesthesia ~7%** (retatrutide-specific). SAEs ~4% (= placebo at Phase 2).
- Glucose: net improvement despite glucagon agonism (monitor). Thyroid: **class/rodent C-cell caution only** (MTC/MEN2 by
  extension; no retatrutide-specific human data; no boxed warning — no label).
- **Anti-doping: NOT separately WADA-listed** (only sema/tirze are monitored; none prohibited). `[regulatory]`
