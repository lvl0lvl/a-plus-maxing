# Retatrutide

## Metadata
- class: peptide — a **triple GIP + GLP-1 + glucagon receptor agonist** (development code **LY3437943**); a single synthetic, acylated peptide engineered to activate all three incretin/glucagon-family receptors simultaneously, not a co-formulation. It is the first molecule of the **triple-agonist** class to reach mid-stage clinical development. The added **glucagon-receptor (GCGR)** arm is the defining mechanistic distinction from tirzepatide (dual GIP/GLP-1) and semaglutide (GLP-1-only).
- manufacturer / development stage: **Eli Lilly and Company.** Administered by **once-weekly subcutaneous injection**. **INVESTIGATIONAL — not approved by the FDA, EMA, or any regulator for any indication anywhere in the world**; the most advanced published evidence is **Phase 2**, with the Phase 3 **TRIUMPH** program ongoing.
- evidence_tier: B — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. A **strong, well-conducted Phase-2 RCT program** (Jastreboff 2023 NEJM, obesity; Rosenstock 2023 Lancet, T2D; Sanyal 2024 Nat Med, MASLD), all in tier-1 journals with monotonic dose-response — materially better than the unapproved no-RCT peptides elsewhere in this wiki. **BUT** there is **no regulatory approval, no peer-reviewed Phase-3 outcome publication** (the TRIUMPH program is ongoing, with only sponsor topline press releases), and the evidence is **100% single-sponsor (Eli Lilly) with zero independent replication**. High design quality but early phase, pre-publication confirmatory data, and total sponsor concentration → a solid mid-tier, not a top tier.
- risk_tier: experimental — retatrutide is **INVESTIGATIONAL and unapproved**, with **no long-term safety data, no cardiovascular-outcomes data, and no human thyroid/C-cell data**. The added glucagon arm brings a **dose-dependent heart-rate increase**, and Phase 2 surfaced a **retatrutide-specific cutaneous-hyperesthesia signal (~7% vs 1%)** and a **transient eGFR dip**. Gray-market "research-chemical" use is **especially premature**: there is no approved product, the confirmatory trials are unfinished, and the supply chain has **documented sterility-test failures**.

> **Read this first.** Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a **triple GIP / GLP-1 / glucagon receptor agonist** — and the **glucagon arm is the defining differentiator** from tirzepatide (dual GIP/GLP-1) and semaglutide (GLP-1-only). In its pivotal Phase 2 obesity trial it produced a least-squares-mean weight reduction of about **−24.2% at 48 weeks on the 12 mg dose** in adults with obesity and **no diabetes** — the **largest mean weight loss reported to date for any incretin-based pharmacotherapy in a clinical trial (Jastreboff 2023)**. That headline must always be carried with two hard qualifiers attached: it is **INVESTIGATIONAL, NOT APPROVED anywhere**, and the entire human efficacy/safety record is **Phase 2 only** — mid-stage, dose-finding, short-duration data that cannot be assumed to predict Phase 3 results, long-term durability, or population-scale safety. The confirmatory **Phase 3 TRIUMPH** program is **ongoing**, and the two readouts that exist are **sponsor TOPLINE PRESS RELEASES** — not peer-reviewed, not presented in full, and explicitly **not to be treated as verified efficacy**. The glucagon component is the mechanistic basis for retatrutide's distinctive emphasis on **energy expenditure and hepatic fat clearance** (liver fat fell ~86% on imaging in a Phase-2a MASLD substudy) — but it also drives a **dose-dependent heart-rate increase** (the signature cardiac caution for an agent with no CV-outcome data), and Phase 2 surfaced a **retatrutide-specific cutaneous hyperesthesia** signal (~7% vs 1% placebo) and a **transient early eGFR decrease that recovers**. The evidence base is **100% Eli Lilly-sponsored** with no independent replication. **Gray-market retatrutide is especially premature**: there is no approved product, no lawful compounding pathway (it cannot be compounded under federal law and is not a component of any FDA-approved drug), and China-sourced research-chemical material has shown **documented sterility-test failures despite "high-purity" COAs**. In sport, retatrutide is **NOT prohibited** by WADA — the 2026 Monitoring Program tracks only **semaglutide and tirzepatide** markers, and retatrutide is not named anywhere on the 2026 Prohibited List. Full prescribing detail, the gray-market/sterility landscape, and the non-English-literature survey live in the sibling layer files of this wiki: [[library/peptides/retatrutide/research-report]]. ## Mechanism
Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a single synthetic, acylated peptide engineered by Eli Lilly to act as a **triple agonist at three incretin/glucagon-family receptors simultaneously: the GIP receptor (GIPR), the GLP-1 receptor (GLP-1R), and the glucagon receptor (GCGR)**. It is built on an incretin/glucagon-family backbone (not the GLP-1 backbone of semaglutide), carries non-coded amino-acid substitutions (Aib at position 2 to resist DPP-4 cleavage; α-methyl-L-leucine at position 13), and is acylated with a fatty-diacid moiety that drives reversible albumin binding and a **terminal half-life of ~6 days**, supporting once-weekly SC dosing. It is a deliberately **balanced tri-agonist** — more potent at GIPR (~8.9×) and less potent than native ligands at GCGR (~0.3×) and GLP-1R (~0.4×). Functionally, the **GLP-1R + GIPR arms reduce caloric intake** (glucose-dependent insulin secretion, satiety, slowed gastric emptying), while the differentiating **GCGR arm increases energy expenditure and promotes hepatic fat oxidation** — combining "calorie-in" suppression with "calorie-out" and liver-fat clearance. The balanced design lets the strong insulinotropic GIP/GLP-1 arms offset glucagon's intrinsic glucose-raising effect, producing net glycemic improvement rather than worsening. See report §1.

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (all Phase 1/2 — INVESTIGATIONAL, none confirmed by a peer-reviewed Phase 3 outcome publication):
- Jastreboff AM, Kaplan LM, et al. *NEJM* 2023 (PMID 37366315; NCT04881760) — `rct` Phase 2, **n=338** adults with obesity WITHOUT diabetes, 48 wk; LS-mean weight **−24.2% at 48 wk (12 mg)** vs −2.1% placebo — the headline, largest incretin weight-loss signal yet, but **Phase 2 / investigational**. [[library/peptides/retatrutide/research-report]]
- Rosenstock J, Frias J, et al. *Lancet* 2023 (PMID 37385280; NCT04867785) — `rct` Phase 2 T2D, **n=281**, vs placebo + dulaglutide 1.5 mg; HbA1c −2.02% and weight −16.94% at top dose (12 mg) at 24/36 wk.
- Sanyal AJ, et al. *Nat Med* 2024 (PMID 38858523) — `rct` Phase 2a MASLD, **n=98** (no diabetes); relative **liver fat −86% at 48 wk (12 mg)** by MRI-PDFF, steatosis resolved in 86% — an **imaging surrogate**, not histology or hard outcome.
- Coskun 2022 (*Cell Metab*) / Urva 2022 (*Lancet*) — discovery + Phase 1/1b proof-of-concept (PK ~6-day t½; HbA1c/weight signal in T2D).

Notes / counterweights:
- **TRIUMPH Phase 3** program is ongoing; the only readouts are **sponsor topline press releases** (TRIUMPH-1, 21 May 2026; TRIUMPH-4, 11 Dec 2025) — **not peer-reviewed and explicitly NOT verified efficacy**.
- Cross-class comparison vs semaglutide/tirzepatide is **indirect/cross-trial**, not head-to-head; no randomized comparator exists.
- Evidence base is **100% single-sponsor (Eli Lilly)**; no independent replication. All magnitudes are Phase 1/2 — **promising but unconfirmed**.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): **GI-dominant**, dose-related, titration-mitigated, mostly mild-to-moderate (nausea up to ~45–60%, vomiting up to ~19–26%, diarrhea, constipation, decreased appetite at top doses); AE-driven discontinuation up to 16% (12 mg). **Glucagon-component heart-rate increase** — dose-dependent, peaks ~week 24 then declines (the signature cardiac caution; arrhythmias of special interest ~6% overall). **Retatrutide-specific cutaneous hyperesthesia** (~7% vs 1% placebo, dose-dependent, none severe/serious). **Transient early eGFR decrease that recovers** (net dose-dependent increase by 48 wk; UACR fell). Despite the glucagon arm, **no net hyperglycemia** (HbA1c fell up to ~2 points in T2D) and **no clinically significant hypoglycemia**; transient ALT >3×ULN in ~1% with no hepatotoxicity signal; rare serious pancreatitis (one case, 12 mg). All short-term (24–48 wk) Phase-2 data only.
- adverse effects (anecdotal): # n/a — a specific mean heart-rate ~bpm magnitude from a secondary vendor source was DROPPED as non-Tier-1; only the dose-dependent rise / week-24 peak (Tier-1 trial) is carried
- limits / class-extrapolated: **No long-term, cardiovascular-outcome, or human thyroid/C-cell safety data exist** (max human exposure 48 wk). The GLP-1-class rodent C-cell tumor concern is a **theoretical class extrapolation**, NOT demonstrated for retatrutide in humans.
- contraindications:    # precautionary only (no formal list — unapproved): pregnancy/breastfeeding; pre-existing tachyarrhythmia / conditions where sustained HR rise is hazardous; history of pancreatitis or symptomatic gallbladder disease; concomitant insulin/sulfonylurea (titration hypoglycemia); class-precautionary caution in personal/family MTC or MEN-2 (extrapolated from GLP-1 labeling)
- monitoring:           heart rate (signature glucagon effect); [[biomarkers/hba1c]] + glucose (especially early / on insulin or secretagogues); renal function/eGFR (transient early dip); weight + nutritional adequacy during rapid loss; pancreatitis/biliary symptoms; injection-site and cutaneous-sensitivity symptoms
- known interactions:   additive hypoglycemia with insulin/sulfonylureas; delayed gastric emptying (class) may affect oral-drug absorption
