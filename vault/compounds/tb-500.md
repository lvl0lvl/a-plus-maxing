# TB-500

## Metadata
- class: peptide (marketed as the synthetic acetylated heptapeptide **Ac-LKKTETQ**, residues 17–23 of Thymosin Beta-4 — NOT the full-length 43-aa Tβ4 that the literature actually studies)
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]] (animal/preclinical-dominant; **no human RCT has met a primary endpoint for any Tβ4 indication**; zero human evidence for the marketed athletic use)
- risk_tier: experimental — unapproved, no controlled human safety dataset, real bidirectional (net solid-tumor pro-metastatic) cancer signal, WADA-banned
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-19

> **Read this first.** The gray-market product sold as "TB-500" is the **Ac-LKKTETQ fragment**, a *different molecule* from the full-length 43-aa **Thymosin Beta-4 (Tβ4)** that nearly all research used. There is **no completed human RCT that met its primary endpoint** for any Tβ4 indication, and **zero human evidence** for the muscle/tendon/"healing" use TB-500 is sold for. FDA **removed** TB-500 from the 503A interim Category 2 list ~April 2026 (one of twelve peptides removed in a parallel FDA 503A interim-bulks action, per FDA's Category-2 page) — this is **not approval** and it remains non-compoundable pending the 23–24 July 2026 PCAC review (the meeting scheduled by FR Doc. 2026-07361, which lists TB-500 among the seven peptides under review). It is **prohibited at all times in sport** (WADA S2.3, Growth Factors). Full depth + every citation: [[library/peptides/tb-500/research-report]].

## Mechanism
Tβ4 is a 43-residue G-actin–sequestering peptide; its actin-monomer binding (2 Å crystal structure, Irobi 2004) underpins cytoskeletal remodeling, cell migration, angiogenesis (HIF-1α/VEGF and Notch pathways), and anti-inflammatory/anti-fibrotic effects in injury models. No validated cell-surface receptor is established; Ku80 is the best-characterized intracellular binding partner. Mechanistic evidence is genuinely deep at the molecular/biochemical level but is largely full-length Tβ4, not the marketed fragment. See report §2.

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (research-execution-verified; weight order):
- Sosne 2023 (SEER-1, NCT02600429) — human Phase 3, neurotrophic keratopathy, n=18, healing 6/10 vs 1/8, **p=0.0656 (primary endpoint MISSED)**; trial **terminated** (business decision). `rct`
- Wang 2021 (NL005, PMID 34346165) — first-in-human recombinant full-length Tβ4, IV, SAD n=54 / MAD n=30, well tolerated to 25 µg/kg; safety/PK only, no efficacy. `open_label`
- Bock-Marquette 2004 (PMID 15565145) — animal cardiac repair (ILK/PINCH/Akt); foundational preclinical. `animal`

Counter-evidence (load-bearing):
- Spurney 2010 (PMID 20126456) — mdx muscle: histological regeneration **without functional benefit** (grip strength, cardiac function unchanged). `animal`
- Zhou/Pu 2012 (PMID 21907210) — **replication failure** of the epicardium→cardiomyocyte reprogramming claim (Tβ4 → fibroblasts/SMC, not cardiomyocytes). `animal`
- No in-vivo tendon-injury study exists despite tendinopathy marketing (only in-vitro scaffold work). See report §7.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): human AE data limited to full-length recombinant Tβ4 (mild–moderate, no SAE/DLT to 25 µg/kg, Wang 2021; 28-day follow-up). No long-term human safety data. No human data for the fragment.
- adverse effects (anecdotal): injection-site reactions, fatigue/head-pressure (forum-level, `anecdote_aggregate`, qualitative only)
- contraindications:    active or recent malignancy (bidirectional tumor signal — pro-metastatic in solid-tumor over-expression models, Cha 2003; mechanism-of-evidence caveat: genetic over-expression ≠ exogenous dosing); tested athletes (WADA-banned); pregnancy/lactation (no data)
- monitoring:           no validated efficacy/PD biomarker exists (honest position); prudent: new/growing-lesion surveillance + baseline malignancy screening, injection-site/hypersensitivity exam
- known interactions:   none characterized
