# LL-37

## Metadata
- class: peptide (human cathelicidin; the 37-residue C-terminal antimicrobial peptide of hCAP18 / the CAMP gene product; vitamin-D-inducible)
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]] (preclinical-dominant; 4 small/weak human administration trials incl. a Phase IIb that FAILED its primary endpoint; no approval)
- risk_tier: experimental — unapproved; **genuinely dual-natured** (a disease-driving autoantigen, not a clean "good" peptide); no controlled chronic human safety data
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-20

> **Read this first.** LL-37 is the body's own cathelicidin antimicrobial peptide (37-aa, from hCAP18/CAMP, induced by vitamin D). It is **genuinely DUAL-NATURED** — antimicrobial / LPS-neutralizing / wound-healing / angiogenic, but **also a documented driver of inflammatory and autoimmune DISEASE**: it is a **psoriasis autoantigen** and the founding **self-DNA/RNA → pDC → type-I-interferon** trigger (psoriasis, lupus/SLE, rosacea, atherosclerosis), and has **context-dependent CANCER effects** (pro-tumor in ovarian/lung/breast/melanoma; anti-tumor in colon/gastric). Human evidence for EXOGENOUS administration = **4 small trials**: a positive Phase I/II venous-leg-ulcer study (Grönberg 2014) that **failed to replicate on the primary endpoint of the larger Phase IIb (Mahlapuu 2021 "HEAL")**; a diabetic-foot-ulcer trial improving only granulation; and a 4-patient melanoma dose-finding study with documented skin toxicity — **no Phase III, no approval.** Endogenous-LL-37-as-biomarker observational data is a separate thing from administration evidence. FDA: not approved; placed in 503A Cat-2 (2023) then **REMOVED ~Apr 2026** as one of twelve peptides dropped from interim Category 2 in a parallel FDA 503A bulks action (FDA Category 2 page) — removal ≠ approval/compoundable; LL-37 is **not** on the Jul-2026 PCAC agenda set by FR Doc 2026-07361 (deferred to a separate PCAC by ~Feb 2027). WADA: not individually named but **prohibited at all times under S0** (no human approval). Native LL-37 is rapidly degraded by proteases (short half-life; no established human PK figure — vendor "90–120 min" claims are unverified). Full depth + citations: [[library/peptides/ll-37/research-report]]. Operator fields blank — goal-agnostic library entry.

## Mechanism
Cationic amphipathic α-helix. Protective arm: membrane disruption (carpet/toroidal-pore), LPS neutralization, FPRL1/FPR2-mediated chemotaxis, wound-healing/angiogenesis. Pathogenic arm: LL-37 complexes with self-DNA/RNA and activates plasmacytoid dendritic cells via TLR9/TLR7 → type-I interferon, the founding mechanism of psoriasis and a driver of lupus/autoimmunity; it also signals pro-tumor in several cancers. The SAME biochemical property (cationic charge + nucleic-acid/membrane affinity) drives both outcomes. All mechanism evidence is in-vitro/animal — no human mechanism data. See report §2 + §8.

## Evidence Summary
- Grönberg 2014 (PMID 25041740) — first-in-human VLU Phase I/II RCT, n=34; positive at 0.5 mg/mL (p=0.003), bell-shaped dose-response. `rct`
- Mahlapuu 2021 "HEAL" (PMID 34687253) — VLU **Phase IIb, n=148, PRIMARY ENDPOINT NEGATIVE** (closure 26.5/24.7/25.3%, ns); only a post-hoc ≥10 cm² subgroup was positive. `rct`
- Carretero 2008 (PMID 17805349) / Koczulla 2003 (PMID 12782669) — preclinical wound-healing + angiogenesis (animal). `animal`

Counter-evidence / the dark side (equal weight):
- Lande 2007 (PMID 17873860) + Lande 2014 (PMID 25470744) — LL-37 = psoriasis autoantigen + self-DNA→pDC→IFN driver. `mechanism`/`cohort`
- Cancer **bidirectional**: pro-tumor ovarian (Coffelt 2009, PMID 19234121) / lung (Ji 2019, PMID 31149039) ; anti-tumor colon/gastric. Receptor/tissue-dependent.
- Miranda 2023 (PMID 37480520) DFU: granulation only, closure ns; melanoma trial (NCT02225366) n=4 dose-finding, documented cutaneous toxicity.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): human-trial AEs limited (Grönberg no safety concerns; HEAL 7.4% SAE = background, not drug-attributable; melanoma n=1 cutaneous toxicity); concentration-dependent hemolysis/cytotoxicity preclinically; no controlled chronic human safety data.
- adverse effects (anecdotal): injection-site reactions (`anecdote_aggregate`, qualitative).
- contraindications:    **psoriasis / autoimmune disease (LL-37 is an autoantigen → flare/autoimmunity risk — mechanistic contraindication)**; active/recent malignancy (context-dependent pro-tumor); known hypersensitivity. Pregnancy/lactation (no data).
- monitoring:           no LL-37-specific validated biomarker; objective monitoring via [[biomarkers/hs-crp]] (hs-CRP) + inflammation markers (IL-6/ESR), CBC/platelets, skin exam — general assays, not validated surrogates of LL-37 activity.
