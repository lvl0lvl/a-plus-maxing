# Semax

## Metadata
- class: peptide (synthetic heptapeptide ACTH(4-7)PGP; sequence Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro / MEHFPGP; derived from the ACTH(4-7) melanocortin "message" domain with an engineered C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro tripeptide for metabolic stability; IMG-RAS / Ashmarin-Myasoedov; intranasal 0.1%/1.0% aqueous drops)
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. Russia-registered for acute ischemic stroke and optic nerve disease, based entirely on open-label, single-lineage Russian clinical studies (four stroke trials n=30–187; two optic neuropathy trials; one ALS trial with a negative primary neurological endpoint). No RCT. No Western trial. No systematic review or meta-analysis. The preclinical BDNF/NGF-induction mechanism is internally consistent across 20+ rodent studies but originates 90–95% from the inventor's own IMG-RAS network — zero independent non-Russian replication exists. The two genuinely independent Western publications on Semax address copper-chelation chemistry and forensic detection, not the BDNF-mechanism or cognitive pharmacology.
- risk_tier: experimental — not FDA/EMA-approved; no published human pharmacokinetic data; no controlled healthy-adult safety data; all clinical safety evidence from elderly Russian disease populations (population mismatch); Russia-registration predates ICH E6 GCP; single-lineage open-label evidence base; no confirmed CNS receptor target; grey-market supply chain with unverified purity/identity; WADA S0 status requires individual ADO adjudication
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-22

> **Honest framing — read before anything else.** Semax (MEHFPGP; ACTH(4-7)PGP) is a Russian-registered neuropeptide with a 20-year preclinical signal. Six load-bearing facts define what it is and what it is not.
>
> **(1) Identity/mechanism:** A synthetic ACTH-derived but **non-corticotropic** neuropeptide (no HPA activation, no cortisol stimulation — the non-corticotropic property is well-established by design). The proposed mechanism — BDNF/NGF-TrkB neurotrophin-cascade induction + melanocortin receptor signaling — is evidence tier C (preclinical, predominantly rodent). The "regulatory peptide" paradigm (pleiotropic cascade modulator) is a Russian-school framework, not adopted by Western mainstream pharmacology. No confirmed direct receptor (MC3R/MC4R/TrkB/p75NTR family) has been published for Semax by any group.
>
> **(2) Human efficacy — open-label ceiling:** Russia-registered for acute ischemic stroke and optic nerve disease, studied for cognitive impairment/ADHD/ALS. **Every human study is open-label, non-randomized, Russian-institutional, and single-lineage.** No RCT. No Western trial. No systematic review. No pre-registration. The sole human BDNF signal (Gusev 2018, n=110) is a single open-label study with no reported effect sizes. The ADHD use traces to a single *Medical Hypotheses* opinion paper. The ALS study produced a **negative result** on the primary neurological endpoint (only a secondary subjective QoL signal at day 10). Human brain network modulation confirmed by two placebo-referenced fMRI studies (n=24–52) — no clinical outcome link.
>
> **(3) Single-lineage concentration:** ~78–89% of verifiable English-language peer-reviewed publications carry authorship from the IMG-RAS/Myasoedov cluster or its direct institutional collaborators; for the mechanistic BDNF corpus this reaches ~90–95%. Myasoedov NF (co-inventor) appears on ≥20 core mechanistic studies across 40 years. **Zero independent non-Russian replication of the BDNF-induction mechanism exists.** The manufacturer (JSC Peptogen) is an IMG-RAS spinout — the science-producer is the commercial beneficiary. This is a structural concentration concern parallel to the BPC-157/Sikiric closed network — not fraud, but never independently stress-tested.
>
> **(4) Regulatory:** Russia-registered (0.1% and 1.0% intranasal, ЖНВЛП list ~2011; registration facts are author-reported via in-apparatus reviewers, not primary GRLS filings). Russia is NOT an ICH member; approval pathway was not harmonized with FDA/EMA Phase I/II/III. NOT FDA. NOT EMA. WADA S0 status requires individual ADO adjudication — athletes must verify with GlobalDRO.
>
> **(5) Safety limits:** No controlled healthy-adult safety data. No human PK data. All clinical safety evidence from elderly Russian patients with cerebrovascular disease or optic neuropathy — a population categorically different from healthy adult nootropic users. Formal contraindications: hypersensitivity, acute psychosis / psychotic disorder history, pregnancy/lactation.
>
> **(6) The NASA distinction:** N-Acetyl-Semax-Amidate (NASA) — the grey-market "enhanced" analog — is a chemically distinct entity (N-terminal acetylation + C-terminal amidation). N-terminal acetylation demonstrably alters copper-chelation chemistry and **abolishes the neuroprotective copper-chelation effect in vitro** (Tabbì 2016). NASA has **near-zero independent evidence of its own** — no human study, no animal pharmacology study, no PK study for NASA as a distinct compound. Its entire claimed evidence base is parent Semax data extrapolated by an unvalidated pharmacological equivalence assumption. Approved nowhere, not even Russia.
>
> **The bottom line:** A biologically plausible, Russia-registered neuropeptide with a genuine preclinical signal that is almost entirely single-lineage, open-label, and unreplicated outside its inventor network. Not a Western-validated nootropic. Full depth: [[library/peptides/semax/research-report]].

## Mechanism
Semax's ACTH(4-7) core (Met-Glu-His-Phe) is the melanocortin "message" domain, retaining CNS activity through MC3R/MC4R engagement while lacking the corticotropic sequences responsible for adrenocortical steroidogenesis — the non-corticotropic property is confirmed across preclinical studies and independent Western ACTH-fragment pharmacology (Strand 1993, NYU). The C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro tripeptide is not merely a stability scaffold: in permanent MCAO rat models, PGP alone activates neurotrophin gene transcription, contributing pharmacologically in its own right. The primary proposed downstream mechanism is upregulation of BDNF and NGF gene expression in rodent brain, with TrkB phosphorylation and downstream cAMP/PKA and MAPK/ERK signaling. Rat studies document 8-fold BDNF mRNA induction in glial cultures (30-minute exposure, Shadrina 2001), rapid region-specific Bdnf/Ngf mRNA increases in hippocampus/frontal cortex/brainstem (Agapova 2007, Shadrina 2010), saturable reversible binding in rat basal forebrain membranes (Kd 2.4 ± 1.0 nM, Dolotov 2006), and 1.4-fold BDNF protein elevation + 1.6-fold TrkB phosphorylation after a single dose (Dolotov 2006). Additional dimensions: monoaminergic modulation (striatal 5-HIAA +25% at 2 hours; dopamine release potentiation under amphetamine, Eremin 2005); anti-inflammatory gene suppression in ischemic brain (IL-1β, IL-6, Ccl3, c-Fos; RNA-Seq 394 DEGs, Filippenkov 2020); anti-apoptotic proteomics (JNK kinase + MMP-9 suppression, phospho-CREB elevation, Sudarkina 2021); and acute intracellular calcium frequency increase in hippocampal CA1 neurons (Kolbaev 2025). **Critical caveats:** The specific MC3R/MC4R/TrkB/p75NTR receptor responsible for Semax's CNS effects has not been identified — no competition binding study against any named receptor has been published. Human CNS bioavailability has never been quantified in any indexed peer-reviewed journal. The entire mechanism literature originates ~90–95% from the IMG-RAS/Myasoedov inventor lineage with zero independent replication. The BDNF-induction signal is consistently larger in ischemic rodent tissue than in healthy brain — the conditional nature of the induction may mean the mechanism is amplified in disease states, with implications for healthy-adult extrapolation that the evidence cannot address.

## Evidence Summary
All evidence tier C or below. No RCT. No independent replication. Strongest sources:

- [Gusev/Martynov/Kostenko et al. 2018, PMID 29798983] — n=110 post-stroke patients, open-label observational four-group design (early/late rehabilitation × Semax/no-Semax); first human plasma BDNF measurement — Semax-associated BDNF elevation correlated with Barthel Index improvement; no effect sizes reported; no pre-registration; authors are Gusev EI, Martynov MY, Kostenko EV, Petrova LV, Bobyreva SN (Pirogov RNRMU / Moscow rehabilitation network — no IMG-RAS/Myasoedov co-authorship); single open-label study. `open_label`. [[library/peptides/semax/research-report]]
- [Gusev/Skvortsova/Chukanova et al. 2005, PMID 15792140] — n=187 cerebrovascular insufficiency patients, open-label; "significant clinical improvement, stabilization of disease progress, reduced TIA/stroke risk"; no quantitative outcome data, no named scales, no control group. `open_label`.
- [Dolotov/Grivennikov/Myasoedov et al. 2006, PMID 16635254 + PMID 16996037] — rat basal forebrain radioligand binding (Kd 2.4 nM) + hippocampal BDNF/TrkB protein elevation + improved conditioned avoidance; the primary BDNF-mechanism anchor; IMG-RAS lineage; independent Western co-author (Engele, Leipzig) on one study. `animal`.
- [Medvedeva/Myasoedov/Dergunova et al. 2014 + 2017, PMID 24661604; PMID 28255762] — rat focal ischemia RNA-Seq (394 DEGs; immune/vascular gene suppression); highest-quality mechanistic preclinical data in the corpus. `animal`.
- [Lebedeva/Myasoedov et al. 2018, PMID 30225715; Panikratova/Myasoedov et al. 2020, PMID 32342318] — placebo-referenced resting-state fMRI in healthy volunteers (n=24 and n=52); Semax modulates default mode network + amygdala-temporal functional connectivity; randomization not documented; no clinical outcome endpoints. `open_label`.

Counter-evidence and structural limits:
- [Serdiuk/Myasoedov/Skvortsova et al. 2007, PMID 18379501] — n=27 ALS, open-label; **negative result on primary neurological endpoint** (no EMG or ALS-FRS change); only positive signal was ALSAQ-40 emotional state at day 10 (subjective, unblinded, single time point). The one published objective pre-specified endpoint in the entire Semax clinical corpus came back null.
- [Tabbì et al. 2016, PMID 27586814] — independent Western (University of Catania); N-terminal acetylation of Semax **abolishes** neuroprotective copper-chelation effect in neuroblastoma/endothelial cells; confirms pharmacological non-equivalence between parent Semax and NASA analog.
- [Mavrych et al. 2026, PMID 42021992, *Front Aging* 2026;7:1790247] — independent 2026 review explicitly characterizes non-approved peptides including Semax as "lacking long-term safety data and systematic validation" requiring "rigorous validation through well-designed clinical trials."
- Single-lineage limit: ~90–95% of the mechanistic BDNF corpus is IMG-RAS/Myasoedov-lineage; zero non-Russian independent replication of the core mechanism.

## Prescribing-Practice Layer
See [[library/peptides/semax/research-report]] §6 for full dosing context. No licensed prescribing practice for Semax exists in any Western jurisdiction.

- **Compounding pharmacy data sheets:** No admissible US-compounding data sheet. Semax is not on any FDA-cleared 503A bulk substances list; it is not an FDA-approved active pharmaceutical ingredient for compounding. No compounding pharmacy protocol for Semax found in the A4M/IFM/Seeds/International Peptide Society practitioner tier.
- **Practitioner reference texts:** No named A4M/IFM/Seeds/International Peptide Society monograph for Semax located.
- **Originator-group recommended dose (Russian registration):** 0.1% intranasal drops (1 mg/mL) for outpatient cerebrovascular and cognitive indications; 1.0% intranasal drops (10 mg/mL) for acute ischemic stroke and optic nerve disease. Stroke rehabilitation literature: 6,000 µg/day (6 mg/day) in divided intranasal doses over 10-day courses with 20-day intervals (Gusev 2018) — highest outpatient/rehabilitation dose; earlier acute stroke trials used 12 mg/day × 5 days (moderate severity) or 18,000 µg/day (18 mg/day) × 10 days (severe presentations, Gusev 1997) — making 18 mg/day the highest documented dose in the clinical literature overall. These doses pertain to the registered Peptogen pharmaceutical product at confirmed identity and concentration — they do not transfer to grey-market Semax powder or NASA analog.
- **Grey-market convention (NOT grounded in human PK):** Intranasal use of grey-market Semax nasal spray cited in nootropic communities. No vendor or community dose figure is cited here per sourcing standards — `vendor_label` and `anecdote_aggregate` sources cannot ground clinical dose guidance.
- **Gap:** No controlled human PK data quantifying systemic exposure, plasma half-life, or CNS bioavailability exists for any dose or formulation. Grey-market product concentrations are unaudited by any regulatory authority. Clinical doses from the Russian literature apply only to the registered Russian pharmaceutical formulation.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): reported as well-tolerated in Russian open-label studies of elderly cerebrovascular/optic-neuropathy patients: "minor percent of side-effects" (Gusev 2005, n=187); no serious adverse events in Gusev 2018 (n=110, 6 mg/day × 10-day courses); non-sedative; non-addictive in studied clinical populations. **Evidence ceiling:** all AE data from elderly disease populations under medical supervision — does not transfer to healthy adults. No long-term (≥1 year) safety data. No placebo-controlled AE ascertainment. No published human PK (half-life, systemic exposure, CNS penetration). Open-label designs with sponsor proximity systematically underreport AEs.
- adverse effects (anecdotal): nasal irritation, headache, transient activation/stimulatory effects, mood variability — forum-level reports; qualitative only, no pharmacovigilance
- contraindications:    **Formal Russian-label contraindications (from registered Peptogen pharmaceutical product labeling, as reported in secondary Russian literature):** (1) hypersensitivity to Semax or any formulation component; (2) acute psychosis or history of psychotic disorders (standard precautionary language for neuropeptides with dopaminergic/behavioral activation effects in Russian labeling); (3) pregnancy and lactation (no controlled human teratogenicity data). **Inferred clinical cautions (not formal label contraindications — practitioner-level reasoning from mechanism and hazard profile):** personal or family history of psychotic illness (population-direction concern for dopaminergic activation consistent with the Russian label rationale above, extended to at-risk relatives); concurrent psychoactive medications without medical supervision (theoretical pharmacodynamic addition with monoaminergic agents).
- monitoring:           [[biomarkers/hs-crp]] — general inflammatory baseline screen appropriate for any experimental protocol; **not** a Semax-specific pharmacodynamic endpoint. There is zero human evidence that Semax modulates systemic hs-CRP; Semax's anti-inflammatory mechanism claims are CNS-directed (rodent ischemia models only) and cannot be tracked via peripheral hs-CRP. Use: obtain a baseline value; if hs-CRP rises above 10 mg/L, evaluate for acute systemic pathology independent of Semax use; this threshold is a general clinical alert, not a Semax-specific signal. [[biomarkers/cortisol]] — primary purpose is to DOCUMENT the non-corticotropic claim: expected finding is NO HPA change (no morning cortisol elevation or suppression); a significant cortisol perturbation would be a clinically important observation that contradicts the compound's core safety design; morning serum or salivary cortisol provides the objective record. NOTE: no validated Semax efficacy biomarker exists — BDNF has not been validated as a clinical monitoring endpoint by Western standards; the primary efficacy monitoring must be cognitive/symptom-scale-based (MoCA, digit-span, attention tests) at baseline, mid-course, and post-course. Mood and behavioral diary (agitation, anxiety, mood instability — psychosis-contraindication surveillance). Nasal mucosal tolerability assessment at each use.
- known interactions:   no characterized human drug interactions; theoretical: pharmacodynamic addition with dopaminergic or serotonergic agents (monoaminergic modulation documented in rodents); neonatal ACTH-analog preclinical data suggest possible seizure-susceptibility modification in developmental pharmacology (mechanistic observation, not a confirmed human clinical contraindication)

## Trial Status
- linked experiment:    none
- baseline biomarkers:  n/a (library entry)
- expected response window: Russian clinical convention: effects observed within 5–10 day courses; fMRI studies document acute network changes within 5–20 minutes of a single dose; no validated timeline for cognitive enhancement in healthy adults — the studied disease populations had very different baseline states
- stopping criteria:    (1) hypersensitivity reaction — nasal anaphylaxis, urticaria, systemic flushing, or dyspnea → stop immediately; (2) any psychotic symptoms, hallucinatory states, acute agitation, or worsening of pre-existing psychiatric conditions → stop immediately; this is a labeled contraindication with a dopaminergic activation rationale; (3) no objective cognitive or symptom-scale improvement after two full standard courses (10-day each with 20-day interval) per the Russian clinical literature dosing pattern — no validated efficacy biomarker exists, so symptom-scale non-response is itself a meaningful null signal; (4) pregnancy recognition → stop immediately; (5) WADA prohibition confirmed by individual ADO ruling for an athlete subject to anti-doping rules → immediate cessation and NADO consultation; (6) hs-CRP >10 mg/L without intercurrent infection or other explanation → evaluate for acute systemic pathology; this is a general clinical threshold, not a validated Semax-specific pharmacodynamic stopping criterion

## Decision Notes
Per [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]], Semax at evidence_tier=C / risk_tier=experimental falls in the **trial-only / doctor-required** cell. The evidence tier is C (not D) because Semax is a genuine Russia-registered pharmaceutical with a 20-year preclinical signal, multiple open-label clinical studies in a real disease population, and a pharmacologically coherent non-corticotropic mechanism — this distinguishes it from a research chemical with no regulatory history. However, evidence tier C combined with risk_tier=experimental requires acknowledging: no confirmed receptor target, no published human PK, no Western RCT, no independent non-Russian replication of the core mechanism, all human data from single-lineage open-label sources, a population mismatch to healthy adults that the evidence base cannot address, and a grey-market supply chain with unverified purity. The NASA analog (N-Acetyl-Semax-Amidate) is evidentially more constrained: near-zero compound-specific evidence + N-terminal modification that demonstrably alters pharmacology + not approved anywhere — it should be assessed as evidence_tier=D / risk_tier=experimental. NASA is discussed within this Semax entry (see §5 The NASA Distinction in the research-report); there is no separate NASA compound page in this wiki.
