# Semaglutide

## Metadata
- class: peptide — an acylated, long-acting **GLP-1 receptor agonist** (a 94%-sequence-homology analogue of native human GLP-1(7-37); a peptide drug, not a small molecule, acting as a full agonist at the class-B GPCR GLP-1R)
- brands / routes: **Ozempic** (SC, once-weekly, T2D) and **Wegovy** (SC 2.4 mg, once-weekly, chronic weight management) are injectable; **Rybelsus** (oral, once-daily, T2D) is the tablet co-formulated with the absorption enhancer SNAC. All three are Novo Nordisk products.
- manufacturer: Novo Nordisk
- evidence_tier: A — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. Multiple large Phase-3 RCTs (SUSTAIN/PIONEER glycemic + STEP obesity programs) plus hard cardiovascular (SELECT) and renal (FLOW) outcome trials, with FDA + EMA approvals across three indications. One structural caveat keeps it honest: **single-sponsor concentration** (~76% of pivotal registrational primaries Novo Nordisk-funded), partially offset by adjudicated hard endpoints, double-blind placebo control, and independent FDA/EMA review.
- risk_tier: medium — approved and well-characterized, but with real adverse effects (GI-dominant; gallbladder; pancreatitis), a **rodent-derived thyroid C-cell boxed warning** (not human-demonstrated), **weight regain on discontinuation**, and **lean-mass loss** that together keep it doctor-required.
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-20

> **Read this first.** Semaglutide is a **genuinely approved, genuinely efficacious GLP-1 receptor agonist** with a landmark randomized-trial base — not a gray-market peptide. The **real benefits** are large and population-specific: substantial glycemic control in T2D (SUSTAIN/PIONEER, ~1.5–1.8% HbA1c at top injectable dose); **a mean ~14.9% weight loss in adults with obesity WITHOUT diabetes** (STEP-1, Wilding 2021, NEJM, PMID 33567185); a **~20% reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events in obesity WITHOUT diabetes** (SELECT, Lincoff 2023, NEJM, HR 0.80, PMID 37952131); and a **~24% reduction in a kidney-failure composite in T2D WITH CKD** (FLOW, Perkovic 2024, HR 0.76). The **real caveats** are equally concrete: **gastrointestinal adverse effects dominate** and are the main discontinuation driver; **weight is largely REGAINED on discontinuation** (~two-thirds of the loss within a year, STEP-1 extension); **lean/muscle-mass loss accompanies the weight loss**; gallbladder disease and (precautionary) pancreatitis are labeled concerns; the **thyroid C-cell / medullary-thyroid-carcinoma BOXED WARNING is RODENT-based and has NOT been demonstrated in humans**; and the 2023 **suicidality signal was investigated and has NOT been confirmed**. **Population matters everywhere**: T2D, non-diabetic obesity, and T2D+CKD are distinct populations with distinct magnitudes and distinct approved indications — figures are NOT interchangeable across them. **One integrity correction the reader must carry: the pivotal obstructive-sleep-apnea trial (SURMOUNT-OSA) studied TIRZEPATIDE, a different drug — do NOT credit OSA benefit to semaglutide.** Regulatory: FDA-approved (Ozempic 2017, Rybelsus 2019, Wegovy 2021), EMA-approved; WADA **does NOT prohibit** semaglutide. ## Mechanism
Semaglutide is an acylated, long-acting analogue of the incretin hormone GLP-1(7-37), engineered with three modifications (Aib8 to resist DPP-4 cleavage, Arg34, and a C18 fatty di-acid lipidation at Lys26 via a γGlu-2×OEG linker) that achieve >99% albumin binding and a ~1-week half-life. As a full GLP-1R agonist it reproduces and amplifies GLP-1's physiology: glucose-dependent insulin secretion from β-cells, glucagon suppression from α-cells, minor delay in gastric emptying, and central/hypothalamic satiety with reduced energy intake — explaining its dual glycemic + weight profile. Oral Rybelsus co-formulates SNAC to enable ~0.4–1% gastric absorption. See report §1.

## Evidence Summary
- Wilding 2021 (STEP-1, PMID 33567185) — n=1,961 adults with obesity **without** diabetes, RCT, 68 wk, semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo; **mean −14.9% weight** (vs −2.4%; difference −12.4 pp). `rct` — the landmark obesity result. [[library/peptides/semaglutide/research-report]]
- Lincoff 2023 (SELECT, PMID 37952131) — n=17,604 adults with established CVD + obesity **without** diabetes, RCT; 3-point MACE 6.5% vs 8.0%, **HR 0.80** (95% CI 0.72–0.90). `rct`
- Marso 2016 (SUSTAIN-6, PMID 27633186) — n=3,297 **T2D**, RCT CVOT; MACE HR 0.74; **integrity note: significant diabetic-retinopathy-complications increase, HR 1.76**. `rct`
- Perkovic 2024 (FLOW, PMID 38785209) — n=3,533 **T2D WITH CKD**, RCT; kidney-failure composite HR 0.76 (24% RRR). `rct`

Notes / counterweights:
- Magnitude is population-specific: ~14.9% (non-diabetic obesity, STEP-1) vs ~9.6% (T2D obesity, STEP-2, PMID 33667417) at the same 2.4 mg dose.
- Durability requires continuation: STEP-4 withdrawal (PMID 33755728) shows regain off-drug.
- **SURMOUNT-OSA studied TIRZEPATIDE, NOT semaglutide** — do not credit OSA benefit here.
- Evidence base is single-sponsor-concentrated (~76% Novo); no independent RWE cohort opened in-corpus.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): **GI dominant** and the main discontinuation driver (Wegovy: nausea 44%, diarrhea 30%, vomiting 24%, constipation 24%; dose-dependent, concentrated in titration window); **gallbladder disease** modestly increased (cholelithiasis ~1.6% vs 0.7%); **pancreatitis** (labeled, precautionary); **gastroparesis/ileus + peri-operative aspiration** (ASA hold ~1 wk for weekly formulations); **diabetic retinopathy** complications signal (SUSTAIN-6, attributed to rapid glycemic improvement); **weight regain on stop** (~two-thirds within a year); **lean-mass loss** (~39–45% of weight lost).
- adverse effects (boxed/qualified): **thyroid C-cell / MTC BOXED WARNING is RODENT-based, NOT human-demonstrated** (human meta-analysis null, certainty very low due to event rarity); **suicidality investigated but NOT confirmed** (RWE cohort points the other way, Wang 2024).
- contraindications:    personal/family history of **medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC)** or **MEN 2**; **pregnancy** (not recommended; long half-life → stop well before conception); prior serious hypersensitivity to semaglutide/excipients.
- monitoring:           [[biomarkers/hba1c]] (glycemic), [[biomarkers/fasting-glucose]], eGFR + serum creatinine (renal/AKI during GI volume loss), weight, gallbladder symptoms, lipase if pancreatitis suspected, retinal status in prior diabetic retinopathy, hypoglycemia if co-given with insulin/sulfonylureas.
- known interactions:   delayed gastric emptying may affect concomitant oral-drug absorption; additive hypoglycemia with insulin/sulfonylureas.
