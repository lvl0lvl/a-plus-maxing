## Mechanism
Engineered GLP-1 analog (94% homology) with Aib8 (DPP-4 resistance) + Arg34 + a C18 fatty-diacid acylation that drives
>99% albumin binding → once-weekly dosing (t½ ~1 wk). GLP-1 receptor agonism → glucose-dependent insulin secretion,
glucagon suppression, slowed gastric emptying; central (area postrema/hypothalamus) appetite suppression → reduced
energy intake. CV benefit partly pleiotropic. `[regulatory]/[mechanism_review]`

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (tier S, all verified):
- STEP 1, NEJM 2021 (PMID 33567185), n=1961 — −14.9% weight vs −2.4% placebo. `[rct]`
- SELECT, NEJM 2023 (PMID 37952131), n=17,604 — MACE HR 0.80 (~20% reduction), non-diabetic CVD+obesity. `[rct]`
- SUSTAIN-6, NEJM 2016 (PMID 27633186) — HbA1c/weight + MACE HR 0.74. `[rct]`
- FLOW (CKD, HR 0.76), STEP-HFpEF, ESSENCE (MASH resolution 62.9% vs 34.3%) — all verified RCTs. `[rct]`

Counter-evidence / the honest state (GOAL MISMATCH — first-class caveat):
- For the operator's goal (post-illness deconditioning recovery / rebuilding lean mass) this is the **wrong tool**: a
  weight-LOSS drug that **reduces absolute lean mass** (−9.7% in STEP 1 DEXA), whose benefit **reverses on stopping**
  (STEP 4 regain), and which suppresses appetite. **NO RCT** for lean-mass building or recovery (verified absence).
- Use only when fat loss is genuinely indicated, with resistance training + protein + MTC/MEN2 screen.

## Non-English Literature Coverage
- Pivotal program is global multicenter but **English-published** (NEJM/Lancet) — **no distinct non-English primary

## Prescribing-Practice Layer
- On-label titration is standard (Wegovy → 2.4 mg SC weekly; Ozempic → 2 mg; Rybelsus 14 mg oral) `[regulatory]`.
  "Microdosing" is an off-label practice with **NO RCT support** (`practitioner_protocol`, dose/route only). See
  [[library/peptides/semaglutide/practitioner-layer]].

## Pharmacokinetics
t½ **~1 week (~165 h)**, >99% albumin-bound; SC bioavailability ~89%; oral (Rybelsus) ~1% (SNAC, fasting + ≥30 min
wait); steady state 4–5 wk `[regulatory: FDA labels; mechanism_review PMC9272494]`.

## Risk Profile
- **risk_tier: moderate.** **Boxed warning:** thyroid C-cell/MTC — contraindicated with personal/family MTC or MEN2.
- Common AEs (Wegovy): nausea 44%/16%, diarrhea 30%/16%, vomiting 24%/6%, constipation 24%/11% `[regulatory]`.
- Serious: pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, AKI (volume depletion), retinopathy complications (T2D), ileus, **NAION**
  (EMA PRAC June 2025: very rare ~1/10,000; cohort PMID 38958939 HR 4.28), **aspiration under anesthesia** (delayed
  gastric emptying — inform anesthesia team).
- Contraindications: MTC/MEN2, hypersensitivity, pregnancy (≥2-mo washout). Caution: prior pancreatitis, gastroparesis.
- **Anti-doping: NOT prohibited** — WADA Monitoring Program (monitored, not banned, as of the 2026 list). `[regulatory]`

## Regulatory
FDA-approved: Ozempic (T2D 2017), Rybelsus (oral T2D 2019), Wegovy (obesity 2021; CV-risk reduction 2024), Ozempic CKD
(2025). EMA-approved. **Compounding wind-down:** FDA declared the shortage resolved Feb 2025 → 503A enforcement ended
Apr 2025, 503B May 2025; compounded **salt forms (sodium/acetate) are NOT the approved API**. Cost ~$1,000–1,350/mo list.
`[regulatory]`
