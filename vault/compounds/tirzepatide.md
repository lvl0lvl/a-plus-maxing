## Mechanism
Single 39-aa peptide agonizing BOTH GIP and GLP-1 receptors, **imbalanced/GIP-biased** (near-native GIPR potency but
~5× lower affinity / ~20× lower cAMP potency at GLP-1R; cAMP-over-β-arrestin bias). C20 fatty-diacid acylation →
>99% albumin binding → ~5-day half-life, once-weekly. Added GIPR engagement proposed to improve adipose insulin
sensitivity + lipid clearance atop GLP-1 appetite/glycemic effects (GIP-specific weight pathway partly inferential). `[in_vitro]/[mechanism_review]`

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (tier S, verified):
- SURMOUNT-1, NEJM 2022 (PMID 35658024), n=2539 — weight −20.9% (15 mg) vs −3.1% placebo. `[rct]`
- SURMOUNT-5, NEJM 2025 (NCT05822830), n=751 — superior to semaglutide for weight (−20.2% vs −13.7%). `[rct]`
- SURPASS-2, NEJM 2021 (PMID 34170647) — superior to semaglutide on HbA1c + weight in T2D. `[rct]`
- SURMOUNT-OSA, NEJM 2024 (PMID 38912654) — AHI −25 to −29 events/h → first FDA OSA drug approval. `[rct]`

Counter-evidence / the honest state (GOAL MISMATCH — first-class caveat):
- Same as semaglutide but more potent: ~25% of the mass lost is **lean tissue** (lean −10.9% vs fat −33.9%, SURMOUNT-1
  DXA substudy, total weight −21.3%); benefit **reverses on stopping** (SURMOUNT-4: continued −25.3% vs placebo −9.9%);
  **NO RCT** for muscle building/recovery (lean-sparing only via an add-on
  anti-myostatin drug). Catabolic-leaning, not anabolic — off-target for post-illness rebuilding.

## Pharmacokinetics
Half-life **~5 days**; Tmax ~24 h; SC bioavailability ~80%; 99% albumin-bound; Vd ~10.3 L; steady state ~4 wk `[regulatory: FDA label / NDA 215866]`.

## Risk Profile
- **risk_tier: moderate.** **Boxed warning:** thyroid C-cell/MTC — contraindicated MTC/MEN2.
- Common AEs (Zepbound): nausea 25–29%, diarrhea 19–23%, constipation 11–17%, vomiting 8–13% `[regulatory]`.
- Serious: pancreatitis, gallbladder disease (0.6% vs 0%), AKI, hypoglycemia (with insulin/SU), anaphylaxis/angioedema,
  **aspiration under anesthesia** (delayed gastric emptying).
- Contraindications: MTC/MEN2, hypersensitivity, pregnancy. Monitoring: thyroid/pancreatitis symptoms, renal in GI AEs, glucose.
- **Anti-doping: NOT prohibited** — WADA Monitoring Program (2026), same as semaglutide. `[regulatory]`

## Regulatory
FDA: Mounjaro (T2D May 2022), Zepbound (obesity Nov 2023; **OSA Dec 2024** — first OSA drug). EMA: Mounjaro Sept 2022.
**Compounding wind-down:** shortage resolved (FDA Dec 19 2024) → 503A Feb 2025, 503B Mar 2025; compounded not approved.
Cost ~$1,080/mo list. `[regulatory]`
