## Mechanism
A synthetic analog of human growth-hormone-releasing hormone, GHRH(1-44), with a trans-3-hexenoyl group on the
N-terminal tyrosine that blocks DPP-4 cleavage (native GHRH is degraded within minutes). It binds the pituitary
GHRH receptor → stimulates synthesis and **pulsatile** release of endogenous GH → raises hepatic IGF-1. Because the
GH pulse is endogenous, normal negative feedback is preserved (the claimed advantage over tonic exogenous rhGH).
GH-mediated lipolysis is **visceral-fat-selective** — the basis of the approved indication. `[regulatory]/[mechanism_review]`

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (all in HIV-associated lipodystrophy unless noted):
- Falutz 2007, NEJM, n=412 — VAT −15.2% vs +5.0% placebo at 26 wk; IGF-1 +81%; TG −50 mg/dL `[rct]` (PMID 18057338).
- Stanley 2014, JAMA, n=50 — VAT −42 cm²; liver fat −2.9% net `[rct]` (PMID 25038357).
- Stanley 2019, Lancet HIV, n=61 — hepatic fat −37% relative `[rct]` (PMID 31611038).
- Badran 2026 meta-analysis — 5 RCTs, VAT −27.71 cm² `[meta_analysis]` (PMID 41545261).
- Baker 2012, Arch Neurol, n=152 — the ONLY non-HIV RCT; endpoint = **cognition**, not body comp `[rct]` (PMID 22869065).

Counter-evidence / the honest state:
- **Population transfer is THE caveat.** Every body-composition RCT is in HIV-lipodystrophy. **No admissible RCT exists**
  for body composition / recovery in healthy, general, or post-illness HIV-negative adults (verified absence).
- **Benefit reverses on discontinuation** — continuous dosing required.
- EMA application **withdrawn 2012** (insufficient long-term benefit-risk); US compounding restricted (503A Category 2).

## Pharmacokinetics
Measured human PK (FDA label): SC bioavailability **<4%**; half-life **26 min (healthy) / 38 min (HIV)** multi-dose
(11 min single-dose for Egrifta WR); Cmax ≈ 2875 pg/mL; Tmax 0.15 h; AUC ~34% higher in HIV `[regulatory]`. Pop-PK:
clearance ~1060 L/h, Vd ~200 L (PMID 25358450) `[open_label/pop-PK]`.

## Risk Profile
- **risk_tier: moderate.** Approved-drug safety record, but real watch-items.
- Contraindications: active malignancy (pro-GH/IGF-1), HPA-axis disruption/hypopituitarism, pregnancy, hypersensitivity `[regulatory]`.
- Common AEs (vs placebo): injection-site 25%/14%, arthralgia 13%/11%, myalgia 6%/2%, peripheral edema 6%/2%, paresthesia 5%/2% `[regulatory]`.
- **IGF-1 elevation:** 36% exceed +3 SDS at 26 wk → monitor IGF-1; discontinue on persistent >+3 SDS `[regulatory]`.
- **Glycemic:** new HbA1c ≥6.5% in 5% vs 1% placebo (HR ~3.3) → monitor fasting glucose / HbA1c `[regulatory]`.
- Monitoring: baseline + interval IGF-1 and glucose/HbA1c; stop on persistent IGF-1 >+3 SDS, dysglycemia, or acute critical illness.

## Regulatory
FDA-approved 2010-11-10 (NDA 022505, Theratechnologies) for **HIV-associated lipodystrophy ONLY**; Egrifta SV (2019),
Egrifta WR (CRL Jan 2024 → approved Mar 2025). **EMA: never approved** (application withdrawn June 2012). Health Canada:
approved (same indication). **WADA-prohibited at all times (S2.2.4, GH-releasing factors; named by token).** US compounding
restricted — tesamorelin is a **deemed biologic** (deemed-BLA effective 2020-03-23), barring bulk 503A/503B compounding
(it earlier appeared in the now-restructured interim 503A Category 2). Off-label body-comp use is not insurance-covered. `[regulatory]`
