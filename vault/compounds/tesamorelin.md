# Tesamorelin

## Metadata
- class: peptide — synthetic analogue of human growth-hormone-releasing factor (GRF / GHRH): the full 44-amino-acid human GRF(1-44) sequence with an N-terminal **trans-3-hexenoyl (C6)** cap on Tyr1 (synonym N-hexenoyl-Tyr1-hGRF(1-44); dev code TH9507)
- brands / sponsor: **Egrifta** (2010), **Egrifta SV** (single-vial, 2019), **Egrifta WR** (weekly-reconstitution, 2025); developed and held by **Theratechnologies Inc.** (Montréal)
- evidence_tier: B — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. The **only FDA-approved GHRH analogue**, backed by two coordinated pivotal Phase 3 RCTs (n≈806 pooled) + 52-week safety extension + an independent 2026 meta-analysis. Above tier C. Not S/A because (a) the proven efficacy is a **single narrow population/endpoint** — reduction of excess visceral abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy; (b) the efficacy base is **single-sponsor-concentrated** (Theratechnologies study drug/sponsorship behind 100% of efficacy primaries; no manufacturer-independent confirmatory program); and (c) the off-label uses people actually want (anti-aging, bodybuilding, athletic, healthy-adult fat loss) have **zero human efficacy data**.
- risk_tier: medium — an **approved Rx with a well-characterized, monitorable, reversible** safety profile (label + RCT data), not "experimental"; but GH-axis/IGF-1 elevation, a glucose/diabetes signal, four formal contraindications, and the need for IGF-1 + fasting-glucose/HbA1c monitoring keep it firmly doctor-required.

> **Read this first.** Tesamorelin is the **ONLY FDA-APPROVED GHRH analogue** in this cluster. It was approved in **November 2010** for one thing — *"the reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adult patients with lipodystrophy"* (exact label wording) — and that approval rests on **real Phase 3 RCT efficacy**: visceral adipose tissue (VAT) fell ~15% at 26 weeks, deepening to ~18% at 52 weeks on continued dosing, and the effect was **visceral-selective, weight-neutral, and REVERSED on discontinuation** (VAT re-accumulated toward baseline). **Critical honesty:** that efficacy is established **only for the narrow approved HIV-lipodystrophy population and the VAT endpoint**. The beyond-label domains — **NAFLD/liver-fat and cognition — are INVESTIGATIONAL** (and the liver-fat work is entirely in HIV-positive populations; the one cognition signal is a single small/short trial). **General anti-aging, bodybuilding, athletic performance, and healthy-adult fat loss have ZERO human efficacy data** — those uses are extrapolation, not evidence. The evidence base is also **single-sponsor-concentrated**: Theratechnologies study drug or sponsorship sits behind 100% of the efficacy primaries, with no manufacturer-independent confirmatory program. Tesamorelin is **prohibited at all times in sport** (WADA S2.2.4, where it is named explicitly) and is **NOT approved in the EU** (the marketing application was withdrawn in the face of a negative CHMP opinion). The FDA brand is an expensive specialty Rx (~$3,000/month), which feeds a gray-market/research-chemical channel outside the approval. ## Mechanism
Tesamorelin reproduces endogenous GHRH physiology. It binds and activates the **GHRH receptor (GHRH-R / GRF receptor)** — a class B (secretin-family) GPCR on the **somatotrophs of the anterior pituitary** — with potency similar to native GRF, stimulating synthesis and **pulsatile release of endogenous GH**, which in turn raises IGF-1 and IGFBP-3 (canonical Gsα → adenylyl cyclase → cAMP → PKA signalling). Because it works **upstream** at the pituitary, it augments the body's own pulsatile GH axis rather than replacing it with flat exogenous rhGH, preserving feedback/pulsatility, and its effects are **fully reversible on discontinuation**. The N-terminal trans-3-hexenoyl cap on Tyr1 blocks DPP-IV cleavage, extending the otherwise seconds-to-minutes survival of native GHRH enough to trigger a physiologic GH pulse (human t½ on the order of minutes). GH then drives hepatic IGF-1 production and peripheral lipolysis (the basis for the visceral-fat effect). See report §1.

## Evidence Summary
- **Falutz 2007** (PMID 18057338, *NEJM*) — n=412, RCT (LIPO-010), HIV-lipodystrophy, tesamorelin 2 mg SC daily ×26 wk: **VAT −15.2% vs +5.0% placebo** (P<0.001), IGF-1 +81.0%, triglycerides improved. `rct` [[library/peptides/tesamorelin/research-report]]
- **Falutz 2010 pooled** (PMID 20554713, *JCEM*) — pooled N=806 RCT: VAT treatment effect **−15.4% / −24 cm²**, **subcutaneous fat unchanged** (visceral-selective, weight-neutral), maintained at 52 wk without tachyphylaxis. `rct/pooled`
- **Falutz 2010** (PMID 20101189, *JAIDS*) — n=404, 52-wk two-phase confirmatory RCT: VAT deepens to ~−18% on continued dosing; **re-accumulates toward baseline on switch to placebo** (reversal). `rct`
- **Badran 2026** (PMID 41545261, *Obes Res Clin Pract*) — meta-analysis of 5 RCTs (HIV-lipodystrophy): VAT MD −27.71 cm², hepatic fat MD −4.28%, no SAT reduction. `meta_analysis`

INVESTIGATIONAL (real signal, no approval, HIV-confined or single-trial):
- **Stanley 2019** (PMID 31611038, *Lancet HIV*) — NAFLD/hepatic fat + limited fibrosis progression; **all participants HIV-positive**. `rct`
- **Baker 2012** (PMID 22869065, *Arch Neurol*) — cognition (executive function p=0.005) in **HIV-negative** older adults/MCI; single small/short surrogate-endpoint RCT. `rct`

Counter / null / nuance:
- **Clemmons DR 2017** (PMID 28617838, *PLoS One*) — n=53 T2DM, 12 wk: no alteration of insulin response/glycemic control; HbA1c +0.1% (2 mg) vs −0.5% placebo. `rct`

**ABSENT domains (the finding is the absence):** bodybuilding/physique/athletic performance, general/healthy-adult fat loss, anti-aging/longevity, and fracture/bone outcomes — **NO human efficacy data of any design.** (One non-HIV muscle-wasting trial, COPD NCT01388920, was terminated with no usable efficacy data.)

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): injection-site reactions (most characteristic, ~25% all-events vs 14% placebo over 26 wk), arthralgia, myalgia, peripheral edema, paresthesia/hypoesthesia, hypersensitivity (4%); supraphysiologic IGF-1 in a meaningful minority (47% >2 SDS / 36% >3 SDS at 26 wk). `regulatory`/`rct`
- glucose signal (both ways): trial means largely unchanged, but the label reports more patients crossing the diabetic HbA1c threshold (5% vs 1%; **intent-to-treat OR 3.3, CI 1.4–9.6**). `regulatory`/`rct`
- adverse effects (anecdotal): off-label/non-HIV use occurs at non-label doses in unstudied populations — any extrapolation of these AE rates is a [route/dose-extrapolation] not supported by the label.
- contraindications:    (1) **HPA-axis disruption** (hypophysectomy, hypopituitarism, pituitary tumor/surgery, head irradiation/trauma); (2) **active malignancy**; (3) **known hypersensitivity** to tesamorelin or excipients (**including mannitol**); (4) **pregnancy**.
- monitoring:           **IGF-1** (keep in normal range), **fasting glucose / HbA1c**, clinical signs of fluid retention; screen for malignancy before initiation; retinopathy in diabetics. See [[biomarkers/igf-1]].
- known interactions:   GH-axis class concerns (neoplasm promotion via IGF-1, glucose intolerance, fluid overload, increased mortality in acute critical illness).
