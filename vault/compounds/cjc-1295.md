# CJC-1295

## Metadata
- class: peptide (synthetic GHRH analogue — a **tetrasubstituted form of human GH-releasing factor hGRF(1-29) = GHRH(1-29)**; exists as two molecules: **with DAC** = Drug Affinity Complex, albumin-binding; and **without DAC** = "Modified GRF 1-29")
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]] (the entire admissible base is **one** biomarker-only human pharmacology study (Teichman 2006) plus **two** corporate-originated preclinical primaries; **zero human efficacy evidence** — the only efficacy-powered trial was terminated with no posted results; ~100% of the discovery-stage efficacy/PK primaries trace to a single originator, ConjuChem)
- risk_tier: experimental — unapproved drug, no controlled long-term human safety dataset; never FDA-approved; the only registered efficacy trial halted after a participant death; the days-long with-DAC half-life means any adverse effect is slow to reverse; not lawfully compoundable (503A); prohibited at all times in sport (WADA S2.2.4)
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-06-20

> **Read this first.** "CJC-1295" is not one molecule, it is **two**, and almost every number attached to the name is meaningless until you say which. **CJC-1295 WITH DAC** ("Drug Affinity Complex") carries a C-terminal maleimido-lysine that covalently binds circulating albumin (at albumin Cys34) in vivo, giving a measured terminal half-life of **~5.8–8.1 days** — this is the molecule in the only published human trial (Teichman 2006) and the only one ConjuChem ever put into a registered efficacy trial. **CJC-1295 WITHOUT DAC = "Modified GRF 1-29"** is the *same* tetrasubstituted GRF(1-29) backbone but **without** the albumin-binding linker, so it is cleared in roughly **30 minutes** and is the form stacked daily with [[compounds/ipamorelin]] in gray-market practice. **PK, efficacy, and trial outcomes are NEVER cross-attributed between the two.** The honest evidence state: the only human **efficacy** trial — ConjuChem's with-DAC Phase II in HIV-associated visceral obesity, **NCT00267527** — was **TERMINATED on 17 July 2006 after a participant died**; causation to the drug was **never established** (the attending physician's working assessment was pre-existing occult coronary disease), and **no CJC-1295 trial of any kind has ever met a body-composition, anti-aging, or wound-healing efficacy endpoint.** The robust GH (2–10×) and IGF-1 (1.5–3×) rises that are real and durable are **biomarkers, not proven clinical benefit.** Regulatorily: **not FDA-approved**; **NOT** among the twelve peptides removed from interim 503A Category 2 by the April 2026 FDA action; the PCAC **voted against** adding it to the positive 503A Bulks List on **4 December 2024**; and WADA **S2.2.4** names it explicitly as prohibited at all times. The load-bearing efficacy/PK evidence is concentrated in a **single corporate originator (ConjuChem)** across both the preclinical and clinical layers. Full depth + citations: [[library/peptides/cjc-1295/research-report]]. Operator fields blank — goal-agnostic library entry.

## Mechanism
CJC-1295 is a **GHRH-receptor agonist** (not a ghrelin agonist). It acts at the GHRH-R — a class B GPCR on anterior-pituitary somatotrophs — via Gαs → adenylyl cyclase → cAMP → PKA → CREB, driving both acute GH release and de-novo GH synthesis, and downstream hepatic IGF-1 production. The four backbone substitutions (D-Ala²/Gln⁸/Ala¹⁵/Leu²⁷) confer protease resistance (notably DPP-IV blockade); the **DAC** adds a C-terminal Nε-maleimidopropionamide-lysine that covalently bonds albumin Cys34, converting a minutes-scale pulsatile signal into days-long continuous exposure. Human sampling (Ionescu 2006) shows the with-DAC form **preserves pulse shape but markedly raises basal/trough GH (~7.5×)** — it is this trough elevation, not a change in pulse amplitude/frequency, that drives the IGF-1 rise. The mechanism is real and coherent; whether chronically elevated non-pulsatile GH/IGF-1 is clinically beneficial was never answered in a completed human trial. See report §2.

## Evidence Summary
- Teichman 2006 (PMID 16352683) — 2× Phase 1 ascending-dose RCT, healthy adults (with-DAC): GH 2–10×, IGF-1 1.5–3×, t½ 5.8–8.1 d; **biomarker only**, no clinical efficacy endpoint. `rct`/PK
- Jetté 2005 (PMID 15817669) — rat discovery/PK; identifies CJC-1295 as the albumin-bioconjugate lead, plasma-detectable beyond 72 h; all authors ConjuChem. `animal`
- Alba 2006 (PMID 16822960) — GHRH-knockout mouse; once-daily CJC-1295 normalizes growth/body composition (dose-frequency gradient); ConjuChem-supplied compound. `animal`

Counter-evidence / caveats:
- **No human efficacy endpoint ever met** (any indication, either form).
- NCT00267527 (with-DAC Phase II, HIV visceral obesity) — **TERMINATED 17 Jul 2006 after a participant death; no results posted** (causation NOT established).
- **Concentration alert:** ~100% (inclusive, 2/2) of the located preclinical efficacy/PK primaries trace to ConjuChem; the human efficacy/PK layer also traces to ConjuChem. No independent ConjuChem-free efficacy/PK primary exists.
- Zero admissible no-DAC human data of any design.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): only Teichman 2006 (with-DAC) — "no serious adverse reactions"; generally mild/transient injection-site reactions, flushing, headache. Circulating per-event incidence percentages are **UNVERIFIED** against the primary. No controlled long-term human safety dataset; no FDA-approved label.
- adverse effects (class-derived, NOT observed in the CJC-1295 trial): GH-axis fluid-retention complex (arthralgia, edema, paresthesia, carpal tunnel), impaired glucose tolerance/insulin resistance (GH is diabetogenic), headache; theoretical IGF-1-mediated proliferation concern (**associational, not causal**); pituitary desensitization under continuous non-pulsatile DAC exposure (most DAC-specific theoretical concern; human magnitude unestablished).
- adverse effects (anecdotal): a real participant death occurred in the with-DAC Phase II (NCT00267527) — **event partially verified, causation NOT established** (attending judgement = occult coronary disease). Online "CJC-1295 deaths" claims are overstated.
- contraindications:    active/recent malignancy; pre-existing diabetes or impaired glucose tolerance; significant/uninvestigated cardiac or fluid-retention status; pregnancy/breastfeeding (no data). The days-long with-DAC half-life amplifies every concern (no rapid washout).
- monitoring:           **serum IGF-1 interpreted against age- and sex-specific reference ranges** (keep within, not above, normal); **fasting glucose + HbA1c** for glycaemic drift. No CJC-1295-specific validated biomarker exists.
- known interactions:   none formally characterized; ipamorelin co-administration is a stack convention (no human efficacy data).
