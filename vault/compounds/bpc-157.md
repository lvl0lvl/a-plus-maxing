## Mechanism
A synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide asserted to be a fragment of a human gastric-juice protein
(no definitive parent protein / accession confirmed). Proposed, mechanistically-demonstrated-in-vitro/animal-only
mechanisms: FAK–paxillin signalling, VEGFR2/eNOS-mediated angiogenesis, nitric-oxide-system modulation,
and growth-factor/tendon-fibroblast effects. **No specific receptor has been characterised** (no Kd/binding data).

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources (all preclinical):
- Sikiric/Zagreb group (originating lab) — broad rat/rabbit tendon, GI-ulcer, vascular, neuro models `[animal]` — the bulk of the corpus.
- Chang Gung (Taiwan, one corroborating lab, shared author J.S. Pang) — tendon-fibroblast + VEGFR2 angiogenesis `[animal]/[in_vitro]`.
- Huang 2015, FMMU/Xi'an (distinct mainland-Chinese center) — alkali-burn wound, SD rat n=58 `[animal]` (non-English layer).

Counter-evidence / the honest state:
- **No completed human RCT with published results.** NCT02637284 (Phase 1, n=42, oral) status UNKNOWN, no results posted; NCT07437547 (Phase 2, SC, hamstring) recruiting. `[rct]`
- **Single-lab concentration: ~85%** of in-vivo efficacy primaries (11/13) are Sikiric/Zagreb; only tendon-repair + VEGFR2-angiogenesis have any independent in-vivo support.

## Non-English Literature Coverage
- Russian / Soviet: surveyed (eLibrary.ru, CyberLeninka) — **no admissible primaries** (community consumes, doesn't produce).
- Chinese: surveyed — **1 new admissible primary** (Huang 2015, *Drug Des Devel Ther*, FMMU/Xi'an); corpus [Xu 2020] is also FMMU.
- Croatian (originator): Zagreb-group doctoral dissertations exist — same originating lab, ground no number.
- Translation notes: CNKI/Wanfang full-text not directly browsable; metadata-level for Croatian repos.

## Prescribing-Practice Layer
- **No admissible compounding-pharmacy clinical data sheet located** (searched Empower, Tailor Made, Belmar, Strive, Hallandale, Revive, Morgan, Vios). No USP/NF monograph.
- Convention (NOT evidence-based): ~250–500 mcg SC once–twice daily, 4–8 wk (acute) to 8–12 wk (chronic) cycles `[practitioner_protocol]`.

## Pharmacokinetics
No measured human PK. The widely-cited "~15-min half-life" is a **rat-IV** value (He 2022), not human — no human t½/Cmax/AUC/%F exists.

## Risk Profile
- **risk_tier: experimental.** Long-term human safety uncharacterised.
- Contraindications (candidate, mechanism/convention-based): active malignancy (pro-angiogenic), pregnancy/lactation, anticoagulant/antiplatelet + bleeding disorders, cardiovascular disease, severe hepatic/renal impairment.
- Monitoring: vital signs, ECG, hepatic/renal/metabolic/thyroid labs (from the n=2 human IV pilot panel) `[open_label]`.
- Stopping criteria: **no validated human-evidence-based stopping rule** (cycling is convention only).

## Regulatory
Unapproved everywhere. FDA placed BPC-157 in 503A **Category 2** (2023-09-29); **removed ~April 2026**
(Federal Register 2026-07361, Docket FDA-2025-N-6895), PCAC review 23–24 July 2026 — **removal does NOT
confer approval or lawful compounding**. WADA-**prohibited (S0)** at all times. TGA Schedule 4 + Appendix D.
