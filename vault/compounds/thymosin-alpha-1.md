## Mechanism
A 28-amino-acid N-acetylated cleavage fragment of prothymosin alpha (thymic origin). Acts as an immune **rebalancer**:
engages Toll-like receptors (TLR9/TLR2; TLR3/4/7 implicated) on dendritic cells and monocytes → DC maturation via
MyD88/p38-MAPK/NF-κB/IRF → Th1 program (IFN-γ, IL-2, IL-12), while the same TLR9 + type-I-IFN signaling co-activates
IDO/Treg — so it restores exhausted T-cell/NK function AND restrains over-inflammation, rather than blunt stimulation. `[mechanism_review]/[animal/in_vitro]`

## Evidence Summary
Strongest sources:
- TESTS, BMJ 2025 (PMID 39814420), n=1,089 — the largest, double-blind sepsis trial: **NEGATIVE** (28-d mortality HR 0.99). `[rct]`
- ETASS, Crit Care 2013 (PMID 23327199), n=361 — earlier, borderline sepsis benefit (RR 0.74). `[rct]`
- Sepsis meta 2025 (PMID 40969554), 11 RCTs — OR 0.73 BUT driven by small single-center trials (high-quality subgroup NS). `[meta_analysis]`
- Hepatitis B meta 2001 (PMID 11736720) — delayed virological response (OR 2.67 at 12 mo post-tx). `[meta_analysis]`
- Elderly flu-vaccine RCT 1989 (PMID 2642497), n=90 — higher antibody response. `[rct]`

Counter-evidence / the honest state:
- **The efficacy story shifted:** the best-quality sepsis trial (TESTS) is null; the positive meta is a small-trial artifact.
- **Goal-fit gap: NO RCT** for general post-illness recovery / deconditioning in healthy adults (verified absence).
- US **doubly restricted:** not FDA-approved AND removed from 503A Category 2 (Sept 2024).

## Non-English Literature Coverage
- The RCT base is heavily **Chinese-authored** (TESTS, ETASS, the sepsis meta, key hep-B RCTs) — but published in
  English-language international journals (BMJ, Crit Care, Clin Infect Dis, J Chin Med Assoc), so citable without
  translation. CNKI-only abstracts not mined to the anti-fabrication bar. See [[library/peptides/thymosin-alpha-1/non-english-layer]].

## Prescribing-Practice Layer
- Only admissible protocol-tier source = **2025 Chinese Expert Consensus** (Infect Microb Dis, DOI 10.1097/IM9.0000000000000176).
  No admissible Western `practitioner_protocol`; circulating "1.6 mg SC twice weekly" off-label is the hepatitis dose
  repurposed (the approved regimen) `[regulatory]`. See [[library/peptides/thymosin-alpha-1/practitioner-layer]].

## Pharmacokinetics
Zadaxin PI: **1.6 mg SC twice weekly** (hep B); Tmax ~2 h; **half-life ~2 h**; dose-proportional, no accumulation,
returns to baseline by 24 h; urinary excretion 31–60%; **SC only (not IV)**; reconstitute → 1.6 mg/mL, store 2–8 °C `[regulatory]`.
Volume of distribution: NO ADMISSIBLE PRIMARY FOUND (commonly-cited ~5–8 L not in the verifiable label).

## Risk Profile
- **risk_tier: low.** Drug-related AEs <1% across >2,000 patients; ETASS no Tα1-related SAE; TESTS safety = placebo.
- Common AE: mild injection-site reactions. Rare: transient muscle atrophy, polyarthralgia/hand edema, rash (qualitative).
- Contraindications/cautions: hypersensitivity; **deliberate immunosuppression / transplant** (mechanism opposes it);
  pregnancy Cat C; theoretical autoimmune-flare caution (low-grade) `[regulatory/vendor_label]`.
- Monitoring: no mandatory regimen; stop on hypersensitivity/intolerance.
- **Anti-doping: NOT on the WADA list** (contrast TB-500/thymosin-beta-4, which IS banned) — re-verify the annual list. `[regulatory]`

## Regulatory
**NOT FDA-approved** (US). Approved in **~30+ countries** (China principal market) for chronic hepatitis B/C, cancer
adjuvant, immune/vaccine enhancement `[regulatory: SciClone SEC — VERIFIED:partial]`. US FDA **orphan designations**
(hep B, HCC, melanoma, DiGeorge; ≠ approval). **Removed from US 503A Category 2 (Sept 27, 2024)**; FDA proposed against
503A inclusion (Dec 2024 PCAC). WADA: not prohibited. `[regulatory]`
