# Selank

## Metadata
- class: peptide — a synthetic heptapeptide, **Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro (TKPRPGP)**: a stabilized analogue of the endogenous immunomodulatory tetrapeptide **tuftsin (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg)**, extended at the C-terminus with a **Pro-Gly-Pro** tail that confers peptidase resistance. Designed and produced in Moscow by the **Institute of Molecular Genetics, Russian Academy of Sciences (IMG RAS)** in cooperation with the **V. V. Zakusov Research Institute of Pharmacology**. Held distinct from **Semax** (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro) and from **N-Acetyl-Selank-Amidate** (a separate, derivatized chemical entity).
- evidence_tier: C — see [[library/methodology/evidence-tiers]]. Selank is a **Russia-registered intranasal anxiolytic** (reg. no. **ЛСР-003338/09**) whose entire admissible evidence base is **preclinical or small-clinical, Russian-language, and single-network**: ~100% concentration in the IMG-RAS / Zakusov / Serbsky-Korsakov lineage (7/7 admissible primaries; 6/7 strictest reading). The human anxiety data are **4 small (n≈60–70), benzodiazepine-comparator (not placebo-controlled), Russian-language trials** from this one lineage — **no independent Western RCT or independent lab confirmation** exists.
- risk_tier: experimental — **NOT FDA/EMA-approved**; outside Russia it is an unapproved gray-market "research chemical," not a sanctioned §503A ingredient (Selank acetate/TP-7 was §503A Category-2, removed Sept 27 2024 by nomination withdrawal, **never Category-1 → not lawfully compoundable**). Favorable Russian safety record (no sedation/dependence/withdrawal vs benzodiazepines) but single-lineage, short-exposure, with **no long-term Western pharmacovigilance**; a Western review flags class-level abuse/dependence concern as unsettled. The framing is "single-source-validated, Western-unreplicated, gray-market injectable/intranasal," not "known to be harmful."

> **Read this first.** Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide (**Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro**), a stabilized **tuftsin analogue** invented at **IMG RAS / Zakusov Institute**. In Russia it is a **fully registered prescription anxiolytic** — an intranasal 0.15% solution, reg. no. **ЛСР-003338/09**, for generalized anxiety disorder and neurasthenia. It is **NOT approved by the FDA or EMA**. The headline claim — anxiolysis **comparable to a benzodiazepine but without sedation, amnesia, dependence, or withdrawal** — is a **credible but single-lineage Russian clinical claim**, supported by 4 small Russian comparator trials (vs. medazepam/phenazepam, n≈60–70) and a non-benzodiazepine-site mechanism, but **not independently replicated in the West**. The whole evidence base (anxiolytic, BDNF, GABAergic gene-expression, antiviral) funnels through **one research network** (~100% single-lineage concentration) — internally consistent, but the *opposite* of independent corroboration. Selank is held **DISTINCT** from Semax and from N-Acetyl-Selank-Amidate. Full depth + citations: [[library/peptides/selank/research-report]]. ## Mechanism
Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro) — tuftsin (TKPR) plus a stabilizing Pro-Gly-Pro tail. Its anxiolytic/nootropic action is attributed to several converging pathways: (1) **inhibition of enkephalin-degrading enzymes**, prolonging endogenous enkephalins (IC50 ≈ 15–20 µM in human plasma/serum) — the most directly evidenced biochemical mechanism and the usual basis for the "no dependence" rationale; (2) **BDNF upregulation** in rat hippocampus after intranasal dosing (250/500 µg/kg); (3) region-/strain-specific **monoamine/serotonergic** modulation; (4) **context-dependent GABAergic gene-expression** modulation — Selank acts as a **positive allosteric GABA modulator at a site distinct from the benzodiazepine site**, with effects seen in vivo but NOT reproduced by direct cell exposure (implying indirect, systemic/receptor-mediated action); and (5) **tuftsin-lineage immunomodulation** (Th1/Th2 balancing, IFN-α induction). The clinically established route is **intranasal** (nose-to-brain delivery despite rapid plasma degradation). See report §1.

## Evidence Summary
- **Russia-registered anxiolytic (reg. no. ЛСР-003338/09)** — intranasal 0.15% solution for GAD/neurasthenia; a genuine national drug approval, NOT FDA/EMA approval. `regulatory` [[library/peptides/selank/research-report]]
- **Zozulia et al. 2008** (PMID 18454096) — pivotal comparator trial: Selank (n=30) vs. medazepam (n=32) in GAD + neurasthenia (n=62). *Russian, single-lineage.* Comparable anxiolytic effect + added antiasthenic/psychostimulant action. `rct/comparative`
- **Medvedev 2014** (PMID 25176261, Selank vs. phenazepam, n=60) + **Medvedev 2015** (PMID 26356395, phenazepam ±Selank, n=70): pronounced anxiolytic + mild nootropic; Selank reduced phenazepam sedation/memory-impairment/withdrawal-period symptoms (UKU scale). *Russian, single-lineage.* `rct/comparative`
- Preclinical substrate (animal/in-vitro): BDNF induction (Inozemtseva 2008), GABAergic gene-expression (Volkova 2016 / Filatova 2017), diazepam potentiation in stressed rats (Kasian 2017), antiviral vs. influenza (Ershov 2009). `animal`/`in_vitro`

Counter-evidence / caveats:
- **~100% single-lineage concentration** (IMG-RAS / Zakusov / Serbsky-Korsakov): 7/7 admissible primaries; **no independent Western RCT or independent lab confirmation**. Internal consistency is the *opposite* of corroboration.
- **No placebo-controlled trial** — all human anxiety data are small, benzodiazepine-comparator, Russian-language.
- The "no dependence/withdrawal" claim rests on **mechanism + lineage assertion**, not a dedicated human dependence/discontinuation trial.
- A Western review (Doyno & White 2021) classes Selank as a poorly-studied GABAergic sold illicitly to US consumers and keeps the abuse/dependence question **open**.

## Risk Profile
- adverse effects (literature): favorable Russian record — anxiolysis **comparable to benzodiazepines (medazepam, phenazepam) without sedation, muscle relaxation, cognitive/amnestic impairment, tolerance, dependence, or withdrawal** [single-lineage; instrument-anchored by Medvedev 2015 UKU data]. Most common *actual* AE: mild local nasal irritation (route-specific). No quantified systemic AE-incidence table; small n (~120 total), short exposure (~14 d).
- adverse effects (anecdotal): gray-market product carries unverified identity/purity/sterility risk independent of the molecule's intrinsic profile (contamination/mislabeling).
- contraindications:    no validated Western label contraindications (unapproved in West). Precautionary: **pregnancy/lactation** (no human data; avoid); **uncharacterized gray-market product** caution; **GABAergic/CNS-depressant co-use** untested (Selank modulates benzodiazepine activity). The intrinsic profile is the strongest argument *for* relative safety *if the single-lineage Russian record holds*.
- monitoring:           clinical, not biomarker-based — **no validated Selank safety biomarker or therapeutic drug level**. Track standardized anxiety instruments (Hamilton, Zung, CGI) + local nasal-tolerance.
- known interactions:   benzodiazepines/alcohol/CNS depressants — modulation reported, safety not characterized; treat as untested.
