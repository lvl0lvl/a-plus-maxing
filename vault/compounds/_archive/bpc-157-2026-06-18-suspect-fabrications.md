# BPC-157

## Metadata
- class: peptide (pentadecapeptide, 15-mer)
- sequence: GEPPPGKPADDAGLV
- aliases: PL 14736, Body Protection Compound 157, "stable gastric pentadecapeptide BPC 157"
- evidence_tier: **C** — pre-clinical-dominant. 52 deduplicated primaries; 80.8% combined Sikirić Zagreb metro (75.0% academic + 5.8% Pliva industrial); 4 published peer-reviewed human studies cumulative n≈60; 100% of post-2003 US human data from one investigator at one clinic
- risk_tier: **experimental** — no FDA/EMA approval; FDA 503A Cat 2 designation 2023 (removed April 22, 2026 via nominations-withdrawal, NOT safety clearance; PCAC review July 23, 2026); WADA S0 prohibited at all times; TGA Schedule 4 + Appendix D clause 5 since June 1, 2024; Health Canada unauthorized
- status: researching
- last_verified: 2026-05-24

## Mechanism
Putative pathway: VEGFR2 → Akt → eNOS angiogenic axis (independent confirmation by Hsieh group, Chang Gung Univ Taiwan; Sci Rep 2020 parallel Src–caveolin-1 route). No specific BPC-157 receptor identified in peer-reviewed literature as of 2026. Dopaminergic/serotonergic and EGR-1 induction pathways have only single-source primary backing. **"GHRH agonist" claim common in practitioner content is unsupported by primary literature.** See research-report.md §3 for full mechanism.

## Evidence Summary
Strongest 2–3 sources by weight:
- **Hsieh MJ et al. 2017** — *J Mol Med* 95:323–333 (Chang Gung, Taiwan). Independent in-vitro + in-vivo demonstration of VEGFR2 → Akt → eNOS axis. `[in_vitro + animal]` `[population-mismatch: rat / endothelial cell]`. Strongest non-Zagreb mechanism source.
- **Xu C et al. 2020** — *Regul Toxicol Pharmacol* 114:104665 (Fourth Military Medical Univ Xi'an, China). GLP preclinical safety package in mice/rats/rabbits/dogs/guinea pigs; "well-tolerated, no serious toxicity, LD1 not establishable." `[animal]`. The single GLP-grade safety primary in the literature.
- **He L et al. 2022** — *Front Pharmacol* 13:1026182, PMID 36588717 (Fourth Military Medical Univ Xi'an, same Xi'an cluster as Xu 2020). Pharmacokinetics in **rats (n=324) and beagle dogs (n=6) only — NO HUMAN SUBJECTS** (corrects prior dispatch's S2 misattribution). Rat IV t½ ≈ 15.2 min; IM F% 14–19% (rat) / 45–51% (dog); kidney dominant tissue depot. `[animal]` `[population-mismatch: rat / beagle]`.

Counter-evidence and counter-framings:
- **McGuire FP et al. 2025** — *Curr Rev Musculoskelet Med* 18(12):611–619 (Univ of Utah). Independent narrative review: "human data is exceedingly sparse… BPC-157 should not be recommended for clinical use in musculoskeletal medicine" until well-designed human trials are conducted; explicit publication-bias caveat. `[mechanism_review]`.
- **Vasireddi et al. 2025** — HSS systematic review of 544 articles; only 1 clinical human study met inclusion; cumulative human n <30 in primary published data. `[mechanism_review]`.
- **FDA 2023 reasoning (despite April 2026 procedural removal)** — impurity profile concerns, insufficient safety data, chronic-exposure unknown. Concerns NOT formally resolved; removal was nominations-withdrawal.

## Risk Profile
- **adverse effects (literature):** Human data: null AE signal across n<100 cumulative exposure in 4 published studies (Veljaca 2003 abstract; Lee 2024 IC pilot n=12; Lee 2025 IV pilot n=2; Lee/Padgett 2021 chart-review). All studies short-duration, no chronic dosing data, no placebo-controlled AE table for Ruenzi 2005 UC Phase 2 (conference abstract only, never published as full paper). Animal toxicology: Xu 2020 GLP package "well-tolerated, no genetic or embryo-fetal toxicity, LD1 not establishable"; mild local irritation only finding. No published 26-week chronic GLP study.
- **adverse effects (anecdotal):** Reddit/forum reports of injection-site irritation, transient brain fog, GI changes — none documented in peer-reviewed literature; `[anecdote_aggregate]` — qualitative leads only, NOT a basis for AE rate.
- **contraindications (mechanism-derived):**
  1. Pregnancy and lactation — no human DART data; Xu 2020 had embryo-fetal screening only, not full reproductive package
  2. Active malignancy or history of cancer — VEGFR2 / FAK-paxillin / EGR-1 pathway activation creates theoretical neoangiogenesis-promoting concern; anti-tumor evidence is 100% Sikirić-cluster, single-cluster signal in either direction
  3. Recent thrombotic event — eNOS / NO-pathway modulation; Stupnisek 2015 demonstrated direction-bidirectional bleeding/clotting interaction (pharmacodynamic-interaction signal, NOT a safety endorsement)
  4. Active autoimmune disease in flare — immunomodulatory effects insufficiently characterized in autoimmune populations
  5. Concurrent VEGF-modulating drugs (bevacizumab, ranibizumab, tyrosine kinase inhibitors) — unstudied interaction
  6. Pediatric population — no data
- **monitoring:** See research-report.md §9. Third-party measurable markers:
  - [[biomarkers/cmp]] — full chemistry panel including AST/ALT/ALP/bilirubin (hepatic) and BUN/Cr (renal); baseline, week 4, end-of-cycle
  - [[biomarkers/cbc]] — with differential; baseline + monthly
  - [[biomarkers/pt-aptt-inr]] — coagulation; baseline + monthly (eNOS / NO-pathway modulation rationale; Stupnisek 2015 anticoagulant-interaction signal)
  - [[biomarkers/cpk-isoenzymes]] — cardiac monitoring (precedent: Lee 2025 IV pilot)
  - [[biomarkers/bnp]] / NT-proBNP — cardiac monitoring
  - [[biomarkers/hs-crp]] — inflammation marker
  - [[biomarkers/tsh]] — endocrine
  - [[biomarkers/prolactin]] — dopaminergic-modulation rationale
  - Tumor markers (precautionary surveillance, NOT diagnostic): CEA, CA-125, PSA — quarterly during exposure for any operator with cancer history or age >50
  - Pancreatic: serum lipase, amylase — baseline + end-of-cycle
- **known interactions:**
  - NSAIDs — well-studied protective interaction (Sikirić 2012, Park 2020 — animal); mechanism is gastroprotection
  - Anticoagulants (heparin, warfarin) — Stupnisek 2015 animal interaction; clinical extrapolation not validated
  - L-NAME / L-arginine (NO-pathway modulators) — Stupnisek 2015 animal interaction; relevant for any operator on nitrate therapy or arginine supplementation
  - Corticosteroids — counteractive in animal MSK models; clinical extrapolation not validated
  - VEGF-targeted oncology drugs — unstudied
  - Compounding-pharmacy source: 503A access via Tailor Made (federal FDCA prosecution 2020), Hallandale, Belmar, et al. — see practitioner-layer.md

Additional decision-relevant facts surfaced by the rebuild:
- The S2 dispatch (2026-05-23) attributed He L 2022 as a "human PK study" — this is incorrect. He L 2022 is rats + beagle dogs only. **No human PK paper exists for BPC-157.** Any downstream agent recommending dose based on "human PK" must instead cite the rat-allometric extrapolation explicitly.
- Edwin Lee is the sole investigator for all post-2003 US human evidence. Lee operates Institute for Hormonal Balance commercially; subjects in Lee studies are likely Lee's own patients, not random recruitment. This is not disqualifying but must be transparent.
- The "Pentadeca Arginate" (PDA) marketing of arginate-salt as "superior oral bioavailability" has zero in-vivo backing. The Diagen patent (WO2014142764A1) reports in-vitro HPLC stability only.
- The April 2026 FDA Cat 2 removal was via nominations-withdrawal, NOT safety clearance. The FDA's 2023 safety reasoning (impurity profile, insufficient safety data, chronic-exposure unknown) remains formally unresolved.
