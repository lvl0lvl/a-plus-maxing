## Mechanism

Pleiotropic / multi-pathway peptide with **no identified canonical receptor** despite ~30 years of research. Replicated pathways (independent labs, mechanism described at protein/transcript level): VEGFR2-Akt-eNOS angiogenesis axis (Hsieh 2017); FAK-paxillin tendon-fibroblast migration (Chang 2011); growth-hormone receptor upregulation in tendon fibroblasts (Chang 2014); Src-Caveolin-1-eNOS vasomotor modulation (Hsieh 2020). All four replicated pathways come from the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital / Chang Gung University group in Taiwan — non-Sikiric. Sikiric-only mechanism claims include NO-system bidirectional modulation, dopamine/serotonin counteraction, and >24h gastric-juice stability; these have partial mechanistic support but no independent in-vivo replication.

## Evidence Summary

Strongest 2-3 sources by evidence weight:

- **[Vasireddi et al. 2025, mechanism_review]** *Emerging Use of BPC-157 in Orthopaedic Sports Medicine: A Systematic Review.* HSS Journal 21:15563316251355551. PMID 40756949. PRISMA systematic review of 36 BPC-157 ortho-sports studies through June 2024 (35 preclinical + 1 uncontrolled human chart review). Confirms zero completed controlled human efficacy trials in MSK; explicitly identifies Sikiric-lab dominance as a principal limitation. **Independent of Sikiric.** [[library/peptides/bpc-157/research-report]] §7
- **[Xu et al. 2022, animal]** *Pharmacokinetics, distribution, metabolism, and excretion of body-protective compound 157 in rats and dogs.* Front Pharmacol 13:1026182. PMC9794587. Single dedicated multi-route PK paper in the literature. t½ <30 min across routes and species; IM bioavailability 14-19% rat, 45-51% dog; urinary + biliary excretion; no major accumulation on 7-day repeat IM. **Non-Sikiric.** [[library/peptides/bpc-157/research-report]] §6
- **[Józwiak et al. 2025, mechanism_review]** *Multifunctionality and Possible Medical Application of the BPC 157 Peptide — Literature and Patent Review.* Pharmaceuticals (MDPI) 18(2):185. Major recent independent synthesis. MDPI flag (open-access lower-trust). [[library/peptides/bpc-157/research-report]] §4

Counter-evidence:
- No null/negative in-vivo efficacy studies located in admissible literature (absence of refutation, not confirmation of effect).
- Independent oral-peptide-delivery review (Brown 2025) does not list BPC-157 as a documented oral-stable exception — contests Sikiric-only gastric-stability claim.
- One in-vivo tumor-bearing model (Kang 2018, C26 colon cachexia) found neither tumor reduction nor acceleration with BPC-157.

  - **Kent Holtorf, MD:** Lyme/CFS/FM/CIRS protocol; **acetylated BPC-157** preferred for systemic mold/CIRS use. Cycles long. Dose specifics not numerically stated in public materials.
  - **Neil Paulvin, DO:** SC default; layering with TB-500 noted; specific µg dose not stated in open materials.
  - **Tracy Gapin, MD:** "I use every day… amazing for gut health/microbiome"; specific dose not stated in retrievable text.
  - **William Seeds, MD:** book (above); 250-500 µg SC daily Seeds-aligned standard.

- **Originator-group recommended dose (Sikiric):** does NOT state a specific human therapeutic dose in open reviews. Anchors rodent at 10 µg/kg, 10 ng/kg, 10 pg/kg IP/IG; emphasizes "wide therapeutic window."
- **Consensus practitioner dose:** **250-500 µg SC once daily, 4-6 week cycles** (subQ); **250-500 µg 1-2× daily oral** for GI; **5 mg single IV infusion** (Lee outlier for acute MSK). Both systemic SC and local SC near injury are accepted; no consensus on superiority.
- **Gap between literature and practice:** practitioner consensus runs ~1.5-3× the Examine rodent-to-human extrapolation (~110-180 µg/day); Lee IV runs ~30-100×. **No human RCT dose exists.** The Pliva PL-14736 Phase II UC program landed on 6 mg/day intra-rectal but was never primary-published.
- **Divergent schools:** standard subQ vs Lee IV; free-base vs acetylated salt forms (Holtorf prefers acetylated for CIRS/mold); conservative (Attia) vs enthusiastic (Holtorf/Lee/Seeds/Paulvin/Gapin) adoption postures.

- **supplied as:** 5 mg lyophilized (also 10 mg) per vial
- **diluent:** bacteriostatic water (0.9% benzyl alcohol), typical 2 mL for 5 mg vial
- **final concentration:** 2.5 mg/mL = 2,500 µg/mL = **25 µg per U-100 insulin unit**

## Risk Profile

- **adverse effects (literature):** Human evidence ≈ n=31 across three small open-label studies (Lee & Burgess 2025 IV n=2; Lee 2024 intravesicular n=12; Lee & Padgett 2021 intra-articular n=17). All reported no AEs but all severely underpowered with no long-term follow-up. PL 14736 Phase II UC trial completed ~20 years ago, never primary-published. Animal: no LD50 identifiable (Xu 2020 limit test 2 g/kg without adverse effects); no teratogenicity, no genotoxicity, no anaphylaxis at tested doses. "No toxicity" finding is dose-narrow (mostly 10 ng/kg and 10 µg/kg tested) and group-narrow (Sikiric-dominant)
- **adverse effects (anecdotal — qualitative patterns only):** injection-site reactions including sensitization; fatigue/lethargy/"brain fog"; anhedonia/mood blunting; acute anxiety/dizziness shortly after injection; transient nausea/headache; "growing pains" during reported regenerative periods. NO numerical rates from anecdote
- **contraindications:** active malignancy or known dysplastic tissue (theoretical, VEGFR2/Akt/eNOS angiogenic mechanism); pregnancy and lactation (no human data); pediatric (no data at all); therapeutic anticoagulation (rat data show BPC-157 reverses bleeding-time prolongation from heparin/warfarin/aspirin); concurrent serotonergic polypharmacy (MAOI + SSRI/SNRI, extrapolation from rodent serotonin-syndrome counteraction); athletes subject to anti-doping (WADA S0); US military (DoD OPSS prohibited list)
- **monitoring (per Lee & Burgess 2025 protocol + theoretical):** CBC, CMP (AST/ALT/ALP/BUN/Cr/electrolytes/glucose), CPK with isoenzymes, BNP, TSH. Theoretical additions given mechanism: coagulation panel (PT/INR, PTT, platelet aggregation); immunogenicity / anti-drug antibody (no standard assay exists); cancer surveillance (age-appropriate screening); IGF-1 (GH-axis given GHR upregulation)
- **known interactions:** NSAIDs — protective in rat models (use case framed); anticoagulants/antiplatelets (heparin, warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel, cilostazol) — functional antagonism of antithrombotic intent; **clopidogrel-induced gastric injury** — attenuated in rats (Wu 2020 DDDT, added 2026-05-23 — opposite-side from antithrombotic antagonism); corticosteroids — BPC-157 reverses corticosteroid impairment of muscle healing in rats; cyclophosphamide — reduced GI/bladder toxicity in rats (animal-only); anthracyclines (doxorubicin) — reversed cardiac biomarker elevations in chronic HF rat model (Sikiric framing asserts preserved anti-tumor efficacy, not measured); monoamine modulators — counteraction of serotonin syndrome / dopamine supersensitivity / neuroleptic catalepsy / ethanol intoxication in rodents (human safety unknown)
