# Targets

> **Status:** Initial draft. To be personalized after first bloodwork (target: July 2026 doctor visit) when baselines are known.

## Body Composition
- Body fat %: TBD baseline
- Lean mass: TBD baseline
- Weight: TBD baseline + target

## Cardiovascular (longevity-medicine consensus starting points)
- ApoB: <80 mg/dL (lower if family hx of CVD)
- Lp(a): <30 nmol/L (mostly genetic; informational)
- LDL-C: <100 mg/dL
- hs-CRP: <1 mg/L
- Homocysteine: <9 µmol/L

## Metabolic
- Fasting glucose: <90 mg/dL
- Fasting insulin: <5 µIU/mL
- HbA1c: <5.4%
- Triglycerides: <100 mg/dL
- Trig/HDL ratio: <1.5

## Sleep (from Oura, once available)
- Total sleep: >7.5 hrs
- Sleep efficiency: >90%
- Resting HR: <55 bpm
- HRV: highly individual — set after 30 days of baseline data
