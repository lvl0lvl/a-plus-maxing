# GHK-Cu (Copper Tripeptide-1) — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English layer for GHK-Cu (Gly-His-Lys:Cu, Copper tripeptide-1).
Tools: WebSearch (US-only) + WebFetch. Tavily not connected (sanctioned fallback).
Integrity stance: only sources with a retrievable record + verifiable bibliographic
metadata are cited. Papers that appeared only as secondhand mentions in popular-science
or commercial pages — with no locatable journal record — are reported as "none located"
with the search terms used, NOT cited. Machine-translated numerics are tagged
[translated:WebFetch] / [translated:WebSearch].

## Russian / Soviet

**Result: CITATIONS LOCATED (3 verified Russian-language peer-reviewed sources).**

Russian-language literature on GHK / copper-tripeptide skin regeneration genuinely
exists and is indexed in eLibrary.ru, CyberLeninka, and Russian medical journals.
Note: GHK-Cu is NOT a Khavinson-school bioregulator; the located Russian work is
mainstream dermatology / experimental-wound research, not the St. Petersburg
peptide-bioregulator tradition.

Verified items (full detail in Bibliography):

1. **Polonskaya A.S., Shatohina E.A., Kruglova L.S. (2020)** — "Роль трипептида меди
   (GHK-Cu) в процессе регенерации кожи" [The role of copper tripeptide (GHK-Cu) in
   skin regeneration]. *Фарматека (Pharmateca)*, 2020, No. 8, pp. 78–82.
   DOI 10.18565/pharmateca.2020.8.78-82; eLibrary ID 43433096. Russian-language
   peer-reviewed **literature review**. Verified directly (DOI + eLibrary record).
   Reports: plasma GHK 200 ng/mL (young) → 80 ng/mL (>60 yr) [translated:WebFetch];
   peak collagen-synthesis stimulation at 10⁻⁹ M [translated:WebFetch]; one animal
   study relative-wound-healing coefficient ×1.8, peak ×2.7 at days 6–10
   [translated:WebFetch]; diabetic-ulcer clinical trial closure ×3 faster vs standard
   + placebo [translated:WebFetch]. (These numerics are review-reported, not primary.)

2. **Rakhmetova K.K., Mishina E.S., Vorvul A.O., Bobyntsev I.I., Dolgintsev M.E.,
   Bezhin A.I. (2022)** — "Регенеративные эффекты пептидов Gly-His-Lys и
   Gly-His-Lys-D-Ala при кожной инфицированной ране" [Regenerative effects of
   Gly-His-Lys and Gly-His-Lys-D-Ala peptides in infected skin wounds].
   *Вестник РГМУ (Bulletin of RSMU / Bulletin of Russian State Medical University)*,
   2022. DOI 10.24075/vrgmu.2022.014. Russian original research (bilingual RU/EN).
   **Caveat:** this study uses **GHK peptide WITHOUT copper** (and the GHK-D-Ala
   analogue), not the GHK-Cu complex — relevant to GHK pharmacology but a distinct
   chemical entity; flagged so it is not miscounted as GHK-Cu evidence. Wistar rats
   (n=150), 250 mm² full-thickness dorsal defect; GHK-D-Ala 0.5 µg/kg reduced
   granulocytes/lymphocytes (p<0.05–0.001), raised fibroblasts/macrophages; cellular
   index 225.6 ± 46.76% at day 10 [translated:WebFetch].

3. **Zadubrovskaya M.S. (2020)** — "Гистологическое исследование репаративных
   изменений в поврежденной коже при применении одного из регуляторных пептидов"
   [Histological investigation of reparative changes in damaged skin following
   application of a regulatory peptide]. *FORCIPE*, 2020, Vol. 3, Spec. Issue,
   pp. 171–172. Russian-language original/conference research, indexed on CyberLeninka
   + eLibrary. Studies **GHK-Cu (Gly-His-Lys-Cu²⁺)** explicitly. Wistar rats (n=64);
   burn-wound area −50% by day 10 vs control, full epithelialization by day 21
   [translated:WebFetch]. (No DOI located; eLibrary/CyberLeninka record exists — lower
   tier than #1/#2 due to conference-abstract format and no DOI.)

Databases/terms searched (Russian): eLibrary.ru, CyberLeninka, Pharmateca journal site,
istina.ips.ac.ru; terms — "GHK-Cu медный пептид трипептид заживление ран",
"трипептид GHK-Cu eLibrary регенерация кожи", "GHK-Cu CyberLeninka трипептид меди",
"медьсодержащий пептид рана крыса Бюллетень экспериментальной биологии медь".

Note on Bull. Exp. Biol. Med. (Бюллетень экспериментальной биологии и медицины):
journal is real and indexed, but no GHK/GHK-Cu-specific article was located there in

## Originator-country / other

**Result: NO DISTINCT NON-ENGLISH ORIGINATOR LITERATURE EXISTS — and this is a valid,
expected finding.**

GHK was discovered in the **United States** by Loren Pickart in 1973 (doctoral work,
University of California, San Francisco / UC), isolated from human plasma albumin
fraction; the copper complex GHK-Cu was characterized subsequently. The originator
country is English-speaking, so the foundational and the bulk of the primary
literature (Pickart, Margolina, and collaborators) is **English-language**. There is
therefore no separate non-English "originator-country" literature stream to survey —
the originator literature *is* the English layer. This is the correct null finding for
a US-discovered peptide.

Other non-English: no Japanese / Korean / German / French peer-reviewed GHK-Cu
primary literature was specifically targeted at this pass (gate required RU + ZH +
originator). Not surveyed = not claimed absent.

  is not folded into GHK-Cu evidence.
