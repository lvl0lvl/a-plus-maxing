# CJC-1295 — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **CJC-1295** (tetrasubstituted hGRF(1-29) GHRH analogue; DAC and no-DAC / "Modified GRF 1-29" forms), companion to [[library/peptides/cjc-1295/research-report]]. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. Every citation was checked for a retrievable identifier (PMID / DOI / stable database URL). No citation is fabricated; items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout (non-English-language vs non-English-author).** A study **authored in Russia/China/Canada but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and is already in the CJC-1295 corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, etc.** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. In particular, the original CJC-1295 pharmacology (ConjuChem, Montreal, Canada) was **published in English** and belongs in the Tier 1/2 English-language sections, not here.

**Headline result: ZERO genuinely non-English admissible CJC-1295 primaries located** across the required tracks. Russian = vendor / landscape material only. Chinese = vendor / reagent-catalog pages plus an NMPA doping-list regulatory artifact, plus the English-published originator work already in the corpus. Originator lineage (ConjuChem) = English-published. This is a clean, well-characterized null — the non-English CJC-1295 footprint is consumption/marketing of the English corpus, not independent non-English-language primary research.

## Italian / originator-country / other (note)

**Result: NO non-English originator-country CJC-1295 primary located — and this is the EXPECTED valid "none," because the originator lineage published in English.**

CJC-1295 was developed by **ConjuChem Biotechnologies (Montreal, Canada)** under the program name DAC:GRF. The originator literature is **English-language**:

- **Jetté L, et al.** "Human growth hormone-releasing factor (hGRF)1-29-albumin bioconjugates activate the GRF receptor on the anterior pituitary in rats: identification of CJC-1295 as a long-lasting GRF analog." *Endocrinology* 2005;146(7):3052–3058. **PMID 15817669; DOI 10.1210/en.2004-1286.** All authors **ConjuChem Inc., Montreal (Canada)** — a Canadian originator group, but an **English-language** publication. Not a non-English source.
- **Teichman SL, et al.** (the only published human pharmacology study) and **Alba M, et al.** (GHRHKO mouse) are likewise **English-language** publications (already corpus refs).

There is no English-vs-non-English conflation here: English-published work by non-English-located (Canadian ConjuChem) authors is **not** counted as non-English-language literature. "None in a non-English language" is therefore a **valid** finding for the originator lineage.

Other languages: none surveyed beyond the tracks above; no incidental admissible non-English primary surfaced in any other language during the searches.
