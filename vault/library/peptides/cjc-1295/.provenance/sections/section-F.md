## Section F — Practitioner-practice + non-English

Scope: prescribing-practice / compounding-pharmacy conventions for CJC-1295 in BOTH its DAC and no-DAC ("Modified GRF 1-29") forms, reconstitution math, and a survey of non-English (Russian/Chinese) primary literature. Per section rules, NONE of the practitioner/vendor/compounding sources below ground any efficacy or adverse-event-rate claim — those require Tier 1/2 and are cited only as regulatory context where noted. The defining DAC-vs-no-DAC distinction is preserved throughout and never cross-attributed.

### Prescribing-practice layer

**Admissible compounding data sheet — FOUND (one, with caveats).** The Tailor Made Compounding (TMC) peptide catalog lists a CJC-1295 monograph as a compounded injectable: dose form `2000 mcg/mL subcutaneous injection in a 2 mL vial`, purity `>98% (HPLC on request)`, molecular weight `3647.15`, molecular formula `C165H269N47O46`, with the suggested administration `0.10 mL subcutaneously 5 of 7 nights per week before bedtime on an empty stomach` and a stated suggestion to combine with ipamorelin [1] `[compounding_data_sheet]`. The catalog's 2000 mcg/mL strength and per-night before-bed/empty-stomach instruction are consistent with the **no-DAC / Modified GRF 1-29** convention (frequent, low-microgram, pulsatile dosing), NOT the once-weekly DAC convention `[compounding_data_sheet]`. Important caveat: TMC received an FDA warning letter (04/01/2020) and CJC-1295 was subsequently swept into the FDA 503A Category 2 list, so this monograph reflects a historical/pre-restriction compounding offering rather than a currently broadly available product [2] `[compounding_data_sheet]`.

**Status of the broader compounding channel.** A compounding-industry guide (503Pharma, "CJC-1295: The Complete Guide for Compounding Pharmacies," dated Jun 19 2026) describes the compounded dose-form envelope as `injectable solutions 2–5 mg/mL, reconstituted with bacteriostatic water` and `lyophilized powder 2–10 mg/vial`, with diluent/excipients `bacteriostatic water, mannitol`, pH adjusted to `5–7`, and reconstituted solutions `stable for 28 days refrigerated` [3] `[compounding_data_sheet]`. The same guide states compounded dosing as `100–300 mcg subcutaneously 1–3 times weekly (with DAC); daily without DAC`, individualized at `1–2 mcg/kg, lower for elderly, titrate based on IGF-1`, and notes ipamorelin co-formulation `for synergistic effects` with formulations lasting `14–28 days` [3] `[compounding_data_sheet]`. Note: the guide's "1–3× weekly with DAC" framing is a more conservative/lower figure than the dominant DAC convention reported by other practice sources (below); it is reported here as-is and not reconciled, because practice sources may not ground a numeric "correct dose." Critically, several major compounding pharmacies (e.g. Empower) no longer surface a public CJC-1295 monograph, consistent with post-Category-2 withdrawal; this is reported as an observed absence, not a verified efficacy/safety finding `[compounding_data_sheet]`.

**Named-prescriber / society protocols.** I did not directly open a named-prescriber primary source (Seeds, Lee, Holtorf, Paulvin) or an A4M/IFM/Peptide Society document with a verifiable venue+date for CJC-1295; targeted searches surfaced only secondary clinic blogs that re-state the conventions below. Per the section's self-check (every `practitioner_protocol` cite must carry name+venue+date), I therefore make **no `practitioner_protocol` claim** here rather than cite an unattributable secondary blog — an honest "not located with sufficient attribution" result.

**The two dosing conventions (kept strictly separate).**
- **CJC-1295 WITH DAC** (long-acting, albumin-binding via the Drug Affinity Complex; multi-day half-life): the dominant practice convention reported across vendor and protocol sources is **~1–2 mg subcutaneously once weekly** (some sources extend to 2 mg/week split into one or two injections), with timing-of-day treated as non-critical because of the long half-life [4] `[vendor_label]`. This is reconstitution/route framing only — NOT an efficacy claim.
- **CJC-1295 WITHOUT DAC = "Modified GRF 1-29"** (~30-minute half-life): practice convention is **~100 mcg (range 100–300 mcg) subcutaneously, dosed 1–3× per day**, classically before bed and ≥2 h after the last meal, and **stacked with ipamorelin** in the dominant commercial "CJC-1295/ipamorelin" blend [1][3] `[compounding_data_sheet]`. The two conventions must never be cross-attributed: the once-weekly milligram dosing belongs ONLY to the DAC form, and the daily-microgram pulsatile dosing belongs ONLY to the no-DAC/Mod-GRF form.

**Reconstitution conventions (vendor_label / math only).** Reported reconstitution math (numbers-as-math only, NOT evidence): a 2 mg vial reconstituted with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → `1 mg/mL`, so a 100 mcg dose = `0.1 mL = 10 units` on a U-100 insulin syringe; a 5 mg vial + 2.0 mL BAC water → `2.5 mg/mL` (1 unit = 0.01 mL = 25 mcg); a 5 mg no-DAC vial + 2.5 mL → `2 mg/mL`, where 100 mcg = `0.05 mL` [5] `[vendor_label]`. Standard handling: inject diluent slowly down the vial wall, swirl (do not shake), refrigerate at 2–8 °C, use within ~4 weeks [5] `[vendor_label]`. These figures are gray-market vendor math and carry no efficacy or purity assurance.

**Regulatory context (Tier — not practitioner-grounded).** For completeness: the FDA placed CJC-1295 in 503A Category 2 (not compoundable under enforcement discretion) citing immunogenicity/aggregation and impurity concerns, and at the December 4 2024 Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) meeting the committee voted AGAINST adding CJC-1295 to the positive 503A Bulks List; CJC-1295 was NOT among the 12 peptides removed from Category 2 by the April 16 2026 FDA action (FR Doc 2026-07361) and remains non-compoundable (see §E) [6]. Any immunogenicity, DNA-damage, or cardiovascular-event statements appearing in that FDA record are regulatory/clinical-tier items and are deliberately NOT used here to ground a practice claim.
