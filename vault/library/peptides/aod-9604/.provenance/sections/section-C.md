## Section C — Other claims (osteoarthritis/cartilage) + concentration audit

**Compound:** AOD-9604 (AOD9604; "anti-obesity drug 9604"); the modified C-terminal hexadecapeptide fragment of human growth hormone, hGH(176-191), with an added N-terminal tyrosine.

**Status banner (applies to every claim below):** AOD-9604 is **INVESTIGATIONAL** for all musculoskeletal indications. **There is no regulatory approval for any osteoarthritis (OA), cartilage, or bone indication anywhere.** A targeted search of clinicaltrials.gov (API v2, query term "AOD-9604") returned **zero registered interventional trials** for any indication, including OA [1]. The OA/cartilage "pivot" therefore rests on **preclinical (animal + in vitro) data and patent disclosures only** — there is no admissible, registered, controlled human OA trial of AOD-9604. Claims circulating in vendor/clinic marketing that AOD-9604 "supports joint health / cartilage repair" in humans are **not grounded in the verifiable literature** and are excluded here per the no-vendor-grounding rule.

### C.1 The post-obesity pivot to osteoarthritis / cartilage

After AOD-9604's obesity program stalled (it met safety endpoints but failed to deliver clinically meaningful weight loss in its Phase IIb obesity work — see Section B), the developer **Metabolic Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd (Melbourne, Australia)** repositioned the molecule toward **cartilage repair / osteoarthritis**, leaning on growth-hormone-axis biology (collagen/proteoglycan synthesis) while claiming AOD-9604 does **not** raise IGF-1 and lacks hGH's metabolic liabilities. This repositioning is documented primarily in the company's own **patent estate**, not in peer-reviewed clinical literature.

**Patent disclosure (regulatory/IP, not peer-reviewed):** US Patent **10,111,933 B2**, *"Use of growth hormone fragments,"* assignee **Metabolic Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd**, inventor David Kenley, priority 2011 / granted 2018, claims AOD-9604 for conditions that benefit from growth hormone — explicitly including **cartilage repair and osteoarthritis** — without typical hGH side effects, and discloses an **in vitro chondrocyte experiment** in which AOD-9604 at **100 µg/mL increased proteoglycan and collagen content of cultured cartilage tissue at 3 weeks, without cytotoxicity** (DNA-content assay at 2 weeks) [2] *(in_vitro; chondrocyte tissue culture; concentration disclosed in patent, not a peer-reviewed paper)*. The same patent family additionally discloses **mesenchymal-stem-cell / bone differentiation** claims (AOD-9604 promoting differentiation of adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cells toward bone) [2] *(in_vitro/mechanism; patent disclosure)*. **Integrity note:** patent specifications are **self-reported by the sponsor and not peer-reviewed**; the in vitro chondrocyte and bone data are therefore treated as **preliminary/uncorroborated**, not as Tier-1 evidence.

### C.2 Preclinical OA — the one verified animal study

The strongest verifiable OA evidence is a single controlled **animal** study:

- **Kwon DR, Park GY. *Effect of Intra-articular Injection of AOD9604 with or without Hyaluronic Acid in Rabbit Osteoarthritis Model.* Ann Clin Lab Sci. 2015;45(4):426-432. PMID 26275694** [3] *(animal; New Zealand white rabbits, n = 32 male, 12 wk old)*.
  - **Model/population:** collagenase type II-induced knee OA (chemically induced, **animal model — not human OA**); 4 arms of 8 animals each.
  - **Intervention:** ultrasound-guided **intra-articular** AOD-9604 **0.25 mg / 0.6 mL weekly**, alone or combined with hyaluronic acid (HA 6 mg); comparators saline and HA-alone.
  - **Arms:** (1) saline 0.6 mL; (2) HA 6 mg; (3) AOD-9604 0.25 mg; (4) AOD-9604 + HA.
  - **Result:** morphological and histopathological cartilage-damage scores were **significantly lower in the combination (AOD-9604 + HA) arm** than either agent alone; return to normal ambulation **11 ± 4 days (combination) vs 25 ± 2 days (control)**. The authors frame HA as chondroprotective and AOD-9604 as supporting cartilage regrowth; the headline benefit is **synergy with HA**, with AOD-9604 monotherapy showing weaker standalone effect.
  - **Sponsor linkage / provenance:** **AOD-9604 was supplied by Metabolic Pharmaceuticals (Melbourne, Australia)**; HA supplied by LG Life Science (Korea); no separate funding statement disclosed [3].

**Caveats:** single laboratory (Kwon DR / Park GY, Catholic University of Daegu–affiliated rehabilitation group, Korea), small per-arm n (8), chemically-induced rather than spontaneous/age-related OA, and the chief positive signal requires HA co-administration. This is **hypothesis-generating preclinical data**, not evidence of human efficacy.

**Unverifiable mention — omitted:** A 2020 human "short-term efficacy of intra-articular AOD9604 injection ... in osteoarthritis of the knee" attributed to "Joint Diseases and Related Surgery" surfaced **only inside an AI-summarized vendor result** and **could not be confirmed to an exact PMID/author/volume on PubMed**. Per the verify-or-omit rule it is **excluded** and must NOT be cited as a human trial until independently verified.

**Vendor/clinic conflation flagged — do NOT propagate:** Multiple secondary sources conflated AOD-9604's OA story with **Paradigm Biopharmaceuticals' knee-OA program**. Paradigm's clinical OA asset is **injectable pentosan polysulfate sodium (iPPS / "Zilosul")** — a **different molecule** — not AOD-9604. AOD-9604 has **no** Phase II human OA program; the Paradigm Phase II/III data belong to pentosan polysulfate and are **out of scope and not attributable to AOD-9604**. This is a recurring grounding error in the secondary literature.

### C.3 Other explored indications (bone, combination products, metabolic)

- **Bone / cartilage combination products:** the patent (US 10,111,933 B2) frames AOD-9604 as a **tissue-regeneration adjunct**, pairing it with **hyaluronic acid** (echoed by the Kwon rabbit study's AOD+HA synergy) and, in disclosure, with cartilage-supportive agents and MSC-based approaches [2][3] *(in_vitro/animal; combination concept; INVESTIGATIONAL, no product)*.
- **Bone / MSC differentiation:** in vitro claims that AOD-9604 promotes adipose-derived MSC differentiation toward bone and does not stimulate IGF-1 appear in patent disclosure only [2] *(mechanism; uncorroborated)*.
- **Metabolic / nutraceutical repositioning (lineage continuity, for audit context):** separate from OA, AOD-9604 was also repositioned as a **nutraceutical/"metabolic health" ingredient**; that line traces to the same molecule and the same originating science group (Ng/Monash) — relevant only as concentration-audit context, not an OA claim.

No approved AOD-9604 product exists for **any** of these indications. All are **INVESTIGATIONAL/preliminary**.

### Concentration audit

**Method:** enumerate the distinct **AOD-9604 primary sources** that anchor this compound's grounding numbers (Sections B + C combined), attribute each to its originating research/IP lineage, then compute the single-lineage share. Denominator = **actual enumerated primaries**, not a guessed count.

**Single-lineage attribution:** All five primaries originate from, or are materially dependent on, the **Monash/Ng → Metabolic Pharmaceuticals** lineage. P1-P4 are direct (originating scientists, sponsor, or sponsor-held patent). P5 is an *independent* investigator group, but the **drug substance itself was supplied by Metabolic Pharmaceuticals**, so the experiment cannot exist without the single-source molecule — it is **not an independent supply chain**.

**Single-lineage share = 5/5 = 100%.** Even counting Kwon/Park as a *partially* independent investigator (independent authors, sponsor-supplied compound), the **molecule/IP single-source share is ≥ 80% under any reasonable accounting and effectively 100% for material supply.**

**FLAG (≥70% threshold breached): YES — single-lineage concentration ~100%.** This is the expected ceiling: AOD-9604 is **essentially one company's molecule** (Metabolic Pharmaceuticals, descended from F.M. Ng's Monash University work). There is **no independent replication of the OA/cartilage claims by a sponsor-independent group using independently sourced material**, and the entire evidence base — obesity *and* OA — funnels through a single originator. **Interpretation:** treat all efficacy claims as **single-source, unreplicated, and high concentration-risk**; the OA/cartilage indication in particular has **one** controlled animal study plus **sponsor patent disclosure**, and **zero** registered human trials.
