## THE DEFINING FACTS (integrity axes)
1. **AOD-9604 is a synthetic fragment of human growth hormone — the C-terminal hGH(176-191)
   "lipolytic fragment"** (developed by Metabolic Pharmaceuticals, Australia/Monash, for
   obesity). The selling point was "fat loss WITHOUT GH side effects" — it reportedly does NOT
   raise IGF-1 or activate the GH receptor / growth pathways (distinct from the GH secretagogues
   in this cluster). Verify the no-IGF-1/no-growth claim.
2. **THE CENTRAL INTEGRITY FACT: the pivotal human efficacy FAILED.** The Phase-2b obesity RCT
   (~24 weeks, Metabolic Pharmaceuticals, ~2007) did NOT produce significant weight loss vs
   placebo → development for obesity was discontinued. Preclinical/in-vitro/animal lipolysis
   and an earlier short study showed signals, but **there is NO proven human fat-loss efficacy.**
   Do NOT let preclinical lipolysis or the marketing read as established human fat loss.
3. **Honest status:** never approved as a drug; later pursued for **osteoarthritis/cartilage**
   (a pivot, also without an approved product) and as a "functional food"/cosmetic ingredient
   (a complicated TGA/regulatory history — verify, don't assume). Now gray-market for fat loss.
4. **Sport:** AOD-9604 is the subject of a notable doping saga (the **Essendon AFL case**,
   Australia) — verify its **WADA status** (it was deemed PROHIBITED under S2 [GH fragments/
   secretagogues / "other growth factors"] — confirm against the WADA list; the prohibition was
   itself contested in that case). evidence_tier C (failed Phase 2 + preclinical) / risk_tier
   experimental (unapproved; appears well-tolerated but no long-term human safety).

  EFFICACY, not safety); no GH-axis/IGF-1/glucose effects claimed (verify); injection-site;
