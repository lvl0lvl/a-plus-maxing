## Section C — Beyond-label evidence + concentration audit

This section covers human evidence for tesamorelin (Egrifta) OUTSIDE its single approved indication. The approved indication — reduction of excess visceral adipose tissue (VAT) in HIV-associated lipodystrophy — is covered in §B and is the ONLY domain with a regulatory label and confirmatory pivotal RCTs. Everything below is demarcated into **INVESTIGATIONAL** (signal exists, no approval) vs **ABSENT** (no human efficacy data exists at all). Every trial's population is annotated; note that the human evidence base for tesamorelin remains almost entirely confined to people with HIV.

### Domain 1 — NAFLD / hepatic fat in HIV — INVESTIGATIONAL

This is NOT an approved use. The signal is real but the population is still HIV throughout, so it cannot be assumed to generalize to HIV-negative NAFLD/MASLD.

- **Anchor RCT.** [rct] In a 12-month, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicentre trial of **61 people with HIV and hepatic fat fraction ≥5%** (30 tesamorelin 2 mg/day, 30 placebo; double-blind phase plus a 6-month open-label phase), tesamorelin reduced hepatic fat fraction relative to placebo (absolute effect −4.1%, 95% CI −7.6 to −0.7, p=0.018), with **35% of the tesamorelin arm reaching hepatic fat fraction <5% vs 4% on placebo** [1]. Population annotation: ALL participants HIV-positive; no HIV-negative arm.
- **Fibrosis.** [rct] In the same trial, paired liver biopsies showed tesamorelin **limited fibrosis progression** over 12 months vs placebo — a histological endpoint, not merely imaging [1]. This remains the strongest beyond-label efficacy claim, but it is a secondary biopsy outcome in 61 HIV patients, not a powered fibrosis trial.
- **Earlier liver-fat signal.** [rct] An earlier 6-month double-blind RCT of **HIV-infected patients with abdominal fat accumulation on antiretroviral therapy** (48 treated; 28 tesamorelin 2 mg/day, 22 placebo) found a liver-fat reduction (median −2.0% [IQR −6.4 to 0.1] vs +0.9% placebo, p=0.003) as a co-endpoint alongside visceral fat [2]. Population: HIV-positive. This is the proof-of-concept that motivated the 2019 dedicated NAFLD trial.
- **Mechanism.** [mechanism_review] A secondary mechanistic analysis using the paired liver biopsies from the 2019 trial (HIV-positive NAFLD) reported that tesamorelin **upregulated oxidative-phosphorylation gene sets and downregulated inflammation/tissue-repair/cell-division gene sets**, with fibrosis-gene-score improvement tracking these shifts — a biological rationale for the histological benefit [3]. This is hypothesis-supporting, not independent efficacy evidence.

**Bottom line for Domain 1:** A coherent INVESTIGATIONAL package (proof-of-concept RCT → dedicated NAFLD RCT with biopsy → mechanism), but every datapoint is in HIV-positive patients. No evidence in HIV-negative NAFLD/MASLD exists.

### Domain 2 — Cognition — INVESTIGATIONAL

This is the one beyond-label domain with **non-HIV human data**.

- **Anchor RCT.** [rct] In a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, **152 adults aged 55–87 (mean 68) — both cognitively healthy older adults and adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment (MCI); no HIV involvement** — received tesamorelin 1 mg/day or placebo for 20 weeks (plus 10-week washout). Tesamorelin improved **executive function (p=0.005)** with a non-significant trend for verbal memory (p=0.08); visual memory was unchanged [4]. Population annotation: community-dwelling older adults / MCI, HIV-negative.
- **Caveats.** [rct] Effects were modest, on a 20-week horizon, with surrogate cognitive endpoints — not dementia incidence or function. No phase-3 confirmation followed. A separate phase-2 trial of tesamorelin for cognition in *aging HIV-infected* persons (NCT02572323) was registered, keeping the cognition program alive but unconfirmed [5].

**Bottom line for Domain 2:** A single positive but small/short RCT in HIV-negative older adults/MCI. Genuinely INVESTIGATIONAL — and the only beyond-label efficacy signal generated outside the HIV population. It does NOT support clinical use for cognitive enhancement or dementia prevention.

### Domain 3 — General population / muscle / athletic performance / anti-aging — ABSENT

There is **NO human efficacy data** for tesamorelin in any of the following uses. State this as a finding, not an omission:

- **Bodybuilding / physique / athletic performance — ZERO human efficacy trials.** [regulatory] All "benefit" claims for these uses trace to vendor/coaching content, not to trials in healthy or athletic populations. Off-label and grey-market use is widespread, but no RCT, cohort, or open-label study has tested tesamorelin for muscle gain, strength, body recomposition, or performance in healthy adults. (No admissible primary exists to cite; the absence is itself the finding.)
- **Muscle wasting outside HIV — one TERMINATED trial, no positive data.** [regulatory] A sponsor (Theratechnologies) trial of tesamorelin + exercise for lean body mass in **COPD patients with muscle wasting (HIV-negative)**, NCT01388920, was **terminated** with no published efficacy results [6]. So even the one non-HIV muscle-wasting attempt produced no usable efficacy evidence.
- **Anti-aging / longevity — ZERO outcome data.** [mechanism_review] Tesamorelin reliably raises IGF-1 by roughly 50–100% (a pharmacodynamic effect, well established in the HIV trials and in pharmacology reviews) [2], but **no human study links tesamorelin to any aging, mortality, frailty, or fracture outcome.** IGF-1 elevation is a surrogate, not an anti-aging endpoint.
- **Fracture / bone outcomes — ZERO trials.** No human study has tested fracture or bone-density endpoints.

**Bottom line for Domain 3:** Bodybuilding, athletic performance, general/healthy-adult fat loss, anti-aging, and fracture prevention are all ABSENT domains — used in the wild, but with no human efficacy evidence whatsoever.

### Concentration audit

Sikiric-style: enumerate the distinct human-efficacy primaries across §B + §C, attribute each to its sponsor/research lineage, and compute the single-lineage / single-sponsor share. Mechanistic secondary analyses (e.g. [3]) are listed but not double-counted as separate primaries.

Enumerated human-efficacy primaries (N = 5):

1. **[2] Stanley 2014, JAMA** — VAT + liver fat, HIV. Lineage: **MGH / Grinspoon–Stanley**. Sponsor relationship: investigator-led with Theratechnologies study drug.
2. **[1] Stanley 2019, Lancet HIV** — NAFLD/fibrosis, HIV. Lineage: **MGH / Grinspoon–Stanley** (Stanley/Fourman/Grinspoon).
3. **[4] Baker 2012, Arch Neurol** — cognition, HIV-negative older adults/MCI. Lineage: **Baker / University of Washington**. Drug: tesamorelin (Theratechnologies).
4. **§B pivotal lipodystrophy RCT(s)** — VAT, HIV. Lineage: **Falutz et al., Theratechnologies-sponsored** registrational program (the approval basis; counted here as one primary lineage for the cross-section audit).
5. **[3] Fourman 2020, JCI Insight** — counted as a mechanistic secondary of primary #2, NOT as an independent efficacy primary (so it does not enter the denominator).

Denominator of distinct efficacy primaries = **4** (items 1–4; item 5 excluded by rule).

Attribution by research lineage:
- MGH / Grinspoon–Stanley: 2 of 4 = **50%**
- Falutz / Theratechnologies registrational: 1 of 4 = 25%
- Baker / UW: 1 of 4 = 25%

**Single-lineage share = 50% (MGH/Grinspoon–Stanley), BELOW the 70% flag threshold.**

Industry-sponsor concentration is a different lens. Tesamorelin is a single-sponsor molecule: **all four primaries used Theratechnologies study drug or sponsorship** in some form (the Falutz program was directly company-sponsored; the MGH and UW studies were investigator-led but used the manufacturer's drug). On a strict sponsor/manufacturer-involvement basis the share is effectively **100% (4/4)**. This is FLAGGED as ≥70% — but for an approved single-source drug this is expected and not by itself a red flag; it does mean there is **no manufacturer-independent confirmatory efficacy program**, and no replication of the beyond-label NAFLD or cognition signals by a fully independent group/sponsor.

Net audit read: the *academic-lineage* concentration is moderate (50%, below flag). The *sponsor/manufacturer* concentration is total (100%, flagged) — honest to report, structurally inevitable for a single-source approved drug, and the main caveat is the lack of independent replication beyond the HIV setting.
