# Section F — Practitioner-practice + non-English

Scope note: Per type-tag discipline, the FDA-label dose below is `regulatory` (authoritative). All practitioner/vendor/compounding material in this section concerns *dosing and prescribing conventions only* — it NEVER grounds an efficacy or adverse-event-rate claim. Efficacy and AE rates belong to the trial/regulatory sections, not here.

### Dose & prescribing conventions

**Authoritative label doses (`regulatory`).** Tesamorelin (brand: Egrifta) is approved for one indication only — *reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adult patients with lipodystrophy* — across all three marketed formulations [3][4]. The three FDA-labeled doses, each verified against DailyMed / accessdata, are:

- **Original EGRIFTA (1 mg/vial formulation):** **2 mg SC once daily**, injected into the abdomen. Because each single-use vial contains 1 mg, the 2 mg daily dose requires reconstituting and administering **two vials** per dose [4][5]. `regulatory`
- **EGRIFTA SV (2 mg/vial formulation):** **1.4 mg SC once daily**, delivered as **0.35 mL of reconstituted solution** into the abdomen [1][3]. `regulatory`
- **EGRIFTA WR (11.6 mg multi-dose vial):** **1.28 mg SC once daily**, delivered as **0.16 mL** [2]. `regulatory`

The successive reformulations (SV, then WR) lowered the milligram dose and the injection volume while delivering comparable tesamorelin exposure — the headline "2 mg → ~1.4 mg → 1.28 mg" reduction reflects formulation/concentration changes, not a deliberate de-escalation of the active. Routes (SC, abdomen, rotate sites) and the once-daily frequency are identical across all three [1][2][4]. `regulatory`

**Off-label practitioner conventions (`practitioner_protocol`).** Tesamorelin is widely discussed in the anti-aging / body-composition prescriber space for visceral-fat reduction in non-HIV adults. The conventions that recur across these sources are: starting at **1 mg SC daily** and titrating toward the labeled **2 mg daily**; dosing at night / on an empty stomach; and intermittent schedules such as **5 days on / 2 days off**, run in **~3-month cycles** with rest intervals, alongside baseline + follow-up IGF-1, fasting glucose / HbA1c, and lipid monitoring [6][7][8].

**Honest attribution gap.** The aplus-research standard requires every `practitioner_protocol` claim to carry a **named prescriber + venue + date**. None of the off-label protocols I was able to open meet that bar — they are anonymous med-spa / clinic blog pages and peptide-vendor "dosing guides" (TrimRX, PerfectB, Swolverine, Muscle&Brawn, PeptideDeck, et al.) with no identified author, institution, or presentation date [6][7][8]. The A4M (American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine) does run CME conferences and a peptide-therapy curriculum that covers tesamorelin dosing, but I did not locate a citable, dated A4M/IFM/seedsscientific lecture or named-prescriber protocol document specifying the dose [9]. **Therefore the off-label conventions above are reported as an unattributed convention-of-the-field, NOT as a properly attributed `practitioner_protocol`.** They should not be relied upon as authoritative, and no efficacy/AE claim is drawn from them.

**Cost (qualitative; `regulatory`/anecdote).** Brand Egrifta is expensive. Patient-assistance/cost aggregators place brand Egrifta SV in the **multiple-thousands-of-dollars-per-month** range for a 30-day supply (reported figures span roughly $2,400 to $5,500/month before insurance, varying by source and pharmacy), with manufacturer (Theratechnologies / THERA) patient-support and copay programs cited for eligible patients [10]. These are price-aggregator/anecdote figures, not a clinical claim. `anecdote`

### Compounding / reconstitution

**Compounding gate result.** I did **not** find a live tesamorelin product page on a major 503A/503B compounder's own catalog (Empower's published catalog surfaced sermorelin acetate injection and other GH-axis peptides, but no tesamorelin product page was retrievable) [11]. A compounding-pharmacy trade explainer (503Pharma) does describe tesamorelin as compoundable "for specific needs under regulations" — suggesting injectable solutions at **1–2 mg/mL** (bacteriostatic water, pH 5–7) or **lyophilized 2 mg/vial** preparations — but it simultaneously flags that tesamorelin's approved/biologic status **restricts bulk compounding** and that any preparation must be patient-specific under 503A/B with documented necessity [12]. `compounding_data_sheet` (trade-explainer, not a true data sheet)

This sits inside an unsettled FDA peptide-compounding landscape: FDA moved a batch of peptides into Category 2 of the interim 503A bulks list (Sept 2023), then in Sept 2024 removed five (AOD-9604, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, thymosin alpha-1, Selank) after nominators withdrew. Tesamorelin was **not** reported among either the added or the removed Category-2 peptides in the sources I opened; its compoundability is constrained chiefly by its approved-drug/biologic status rather than by an explicit bulks-list placement [13]. **Net gate result: no verified compounding data sheet from a named compounder offering tesamorelin was located; only a trade explainer asserting conditional compoundability.** `compounding_data_sheet`

**Reconstitution (math only; `regulatory` for label, `vendor_label` for off-label).**
- **EGRIFTA SV (label):** reconstitute one 2 mg lyophilized vial with **0.5 mL Sterile Water for Injection** → **2 mg / 0.5 mL** (4 mg/mL); roll gently 30 s, do not shake; draw **0.35 mL = 1.4 mg**; use immediately, discard remainder [1][3]. `regulatory`
- **EGRIFTA WR (label):** reconstitute the 11.6 mg vial with **1.3 mL Bacteriostatic Water for Injection** → seven daily doses of **1.28 mg / 0.16 mL**; one vial mixed per week; store at room temperature, do not freeze/refrigerate after mixing [2]. `regulatory`
- **Original EGRIFTA (label):** each 1 mg vial reconstituted with **2.2 mL** diluent; two vials per 2 mg dose [4][5]. `regulatory`
- **Off-label/vendor reconstitution math (no efficacy):** vendor and clinic guides commonly describe a **10 mg "research" vial reconstituted with ~2.5 mL bacteriostatic water**, where "25 units" on an insulin syringe ≈ **1 mg** [6][14]. This is gray-market reconstitution arithmetic only and is flagged as such. `vendor_label`

### Non-English literature

**Russian (eLibrary / CyberLeninka).** A targeted search using the Cyrillic term **тезаморелин** plus releasing-hormone / GHRH terms returned **no Russian-language primary clinical or review article on tesamorelin specifically**. The hits were adjacent releasing-hormone topics — thyrotropin-, corticotropin-, and gonadotropin-releasing hormone, and IGF-1 in aging/metabolism — none of them about tesamorelin [15]. **Russian-language literature on tesamorelin: none located** (honest gap, not fabricated).

**Chinese (CNKI / Wanfang / drug-intelligence).** No **Chinese-language primary clinical study** of tesamorelin (替莫瑞林 / 替莫瑞林乙酸盐) was located in CNKI/Wanfang surfacing [16]. What does exist is a Chinese **drug-tracking database entry** (Synapse / 智慧芽), which is a pipeline-intelligence record, not a primary study: it lists tesamorelin's target as GHRH, its global in-research indications (HIV infection, lipodystrophy, hepatic fibrosis), notes **no recorded China development/approval stage historically**, and flags one **emerging China-based trial** — a Phase 2 placebo-controlled study in metabolic-associated steatotic liver disease (NCT07481734), sponsored by Hudson (Tianjin) Biotech, dated 2026 [17]. **Chinese-language primary literature on tesamorelin: none located**; only a database/pipeline entry plus one recently-registered China-sited trial (English-protocol).

**Language vs. authorship distinction.** The bulk of tesamorelin's evidence base is English-language work led by North American groups (Theratechnologies, MGH/Grinspoon). I found **no non-English-LANGUAGE primary** that would add independent evidence; the only non-English-origin signal is the China-sited NCT07481734 registration, which is an English-protocol trial conducted in China, not a Chinese-language primary publication [17].
