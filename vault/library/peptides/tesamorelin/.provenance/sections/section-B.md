## Section B — Approved-indication human efficacy

**Scope note / integrity boundary.** Every efficacy figure in this section was generated in the *single* population in which tesamorelin's efficacy is FDA-established: **HIV-infected adults on antiretroviral therapy (ART) with lipodystrophy and excess visceral/abdominal adipose tissue (VAT)**. The FDA-approved indication is "the reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adult patients with lipodystrophy" `[6]`. The label explicitly states the drug is "**not indicated for weight loss management as it has a weight neutral effect**" `[6]`. None of the numbers below should be read as evidence for general anti-aging, bodybuilding, or non-HIV fat-loss efficacy; such uses are off-label extrapolation and belong in Section C/E. The defining, verified fact of this section is that the proven endpoint is *selective visceral (intra-abdominal) fat reduction in HIV lipodystrophy*, not weight, not subcutaneous fat, and not lean mass.

### Mechanism in one line (regulatory/mechanism_review)
Tesamorelin is a synthetic analogue of human growth-hormone–releasing factor (GHRH/GRF[1-44]) that stimulates endogenous pulsatile growth-hormone secretion, raising IGF-1 toward the physiologic range; in HIV lipodystrophy this preferentially mobilizes visceral adipose tissue [mechanism_review] `[5]` `[6]`.

### The pivotal trial: Falutz 2007 (NEJM) — LIPO-010 [rct]
The pivotal Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial randomized **412 HIV-infected patients** (86% male) with ART-associated abdominal fat accumulation to **tesamorelin 2 mg subcutaneously once daily** or placebo for **26 weeks**; the prespecified primary endpoint was percent change in VAT measured by CT [rct] `[1]`. Results (all **in HIV-lipodystrophy patients**):

- **VAT: −15.2% with tesamorelin vs. +5.0% with placebo** at 26 weeks (P<0.001) — i.e., the drug reversed the ongoing visceral-fat accumulation seen in the placebo arm [rct] `[1]`.
- **IGF-1: +81.0% vs. −5.0%**, confirming on-target GHRH-axis stimulation [rct] `[1]`.
- **Triglycerides: −50 mg/dL vs. +9 mg/dL**, with a significant improvement in the total-cholesterol/HDL ratio [rct] `[1]`.
- **No significant difference in glycemic measures** between arms at 26 weeks; more tesamorelin patients withdrew for adverse events, though overall AE rates did not differ significantly [rct] `[1]`.

The trial's own framing is that the reduction was "**highly significant and selective**" for visceral fat — establishing the visceral-specific signature that distinguishes this approved endpoint from generalized fat or weight loss `[1]`.

### The confirmatory trial + durability/reversal: Falutz 2010 (JAIDS) [rct]
The confirmatory Phase 3 trial randomized **404 HIV-infected patients with excess abdominal fat** across **two sequential 26-week phases (52 weeks total)** [rct] `[3]`. Design is the critical part for the honesty of this section: Phase 1 (months 0–6) randomized 2:1 to tesamorelin 2 mg SC daily vs. placebo; Phase 2 (months 6–12) **re-randomized** the tesamorelin responders to continue or **switch to placebo**, while the original placebo arm crossed to active drug [rct] `[3]`.

- At 6 months, **VAT fell −10.9% (−21 cm²) with tesamorelin vs. −0.6% (−1 cm²) with placebo** [rct] `[3]`.
- With continued treatment to 12 months, VAT reduction deepened to **roughly −18%** [rct] `[3]`.
- **Effect reverses on discontinuation (key honest point):** in patients switched from tesamorelin to placebo at week 26, "the initial improvements over 6 months in VAT were rapidly lost" — visceral fat re-accumulated toward baseline [rct] `[3]`. This is corroborated by independent summaries reporting return to near-baseline VAT within ~3 months of stopping `[5]`. Tesamorelin is therefore a **maintenance therapy, not a one-time fix**: the benefit is contingent on continued dosing.
- IGF-1 increased significantly (P<0.001) without adverse glucose changes [rct] `[3]`.

### Pooled analysis of both Phase 3 trials: Falutz 2010 (JCEM) [rct, pooled]
The two coordinated pivotal trials were pooled (**N = 806 ART-treated HIV patients** with excess abdominal fat, randomized 2:1 to tesamorelin 2 mg SC daily [n=543] vs. placebo [n=263]; primary endpoint percent change in VAT at week 26) `[2]`.

- **VAT: −24 cm² (tesamorelin) vs. −2 cm² (placebo)**, treatment effect **−15.4%** at week 26 (P<0.001) [rct] `[2]`.
- **IGF-1: +108 ± 112 vs. −7 ± 64 ng/mL** (P<0.001) — confirming the axis effect across the combined population [rct] `[2]`.
- **Triglycerides: −37 mg/dL vs. +6 mg/dL**, treatment effect **−12.3%** [rct] `[2]`.
- **Subcutaneous fat: NO significant change** (−2 ± 32 vs. +2 ± 29 cm², P=0.08) — the formal demonstration that the effect is *visceral-selective and weight-neutral*, not a generalized fat- or weight-loss effect [rct] `[2]`.
- At 52 weeks, continued-treatment patients **maintained** VAT reduction (−35 cm²) with no tachyphylaxis [rct] `[2]`.
- "No clinically meaningful differences ... in glucose parameters at wk 26 and 52"; generally well tolerated [rct] `[2]`.

### Enumerated pivotal trial program (the "two coordinated Phase 3 trials")
There are exactly **2** pivotal Phase 3 efficacy trials underpinning the approval:

1. **LIPO-010** — n=412 — NCT00123253 — primary report Falutz 2007 NEJM `[1]` `[2]`.
2. **Confirmatory Phase 3 trial** — n=404 — 52-week 2-phase design — Falutz 2010 JAIDS `[3]`; pooled with #1 in Falutz 2010 JCEM `[2]`.

(Related extension registration NCT00608023 covered open-label safety extension; it is not a separate pivotal efficacy trial.) The trials were both 26-week main + 26-week extension, 2 mg SC daily, VAT-by-CT primary endpoint, **all in HIV-associated lipodystrophy** `[1]` `[2]` `[3]`.

### Independent confirmation: meta-analysis (meta_analysis)
A 2026 meta-analysis (Badran et al., *Obes Res Clin Pract*) of **five RCTs** of tesamorelin in HIV-associated lipodystrophy found a **significant pooled VAT reduction (MD = −27.71 cm², P<0.001)**, a significant reduction in **hepatic fat (MD = −4.28%, P<0.001)**, improved IGF-1, and — consistent with the pivotal data — **no significant reduction in subcutaneous adipose tissue**, with no glucose perturbation and no serious safety signal beyond arthralgia/myalgia/paresthesia/injection-site reactions [meta_analysis] `[4]`. This reinforces the visceral-selective, weight-neutral profile **in the HIV-lipodystrophy population** and adds liver-fat reduction as a downstream visceral correlate.

### Magnitude, durability, reversal, and IGF-1 targeting — summary (all in HIV lipodystrophy)
- **Magnitude:** ~15% VAT reduction at 26 weeks, deepening to ~18% by 52 weeks on continued dosing `[1]` `[2]` `[3]`; pooled treatment effect −15.4% / −24 cm² `[2]`; meta-analytic MD −27.71 cm² `[4]`.
- **Selectivity (endpoint discipline):** the proven endpoint is **visceral/intra-abdominal fat by CT**. Subcutaneous fat, body weight, and BMI are **unchanged** (label: "weight neutral") `[2]` `[6]` — so this is not generalized fat loss.
- **Durability:** maintained through 52 weeks without tachyphylaxis `[2]`.
- **Reversal on stopping:** VAT re-accumulates rapidly toward baseline once tesamorelin is discontinued — efficacy requires ongoing therapy `[3]` `[5]`.
- **IGF-1 normalization-range targeting:** therapy raises IGF-1 from the often-low baseline of lipodystrophy toward the physiologic range; the FDA label directs clinicians to **monitor IGF-1 and consider discontinuation for persistent elevation (e.g., >3 SDS)**, i.e., dosing aims for normalization rather than supraphysiologic IGF-1 [regulatory] `[6]`.
- **Note on formulation/dose:** pivotal trials used the original **2 mg SC daily**; the reformulated EGRIFTA SV is dosed **1.4 mg SC once daily**, bioequivalent to the original 2 mg `[6]`.
