Winner = Tesamorelin (21/25): highest score AND the most robust *real* evidence base, so a deep entry
can be written on verified data with no fabrication pressure. Its honest first-class caveat
(HIV-population-only efficacy) is exactly the kind of caveat the integrity gate exists to surface.

Runner-up Thymosin Alpha-1 (19/25) is logged in the triage ranked queue as the next deep-pass candidate.
