# Tesamorelin — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **tesamorelin** (synthetic GHRH(1-44) analogue; brands Egrifta / Egrifta SV / Egrifta WR), companion to `vault/library/peptides/tesamorelin/research-report.md`. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. Tools: WebSearch + WebFetch. Every citation below was checked for a retrievable identifier (PMID / DOI / NCT / stable database URL). No citation is fabricated; items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in China/Russia but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and already in the tesamorelin corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian or Chinese** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. This is the non-English-**LANGUAGE** vs non-English-**AUTHOR** distinction, applied strictly.

**Headline result: ZERO genuinely non-English admissible tesamorelin primaries located.** Russian (eLibrary / CyberLeninka) = none; the Cyrillic search returned only adjacent releasing-hormone topics, none about tesamorelin. Chinese (CNKI / Wanfang) = no Chinese-language primary; only a **drug-intelligence database entry** (Synapse / 智慧芽, a pipeline-tracking record, not a study) plus **one China-sited but English-protocol trial** (NCT07481734, MASLD, Hudson [Tianjin] Biotech, 2026, unstarted/unpublished). The originator evidence base is English-published by North-American groups (Theratechnologies, MGH/Grinspoon). This is a clean, well-characterized null: the non-English tesamorelin footprint is consumption/marketing and pipeline-tracking of the English corpus, not independent non-English-language primary research.

## Other / originator (note)

**Result: the originator evidence base is English-published — "none in another language" is the EXPECTED valid finding.**

Tesamorelin was developed by **Theratechnologies Inc.** (Montréal, Canada), and the pivotal/beyond-label evidence comes from North-American groups — the Falutz registrational program and the MGH/Grinspoon–Stanley investigator-led studies — **all published in English** (NEJM, JCEM, JAIDS, Lancet HIV, JAMA, Arch Neurol). Where the originator literature is English, "none in another language" is a valid finding, not a coverage gap. No incidental admissible non-English primary surfaced in any other language (Japanese, German, French, Spanish) during the searches above; these tracks were not separately surveyed because the originator and pivotal literature are wholly English and no foreign-language signal pointed to them.
