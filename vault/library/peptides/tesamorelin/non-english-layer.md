# Tesamorelin — Non-English Literature Coverage

Per the whitelist, non-English peer-reviewed literature is admissible under the SAME tier rules as
English. This layer documents what was surveyed and found.

## Survey performed
- **Chinese:** searched (CNKI-adjacent hits, Chinese drug-database/Synapse profiles, MedSci write-ups,
  reagent-vendor pages). All located items are **secondary commentary, drug-database entries, or
  Chinese-language summaries of the English-language MGH/Theratechnologies trials** — not original
  non-English primary research. **No admissible Chinese primary located.**
- **French:** despite the Canadian (Theratechnologies, Québec) origin of the drug, **no French-language
  primary located**. The pivotal program published in English (NEJM, JAIDS, JAMA, Lancet HIV).
- **Russian / other:** not indicated for this compound (no originator-country signal pointing there);
  not surveyed beyond the general sweep.

## Result
**NO ADMISSIBLE NON-ENGLISH PRIMARY LITERATURE LOCATED.** Tesamorelin's evidence base is
English-language, centered on the Massachusetts General Hospital (Grinspoon/Stanley) group and the
Theratechnologies multicenter Phase-3 program. This is unsurprising for a Western-developed,
FDA-approved peptide — contrast with BPC-157 (Croatian originator) or the Russian cognitive peptides,
where non-English coverage is material.

## Translation notes
- None required — no source-language-only primary was admitted.
