# Tesamorelin — Prescribing-Practice Layer

Documents how the compound is ACTUALLY dosed/cycled/routed by prescribers. Per the source
whitelist, `practitioner_protocol` cites may support dose/route/cycle conventions ONLY — never
efficacy, AE rates, or mechanism (those require Tier 1/2).

## On-label dosing (the authoritative reference — `regulatory`, not practitioner)
- **Egrifta SV: 1.4 mg subcutaneous once daily** into the abdomen (rotate sites). Original
  **Egrifta: 2 mg** SC daily; **Egrifta WR: 1.28 mg** SC daily — the three are **exposure-matched
  and NOT interchangeable**. `[regulatory: FDA Egrifta SV label, NDA 022505]`
- Reconstitution (SV): lyophilized powder + 0.5 mL Sterile Water → 2 mg/0.5 mL; roll gently 30 s,
  do not shake; inject 0.35 mL immediately; discard remainder; room-temp storage, protect from light.
  (Egrifta WR uses bacteriostatic water — different diluent.) `[regulatory]`

## Off-label prescribing conventions
- **NO ADMISSIBLE `practitioner_protocol` SOURCE LOCATED.** A dedicated search for an attributable,
  citable off-label tesamorelin protocol (a named prescriber — e.g. a published Seeds / A4M / IFM
  protocol, or a named-physician stated regimen in a citable venue) returned nothing meeting the
  admissibility bar.
- Off-label regimens that circulate online (e.g. "1–2 mg nightly, 5-on/2-off") are **vendor- or
  forum-sourced** → `anecdote_aggregate`, which may NOT ground a dose convention in this wiki.
- **Consequence:** there is no admissible non-label dosing convention to report. Any off-label use
  defaults to the on-label dose/route as the only authoritative reference, under MD supervision.

## Monitoring conventions (from the label, applicable to any use)
- Baseline + interval **IGF-1** (discontinue on persistent >+3 SDS) and **fasting glucose / HbA1c**.
- Active malignancy, pregnancy, HPA-axis disruption = contraindicated. Discontinue in acute critical illness.

## Doctor-handout note
Because non-HIV use is off-label, costly, and unproven in this population, tesamorelin belongs on the
**July-2026 doctor-handout queue** for a supervised discussion — not a self-sourced gray-market protocol.
