# Hexarelin (Examorelin) — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **hexarelin (examorelin; His-D-Mrp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH₂)**, companion to [[library/peptides/hexarelin/research-report]]. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. Searched Russian (CyberLeninka / eLibrary), Chinese (CNKI / Wanfang / VIP-Qikan), and Italian (originator country).

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in Italy/Russia/China but published in English** (a PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and already in the hexarelin corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, or Italian** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. This non-English-**LANGUAGE** vs non-English-**AUTHOR** distinction is load-bearing for hexarelin specifically, whose substantive originator literature (Deghenghi, Ghigo/Turin, Locatelli/Müller/Milan) is English-published.

**Headline result (per Section F):** one Russian-language **secondary review** located (context-only, grounds nothing); **no Chinese-language primary located**; **no Italian-language primary located** (the originator country's work is English-published). This is a clean, well-characterized near-null — the non-English hexarelin footprint is consumption/marketing of the English corpus, not independent non-English-language primary research.

## Russian / Soviet

**Result: ONE genuine Russian-language source located — a secondary review, NOT a primary; grounds no efficacy.**

- **Brylëv MI, Kozin SV, Korolëv VL, Lotorev DS, Pavlova LA, Alekseev AA.** «Пептиды, повышающие секрецию соматотропного гормона, и их влияние на сердечно-сосудистую систему» ("Peptides enhancing somatotropin secretion and their effects on the cardiovascular system"). *Ученые записки КГУ* (*Scholarly Notes, Kursk State University*), **2013**, CyberLeninka — full text in Russian. tag=`academic_secondary`; tier=3; language=**Russian**. It discusses hexarelin's cardiovascular effects (left-ventricular ejection-fraction improvement in ischemic cardiomyopathy, anti-ischemic protection, multiple administration routes). **It is a literature review, so it cannot ground efficacy here** — listed only as evidence that a Russian-language secondary literature exists, not as a primary finding. The underlying cardiac physiology must be sourced from the research-report §3 primary literature, not from this review.

Other Russian-language hits (SportWiki and vendor sites such as russianpeptide.com, aasraw, eko-tec) are **wiki/vendor and excluded** — SportWiki is wiki-type (HALT-adjacent) and vendors ground nothing. They re-state the English corpus and contain no original data.

**Explicit none-located:** no Russian-language hexarelin **primary** (original experiment, clinical series, or registered trial). The single Russian-language record is a non-groundable secondary review.

## Italian / originator-country / other

**Result: NO Italian-LANGUAGE hexarelin primary located — and this is the EXPECTED valid "none," because the originator lineage published in English.**

Hexarelin originated at **Mediolanum Farmaceutici (Milan / Turin), with R. Deghenghi as principal investigator, in the early 1990s** — an Italian developer and an Italian endocrine research lineage (Ghigo's Turin group; Locatelli/Müller in Milan). But, consistent with the brief's caveat, **the originator group's substantive work was largely English-published** (e.g. the dose-response, PK/PD, and GH-secretagogue papers in English-language journals — Ghigo 1994 *JCEM*, Arvat *Peptides*/*Eur J Endocrinol*, Locatelli 1999 *Endocrinology*, Bodart 2002 *Circ Res*). That work belongs to the English layer (research-report §1–§3), not here.

Italian-**language** searches surfaced only **vendor pages** (e.g. SCBT-IT, Peptidy-IT), a generic SIF/GH-overview magazine piece, and general GH-deficiency reviews — **no Italian-language hexarelin primary**. These are commercial/marketing or general-review pages with no original data, not admissible.

**Explicit none-located:** no Italian-language hexarelin primary. The substantive originator literature exists but is **English-language**; "none in Italian" is a **valid** finding per the originator-country rule.

**Other languages:** none surveyed beyond the three required tracks; no incidental admissible non-English primary surfaced.
