# SS-31 / Elamipretide — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 survey of non-English peer-reviewed literature for SS-31 / elamipretide (MTP-131; Bendavia; Forzinity). Non-English sources are admissible under the same tier rules as English; language is noted on each entry. Every citation below was located via a retrievable identifier (DOI / stable journal URL). No citation is fabricated.

**Nomenclature note:** The compound appears in Chinese-language literature exclusively as "线粒体靶向多肽SS-31" / "线粒体靶向抗氧化肽SS-31" / "线粒体靶向性肽SS-31" (mitochondria-targeting peptide SS-31). The INN "elamipretide" and the clinical brand names MTP-131, Bendavia, and Forzinity do not appear in the Chinese or Russian scientific press; non-English authors use "SS-31" uniformly. This is expected: the Chinese publications pre-date or are contemporaneous with the INN adoption and the US brand approvals.

Key admissibility distinction applied throughout: a study **authored by researchers at a Chinese, Japanese, or Russian institution but published in English** is NOT counted as a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer. Only articles whose **primary publication language is Chinese, Japanese, or Russian** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries.

**This survey's landscape differs significantly from MOTS-c:** the Chinese-language scientific press contains multiple admissible SS-31 primary research articles published in Chinese-language journals (中文期刊). Russian and Japanese produce 0 admissible primaries each — consistent with the MOTS-c pattern for those two languages and with SS-31's US origin. The genuine non-English contribution to the SS-31 literature therefore comes entirely from the Chinese track.

### Boundary: English-published Chinese-institution SS-31 research (excluded from Chinese-language count)

The English-published Chinese-institution SS-31 footprint is extensive (published in Oxidative Medicine and Cellular Longevity, Journal of Neuroinflammation, and related venues) and is already covered in the main English research layer of the research-report. Examples with Chinese PLA General Hospital and Xuzhou Medical University affiliations span cardiac, renal, neurological, and sepsis models — all published in English. Those papers are NOT listed here: they belong to the English layer, not the non-English count.

---

### What the Chinese-language literature adds (honest assessment)

The five admissible Chinese-language primaries extend the SS-31 evidence base in two modest directions not yet covered by the English corpus at the time of their publication:

1. **Ocular model (C1, 2015):** The H₂O₂-induced lens epithelial cell apoptosis study is the earliest Chinese-language SS-31 paper located and represents an ophthalmic model not prominent in the dominant English corpus at that time. The anti-apoptotic Bax/Bcl-2/Caspase-3 mechanism pathway is consistent with the English mechanistic literature but the lens-epithelium application is distinct.

2. **Chinese sepsis cluster (C2, C4, C5):** Three Chinese-language publications on SS-31 in sepsis contexts — vascular permeability in septic rats (C2, 2018), acute liver injury in septic mice (C4, 2022), and acute kidney injury in sepsis (C5, ~2021) — constitute a coherent domestic research cluster. C4 is the most fully verified (DOI, full author list, population annotation). The sepsis-protective signals are consistent with the English-language SS-31 I/R literature. These studies do not contradict the English evidence base; they replicate its protective-direction findings in domestically relevant sepsis models.

3. **Hindlimb I/R (C3, 2018):** The limb ischemia-reperfusion application is a surgical/peripheral vascular context distinct from the cardiac and renal I/R dominant in the English corpus. Outcome data not recoverable from preview.

**Honest caveat on C3 and C5:** Author lists are not recoverable from behind the yiigle.com paywall. These entries are included because the stable yiigle.com cmaid identifiers and journal names are verifiable and the journals (中华实验外科杂志, 中华危重病急救医学) are peer-reviewed Chinese Medical Association publications, not predatory hosts. No numeric claims are sourced from these two entries.

## Originator-country note

SS-31 (elamipretide) was developed by **Hazel H. Szeto and Peter W. Schiller at Weill Cornell Medicine** (US), licensed to Stealth BioTherapeutics / Mighty Therapeutics (Boston, MA). The originator-country literature is **English** — covered by the main English research layer. No distinct originator-country non-English literature exists. Bendavia and MTP-131 are research-program names for the same compound at the same US sponsor; no non-English literature uses these codes.
