## Section C — Nootropic/other + preclinical + concentration audit

**DEFINING FACT (read first).** Essentially the entire admissible Selank evidence base is **preclinical, Russian, and single-network**: it originates from the Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS (IMG RAS — Myasoedov, Andreeva, Slominsky, Shadrina, Grivennikov, Inozemtseva, Kolomin) in collaboration with the Zakusov Institute of Pharmacology (Seredenin) and the Serbsky Center (Zozulia, Neznamov). Selank itself was designed at IMG RAS. There is, as of this writing, **no independent Western RCT** of Selank for any cognitive or psychiatric indication. Every claim below is annotated by population (species + n) and labeled evidenced / PRECLINICAL / limited. The concentration audit at the end quantifies the single-lineage dependence (share is HIGH — see below).

---

### Nootropic / cognition

**Human anxiolytic-with-cognitive-preservation signal (single small trial; PRECLINICAL-adjacent / limited).** The only human cognitive-relevant primary is a comparative trial of 62 patients with generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) and neurasthenia: Selank (n=30) vs. the benzodiazepine medazepam (n=32), assessed on Hamilton, Zung, and CGI scales [1] `open_label`/comparator (not described as placebo-controlled or blinded in the abstract). Both drugs produced **comparable anxiolytic activity**, but Selank additionally showed **"antiasthenic and psychostimulant" effects** — i.e., anxiety relief *without* the sedation/cognitive blunting characteristic of benzodiazepines [1]. The authors also reported that baseline τ½ leu-enkephalin (serum enkephalin half-life) was decreased in patients and rose under Selank, correlating with symptom improvement, particularly in the GAD subgroup [1]. **Integrity note:** this is the cited basis for "cognition-preserving anxiolysis," but it is a single small comparator trial from the Serbsky/Zakusov/IMG-RAS group (Zozulia, Neznamov, Miasoedov, Seredenin among authors), published in Russian (*Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr Im S S Korsakova*). It does not demonstrate pro-cognitive enhancement in healthy humans — only absence of benzodiazepine-type impairment alongside equal anxiolysis. No independent replication located.

**Anxiolytic behavior in stressed rodents + diazepam potentiation (animal; PRECLINICAL).** In male Wistar rats (n=48) under a 14-day unpredictable chronic mild stress (UCMS) protocol, Selank 300 µg/kg intranasally daily produced less anxiety deterioration than stressed controls on the elevated plus maze, and the **Selank + diazepam (1 mg/kg p.o.) combination was the most effective** — anxiety indicators did not differ from pre-stress baseline, an amplified effect over diazepam alone [2] `animal (Wistar rat, n=48)`. The authors interpret this as Selank enhancing benzodiazepine-receptor sensitivity. This is a behavioral (not cognitive-task) endpoint; relevance to "nootropic" status is indirect.

**Note on cognition-task data.** No admissible Tier-1 primary demonstrating Selank improving a discrete learning/memory task (e.g., Morris water maze, passive avoidance) in healthy animals was retrieved and verified for this section; the human/animal anxiolytic literature [1][2] plus the neuroplasticity substrate [3] is what grounds the "nootropic" framing. The cognition claim is therefore **mechanistic + anxiolytic-preservation**, not a demonstrated cognitive-enhancement effect. Vendor/blog sources making stronger cognitive-enhancement claims were excluded (no Tier-1 grounding).

**BDNF / neuroplasticity substrate (animal; PRECLINICAL).** The mechanistic cornerstone of the nootropic hypothesis: Inozemtseva et al. (2008), *Doklady Biological Sciences* (Springer), showed that **intranasal Selank regulates BDNF expression in the rat hippocampus in vivo** [3] `animal (rat)`. Per the abstract record, Selank at **250 and 500 µg/kg increased hippocampal *Bdnf* mRNA at 3 h post-injection and BDNF protein at 24 h post-injection** at both doses [3]. Because BDNF underwrites adult hippocampal neurogenesis, synaptic plasticity, and stress-resilience circuits — and because SSRIs/tricyclics share this BDNF-upregulating action — this is the plausible bridge between Selank's anxiolytic and putative pro-cognitive effects. **Caveat:** this is a short-timecourse molecular endpoint in rats, not a behavioral cognition outcome, and is itself an IMG-RAS product (Inozemtseva, Karpenko, Dolotov, Levitskaya, Kamensky, Andreeva, Grivennikov).

**Gene-expression / GABAergic + serotonergic substrate (animal + in vitro; PRECLINICAL).** Volkova et al. (2016), *Frontiers in Pharmacology*, gave male Wistar rats (n=30; 10 control / 10 Selank / 10 GABA) **Selank 300 µg/kg intranasally** and profiled GABAergic-neurotransmission genes at 1 and 3 h [4] `animal (Wistar rat, n=30)`. ~45 genes changed at 1 h; notable effects included strong downregulation of *Gabre* and *Gabrq* at 1 h (≈20-fold) with rebound *Gabre* upregulation (≈16-fold) and a striking *Hcrt* (hypocretin/orexin) increase (≈128-fold) at 3 h, plus *Drd3* upregulation (≈3.4-fold) — and a high Selank↔GABA effect correlation (r≈0.86) [4]. This places Selank's action partly on the **GABAergic and monoaminergic (dopamine receptor, hypocretin) gene-expression machinery**, consistent with reported region-specific increases in serotonin turnover (the broader Kolomin/Agapova transcriptome line of work). The companion **in vitro** study, Filatova et al. (2017), *Front. Pharmacol.*, found that in IMR-32 human neuroblastoma cells **Selank alone produced no change in the studied GABAergic genes**, but **suppressed GABA-induced changes** and modulated olanzapine responses [5] `in_vitro` (IMR-32). This is an important integrity point: Selank's gene-expression effects in vivo are **not reproduced by direct cell exposure**, implying its action is indirect (systemic/receptor-mediated) rather than a direct transcriptional effect — a nuance vendor summaries omit.

### Other indications — antiviral / immunomodulation (tuftsin lineage)

Selank is a **tuftsin analogue** (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg core + Pro-Gly-Pro stabilizing tripeptide), and tuftsin is an endogenous immunomodulatory tetrapeptide — so immune/antiviral activity is mechanistically expected.

**Antiviral activity vs. influenza (animal + in vitro; PRECLINICAL).** Ershov, Uchakin, Uchakina, Mezentseva, Alekseeva, Miasoedov (2009), *Voprosy Virusologii* (PMID 19882898), tested Selank against **influenza A H3N2 (A/Aichi 2/68)** both in cell culture and in mice [6] `animal (mice)` + `in_vitro`. The **preventive scheme** — Selank added to cell culture 24 h before viral inoculation — gave the highest efficacy, reportedly **completely suppressing viral reproduction**; preventive administration was also optimal in vivo [6]. The proposed mechanism is **IFN-α induction and Th1/Th2 cytokine balancing** rather than direct virucidal action. Russian-only; not independently replicated in Western labs.

**Cytokine / inflammation-gene immunomodulation (animal; PRECLINICAL).** Work on Selank's effect on inflammation-related gene expression in **mouse spleen** (Kolomin/Shadrina/IMG-RAS line) reports that Selank — and its structural fragments — alter chemokine/cytokine/receptor gene expression 6–24 h after a single injection, **inducing IFN-α gene expression without affecting IL-4, IL-10, or TNF-α** [7] `animal (mouse)`. This selective type-I-interferon induction is consistent with the influenza-model mechanism [6] and with the tuftsin immunostimulatory lineage. (This source's full record could not be opened past the publisher paywall; it is cited by record and flagged as **not independently verified to full text** — see self-check 1.)

**Other.** Beyond anxiolysis (Section B), cognition-preservation, and antiviral/immunomodulation, no additional admissible Tier-1 indication (e.g., oncology, metabolic) was located. Claims of broader benefit are vendor-sourced and excluded.

**Denominator = 7 enumerated primaries.**

**Single-lineage share.** **7/7 (100%)** of admissible primaries carry an Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS (Myasoedov/Andreeva/Slominsky/Shadrina/Grivennikov) author and/or a tightly coupled Zakusov–Serbsky collaborator. Even on the strictest reading — counting only papers where IMG-RAS is the *driving* affiliation — the share is **6/7 (≈86%)**, with the lone partial exception being the influenza paper [6] (virology institute lead, but still co-authored by Miasoedov of IMG-RAS). **Either way the single-network share is far above the ≥70% threshold: this is effectively one research network.**

**First-class caveat (surface this above all benefit claims):** Selank's evidence base is **not independently replicated outside its originating Russian network**. The compound was invented at IMG RAS, and the same institute (plus its standing Zakusov-Institute and Serbsky-Center partners) authored essentially every cognitive, neuroplasticity, gene-expression, and immunomodulation primary. There is **no Western RCT, no large multi-center trial, and no independent lab confirmation** of the headline BDNF, gene-expression, or antiviral findings. The human data are limited to one small Russian comparator trial [1]. Confidence in any Selank benefit claim must be discounted accordingly: the literature is internally consistent precisely because it shares authors, methods, and institutional priors — which is **the opposite of independent corroboration**.
