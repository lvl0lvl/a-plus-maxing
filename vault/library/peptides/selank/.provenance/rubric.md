## THE DEFINING FACTS (integrity axes)
1. **Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide — Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro — an analogue of the
   immunomodulatory peptide TUFTSIN (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg) extended with Pro-Gly-Pro for stability**;
   developed by the **Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS + Zakusov Institute of Pharmacology**
   (Moscow). It is an **anxiolytic/nootropic**, registered as a drug **in RUSSIA** (intranasal),
   but **NOT FDA/EMA-approved** anywhere in the West.
2. **THE EVIDENCE BASE IS PREDOMINANTLY RUSSIAN-LANGUAGE + SINGLE-INSTITUTE.** Most clinical and
   preclinical studies are from IMG RAS / Zakusov / Serbsky and published in Russian (or Springer-
   translated, e.g. Bulletin of Experimental Biology and Medicine, Zhurnal Nevrologii i
   Psikhiatrii). Be HONEST: there is **limited independent Western replication**; the
   concentration audit will show heavy single-lineage. The non-English (Russian) layer here is
   genuinely SUBSTANTIVE (not "none located") — survey it properly with translation handling.
3. **The headline claim:** anxiolytic comparable to benzodiazepines but **WITHOUT sedation,
   amnesia, dependence, or withdrawal** (the Russian-trial selling point) — report it as a
   Russian-clinical claim, honestly scoped (not Western-replicated). Mechanism: modulation of
   BDNF/monoamines (serotonin)/GABA-ergic tone + inhibition of enkephalin-degrading enzymes
   (prolongs endogenous enkephalins) + Tuftsin-lineage immunomodulation.
4. **Keep DISTINCT** from **Semax** (a different IMG-RAS peptide — the OTHER session's, bottom-13)
   and from **N-Acetyl-Selank-Amidate** (a stabilized Selank derivative — also the other
   session's). This entry is SELANK only. evidence_tier C (Russian clinical + preclinical, limited
   Western validation) / risk_tier experimental (Russia-registered but unapproved in the West, no
   long-term Western safety data).

## Section plan (6 lean sections)
- **A — Identity, structure, mechanism.** Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro; Tuftsin analogue + Pro-Gly-Pro;
  IMG RAS/Zakusov; mechanism (BDNF, serotonin/monoamine, GABA, enkephalinase inhibition,
  immunomodulation/cytokines [IL-6 etc.]); intranasal PK (rapid, short plasma but CNS effect).
- **B — Anxiolytic clinical evidence (the core; heavily Russian).** The Russian clinical studies
  in GAD / anxiety / neurasthenia (e.g. Zozulia, Medvedev) — anxiolytic efficacy + the
  no-sedation/no-dependence/no-withdrawal claim vs benzodiazepines. Honestly scoped (Russian,
  often small, single-lineage). Verify the PubMed-indexed/Springer-translated ones.
- **C — Nootropic/other + preclinical + concentration audit.** Cognition/attention; BDNF &
  neuroplasticity preclinical (Volkova/Inozemtsev); antiviral/immunomodulation (Tuftsin lineage);
  the heavy IMG-RAS/Zakusov single-lineage concentration (flag ≥70%); the limited-Western-
  replication caveat.
- **D — Safety / adverse effects.** Favorable in the Russian trials — well-tolerated intranasal,
  **no sedation, no dependence/withdrawal** (the differentiator vs benzodiazepines); no long-term
  Western safety data; contraindications (pregnancy/lactation no data); monitoring (largely
  clinical — no validated Selank biomarker).
- **E — Regulatory & sport.** **Russia-registered** anxiolytic (intranasal, in the Russian State
  Register of Medicines ~2009/2010 — verify) but **NOT FDA/EMA-approved**; gray-market nootropic
  in the West (research-chemical); WADA: NOT on the Prohibited List (verify — it's not a hormone/
  GH-secretagogue). Keep distinct from N-Acetyl-Selank-Amidate.
- **F — Practitioner-practice + non-English.** Intranasal dosing conventions (the Russian Selank
  0.15% nasal-drops regimen + the gray-market nootropic dosing); **the substantive Russian
  literature survey** (eLibrary/CyberLeninka/IMG-RAS; Springer-translated journals) with proper
  translation handling.
