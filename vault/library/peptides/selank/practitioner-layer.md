# Selank (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro) — Prescribing-Practice Layer

> **Scope discipline.** This document records *how Selank is dosed, accessed, and reconstituted in practice* — the Russia-registered label regimen, the gray-market Western conventions, and the regulatory/access facts. It is **not** an efficacy document and **must not** be read as one. Per the source-tagging rule, `regulatory`, `practitioner_protocol`, and `vendor_label` sources are admissible **only** for dose/cycle/route/reconstitution/access facts. **None** of these grounds an efficacy or adverse-event-rate claim, and none appears here for that purpose — those Tier-1 claims live in the research report (§§2–4).
>
> **Read this first (defining fact).** Selank has TWO sharply different dosing worlds. In **Russia** it is a **fully registered prescription anxiolytic** — an intranasal 0.15% solution, reg. no. **ЛСР-003338/09** — with a genuine national label regimen (`regulatory`). In the **West** it is **unapproved and not lawfully compoundable**; every Western "dose" is gray-market convention from vendors/protocol aggregators (`vendor_label` / `practitioner_protocol`), never a label. The two must never be conflated.

## Dose/route conventions

**The Russia-registered intranasal regimen (`regulatory`).** Selank is marketed in Russia as **Селанк 0.15% intranasal drops** (kapli nazal'nye Selank 0,15%), reg. no. **ЛСР-003338/09**, developed jointly by IMG RAS (acad. N.F. Myasoedov) and the Zakusov/RAMS Research Institute of Pharmacology (acad. S.B. Seredenin). The labeled formulation delivers **75 mcg of active substance per drop** of 0.15% solution. The registered anxiety regimen is **2-3 drops per nostril, three times daily, for a 14-day course** — a total daily dose in the **~1.5-2.25 mg/day** band — with the registered clinical-trial regimen running as high as **2700 mcg/day** in the pivotal GAD/neurasthenia study. Registered indications are **generalized anxiety disorder, neurasthenia, and adjustment disorders**. This is a genuine national drug label, **not** a convention — but it is a *prescribing* fact, not an efficacy claim; the efficacy/AE record lives in the research report (§§2–4). `[regulatory]`

**Western gray-market nootropic convention (`practitioner_protocol` / `vendor_label`).** Outside Russia, Selank has no approved medicinal status, so all Western dosing is gray-market convention reported by nootropic vendors and protocol aggregators — NOT a label and NOT efficacy-grounding. The reported convention clusters at **intranasal ~250-500 mcg per dose, 1-3 times daily**, run in short cycles (commonly **2-4 weeks on, ~1-2 weeks off**; some sources cite 10-day-on / 5-day-off blocks). Vendor pages report that lower doses (~250-500 mcg) are used for the anxiety endpoint and higher (~1500-3000 mcg/day) for the cognitive endpoint, with a "start low, titrate after 3-5 days" pattern. This is reported strictly as **convention**, not as evidence of dose-response — the only controlled human dose-response data are the Russian trials in the research report (§§2–4). `[practitioner_protocol` for the cycling patterns; `vendor_label` for the per-dose mcg figures]`

**Distinction from N-Acetyl-Selank-Amidate.** Gray-market vendors sell both plain Selank and "N-Acetyl-Selank-Amidate." Western dosing pages frequently blur the two; the figures above are for **plain Selank** only. The amidated analogue (N-terminal acetyl + C-terminal amide) is a distinct chemical entity with its own (sparser, non-clinical) vendor literature and is out of scope here. Selank is likewise held distinct from **Semax** (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro), a separate IMG-RAS heptapeptide. `[vendor_label]`

**Self-check (dosing).** The Russia-registered figures (75 mcg/drop; 2-3 drops/nostril × 3/day × 14 d; ~1.5-2.25 mg/day; up to 2700 mcg/day in-trial) are reported strictly as `regulatory`/label facts. The Western ~250-500 mcg/dose and cycling figures are reported strictly as gray-market convention (`vendor_label`/`practitioner_protocol`). **No efficacy attaches to either** — both worlds defer to the Tier-1 research report.

## Compounding / sourcing reality

**Compounding gate result: Selank is NOT a sanctioned §503A ingredient and is NOT lawfully compoundable for human use in the US.** The mechanical chain `[regulatory]`:

- Selank is **not FDA-approved** for any indication; for its entire Western history it could be sold only as a **research chemical under research-use-only / not-for-human-consumption labeling**, with marketing for human consumption illegal under federal law.
- Selank acetate (designated **TP-7**) had been placed on the FDA's **Category 2** of the interim §503A bulks list — the "may present significant safety risks" bucket that effectively bars compounding use.
- It was **removed from Category 2 in September 2024 (effective Sept 27, 2024) — but only because the nomination was withdrawn**, not because it was cleared. Critically, Selank acetate **was never on Category 1**, and only Category-1 substances may be lawfully compounded under §503A. **Removal from Category 2 did NOT make it compoundable.**
- A 2026 FDA interim-policy update (published April 2026, PCAC meeting noticed for **23-24 Jul 2026**) again removed peptides from Category 2 and referred a slate of substances to PCAC for *consideration* for the 503A bulks list. Popular/vendor commentary framing this as an "RFK Jr Category-1 reclassification" is **not** established by any finalized FDA rule. The lawful-compounding gate is therefore **NOT lawfully compoundable — pending/not finalized**, never "now legal." Even if future bulks-list inclusion were finalized, it **would not establish proven safety, efficacy, or standardized dosing.**

**Compounding data-sheet search result: NONE FOUND.** A targeted search for compounding-pharmacy data sheets (Empower, Tailor Made, Hallandale, Belmar, APS, Strive) returned **no Selank compounding data sheet** — the *expected* result given the regulatory gate, since a non-approved, non-sanctioned-§503A peptide should not be carried by reputable 503A/503B compounders. The practical Western access channel today is therefore **gray-market research-chemical vendors**, sold "for research use only," with vendor-self-reported COA/purity but **no clinical labeling**. `[vendor_label]`

**The one lawful clinical channel is Russia.** Unlike most gray-market peptides, Selank *does* have a sanctioned clinical-supply channel — but only in the Russian Federation, where it is a lawfully marketed registered drug (reg. no. **ЛСР-003338/09**; manufacturer ЗАО «ИНПЦ «Пептоген», Moscow). This is the lone jurisdiction where Selank is a pharmacy-dispensable medicine rather than a research chemical. `[regulatory]`

**Cost (qualitative).** Western gray-market pricing is in the commodity research-peptide range; Russian-registered product is sold through pharmacies at national-drug pricing. No US clinical-grade pricing exists because there is no US-approved product. `[vendor_label / regulatory]`

They are the trial *investigators* documented in the research report (§§2–4), not prescribing-practice protocol sources, and their efficacy/AE findings are Tier-1 and live there — they are **not** repackaged here as practitioner dosing convention.

## Reconstitution / handling notes (vendor_label — arithmetic only)

The Russia-registered product is a **ready-to-use 0.15% intranasal solution** (no reconstitution; 75 mcg/drop, 3 mL bottle) — handling is per the registered label `[regulatory]`. Gray-market Western material is supplied as a lyophilized powder (identity/purity vendor-self-reported, not buyer-auditable) for buyer reconstitution into an intranasal spray/dropper; there is **no standardized Western reconstitution** because no approved Western product exists. Any reconstitution arithmetic encountered on vendor pages is admissible as math only and carries **no efficacy or safety weight** `[vendor_label]`. (Selank's clinically established and registered route is **intranasal**, not subcutaneous; vendor pages occasionally describe SC/IM use, which has no registered-label basis.)
