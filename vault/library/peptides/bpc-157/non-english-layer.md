# BPC-157 — Non-English Literature Layer (Phase 8)

> **Purpose.** Phase-8 non-English survey for the BPC-157 entry, per `references/health-gates §4.2`. It surveys non-English-language literature in **Russian**, **Chinese**, and the **originator country (Croatia)**, and either documents *"no admissible primaries located"* (with what was searched) or surfaces **real new citations not already in the English corpus** (`research-report.md`, refs [1]–[35]). Citations here are numbered independently with an `NE-` prefix to avoid collision with the English corpus, and carry canonical type-tags. Population/route rules apply: animal cites carry species + n where available; no anecdote grounds a number.

**Headline result.** One genuinely new admissible primary surfaced (**Chinese**, mainland — Huang 2015, alkali-burn wound, n=58 SD rats). **Russian**: no admissible primaries located (the entire Russian-language footprint is Wikipedia plus vendor/biohacker re-translations of the Zagreb/English corpus). **Croatian (originator)**: a large body of Croatian-language doctoral dissertations from the Zagreb (Sikiric/Seiwerth) group exists and is admissible as originator-country primary literature, but it is **same-single-lab provenance** — it does not broaden the lab-diversity concern flagged in §4 of the main report, and most of the group's peer-reviewed output is published in English (already captured in the corpus). One representative dissertation cluster is cited below as evidence-of-existence; they are not counted toward independent-lab corroboration.

---

## Russian — *no admissible primaries located*

**Databases / sources queried:** eLibrary.ru (targeted), CyberLeninka (targeted), plus open-web Russian-language searches for клиническое применение / экспериментальное исследование / диссертация / автореферат (dissertation / synopsis) on «BPC-157 / БПК-157 / пентадекапептид».

**Result.** No Russian-language **primary** study (original experiment, clinical series, or registered trial) was located. Every Russian-language hit falls into one of two non-admissible buckets:

- **Tertiary encyclopedic.** Russian Wikipedia, «BPC 157 (пентадекапептид)», which itself simply summarizes the Zagreb animal corpus already in `research-report.md` (skin/mucosa/cornea/muscle/tendon/ligament/bone healing; NO-pathway framing) — not a primary source [NE-1, tertiary_reference].
- **Vendor / biohacker secondary.** Commercial peptide-seller and biohacker explainers — russianpeptide.com, peptipedia.ru, braint.ru, resource-clinic.ru, podpitka.ru, santeclinics.com, refresh.doctor, neuralhack.org, medicalinsider.ru, and a LiveJournal repost — all of which re-translate the same Zagreb/English findings and (in several cases) sell or promote the compound [NE-2, anecdote_aggregate]. These ground **no admissible number** (no species+n original data; no original clinical enrollment).

Despite BPC-157's heavy use in the Russian-speaking peptide/clinical/biohacker community, that use is **practice/marketing**, not indexed primary research: the Russian community **consumes** the Zagreb literature rather than **producing** its own. **No new citation added from Russian.**

## Chinese — *1 new admissible primary (mainland) + provenance note on corpus refs*

**Databases / sources queried:** CNKI-oriented and Wanfang-oriented queries (中国知网 / 万方; «BPC-157 肽 修复 药理 研究», «大鼠 实验 肌腱 愈合 学位论文»), Baidu Health, Zhihu, plus PubMed cross-checks for China-affiliated author groups (角度: angiogenesis / colon / cornea / wound / muscle).

**New primary surfaced (NOT in the English corpus):**

- **Huang T, Zhang K, Sun L, Xue X, Zhang C, Shu Z, Mu N, Gu J, Zhang W, Wang Y, Zhang Y, Zhang W. "Body protective compound-157 enhances alkali-burn wound healing in vivo and promotes proliferation, migration, and angiogenesis in vitro." *Drug Des Devel Ther* 2015;9:2485–2499** [NE-3, animal]. **Mainland-Chinese group** (State Key Laboratory of Cancer Biology, School of Pharmacy, **Fourth Military Medical University (FMMU)**, Xi'an, + Northwest University; + Dept. of Ophthalmology, Xijing Hospital). **In vivo:** male **Sprague-Dawley rats, n=58 total (n=10 per treatment group)**, alkali-burn skin model, **topical** application (cotton swab, twice daily × 18 d), BPC-157 at 200/400/800 ng/mL. **In vitro:** HUVEC proliferation/migration/tube-formation. **Findings:** accelerated wound closure (e.g., 14.13%±4.91% vs 10.80%±4.84% bFGF vs 5.42%±2.09% model control at day 4), increased granulation/re-epithelialization/collagen, raised VEGF in wound tissue, and ERK1/2 → c-Fos/c-Jun/Egr-1 signaling. *This is an independent (non-Zagreb, non-Chang-Gung) lab and is **new** relative to the English corpus.* Tag: `animal` (in-vivo arm carries species + n) with an in-vitro mechanistic arm.

**Provenance correction on an existing corpus ref (no new citation, flagged for the main report):**

- Corpus ref **[28] Xu C. et al., "Preclinical safety evaluation of body protective compound-157…," *Regul Toxicol Pharmacol* 2020;114:104665 (PMID 32334036)** is **also a mainland-Chinese FMMU study** (State Key Laboratory of Cancer Biology, Biotechnology Center, School of Pharmacy, Fourth Military Medical University, Xi'an; multi-species — mouse/rat/rabbit/dog). It is already counted in the English corpus, so it is **NOT** re-counted here, but its Chinese provenance was not previously surfaced. Together [28] and NE-3 show FMMU/Xi'an is a **second mainland-Chinese center** (distinct from the Taiwanese Chang Gung group at corpus refs [3]/[5]/[8]) doing original BPC-157 work — a fact the §4 single-lab audit should note: the non-Zagreb base is broader than "one Taiwanese lab" once the Chinese-language/China-affiliated literature is included, though it remains preclinical/animal.

**Result.** Chinese: **citations found — 1 new admissible primary (NE-3)**; plus a provenance note that corpus [28] is China-affiliated. CNKI/Wanfang full-text indices were not directly browsable from this environment, but the China-affiliated primary literature is captured via its PubMed-/journal-indexed English-language outputs (FMMU publishes its BPC-157 work in English-language journals).

## Croatian (originator country) — *admissible originator-language primaries exist, same-lab provenance; counted as evidence-of-existence, not independent corroboration*

**Databases / sources queried:** University of Zagreb digital repositories — **repozitorij.mef.unizg.hr** (Medical Faculty / "Dr Med"), **repozitorij.pmf.unizg.hr** (Faculty of Science), and the **Nacionalni repozitorij disertacija** (dr.nsk.hr) — searched for «pentadekapeptid BPC 157», «štakor» (rat), «disertacija». (Repository servers returned HTTP 418/403 to automated fetch; bibliographic metadata below is from repository listing snippets.)

**As the task notes, most Zagreb-group output is English-language** and is already in the corpus (the Sikiric/Seiwerth peer-reviewed papers, e.g. corpus [6][9][17]–[27][31][34]). What is *additionally* visible in **Croatian** is a substantial set of **Croatian-language doctoral dissertations / theses** by trainees of the Zagreb group — original primary experimental work in Croatian, not duplicated verbatim in the English corpus. Representative items (all Sveučilište u Zagrebu; all rat models; all Croatian-language) include:

- **"Citoprotektivni učinak želučanog pentadekapeptida BPC 157 kod okluzija velikih krvnih žila"** (Cytoprotective effect of gastric BPC 157 in large-vessel occlusions), V. Dretar, Medicinski fakultet, Zagreb 2022 — rat major-vessel-occlusion model [NE-4, animal].
- **"Učinak pentadekapeptida BPC 157 na regeneraciju jetara štakora nakon subtotalne resekcije"** (Effect of BPC 157 on rat liver regeneration after subtotal resection), Medicinski fakultet [NE-5, animal].
- **"Učinci pentadekapeptida BPC 157, L-NAME i L-arginina na serijski prijelom rebara u štakora"** (Effects of BPC 157, L-NAME and L-arginine on serial rib fracture in rats), Medicinski fakultet [NE-6, animal].
- **"Učinak primjene pentadekapeptida BPC 157 u atrofiji mišića nakon resekcije patelarnoga ligamenta na modelu štakora"** (Effect of BPC 157 on muscle atrophy after patellar-ligament resection in rats), Prirodoslovno-matematički fakultet [NE-7, animal].
- **"Učinak pentadekapeptida BPC 157 na ozlijeđene tetive nadgrebnog i podgrebnog mišića u štakora"** (Effect of BPC 157 on injured supraspinatus/infraspinatus tendons in rats), Nacionalni repozitorij disertacija [NE-8, animal].

**Admissibility & weighting.** These are **admissible** as originator-country, Croatian-language primary experimental documents (rat models; originator-group methodology). However, they are **NOT independent corroboration**: every one traces to the **same single originating lab (Sikiric/Seiwerth, University of Zagreb)** whose concentration is the central evidentiary caveat in §4 of `research-report.md`. They therefore **do not move** the "distinct labs/groups = 2" finding. They are recorded here so the corpus reflects that the Croatian-language layer = *additional same-lab dissertations*, not a separate national literature. Per-dissertation n is not extracted (repository full-text was not machine-accessible); these cites are not used to ground any efficacy number — they document existence and topic only. **No efficacy number imported from these.**

**Result.** Croatian: **admissible originator-language primaries exist (NE-4…NE-8)** but are same-lab; **0 independent new findings.** Net new *independent* citations from Croatian = 0.

## Self-check

- **Languages surveyed:** Russian, Chinese (mainland; plus China-affiliation cross-check), Croatian (originator country). **3 languages — meets §4.2 minimum.**
- **Databases / indices queried:** eLibrary.ru and CyberLeninka (Russian); CNKI/Wanfang-oriented queries + PubMed China-affiliation cross-check (Chinese); University of Zagreb repositories (repozitorij.mef.unizg.hr, repozitorij.pmf.unizg.hr) and Nacionalni repozitorij disertacija dr.nsk.hr (Croatian). Open-web Russian/Chinese/Croatian searches throughout.
- **Admissible vs not:**
  - **Russian — none admissible.** Only Wikipedia (tertiary) and vendor/biohacker secondaries that re-translate the Zagreb/English corpus. No original experiment, clinical series, or trial.
  - **Chinese — 1 new admissible primary (NE-3, Huang 2015, FMMU; SD rat n=58, topical alkali-burn).** Plus provenance note that corpus [28] (Xu 2020) is also FMMU/China-affiliated (already counted; not re-counted).
  - **Croatian — admissible but same-lab.** Croatian-language Zagreb-group dissertations (NE-4…NE-8) exist and are admissible as originator-country primaries, but add no independent-lab corroboration and ground no number.
- **New independent citations added to the corpus by this layer:** **1** (NE-3, Chinese). Croatian dissertations are recorded as same-lab evidence-of-existence; Russian added none.
- **Fabrication check:** every citation here came from a real WebSearch/WebFetch result; Huang 2015 (NE-3) and Xu 2020 ([28]) bibliographic details were verified against PMC/PubMed metadata. Croatian dissertation titles/faculties are from live Zagreb-repository listing snippets (full-text blocked by 418/403, noted explicitly). No source was invented.
- **Tag discipline:** canonical enum used (`animal`, `anecdote_aggregate`, `tertiary_reference`); animal cite NE-3 carries species + n; no anecdote grounds a number.
- **Limitations:** CNKI/Wanfang and eLibrary.ru full-text were not directly browsable from this environment, so a deeper Chinese-only or Russian-only **CNKI/eLibrary full-text** sweep (behind those paywalled indices) could surface additional China-/Russia-language-only items; the China-affiliated literature appears to publish in English-indexed venues (captured), and the Russian footprint appears genuinely derivative, so the residual risk is concentrated in untranslated CNKI/Wanfang theses.
