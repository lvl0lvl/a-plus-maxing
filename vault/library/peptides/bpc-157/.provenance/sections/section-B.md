# Section B — Human Clinical Evidence & Regulatory Status (BPC-157)

> **Citation-integrity note.** This section was re-run because a prior version contained fabricated citations. Every trial registration, regulatory action, and numerical figure below was retrieved from a source that was actually fetched (registry API, regulatory page, or named journalism/legal analysis). Where human evidence does not exist, that absence is reported as a finding rather than filled. Trial registrations are cited only from records located on ClinicalTrials.gov's structured data API.

## B.1 Human clinical trials — what actually exists

A search of ClinicalTrials.gov for the intervention "BPC-157" returns **exactly two records**, and **neither has posted results** [1, rct][2, rct]. There is **no completed human randomized controlled trial of BPC-157 with published, peer-reviewed results** that this search located.

**(1) NCT02637284 — "PCO-02 Safety and Pharmacokinetics Trial."** A Phase I, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot in **42** healthy volunteers, sponsored by PharmaCotherapia d.o.o., testing an oral BPC-157 formulation (labeled Bepecin / PCO-02) versus placebo (PCO-03) for safety and pharmacokinetics. Listed start October 2015, estimated completion March 2016. Its overall status on the registry is **"UNKNOWN"** and **hasResults = No** [1, rct]. Independent reporting indicates results were briefly submitted to ClinicalTrials.gov (reported around May 2016) and then **withdrawn before quality-control / outside review**, so no vetted results entered the record; a 2025 review by the originating group asserts the trial showed safety but does so **without sharing the underlying data or a citation** [3, mechanism_review]. The "42" enrollment figure is grounded in the registry record [1, rct]; no efficacy outcome can be grounded because none was published.

**(2) NCT07437547 — "BPC 157 for Acute Hamstring Muscle Strain Repair."** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled **Phase 2** trial, sponsor Hudson Biotech, of subcutaneous pentadecapeptide BPC-157 once daily for 14 days versus matching placebo in MRI-confirmed acute Grade II hamstring strain. Estimated enrollment **120**; registry start 2 February 2026; estimated primary completion 14 February 2027. Overall status is **"RECRUITING"** with **hasResults = No** [2, rct]. This trial is **registered and ongoing — not completed, with no results** — and therefore provides **no efficacy or safety evidence yet**.

**Older PLIVA / ulcerative-colitis-era work.** Reporting describes clinical work in the 2000s (BPC-157 enemas for ulcerative colitis, associated with the PLIVA company and the Sikiric group), but the resulting papers **"do not appear to be indexed in major global databases like PubMed"** and could not be located or verified here [3, mechanism_review]. Because these cannot be retrieved and adjudicated, **no claim is grounded on them**; they are noted only as unverifiable.

**Provenance of the evidence base.** Essentially all BPC-157 efficacy research is preclinical (animal models), and the great majority originates from a single Croatian research group led by Predrag Sikiric; human data are characterized as "very little," involving small numbers of subjects without rigorous designs [3, mechanism_review].

## B.2 Regulatory status

**United States — FDA (compounding).** On **29 September 2023**, FDA placed BPC-157 into **Category 2** of the 503A bulk drug substances evaluation — the category for substances that "raise significant safety concerns" and are **not eligible** for the interim policy that protects compounding of Category 1 substances [4, regulatory][5, regulatory]. The cited concerns include **immunogenicity for certain routes of administration**, complexities regarding **peptide-related impurities and active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) characterization**, and only **limited safety-related information** for the proposed routes [4, regulatory][5, regulatory]. Practically, Category-2 placement means BPC-157 **cannot be lawfully compounded** under 503A while in that category. As of mid-2026, BPC-157 was among a set of peptides scheduled for a **Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) review (reported for 23 July 2026)**; any move out of Category 2 would require notice-and-comment rulemaking and is neither final nor imminent [5, regulatory]. The documented, authoritative status therefore **remains FDA 503A Category 2 (placed 2023-09-29)**. A purported **HHS removal of BPC-157 from Category 2 on 2026-04-15 is reported only by a secondary low-trust source (a longevity blog, agemd.com) and is NOT confirmed against an FDA/HHS primary** as of 2026-06-18 — that source traces the claim to an RFK Jr. podcast statement and concedes the FDA "has not yet published its formal updated list" — so it is **treated as unverified** and does not change the documented Category-2 status. FDA has **not approved BPC-157 as a drug for any indication**, and there is no legal basis to sell it as a drug, food, or dietary supplement [6, regulatory].

**Australia — TGA.** Following an interim decision (Nov 2023), the TGA amended the Poisons Standard to place BPC-157 in **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** and add it to **Appendix D**, with the change reported to take effect **1 June 2024**; the action was prompted in part by importation referrals (reported as 48 since 1 July 2022) and reflects the lack of established human safety/efficacy data [7, regulatory]. *(Caveat: the TGA primary pages/PDF repeatedly timed out on fetch; the Schedule-4 / Appendix-D / 1-June-2024 specifics are corroborated across secondary sources and the TGA notice listing, but I could not render the full primary decision text — flagged for a verifier.)*

**Europe — EMA.** No EMA marketing authorization for BPC-157 was located; BPC-157 is **not an EMA-approved medicine**. No specific EMA decision document was retrieved, so no EMA action is asserted beyond the absence of authorization.

**Sport — WADA / USADA.** BPC-157 is **prohibited at all times (in and out of competition)** under the WADA Prohibited List **category S0 (Non-Approved / Unapproved Substances)**, because it is not approved by any governmental regulatory health authority for human therapeutic use; it is **not eligible for a Therapeutic Use Exemption** [6, regulatory]. USADA states plainly that "because BPC-157 has not been extensively studied in humans, no one knows if there is a safe dose, or if there is any way to use this compound safely," and that its use "is not supported by medical literature or by any medical associations" [6, regulatory].

## B.3 Human safety data

There is **no controlled human safety dataset** for BPC-157. The one Phase I human PK/safety trial (NCT02637284) never published vetted results [1, rct][3, mechanism_review], and the ongoing Phase 2 trial has no results yet [2, rct]. Regulatory bodies explicitly frame the human safety picture as **unknown** — no established safe dose, no characterized adverse-event profile in a controlled setting [6, regulatory]. Any adverse-event reports circulating are anecdotal/forum-level and are **not** suitable to ground a numerical AE rate; **no human AE rate is reported here** because no admissible source provides one. (No `anecdote_aggregate` source was used to ground any number, per the integrity rules.)

## Evidence-state summary

**There is no admissible human efficacy evidence for BPC-157.** The ClinicalTrials.gov registry contains only two BPC-157 records: a 2015 Phase 1 oral safety/PK trial in 42 healthy volunteers (NCT02637284) whose status is "UNKNOWN" and whose results were withdrawn before review (no vetted results published), and a Phase 2 hamstring-strain trial (NCT07437547) that is recruiting as of 2026 with no results yet. No completed human RCT with published, peer-reviewed efficacy results was located; older PLIVA-era ulcerative-colitis trials are not indexed/retrievable and ground no claim. The evidence base is overwhelmingly animal/preclinical and largely single-group in origin. **Regulatory status:** BPC-157 is unapproved as a drug anywhere located; FDA placed it in 503A **Category 2** (significant safety concerns — immunogenicity, impurities, API characterization) on 29 Sep 2023, effectively barring compounding, with a PCAC review reported for July 2026; Australia's TGA scheduled it as **Schedule 4 + Appendix D** (reported effective 1 June 2024); no EMA authorization exists; and **WADA prohibits it at all times under S0 (Non-Approved Substances)**, with no TUE available. Human safety in a controlled setting is **unknown**, and no admissible source supports a numerical adverse-event rate.
