# LL-37 (Cathelicidin) — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **LL-37 (human cathelicidin / CAMP gene product / hCAP18 C-terminal peptide)**, companion to `vault/library/peptides/ll-37/research-report.md`. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. Tools: WebSearch (US-only) + WebFetch. Tavily not connected — sanctioned substitution. Every citation below was checked for a retrievable identifier (DOI / journal+volume+issue+page / stable database URL); no citation is fabricated. Items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in China/Russia/Sweden but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and (for the prolific Chinese-authored, English-published LL-37 corpus) is overwhelmingly already represented there. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, or Swedish** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. The dispatch correctly flagged that English-published Chinese-authored LL-37 work is abundant in PubMed and does NOT count.

**Headline result: LL-37 — unlike KPV/TB-500 — has a genuine, non-trivial non-English-LANGUAGE primary footprint.** Located and verified: **Russian-language** original research (St Petersburg ENT/IEM group: nasopharyngeal + middle-ear cathelicidin gene-expression studies) plus Russian-language reviews; **Chinese-language** original research (Southern Medical U: *A. baumannii* biofilm; Nanhua U: colon-cancer mouse model; Yiwu Central Hospital: sepsis/ALI nanomicelles) plus a Chinese-language review track; and — notably — a **Swedish-language** peer-reviewed (referentgranskad) article in the originator country (Tandläkartidningen 2018, oral health / periodontitis), so the originator-country track is a *positive* finding, not the expected null. This reflects LL-37's status as an endogenous human peptide of broad clinical interest rather than a niche injectable "research peptide."

**Genuinely non-English admissible primaries added by this layer: 5** (3 Chinese original-research + 2 Russian original-research). Additional non-English **reviews** (Russian ×2, Chinese ×2, Swedish ×1) are recorded as context, not counted as primaries.

- **PRIMARY (counted).** Тырнова Е.В., Алешина Г.М., Янов Ю.К., Кокряков В.Н. «Изучение экспрессии гена кателицидина LL-37 в слизистой оболочке носоглотки» (Investigation of cathelicidin LL-37 gene expression in the nasopharyngeal mucosa). *Российская оториноларингология* (Russian Otorhinolaryngology), 2014, **No. 3 (70)**. **Russian-language original research** (English abstract). Design: **n = 61** surgical tissue samples (patients with nasopharyngeal disease + chronic decompensated tonsillitis); LL-37 vs β-actin mRNA by real-time RT-PCR in surface epithelium. Findings [translated:WebFetch]: LL-37 mRNA detected in **all** tonsillar epithelium samples; absent in **11.1%** of maxillary-sinus and **12.5%** of hypertrophic inferior-turbinate samples; lowest expression in tonsillar + maxillary-sinus tissue (**p < 0.001**); microbial colonization in chronic rhinosinusitis was **not** attributable to impaired LL-37 expression. Stable URL: cyberleninka.ru/article/n/izuchenie-ekspressii-gena-katelitsidina-ll-37-v-slizistoy-obolochke-nosoglotki (full text also in *Российская оториноларингология* 5/2014 PDF at entru.org).
