# Section B — Mechanism of Action (LL-37, dual nature)

LL-37 (human cathelicidin; the 37-residue C-terminal peptide of hCAP18) is a
cationic, amphipathic α-helical host-defense peptide. Its mechanisms span two
opposing axes: a protective axis (direct microbicide, LPS-neutralizer,
chemoattractant, wound-healing/angiogenic factor, anti-amyloid chaperone) and a
pathogenic axis (a self-nucleic-acid carrier that breaks innate-immune tolerance
to drive type-I-IFN autoimmunity, and a pro-tumor growth/recruitment signal in
several cancers). Direction is set by context — concentration, timing relative to
other signals (e.g. LPS priming), tissue, post-translational state
(citrullination), and binding partner (microbial membrane vs. self-DNA/RNA vs.
tumor-cell receptor). The same biochemical property — high cationic charge and
membrane/nucleic-acid affinity — underlies BOTH the beneficial and the harmful
outcomes. This section represents both honestly.

## Claims

### Antimicrobial / membrane disruption (PROTECTIVE axis)

1. LL-37 is directly bactericidal by physically permeabilizing the microbial
   cytoplasmic membrane; its mechanism is a hybrid that shows features of both the
   toroidal-pore model (peptide-lined transmembrane channels with induced positive
   curvature) and the carpet model (surface accumulation to a threshold followed by
   membrane breakdown/lipid extraction). [1, mechanism_review]
2. Structurally, LL-37 oligomerizes on membrane mimics: a crystal structure
   obtained in the presence of detergents resolved a narrow **tetrameric channel**
   with a strongly charged core, and planar-lipid-membrane electrophysiology
   demonstrated a small but defined transmembrane conductance, with MD simulations
   showing the channel is stable in membrane and passes water. This is direct
   structural/biophysical evidence that LL-37 forms defined channels (a deviation
   from the simplest toroidal-pore picture). [2, in_vitro]
3. LL-37 is broad-spectrum, with activity reported against Gram-positive and
   Gram-negative bacteria, certain fungi, and enveloped viruses; the cationic
   peptide is electrostatically drawn to the anionic surfaces (LPS, lipoteichoic
   acid, anionic phospholipids) that distinguish microbial from host membranes.
   [1, mechanism_review]

### LPS neutralization (PROTECTIVE / anti-inflammatory)

4. LL-37 binds and neutralizes LPS (endotoxin), blunting TLR4-driven inflammation:
   in THP-1 human monocytic cells, LL-37 pretreatment (5–10 µg/ml) inhibited
   *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* LPS-induced IL-8 production by ~90% and dampened NF-κB
   activation. Critically, the effect required LL-37 to remain present — removing
   peptide from the medium before LPS stimulation abolished inhibition — showing the
   mechanism is largely **extracellular LPS sequestration**, not an intracellular
   signaling block. [3, in_vitro] (ANTI-inflammatory direction)

### Immunomodulation / chemotaxis (DUAL — primarily pro-inflammatory recruitment, plus anti-inflammatory arms)

5. LL-37 is a chemoattractant for human peripheral-blood neutrophils, monocytes,
   and T cells, acting through the formyl-peptide-receptor-like 1 (FPRL1 / FPR2)
   G-protein-coupled receptor and inducing calcium mobilization — recruiting innate
   and adaptive immune cells to sites of microbial invasion. [4, in_vitro]
   (PRO-inflammatory direction)
6. LL-37 exerts BOTH pro- and anti-inflammatory effects depending on
   microenvironment and timing. ANTI-inflammatory: it down-regulates TLR4 signaling
   by binding LPS (see [3]) and can drive anti-inflammatory mediators (e.g. IL-1RA).
   PRO-inflammatory: when macrophages/monocytes are **primed with LPS before** LL-37
   exposure, LL-37 enhances TNF-α release and enables inflammasome activation with
   IL-1β/IL-18 production; and the presence of ~10 µg/ml LL-37 during monocyte→
   macrophage differentiation down-regulates IL-10 and up-regulates IL-12p40,
   polarizing toward a pro-inflammatory M1 phenotype. The order of signals is the
   switch. [5, mechanism_review] (explicitly DUAL)

### Wound healing / angiogenesis / re-epithelialization (PROTECTIVE)

7. LL-37 promotes wound re-epithelialization: in vitro it drives migration of the
   human HaCaT keratinocyte line (actin remodeling; increased tyrosine
   phosphorylation of focal-adhesion kinase and paxillin) via transactivation of
   EGFR and induction of the FPRL-1 receptor, engaging Snail/Slug, MMPs, and
   MAPK and PI3K/Akt signaling; in vivo, adenoviral delivery of LL-37 to excisional
   wounds in **ob/ob mice** significantly improved re-epithelialization and
   granulation-tissue formation. [6, animal] (also in_vitro for the HaCaT arm)
8. LL-37 is directly angiogenic: acting through FPRL1 on endothelial cells, LL-37
   (~5 µg/pellet) significantly increased vessel formation in the chick
   chorioallantoic-membrane assay, increased collateral vessel growth in a rabbit
   hindlimb-ischemia model, and CRAMP-deficient mice (CRAMP is the murine
   LL-37 ortholog) showed significantly reduced wound revascularization.
   [7, animal] (NOTE: the same FPRL1-driven angiogenesis is co-opted by tumors — see
   the ovarian pro-tumor claim [10] — so this protective mechanism is double-edged.)

### THE DARK SIDE (PATHOGENIC axis)

9. **Self-DNA carrier → break of innate tolerance → type-I IFN (psoriasis driver).**
   In normal physiology pDCs do not respond to self-DNA. LL-37 binds self-DNA
   released from dying cells into condensed, aggregated, degradation-resistant
   complexes that are delivered to endosomal **TLR9** in plasmacytoid dendritic
   cells, triggering robust type-I interferon (IFN-α) production. This is the
   founding mechanism by which an antimicrobial peptide converts inert self-DNA into
   a potent autoimmune trigger and is central to psoriasis pathogenesis.
   [8, mechanism] (in human pDCs; psoriatic skin)
10. The same carrier logic extends to RNA and to systemic lupus (SLE). LL-37 also
    protects self-RNA, enabling endosomal **TLR7** activation and IFN-α in pDCs. In
    SLE, neutrophil-extracellular-trap (NET)-derived DNA externalized with LL-37
    forms immune complexes that stimulate pDC TLR9; LL-37 itself becomes a target of
    pathogenic autoantibodies, and anti-LL-37 antibodies can themselves induce NETs,
    creating a self-amplifying inflammatory loop. LL-37 also enhances dsDNA uptake by
    monocytes, enabling cytosolic STING/TBK1 sensing. [5, mechanism_review]
    (HARMFUL direction; psoriasis + SLE)
11. **Post-translational control of the dark side: citrullination.** At
    inflammatory foci, peptidyl-arginine deiminases (PAD2/PAD4 — highly active during
    NETosis) convert LL-37's arginines to citrulline. Citrullination abolishes
    LL-37's bactericidal activity and alters its immunomodulatory/nucleic-acid-
    handling functions; a homoarginine-substituted, citrullination-RESISTANT variant
    retained antimicrobial potency even after PAD4 treatment or NET exposure. This
    shows the native vs. modified state of the SAME peptide flips its functional
    output and is mechanistically tied to autoimmune (NET-rich) environments.
    [9, in_vitro]
12. **Pro-tumor signaling — ovarian.** LL-37 (over-expressed in ovarian, breast, and
    lung cancers) promotes ovarian tumor progression by recruiting bone-marrow-
    derived multipotent mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs) through **FPRL1**; recruited
    MSCs secrete pro-angiogenic VEGF, IL-6, and active MMP-2. In human ovarian-tumor
    xenografts in **SCID mice**, neutralizing-LL-37 antibodies significantly reduced
    MSC engraftment and tumor weight (P<0.01) and disrupted the fibrovascular
    network. [10, animal] (HARMFUL; the same FPRL1/angiogenesis machinery from [7]/[8])
13. **Pro-tumor signaling — lung.** Myeloid-cell-derived LL-37 promotes lung-cancer
    growth by binding **TLR4** on tumor cells → Akt activation → GSK3β(Ser9)
    inactivation → β-catenin stabilization → Wnt/β-catenin (TCF/LEF) transcription.
    In patients, serum LL-37 was ~26-fold elevated in NSCLC vs. healthy controls
    (7,312±532 vs. 275.6±31 µg/L) and predicted worse survival; **Cramp⁻/⁻ mice**
    (LL-37 ortholog knockout) given Lewis lung carcinoma showed reduced mortality,
    fewer tumors, and lower proliferation markers (PCNA, Ki-67, cyclin D1).
    [11, animal] (HARMFUL)
14. **Amyloid / aggregation — genuinely double-edged.** LL-37's active core
    (residues 17–29) self-assembles into thermostable, ribbon-like helical fibrils
    that co-localize with bacteria — a functional supramolecular state implicated in
    antimicrobial action and aggregation behavior. [12, in_vitro] Conversely,
    full-length LL-37 can act as an anti-amyloid chaperone: it binds islet amyloid
    polypeptide (IAPP) with nanomolar affinity and effectively suppresses IAPP
    amyloid self-assembly and the associated pancreatic β-cell damage in vitro.
    [13, in_vitro] (The suppression of amyloid-β self-assembly is a plausible
    extension of the same chaperone activity but is not separately grounded here;
    the verified, primary-sourced number is the nanomolar IAPP inhibition.) So LL-37
    BOTH self-aggregates AND inhibits the aggregation of disease amyloids, depending
    on partner and context.
