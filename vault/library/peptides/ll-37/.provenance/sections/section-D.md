# Section D — Pathogenic Side: Autoimmunity / Inflammatory Disease / Cancer / Amyloid (LL-37)

LL-37 (the C-terminal peptide of human cathelicidin hCAP-18, gene *CAMP*) is protective as an antimicrobial/immunomodulatory peptide, but in several human diseases it is a *driver* of pathology. This section catalogs the verified pathogenic and disease-context evidence: psoriasis (its best-established disease role, both innate and adaptive), rosacea, lupus/SLE and other autoimmunity, atherosclerosis/cardiovascular disease, the genuinely bidirectional cancer literature, and amyloid biology (also bidirectional/debated). Cancer direction and amyloid direction are stated explicitly per system, per scope.

---

## Claims

### 1. Psoriasis — innate driver: LL-37 converts self-DNA into a TLR9/type-I-IFN trigger in pDCs

1. In psoriatic skin, plasmacytoid dendritic cell (pDC) TLR9 is activated by LL-37 in complex with endogenous self-DNA released from dying cells; LL-37 binds and condenses otherwise-inert self-DNA into aggregated structures retained in early endosomes, converting it into a potent trigger of type-I interferon (IFN-α) production — a foundational mechanism of psoriasis initiation. [1, mechanism_review/in_vitro]

2. This pDC self-DNA–LL-37–TLR9–IFN-α axis is the originating "break-self-tolerance" event linking the antimicrobial peptide to autoimmune/autoinflammatory skin disease; LL-37 is overexpressed in psoriatic (lesional) skin. [1, mechanism_review/in_vitro]

3. LL-37 also binds self-RNA released by dying cells, protects it from degradation, and delivers it to endosomes: self-RNA–LL-37 complexes activate pDCs via TLR7 (IFN-α) and additionally activate classical myeloid DCs (mDCs) via TLR8 (TNF-α, IL-6, mDC maturation) — broadening the innate activation beyond the DNA/TLR9 pathway to a second DC subset. [2, in_vitro]

4. Neutrophil extracellular trap–associated RNA (naRNA) complexed with LL-37 triggers TLR8 (and murine TLR13)–mediated cytokine and further NET release by neutrophils, establishing a self-propagating ("self-amplifying") inflammatory cycle in psoriasis; RNA is abundant in NETs and in psoriatic but not healthy skin. Shown in vitro and in vivo. [3, in_vitro/animal — mouse mechanistic component, n not specified for the in vivo arm]

### 2. Psoriasis — adaptive driver: LL-37 is a bona fide T-cell autoantigen

5. LL-37 is a T-cell autoantigen in psoriasis: roughly two-thirds of patients with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis harbor CD4+ and/or CD8+ T cells specific for LL-37. LL-37-specific T cells produce IFN-γ, and the CD4+ subset also produces Th17 cytokines. [4, cohort/in_vitro]

6. LL-37-specific T cells infiltrate lesional skin and can be tracked in patient blood by tetramer staining; the frequency of circulating LL-37-specific T cells correlates significantly with disease activity, implicating LL-37 in adaptive (not only innate) psoriasis pathogenesis. [4, cohort]

### 3. Rosacea — abnormal cathelicidin processing produces pro-inflammatory peptide forms

7. Rosacea patients express abnormally high cathelicidin levels in facial skin, and — distinctively — the proteolytically processed *forms* of cathelicidin peptide differ from those in normal skin. These abnormal forms arise from a post-translational processing defect driven by increased epidermal serine protease activity (stratum corneum tryptic enzyme / kallikrein-5, KLK5), which cleaves the hCAP-18 proprotein. [5, in_vitro/animal]

8. The abnormal cathelicidin peptides are directly pathogenic: injection of these rosacea-type cathelicidin peptides — or of the protease — into mouse skin reproduces rosacea-like skin inflammation, establishing causality for the cathelicidin/protease axis rather than mere association. [5, animal — mouse; experimental n not specified in abstract-level source]

### 4. Lupus/SLE & other autoimmunity — LL-37–DNA complexes, NETs, and LL-37-specific T-cell help

9. In SLE, activated neutrophils release self-DNA–antimicrobial-peptide complexes in the form of NETs; these LL-37 (and HNP)–self-DNA complexes are found in SLE patient sera and efficiently trigger pDC activation via TLR9, driving IFN-α — translating the psoriasis innate mechanism into a systemic autoimmune disease. [6, cohort/in_vitro]

10. LL-37 is itself an autoantigen in SLE at the adaptive level: native and citrullinated LL-37–specific T cells are present in SLE patients and provide help to B cells for autoantibody production (including anti-LL-37 and anti-dsDNA responses), linking the AMP to the hallmark autoantibody pathology of lupus. Citrullination (a post-translational modification) shapes the autoreactive response. [7, cohort/in_vitro]

### 5. Atherosclerosis / cardiovascular — LL-37 in plaques, pro-atherogenic LDL uptake

11. LL-37 is present in human atherosclerotic plaques: a ~6-fold increase in hCAP-18/LL-37 transcript was found in human atherosclerotic lesions versus normal arteries; the peptide is expressed mainly by plaque macrophages and some endothelial cells, with active peptide and abundant proprotein detectable in atheroma. [8, in_vitro/cohort — human tissue]

12. Functionally, LL-37 in the vessel wall acts as an immune modulator, inducing endothelial expression of the adhesion molecule ICAM-1 and the chemokine MCP-1 (CCL2), thereby amplifying innate immunity and leukocyte recruitment within the lesion. [8, in_vitro]

13. LL-37 is mechanistically pro-atherogenic via lipid handling: it enhances macrophage LDL uptake through LDLR, SR-B1, and CD36, increasing cytosolic cholesterol. Apoe-deficient mice transgenically expressing LL-37 (LL37tg/tg *Apoe*–/–) developed larger atheroma than control *Apoe*–/– mice (en face: n=12 vs n=13; aortic sinus: n=11 vs n=13), without a difference in serum cholesterol. In humans, plasma LL-37/hCAP-18 positively correlated with OxPL-apoB (an atherosclerosis predictor) in individuals with cardiovascular disease (n=20, P<0.01). [9, animal/cohort — mouse (species: Mus musculus, n=11–13/group) + human n=20]

### 6. Cancer — genuinely context-dependent (BOTH directions represented)

14. LL-37's role in cancer is bidirectional and tissue-specific, governed largely by which membrane receptors a given tumor expresses (e.g., FPRL-1/FPR2, EGFR, ErbB2, IGF-1R vs. apoptosis-linked GPCR/p53 pathways). The same peptide can be a growth-promoting "tumor-derived growth factor" in some tissues and a pro-apoptotic tumor suppressor in others. [10, mechanism_review]

15. **PRO-TUMOR** — LL-37/hCAP-18 overexpression promotes development/progression of **ovarian** (proliferation, invasion, angiogenesis, immune-cell recruitment; FPRL-1/TLR9), **lung** (acts as a growth factor; EGFR phosphorylation, MEK/ERK), and **breast** (ErbB2/heregulin mitogenic signaling) cancers; pro-tumor effects are also reported in **prostate** (IGF-1R; Erk1/2, Akt) and **melanoma**. Direction: TUMOR-PROMOTING. [10, mechanism_review]

16. **ANTI-TUMOR** — LL-37 (and its fragment FK-16) suppresses **colon cancer** via caspase-independent apoptosis (GPCR→p53→Bcl-2/Bax/Bak→AIF/EndoG) and autophagic cell death, and suppresses **gastric cancer** (cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis; proteasome inhibition / BMP-pathway activation; anti-angiogenic). Consistent with a tumor-suppressor role, hCAP-18/LL-37 expression is *downregulated* in gastric adenocarcinoma and in colon cancer tissue (strong in normal colon mucosa). Direction: TUMOR-SUPPRESSING. [10, mechanism_review]

### 6b. Amyloid / amyloidogenicity — relevant but bidirectional and partly debated

17. LL-37 INHIBITS pathological amyloid aggregation of islet amyloid polypeptide (IAPP/amylin), binding IAPP with nanomolar affinity and suppressing its amyloid self-assembly and IAPP-related pancreatic β-cell toxicity in vitro — suggesting a *protective* (not pathogenic) role in the amyloid of type-2 diabetes. Direction: ANTI-AMYLOID. [11, in_vitro]

18. A 2024 review synthesizes LL-37 as broadly *inhibitory* toward disease amyloids: it inhibits amyloid-β (Aβ42) fibril formation and reduces associated neuronal toxicity, and inhibits IAPP aggregation. This source characterizes LL-37's own self-association as amphipathic helical oligomers rather than classical amyloid. Direction: ANTI-AMYLOID for Aβ and IAPP. [12, mechanism_review]

19. CONTESTED / OPEN — LL-37's *own* self-assembly: a verified Tier-1 primary (Engelberg & Landau 2020) shows the LL-37 active core, LL-37(17–29), self-assembles into densely packed helical fibrils (associating four-helix bundles) whose formation is causally linked to antibacterial activity by structure-guided mutagenesis (e.g., core mutation I24A abolishes both fibril formation and killing). Critically, that primary explicitly reports the assembly *lacked* amyloid continuous cross-sheets and did *not* bind Thioflavin T — in contrast to the cross-α amyloid fibrils of the bacterial PSMα3 peptide it otherwise resembles. So the strongest verified primary on LL-37 self-assembly characterizes it as functional helical fibrils, NOT bona fide cross-α amyloid — consistent with, not contradicting, the review's "helical oligomers not amyloid" framing [12]. The genuinely open question is therefore narrower: LL-37(17–29) belongs to the broader "antimicrobial peptides that self-assemble into supramolecular fibrils" family (the same lab that defined cross-α amyloid), and whether any LL-37 form crosses the line into true amyloid — and whether AMP–amyloid cross-interactions are net-protective or net-harmful in vivo — remains unsettled. No Tier-1 source verified here establishes LL-37 *forming* cross-α amyloid; the [12] mis-attribution to a "cross-α-forming" claim is removed. [13 primary + 12 review, in_vitro — self-assembly = helical fibril (no ThT), cross-α-AMYLOID status open; see Coverage gaps]

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

1. **PMID precision for a few refs.** PMIDs for [4] (25470744), [9] (38194294), and [11] (31999880) were resolved from indexed metadata/standard records rather than re-read off a live PubMed page (PubMed served reCAPTCHA during verification). Titles, authors, journals, and DOIs are directly verified; the DOIs are the most load-bearing identifiers and are confirmed. [9] and [11] PMIDs were subsequently re-verified via WebSearch (see Post-fix grep audit, integrity-gate remediation). Treat [4] PMID as high-confidence but secondarily-sourced.

2. **Animal n underspecified for two claims.** Ref [5] (rosacea mouse injection) and the in vivo arm of [3] (naRNA psoriasis mouse component) demonstrate causality in mouse but the abstract-level sources did not give per-group n. Only [9] provided explicit mouse n (11–13/group). Per type-tag rules, [5]/[3] animal claims are flagged species=mouse, n=not specified.

3. **Amyloid direction is genuinely contested — but claim 19 self-assembly now grounded on a verified primary.** The strongest verified amyloid finding is that LL-37 *inhibits* pathological amyloids (IAPP [11]; Aβ42 and IAPP per review [12]) — i.e., amyloid-protective, not amyloid-pathogenic. For LL-37's *own* self-assembly, the Tier-1 primary Engelberg & Landau 2020 [13] (PMC full text verified) was added: it shows LL-37(17–29) forms functional helical fibrils causally tied to antibacterial activity but explicitly LACKING amyloid cross-sheets and NOT binding Thioflavin T, i.e., distinguished from PSMα3 cross-α amyloid. This is *concordant* with review [12]'s "helical oligomers not amyloid" framing, so the earlier "honest conflict" was partly a false dichotomy. No Tier-1 source verified here establishes LL-37 *forming* bona fide cross-α amyloid; whether any LL-37 form crosses into true amyloid remains the genuine open question. Claim 19 is presented as that narrowed open question, no longer mis-attributing a cross-α-forming claim to [12].

4. **Cancer evidence leans on one comprehensive review [10].** Direction-per-cancer is well-supported by that review, but most individual pro-/anti-tumor mechanisms trace to primary papers not separately re-verified here (ovarian versican/TLR2, lung Wnt/β-catenin, colon FK-16, etc., surfaced in searches but cited only through the review). The bidirectional conclusion is robust; specific receptor mechanisms per tumor should be treated as review-level synthesis.

5. **Rosacea uses the seminal mechanism paper [5]** but I did not separately verify a citrullination-of-LL-37-in-rosacea or downstream-KLK5-genetics line; the KLK5/cathelicidin axis is well-established, deeper rosacea genetics is out of verified scope.

6. **No regulatory or RCT-tier evidence exists** for LL-37 disease-driving claims (these are mechanism/cohort/animal by nature); absence of rct/regulatory tags is expected, not an omission.
