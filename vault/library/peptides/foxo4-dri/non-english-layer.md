# FOXO4-DRI — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 survey of non-English peer-reviewed literature for FOXO4-DRI (D-retro-inverso peptidomimetic senolytic; also called Proxofim). Non-English sources are admissible under the same tier rules as English; language is noted on each entry. Every citation listed carries a retrievable identifier (PMID / DOI). No citation is fabricated.

Key admissibility distinction applied throughout: a study **authored by researchers at a Chinese, Russian, or Japanese institution but published in English** is NOT counted here — it is already reachable by the English layer. Only articles whose **primary publication language is Chinese, Russian, or Japanese** are counted as genuine non-English primaries. FOXO4-DRI has a large Chinese-institution body of independent work (Sun Yat-sen University, Peking Union Medical College, Wenzhou Medical University, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences) — all published in English. The question asked here is whether any *Chinese-language* FOXO4-DRI literature exists in parallel.

A secondary admissibility distinction is applied within Chinese-language results: articles about the **FOXO4 transcription factor** (e.g., FOXO4 signaling cascades in TCM herbal studies, FOXO4 expression in testicular tissue) are distinct from articles about the **FOXO4-DRI peptide** (the synthetic D-retro-inverso senolytic derived from the FOXO4 forkhead domain). The former are FOXO4 biology literature; the latter are FOXO4-DRI senolytic literature. Only the latter are admitted as relevant non-English primaries for this layer.

## Chinese (surveyed)

**Result: 1 genuinely Chinese-language primary admissible as a FOXO4-DRI mention — a review article that cites FOXO4-DRI briefly as an anti-aging compound among others.** No Chinese-language FOXO4-DRI primary experimental study (in vitro or in vivo) was located.

**Landscape note — the Chinese-institution / English-published distinction.** The Chinese-institution FOXO4-DRI literature is extensive and constitutes approximately half of all independent FOXO4-DRI publications in the research report's primary corpus: Zhang et al. 2020 (Sun Yat-sen University, *Aging*), Li et al. 2024 (Sun Yat-sen University Sixth Affiliated Hospital, *Experimental Gerontology*), Han et al. 2022 (Peking Union Medical College, *Journal of Cellular and Molecular Medicine*), Kong et al. 2025 (Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences and PUMC, *Communications Biology*), and Hu et al. 2026 (Wenzhou Medical University, *Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology*). All five are published in English-language international journals, not in Chinese-language journals. They belong to the English layer. **Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 2017 Aug; 42(16):3065–3071

**PMID:** 29171222 | **DOI:** 10.19540/j.cnki.cjcmm.20170731.001

**Language:** Chinese (PubMed LA: chi; "[Article in Chinese]")

**Affiliation:** Department of Nephrology, The Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing University of Chinese Medicine (Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital), Nanjing, China; one co-author (Wan ZY) at Meiji Gakuin University, Tokyo.

**Study type:** Review. Surveys molecular mechanisms of aging (telomeres, oxidative stress, DNA methylation, mtDNA, autophagy) and anti-aging drug candidates including NMN and FOXO4-DRI. Concludes with the potential of Chinese herbal medicine extracts (curcumin, resveratrol) in this landscape.

**FOXO4-DRI content:** The article mentions FOXO4-DRI in one sentence as a candidate compound: "…based on the molecular regulative mechanisms of aging, a number of the promising anti-aging drugs have been found, including nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) and FOXO4-DRI, a peptide of anti-aging." This is a passing citation in a 2017 Chinese-medicine-context review written shortly after the Baar 2017 Cell paper (PMID 28340339) was published. The article does not discuss FOXO4-DRI mechanism, dose, efficacy data, or safety.

**Population note:** Review; no experimental subjects. Underlying data synthesized from English primary literature.

**Key mechanistic content:** FOXO4-DRI is listed alongside NMN as a newly identified promising anti-aging compound. No mechanistic detail beyond naming is present.

**Translation provenance:** Abstract rendered in English by NCBI (PubMed English abstract). The single FOXO4-DRI mention quoted above is from the English PubMed abstract; main text confirmed as Chinese by LA: chi tag and journal identity.

### Chinese landscape boundary note

The single admissible Chinese-language item (C1) is a 2017 passing citation in a TCM-context review, not a Chinese-language primary experimental study of FOXO4-DRI. The substantive FOXO4-DRI experimental work by Chinese institutions is exclusively English-published (Zhang 2020, Li 2024, Han 2022, Kong 2025, Hu 2025 — all in English-language international journals). This is the expected pattern: Chinese research groups producing internationally targeted work for the compound's growing application literature publish in English to reach the relevant scientific audience. The Chinese-language medical press (Zhongguo Zhong Yao Za Zhi, Zhonghua Nan Ke Xue, Sheng Li Xue Bao) has covered FOXO4 transcription factor biology in Chinese but has not produced Chinese-language primary experimental studies of FOXO4-DRI as a senolytic.

**Databases searched:** PubMed language filter (Chinese + FOXO4-DRI[tiab]: 1 result, C1 above); PubMed batch analysis of 88 FOXO4/senescence/aging PMIDs identified 4 Chinese-language articles, evaluated individually (3 excluded as FOXO4 transcription factor only, 1 = C1); CNKI (site-search via Google: no additional FOXO4-DRI Chinese-language articles found); Wanfang Data (site-search: no result); CQVIP (site-search: no result).

**Search terms tried in Chinese:** 「FOXO4-DRI」、「FOXO4 衰老 肽 中文」、「老化 FOXO4 DRI 肽 衰老细胞」、「衰老细胞溶解 FOXO4」、「FOXO4-DRI 抗衰老 综述」.

## What the non-English literature adds (honest assessment)

**Chinese (1 admissible — 1 review with a passing citation):** The single admissible Chinese-language item (C1, Liu BH et al. 2017) is a TCM-context aging review that lists FOXO4-DRI alongside NMN as a recently discovered anti-aging compound. It adds no mechanistic, efficacy, or safety information beyond what the founding Baar 2017 paper already establishes. Its value is documentary: it shows that FOXO4-DRI entered the Chinese-language anti-aging literature in the same year of its Cell publication, reaching the Chinese medicine research community. The substantive FOXO4-DRI experimental contributions from China are all in the English-published primary corpus.

**Russian (0 admissible):** No Russian-language FOXO4-DRI primary exists in the indexed literature. The compound has not been taken up by the Russian-language biogerontology press.

**Japanese (0 admissible):** No Japanese-language FOXO4-DRI primary exists in the indexed literature. Neither J-STAGE nor CiNii nor PubMed Japanese-filter searches returned any result.

**Net contribution:** The non-English layer adds no experimental data, mechanistic characterization, or safety information that is not already in the English layer. The single admissible item (C1) confirms early uptake of the FOXO4-DRI concept in the Chinese-language review press in 2017 but contains no primary data. The three excluded Chinese-language FOXO4 biology articles (PMIDs 32820304, 37462482, 37381959) demonstrate that the Chinese-language scientific press has engaged with FOXO4 *transcription factor* biology independently of the senolytic peptide context, but this work is not FOXO4-DRI research.
