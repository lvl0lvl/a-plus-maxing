# Sermorelin (GRF(1-29)NH2) — Prescribing-Practice Layer

> **Scope discipline.** This document records *how sermorelin is actually prescribed, compounded, dosed, and reconstituted in practice* — the dose/cycle/route/reconstitution conventions that circulate among prescribers, peptide clinics, and compounding pharmacies. It is **not** an efficacy document and **must not** be read as one. Per the source-tagging rule, `practitioner_protocol`, `compounding_data_sheet`, and `vendor_label` sources are admissible **only** for dose/cycle/route/reconstitution conventions and (for vendor labels) purity/reconstitution math. **None** of these source classes grounds an efficacy or adverse-event-rate claim, and none appears here for that purpose. The efficacy state (genuine pediatric-GHD + diagnostic evidence; biomarker-only adult evidence) lives in [[library/peptides/sermorelin/research-report]] and is unaffected by anything below.
>
> **Regulatory state, read first.** Sermorelin has an **unusual pedigree among compounded peptides**: it was a *real FDA-approved drug* (GEREF, sermorelin acetate) before being discontinued ~2008 — and the FDA's **March 4, 2013 Federal Register determination** affirmed it was **NOT withdrawn for reasons of safety or effectiveness**. That prior-approval record is the structural reason sermorelin remains **broadly available through §503A compounders** (it qualifies via the component-of-an-approved-drug pathway), and it sets sermorelin apart from never-approved peptides whose compounding status is contested. Sermorelin was **NOT** swept up in the April 2026 FDA peptide actions.

## Dose/route conventions

**Pediatric Geref label dose (kept DISTINCT — `regulatory`).** The approved Geref pediatric idiopathic-GHD therapeutic dose was **30 µg/kg/day SC at bedtime**. This is the labeled therapeutic dose for *children with GH deficiency* and is **not** an adult convention. It must not be conflated with the adult compounding figures below.

**Adult compounding convention (`compounding_data_sheet` / `practitioner_protocol`).** Across admissible §503A data sheets, the recurring adult prescribing convention is **~100–500 mcg SC at night, ~5 of 7 nights, on an empty stomach (≥60–90 min post-meal)**, timed to the nocturnal GH pulse [1][2][3]. By pharmacy:

- **Empower Pharmacy** — sermorelin acetate injection as a **lyophilized powder in 6 mg, 9 mg, and 15 mg per vial** strengths, reconstituted with diluent (bacteriostatic water), "mixed gently … avoiding shaking, which can denature the peptide." Stated adult-administration convention: **"0.2 to 0.3 mg … injected subcutaneously once daily at bedtime"** (200–300 mcg); pediatric weight-based convention ~**0.03 mg/kg (30 µg/kg)** once daily at bedtime. Empower also markets a sermorelin acetate **ODT (oral-disintegrating)** dose form, and presents sermorelin as a standalone product (no GHRP blend on that page) [1].
- **Strive Pharmacy** — provider guide lists three dose forms: **injectable at 1 mg/mL**, **mini-troches at 600 mcg**, and **nasal spray at 1000 mcg/mL**. Injectable convention: **"Inject 0.15–0.3 mL SUBQ nightly on an empty stomach for 5 out of 7 days per week"** (≈150–300 mcg nightly, 5/7 nights, at 1 mg/mL). Troche: dissolve one buccally nightly, 5/7. Nasal: 1 spray up to 3×/day, 5/7. Includes the standard caveat that "outcomes are not guaranteed" [2].
- **Olympia Pharmaceuticals** — patient dosing chart at **0.9 mg/mL** (SubQ or IM per prescriber), worked example: **"Inject 300 mcg (33 units)" nightly = 0.333 mL** on a U-100 syringe, given "sixty to ninety minutes after your last meal," held consistent across weeks 1–3+ [3].

> **Keep sermorelin dosing SEPARATE from CJC-1295 / tesamorelin.** This ~100–500 mcg SC nightly convention is sermorelin-specific and must **not** be cross-attributed: tesamorelin carries a labeled **2 mg/day SC** dose; CJC-1295 (DAC) carries a **weekly long-acting** cadence — neither applies to sermorelin [1][2][3].

**GHRP co-administration (`practitioner_protocol` — practice convention only).** Sermorelin is frequently stacked with a ghrelin-receptor agonist/GHRP (**ipamorelin, GHRP-2, or GHRP-6**) on the rationale of pairing a GHRH analogue with a GHRP for greater GH-pulse amplitude. A commonly described convention is **~300 mcg of each, 5×/week at night** [4][5]. This is a practice/marketing convention, **NOT** an efficacy or AE-rate claim.

**Gate finding: sermorelin IS commonly compounded — admissible §503A data sheets WERE located.** This stands in deliberate contrast to peptides with no monograph and no approval history; sermorelin's prior-approval pedigree (component of the formerly FDA-approved Geref) is the statutory basis for its broad §503A availability. ## Reconstitution / formulation notes (purity/dilution arithmetic only)

Worked from the published concentrations above (arithmetic, not a dose recommendation):

| Source / form | Concentration | Worked draw |
|---|---|---|
| Strive injectable | 1 mg/mL | 0.15–0.3 mL = 150–300 mcg [2] |
| Olympia chart | 0.9 mg/mL | 0.333 mL = ~300 mcg = ~33 units on U-100 [3] |
| Empower 6 mg vial in 3 mL | 2 mg/mL | 0.15 mL = 300 mcg [1] |

General rule for Empower lyophilized vials: concentration = (vial mg) / (mL diluent added). Reconstituted compounded sermorelin is conventionally **refrigerated (2–8 °C)**; follow each pharmacy's assigned beyond-use date [1]. The standard reconstitution caution is to **mix gently and avoid shaking** (shaking can denature the peptide) [1]. This is route/strength bookkeeping only and carries **no efficacy or safety implication**.

**Cost (qualitative).** Compounded sermorelin is consistently positioned as relatively inexpensive versus brand recombinant-GH and versus other branded peptides — a direct consequence of its lapsed-approval / broad-§503A-availability status [1]. No specific dollar figure is treated as a verified claim here (vendor pricing is not an admissible quantitative source).
