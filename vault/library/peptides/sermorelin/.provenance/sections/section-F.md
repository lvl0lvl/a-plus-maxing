## Section F — Practitioner-practice + non-English

Sermorelin (GRF(1-29) amide / hGRF(1-29)) is among the most-compounded growth-hormone-releasing peptides. Unlike most peptides on the FDA's interim 503A Category-2 list, sermorelin has an unusual regulatory pedigree: it was an *approved* drug (GEREF, sermorelin acetate) before being discontinued for manufacturing — not safety/efficacy — reasons, and the FDA formally affirmed this. This approval history is the structural reason sermorelin remains broadly available through 503A compounders today, and it sets sermorelin apart from never-approved peptides whose compounding status is contested.

### Prescribing-practice layer

**Regulatory anchor (regulatory).** GEREF (sermorelin acetate) injection was FDA-approved (1990s) and later discontinued. The FDA's Federal Register determination (March 4, 2013) explicitly found that GEREF injection — 0.5 mg base/vial, 1.0 mg base/vial, and 0.05 mg base/amp — "[was] not withdrawn from sale for reasons of safety or effectiveness" [1]. This retained-label record is the only fully admissible *dose* anchor in this section: the historically labeled diagnostic-product strengths were 0.5 mg and 1.0 mg base per vial [1]. (Efficacy claims for GH-deficiency *treatment* belong to the primary-literature sections, not here.)

**Compounding data sheets (compounding_data_sheet).** Admissible 503A data sheets were located:

- *Empower Pharmacy* lists sermorelin acetate injection as a lyophilized powder in **6 mg, 9 mg, and 15 mg per vial** strengths, reconstituted with diluent (bacteriostatic water for injection) and "mixed gently … avoiding shaking, which can denature the peptide." Empower's stated adult-administration convention is "0.2 to 0.3 mg … injected subcutaneously once daily at bedtime," with a pediatric weight-based convention of ~0.03 mg/kg (30 µg/kg) once daily at bedtime [2]. Empower also markets a sermorelin acetate ODT (oral-disintegrating) dose form [2]. Empower's page presents sermorelin as a standalone product (no GHRP blend on that page).
- *Strive Pharmacy*'s provider guide lists three dose forms: injectable at **1 mg/mL**, mini-troches at **600 mcg**, and nasal spray at **1000 mcg/mL**. Its injectable administration convention is "Inject 0.15–0.3 mL SUBQ nightly on an empty stomach for 5 out of 7 days per week" — i.e., ~150–300 mcg nightly, 5/7 nights, at the 1 mg/mL concentration [3]. Troche convention: dissolve one buccally nightly, 5/7. Nasal: 1 spray up to 3×/day, 5/7. The guide includes the standard practitioner caveat that "outcomes are not guaranteed" [3].
- *Olympia Pharmaceuticals* publishes a patient dosing chart at **0.9 mg/mL** (SubQ or IM per prescriber), with a worked administration example: "Inject 300 mcg (33 units)" nightly = 0.333 mL on a U-100 syringe, given "sixty to ninety minutes after your last meal," held consistent across weeks 1–3+ [4].

**Reconstitution math (vendor_label / compounding sheet — math only).** Worked from the published concentrations above (arithmetic, not a dose recommendation): at Strive's 1 mg/mL, a 0.15–0.3 mL draw = 150–300 mcg [3]. At Olympia's 0.9 mg/mL, 0.333 mL = ~300 mcg, which on a U-100 (100 units = 1 mL) insulin syringe reads as ~33 units [4]. Empower's lyophilized 6/9/15 mg vials, once reconstituted with a chosen diluent volume, yield concentration = (vial mg)/(mL added); e.g., a 6 mg vial in 3 mL bacteriostatic water = 2 mg/mL, so a 0.15 mL draw = 300 mcg [2]. Reconstituted compounded sermorelin is conventionally refrigerated (2–8 °C); follow each pharmacy's assigned beyond-use date [2].

**Convention summary (compounding_data_sheet / practitioner_protocol).** Across admissible sheets, the recurring prescribing convention is **~100–500 mcg SC at night, 5 of 7 nights, on an empty stomach (≥60–90 min post-meal)**, timed to the nocturnal GH pulse [2][3][4]. This is the sermorelin-specific convention and should NOT be cross-attributed to CJC-1295 or tesamorelin (which carry their own distinct dosing — tesamorelin's labeled 2 mg/day SC, CJC-1295's weekly long-acting cadence — neither of which applies here).

**GHRP co-administration (practitioner_protocol — practice convention only).** Sermorelin is frequently stacked with a ghrelin-receptor agonist/GHRP (ipamorelin, GHRP-2, or GHRP-6) on the rationale of pairing a GHRH analog with a GHRP for greater GH-pulse amplitude. A commonly described practice convention is ~300 mcg of each, 5×/week at night [5][6]. This is a practice/marketing convention, NOT an efficacy or AE-rate claim — no efficacy or adverse-event numbers in this section are grounded on practitioner, vendor, or compounding sources.

**Named-prescriber conventions (practitioner_protocol).** I did **not** locate a sermorelin protocol with a properly attributable name + venue + date (e.g., a specific A4M/IFM faculty lecture, or a named prescriber with a dated talk). The clinic-level sources surfaced (Houston Men's Health Clinic, PRAMAH Tampa, etc.) are unsigned clinic marketing pages, not attributable named-prescriber protocols, and are therefore reported only as generic practice conventions, not as named protocols. Honest result: **no admissible named-prescriber (name+venue+date) sermorelin protocol found.**

**Cost (qualitative).** Compounded sermorelin is consistently positioned as relatively inexpensive versus brand recombinant-GH and versus other branded peptides — a direct consequence of its lapsed-approval/broad-503A-availability status [1][2]. No specific dollar figure is treated as a verified claim here (vendor pricing is not an admissible quantitative source).

### Non-English literature

**Russian (eLibrary / CyberLeninka).** A genuine Russian-LANGUAGE secondary source was located: Brylëv M.I., Kozin S.V., Korolëv V.L., Lotorev D.S., Pavlova L.A., Alekseev A.A., "Пептиды, повышающие секрецию соматотропного гормона, и их влияние на сердечно-сосудистую систему" ("Peptides increasing somatotropin secretion and their effect on the cardiovascular system"), *Uchenye zapiski. Elektronnyy nauchnyy zhurnal Kurskogo gosudarstvennogo universiteta* (Kursk State University), 2013 — hosted on CyberLeninka [7]. It is a **review (literature analysis), not a primary study**. It describes серморелин (sermorelin) as the 29-residue N-terminal GHRH fragment that fully retains hormonal activity, notes the diagnostic IV convention of 1.0 µg/kg with peak serum GH at 10–60 min and return to baseline at 2–3 h, and discusses GH-secretagogue peptides in the context of cardiovascular protection [7]. As a non-English *review*, it is admissible for context only — it does not ground any efficacy or AE-rate claim in this wiki, and the cardiovascular framing is a hypothesis-level review claim, not verified primary evidence.

Other Russian hits (allmed.pro, medum.ru drug-reference pages; the Russian-language Wikipedia "Соматолиберин" entry) were **excluded**: Wikipedia is a HALT source, and the .ru drug-monograph pages are tertiary reference compilations, not primary literature. No Russian-language *primary* (original experimental/clinical) sermorelin/GHRH(1-29) study was located on eLibrary/CyberLeninka.

**Chinese (CNKI / Wanfang).** No Chinese-LANGUAGE *primary* sermorelin (舍莫瑞林 / GHRH 1-29 / GRF(1-29)酰胺) study was located. The Chinese-language hits surfaced were: a Baidu Health drug entry, a Chinese-language patent (CN103547591A, GRF analogs), drug-database/synapse pharmacodia entries (醋酸舍莫瑞林 target/clinical/patent records), and vendor pages — i.e., tertiary database, patent, and commercial material, none of which is an admissible CNKI/Wanfang primary research article. The English-language items returned (e.g., the PubMed review and the GEREF International Study Group trial) are English-published work and belong to the primary-literature sections, not here. **Honest non-English result: one Russian-language review located (context only); no Chinese-language primary located; no non-English primary research grounds any claim.**

**Disambiguation note.** Several Chinese database entries co-list 舍莫瑞林 (sermorelin) alongside 替莫瑞林 (tesamorelin) and GRF(1-44); these are distinct molecules and their dosing/data were not borrowed into sermorelin's record.
