# Sermorelin — Phase 2.5 Rubric + Section Plan (deep mode, efficient calibration)

## Mode
deep mode (25+ sources, ~10k words) under the **efficient calibration**: judge bar **92**;
genuine-defects-only; ONE remediation round per real defect.

## THE DEFINING FACT (integrity axis)
Sermorelin = **GRF(1-29)NH2** — the first 29 amino acids of human GHRH, the **shortest fully
bioactive GHRH fragment** and the PROTOTYPE of the GRF(1-29) class (CJC-1295 no-DAC = "Mod GRF
1-29" is a sermorelin analogue). Regulatory history is the distinguishing feature: it WAS
**FDA-approved as Geref (Serono)** — for (a) diagnostic assessment of pituitary GH secretory
capacity and (b) treatment of **idiopathic GH deficiency in CHILDREN (pediatric GHD growth
failure)** — but **Geref was WITHDRAWN from the US market (~2008) for COMMERCIAL/business
reasons, NOT safety or efficacy.** It is now off-market and **heavily COMPOUNDED** for off-label
ADULT anti-aging / GH-optimization / body-composition use.

INTEGRITY AXIS: demarcate sharply — the APPROVED/evidenced use was **pediatric GHD + diagnostic**;
the popular ADULT anti-aging / body-comp / sleep / "GH-optimization" use is **off-label and
weakly/​un-evidenced in healthy adults**. Do NOT let pediatric-GHD efficacy or "raises GH/IGF-1"
(a biomarker) read as proven adult anti-aging benefit. Also: "withdrawn" ≠ "banned/unsafe" —
state the commercial-withdrawal reason accurately.

Mechanism: GHRH-receptor agonist → pulsatile GH → IGF-1; short half-life (~10-20 min). Often
compounded/stacked with a GHRP (GHRP-2/GHRP-6/ipamorelin).

## Section plan (6 lean sections)
- **A — Identity, structure, mechanism.** GRF(1-29)NH2; the prototype GHRH fragment; GHRH-R →
  cAMP/PKA → pulsatile GH → IGF-1; short half-life; relation to CJC-1295(no-DAC)/tesamorelin;
  GHRP-synergy rationale.
- **B — Approval history + evidenced (pediatric GHD / diagnostic) efficacy.** Geref approval
  (diagnostic + pediatric GHD growth); the pediatric GHD trial evidence (growth velocity);
  the diagnostic-test use; the ~2008 commercial WITHDRAWAL (not safety). Verify the approval
  facts + key trial PMIDs.
- **C — Adult / off-label evidence (demarcate honestly) + concentration audit.** Adult GHD,
  anti-aging, body-composition, sleep, exercise-recovery — what human data actually exists
  (mostly old/small; aging-adult GHRH studies e.g. Vittone/Khorram/Merriam; the Baker-type
  GHRH cognition work used tesamorelin not sermorelin — keep straight). Label each
  APPROVED-pediatric / INVESTIGATIONAL / ABSENT. Concentration/lineage audit.
- **D — Safety / adverse effects.** From the Geref label + trials: injection-site reactions
  (most common), flushing, headache, dysphagia, hyperactivity, somnolence; GH-axis risks;
  contraindications; pregnancy; monitoring IGF-1 + glucose. Generally favorable short-term
  safety (it augments physiologic pulsatile GH).
- **E — Regulatory & sport.** Geref FDA approval + ~2008 withdrawal (commercial); current
  status = no marketed FDA product but **widely COMPOUNDED** (503A) — verify sermorelin's
  503A/compounding standing (it is one of the more-available compounded GH peptides; was it in
  the Apr-2026 FR Doc 2026-07361 removed-12? — the canonical removed-12 does NOT include
  sermorelin; verify its actual 503A status). WADA S2.2 prohibited (GHRH analogue). EU status.
- **F — Practitioner-practice + non-English.** Compounded sermorelin is common (Empower etc.);
  typical dose ~100-500 mcg SC nightly, often + GHRP/ipamorelin; reconstitution; named-
  prescriber conventions; Russian/Chinese literature survey.

## Mandatory rubric dimensions (health)

## RECURRING-ERROR SELF-CHECKS — every retrieval agent runs these before returning
1. **Verify every PMID/DOI/NCT** to exact author+year+journal; omit if unverifiable.
2. **Regulatory:** state the Geref approval (indications) + the ~2008 WITHDRAWAL reason
   (commercial, NOT safety) accurately; "withdrawn" ≠ "banned/unsafe"; verify current 503A
   status (sermorelin NOT in the Apr-2026 removed-12 — verify). Approval history ≠ current
   approval, and the approved use (pediatric GHD/diagnostic) ≠ the adult off-label use.
3. **NO Wikipedia citations** (HALT). No vendor/anecdote grounding a number.
4. **Tally-vs-enumeration:** any "N trials" = enumerated list of exactly N.
5. **Population discipline:** pediatric-GHD / aging-adult / healthy-adult annotated on every
   efficacy figure; don't let pediatric-GHD or GH/IGF-1-rise read as adult anti-aging efficacy.
6. **Don't confuse sermorelin with tesamorelin/CJC-1295** (e.g. the Baker cognition trial used
   tesamorelin; CJC-1295 has the DAC PK) — keep the GRF(1-29) family members straight.
7. **Type-tag every claim**; vendor/anecdote never ground numbers.
