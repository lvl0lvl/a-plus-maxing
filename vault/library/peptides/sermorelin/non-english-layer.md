# Sermorelin (GRF(1-29)NH2) — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **sermorelin (GRF(1-29)NH2 / GHRH(1-29)NH2)**, companion to [[library/peptides/sermorelin/research-report]]. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. No citation is fabricated; items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in Russia/China/elsewhere but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and is already in the sermorelin corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, or another non-English language** are counted here as genuine non-English sources. This is the **non-English-LANGUAGE vs non-English-AUTHOR** distinction.

**Headline result.** Russian = **one** genuine Russian-LANGUAGE source located — a *review* (Brylëv et al. 2013, CyberLeninka), admissible for **context only**, grounding **no** efficacy or AE-rate claim. Chinese = **none** Chinese-LANGUAGE *primary* located (only vendor / patent / drug-database material). Originator lineage = English-published (the GEREF International Study Group trial and PubMed reviews are English work, belonging to the primary sections, not here). **Net: no non-English *primary* research grounds any claim in the sermorelin wiki.**

## Russian / Soviet

**Result: ONE genuine Russian-LANGUAGE source located — a review (context only).**

- **Brylëv M.I., Kozin S.V., Korolëv V.L., Lotorev D.S., Pavlova L.A., Alekseev A.A.** «Пептиды, повышающие секрецию соматотропного гормона, и их влияние на сердечно-сосудистую систему» ("Peptides increasing somatotropin secretion and their effect on the cardiovascular system"). *Uchenye zapiski. Elektronnyy nauchnyy zhurnal Kurskogo gosudarstvennogo universiteta* (Kursk State University), 2013. Hosted on CyberLeninka. https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/peptidy-povyshayuschie-sekretsiyu-somatotropnogo-gormona-i-ih-vliyanie-na-serdechno-sosudistuyu-sistemu — language=**Russian**; type=`mechanism_review`.
  - It is a **review (literature analysis), not a primary study.** It describes серморелин (sermorelin) as the 29-residue N-terminal GHRH fragment that fully retains hormonal activity, notes the diagnostic IV convention of **1.0 µg/kg with peak serum GH at 10–60 min and return to baseline at 2–3 h**, and discusses GH-secretagogue peptides in a cardiovascular-protection context.
  - **As a non-English *review*, it is admissible for CONTEXT ONLY** — it does **not** ground any efficacy or AE-rate claim in this wiki, and the cardiovascular framing is a hypothesis-level review claim, not verified primary evidence.

**Excluded Russian hits.** allmed.pro and medum.ru drug-reference pages, and the Russian-language Wikipedia «Соматолиберин» entry, were excluded: Wikipedia is a HALT source, and the .ru drug-monograph pages are tertiary reference compilations, not primary literature. **No Russian-language *primary* (original experimental/clinical) sermorelin / GHRH(1-29) study was located on eLibrary / CyberLeninka.**

Searched (databases / portals): eLibrary.ru, CyberLeninka.ru, open Russian web. Author/lineage and Cyrillic compound strings: серморелин, ГРФ(1-29), соматолиберин фрагмент, гормон роста секреция.
