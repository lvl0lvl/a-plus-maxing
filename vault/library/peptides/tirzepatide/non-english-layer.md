# Tirzepatide — Non-English Literature Coverage

## Survey result
**No distinct material non-English primary literature located.** Tirzepatide's pivotal program (SURPASS for diabetes,
SURMOUNT for obesity, SURMOUNT-OSA for sleep apnea) is global and multicentre but **published in English** in the major
journals (NEJM, JAMA, Lancet). Foreign-population data are folded into these English-language global publications rather
than appearing as separate non-English primaries; FDA/EMA reviews add no non-English primary beyond them.

## Net
Like semaglutide, tirzepatide has **no material non-English literature gap** — a Western-developed (Eli Lilly), globally
trialled, English-indexed drug. No translation handling required. (Contrast Thymosin Alpha-1, where the Chinese
literature is material, or BPC-157's Croatian origin.)
