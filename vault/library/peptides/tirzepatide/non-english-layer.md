# Tirzepatide — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **tirzepatide** (dual GIP + GLP-1 receptor agonist), companion to `vault/library/peptides/tirzepatide/research-report.md`. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. No citation is fabricated; items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in Japan/China/Russia but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and is already in the tirzepatide corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, Japanese, etc.** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. This **non-English-LANGUAGE vs non-English-AUTHOR** distinction is the load-bearing rule for tirzepatide, because its pivotal program was run in international (including non-English-speaking, notably Japanese and Chinese) populations but published in English.

**Headline result: ZERO genuinely non-English-LANGUAGE admissible tirzepatide primaries located.** Tirzepatide's evidence base is overwhelmingly global and **English-published**, even for trials run entirely outside English-speaking countries. The pivotal programs — SURPASS (glycemic) and SURMOUNT (weight) — are international Eli Lilly trials whose primary results appear in English-language journals (NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology). Dedicated **Asian-population trials** exist (SURMOUNT-J in Japanese obesity; SURMOUNT-CN in Chinese obesity; SURPASS J-mono/J-combo in Japanese T2D), but their primary results were published **in English**, not in Japanese- or Chinese-language journals. There is therefore **no separate non-English primary evidence stream to integrate** for tirzepatide; the non-English footprint is **localized regulatory documentation** plus consumption/translation of the English corpus, not independent non-English-language primary research.

## Japanese / other

**Result: NO Japanese-language (or other-language) tirzepatide primary located that adds findings beyond the English corpus.**

Tirzepatide was studied and is marketed in Japan (under マンジャロ／ゼップバウンド), and Japanese-population trial data exist, but — consistent with the global pattern — the authoritative primary publications appear in **English-language** international journals:

- **SURMOUNT-J** — Japanese adults with obesity disease; multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 — published **in English** in *The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology* (2024) [2]. Cited only to characterize the literature.
- **SURPASS J-mono / J-combo** — tirzepatide in Japanese T2D — English-published phase 3 trials.

The genuinely non-English **documents** that do exist are **regulatory/practitioner materials, not new primary evidence**: the Japanese-language package-insert / interview-form (添付文書／インタビューフォーム) for マンジャロ／ゼップバウンド, and Chinese-language drug-information pages for 替尔泊肽. These mirror the same label content and add **no independent efficacy or AE data** [3].

Other languages: no incidental admissible non-English-language tirzepatide primary surfaced in any other language during the survey.

Explicit none-located: **no Japanese-language (or other-language) tirzepatide primary** distinct from the English corpus.
