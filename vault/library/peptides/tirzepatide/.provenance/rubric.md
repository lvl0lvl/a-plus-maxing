# Tirzepatide — Phase 2.5 Rubric + Section Plan (deep mode, efficient calibration)

## Mode
deep mode (25+ sources, ~10k words) under the **efficient calibration**: judge bar **92**;
genuine-defects-only; ONE remediation round per real defect. Huge high-quality RCT base —
prioritize LANDMARK trials (SURPASS, SURMOUNT, SURMOUNT-OSA, SURPASS-CVOT, SUMMIT).

## THE DEFINING FACTS (integrity axes — A-tier approved drug, like semaglutide)
1. **Tirzepatide is a GENUINELY APPROVED, GENUINELY EFFICACIOUS DUAL GIP + GLP-1 receptor
   agonist** ("twincretin"; Eli Lilly; Mounjaro for T2D, Zepbound for obesity/OSA). Second
   A-tier entry. Integrity job = BALANCE (real large benefits + real caveats). It is
   chemically/mechanistically DISTINCT from semaglutide (which is GLP-1-only) — the GIP
   component is the difference. Once-weekly SC, acylated (C20 diacid).
2. **The honest benefit picture (landmark RCTs):** glycemic (SURPASS 1-5, T2D — and **SURPASS-2
   head-to-head BEAT semaglutide on HbA1c + weight**); weight (**SURMOUNT-1, Jastreboff 2022,
   ~20.9% mean weight loss at 15 mg** — larger than semaglutide's ~15%); **OSA (SURMOUNT-OSA,
   Malhotra 2024 — APPROVED indication, this IS tirzepatide)**; CV (SURPASS-CVOT vs dulaglutide);
   HFpEF (SUMMIT); MASH (SYNERGY-NASH). Emerging: SURMOUNT-MMO (morbidity/mortality, ongoing).
3. **The honest caveats/risks (same GLP-1/incretin class):** GI AEs dominant (discontinuation
   driver); **weight REGAIN on discontinuation (SURMOUNT-4)**; **lean/muscle-mass loss**;
   gallbladder; pancreatitis; the **thyroid C-cell/medullary-carcinoma BOXED WARNING is
   RODENT-based and NOT demonstrated in humans — state precisely**; gastroparesis/peri-op
   aspiration. Cost/access.
4. Regulatory: FDA-approved (Mounjaro T2D 2022, Zepbound obesity 2023, Zepbound OSA 2024);
   the compounded-tirzepatide gray market tied to the shortage (resolved ~late 2024, compounding
   wind-down). WADA: GLP-1/GIP agonists are **NOT on the WADA Prohibited List** (verify; possibly
   on the Monitoring Program — flag as reported if only secondary).

## Section plan (6 lean sections)
- **A — Identity, structure, mechanism.** Dual GIP + GLP-1 agonist (single 39-aa peptide, C20
  diacid acylation → ~5-day t½, once-weekly SC); the GIP+GLP-1 synergy rationale; contrast
  semaglutide (GLP-1-only) + the incretin physiology.
- **B — Glycemic + weight efficacy (STRONG).** SURPASS 1-5 (T2D HbA1c, dose-dependent); **SURPASS-2
  head-to-head vs semaglutide (superior)**; **SURMOUNT-1 (~20.9% at 15 mg, non-diabetic obesity);
  SURMOUNT-2 (T2D, lower); SURMOUNT-3/4 (maintenance/lifestyle)**. Population-annotated. Verify PMIDs.
- **C — OSA / CV / HFpEF / MASH / other + concentration audit.** **SURMOUNT-OSA (Malhotra 2024 —
  approved OSA indication)**; SURPASS-CVOT; SUMMIT (HFpEF); SYNERGY-NASH (MASH); SURMOUNT-MMO
  (ongoing). Approved vs INVESTIGATIONAL labeled. + Lilly sponsorship concentration (honest).
- **D — Safety / adverse effects.** GI dominant; regain on stop (SURMOUNT-4); lean-mass loss;
  gallbladder; pancreatitis; the **rodent thyroid C-cell boxed warning (not human-demonstrated)**;
  gastroparesis/peri-op aspiration; contraindications (personal/family MTC or MEN2, pregnancy);
  monitoring; stopping.
- **E — Regulatory & sport.** FDA approvals (Mounjaro 2022 / Zepbound 2023 / Zepbound-OSA 2024) +
  exact indications; EMA; the shortage + compounded-tirzepatide arc + resolution/wind-down +
  counterfeit/salt-form warnings; WADA **NOT prohibited** (verify; flag Monitoring-Program as
  reported if secondary-only).
- **F — Practitioner-practice + non-English.** Titration (2.5 → 5 → 7.5 → 10 → 12.5 → 15 mg,
  4-wk steps, GI mitigation); brand vs compounded; muscle-preservation adjuncts; non-English.
