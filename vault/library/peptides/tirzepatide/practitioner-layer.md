# Tirzepatide — Prescribing-Practice Layer

`practitioner_protocol` cites may support dose/route/cycle conventions ONLY — never efficacy.

## On-label dosing (regulatory — authoritative)
- **SC once weekly, titrate 2.5 → 5 → 7.5 → 10 → 12.5 → 15 mg**, ≥4 wk per step; **2.5 mg is initiation, not a
  maintenance/therapeutic dose** `[regulatory: FDA Mounjaro/Zepbound labels]`.
- Mounjaro (T2D) max 15 mg; Zepbound (obesity) maintenance 5/10/15 mg; **OSA dosing 10 or 15 mg**.
- Administration: SC abdomen/thigh/upper-arm, rotate; storage refrigerated (room temp ≤30 °C up to 21 d).

## Off-label conventions
- **"Microdosing"** (sub-therapeutic fractional dosing) is community/clinic practice with **NO Tier-1/2 RCT support** —
  `practitioner_protocol` only; no admissible primary establishes efficacy/safety. Do not assign efficacy/PK numbers.
- **Compounded tirzepatide:** not FDA-approved/quality-assured; with the shortage resolved (Dec 2024) and enforcement
  discretion ended (503A Feb 2025 / 503B Mar 2025), routine compounding on shortage grounds is no longer permitted —
  branded product is the realistic, quality-assured route.

## Doctor-handout note
FDA-approved and prescribable (T2D, obesity, OSA) — but **off-target for the operator's recovery/rebuilding goal**.
Raise at the July-2026 visit only if fat loss or OSA is genuinely indicated; if used, pair with resistance training +
adequate protein to defend lean mass, and screen out personal/family MTC or MEN2 first.
