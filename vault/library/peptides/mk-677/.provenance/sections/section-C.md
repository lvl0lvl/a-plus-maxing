## Section C — Failed programs + other + concentration audit

**Compound class note (self-check 3):** MK-677 (ibutamoren mesylate; development code MK-0677; chemical lineage L-163,191) is an orally active, **non-peptide small-molecule** ghrelin/GH-secretagogue-receptor (GHS-R1a) agonist. It is NOT a peptide. Throughout this section, a rise in serum IGF-1 ("target engagement") is treated as a **biomarker, not a clinical benefit** — the two largest disease programs below demonstrate exactly this dissociation.

**Demarcation legend:** EVIDENCED (positive RCT/controlled human data on a clinical endpoint) · INVESTIGATIONAL (mechanistic / biomarker / small-n signal, no confirmatory endpoint) · **FAILED** (adequately-powered program that missed its primary clinical endpoint and/or was halted for safety) · ABSENT (no admissible human controlled evidence located).

---

### Alzheimer's disease — **FAILED** (primary cognitive endpoint missed despite biomarker engagement)

**Sevigny et al. 2008** [1] — *rct*. Double-blind, multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (MK-677 Protocol 30 Study Group). **Population:** patients with **mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease** (n = 563 randomized; 416 completed 12-month assessments). **Intervention:** MK-677 25 mg/day vs placebo for **12 months**. **Target engagement:** serum IGF-1 rose +60.1% at 6 weeks and +72.9% at 12 months. **Primary outcome:** NO significant between-group difference on the co-primary CIBIC-plus or on ADAS-Cog, and none on ADCS-ADL or CDR-sob. The verbatim conclusion: *"Despite evidence of target engagement as indicated by an increase in serum insulin-like growth factor-1, the human growth hormone secretagogue MK-677 25 mg was ineffective at slowing the rate of progression of Alzheimer disease."* The authors further argued that age-related somatotropic decline is **not** in the causal pathway of AD. This is the canonical "biomarker↑ ≠ benefit" failure for MK-677: a large, well-powered Phase 2b/3-scale trial that hit its IGF-1 pharmacodynamic target and still missed every cognitive and functional endpoint. **Status: FAILED.**

---

### Hip-fracture functional recovery — **FAILED** (endpoint missed + safety signal → early termination)

**Adunsky et al. 2011** [2] — *rct*. Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled **Phase IIb** trial. **Population:** **elderly patients recovering from hip fracture** (n = 123; MK-0677 n = 62, placebo n = 61). **Intervention:** MK-0677 25 mg/day for up to 24 weeks. **Target engagement:** plasma IGF-1 rose +51.4 ng/mL vs placebo (95% CI 34.42–68.44; p < 0.001). **Primary functional endpoint:** NOT met — although gait-speed score favored treatment (p = 0.011) and stair-climbing power was numerically higher but non-significant (+12.5 W; 95% CI −10.95 to 35.88; p = 0.292), the prespecified rank analysis showed **no improvement across most functional performance measures**. A secondary falls signal favored treatment (fewer falls; p = 0.096, NS). **Safety:** the trial was **terminated early** for a **congestive-heart-failure safety signal** (reported in the literature as ~4/62 [6.5%] on MK-0677 vs ~1/61 [1.7%] placebo — mechanistically consistent with GH-mediated fluid retention unmasking subclinical cardiac dysfunction in a frail geriatric population). Verbatim conclusion: *"…the increase in plasma IGF-1 levels was not paralleled by improvement in most functional performance measures. MK-0677 has an unfavorable safety profile in this patient population."* **Status: FAILED (efficacy) + safety-halted.** Together with the AD result, this program drove Merck's discontinuation of MK-677 as a therapeutic.

### Other evidence (each labeled)

**Catabolic states (diet-induced nitrogen wasting) — INVESTIGATIONAL (positive short-term surrogate, no disease endpoint).**
**Murphy et al. 1998** [3] — *rct* (crossover). n = 8 healthy volunteers (ages 24–39), calorically restricted (18 kcal/kg/day) over two 14-day periods, MK-677 25 mg vs placebo during the final 7 days of each. Nitrogen balance shifted from catabolic to near-neutral: +0.31 g N/day on MK-677 vs −1.48 g N/day on placebo (p < 0.01); IGF-1 264 ± 31 vs 188 ± 19 ng/mL (p < 0.01). The reversal of nitrogen wasting is real but is a **metabolic surrogate in healthy dieters**, not a clinical outcome in a diseased catabolic population — the authors themselves frame it conditionally ("*if* these short-term anabolic effects are maintained in patients who are catabolic because of…disease"). **Status: INVESTIGATIONAL.**

**Obesity — INVESTIGATIONAL (body-composition/metabolic surrogates only).**
**Svensson et al. 1998** [4] — *rct*. n = 24 obese males, MK-677 25 mg (n = 12) vs placebo (n = 12) for 8 weeks. IGF-1 rose ~40%; fat-free mass increased (sustained) with a transient rise in basal metabolic rate. No fat-loss or hard clinical-outcome endpoint; FFM gain ≠ demonstrated functional or cardiometabolic benefit. **Status: INVESTIGATIONAL.**

**Frailty / sarcopenia of aging — INVESTIGATIONAL (body-composition signal, function NOT improved).**
**Nass et al. 2008** [5] — *rct* (2-year, modified-crossover). n = 65 healthy older adults (60–81 y), MK-677 25 mg/day. Fat-free mass increased (+1.1 kg vs −0.5 kg placebo; p < 0.001) and GH/IGF-1 were restored toward young-adult levels. **Critically, the increased fat-free mass did NOT translate into improved muscle strength or physical function.** This is a second clean dissociation between an MK-677 surrogate (lean mass, IGF-1) and the outcome that matters (function). **Status: INVESTIGATIONAL** (anti-frailty *efficacy* unproven; the functional endpoint was effectively negative).

**GH-deficiency diagnostics / GH-axis stimulation — EVIDENCED (as a pharmacologic GH provocation/stimulation tool, not as therapy).**
**Chapman et al. 1996** [6] — *rct*: n = 32 healthy elderly (64–81 y), MK-677 2/10/25 mg over 14- and 28-day periods; 25 mg/day enhanced pulsatile GH release and restored IGF-1 to young-adult levels (~+97% 24-h mean GH). **Chapman et al. 1997** [7] — *open_label/mechanism*: n = 9 selected GH-deficient adults; oral MK-677 stimulated the GH/IGF-1 axis (IGF-1 +52% at 10 mg, +79% at 50 mg). These establish reliable, dose-dependent GH-axis stimulation (the basis for MK-677's 2017 FDA orphan-drug designation for GH deficiency and its use as an oral GH-provocation/diagnostic agent) — but they evidence **pharmacology, not a treated clinical endpoint.** **Status: EVIDENCED (pharmacodynamic/diagnostic); therapeutic GHD efficacy INVESTIGATIONAL.**

**Slow-wave / REM sleep — INVESTIGATIONAL (small-n polysomnography signal).**
**Copinschi et al. 1997** [8] — *rct* (crossover). Young (n = 8, 18–30 y; 5 & 25 mg) and older (n = 6, 65–71 y; 2 & 25 mg) subjects, 7–14-day periods. High-dose MK-677 produced ~50% increase in stage IV slow-wave sleep and >20% increase in REM in young subjects, and ~50% increase in REM with reduced REM latency in older subjects; sleep abnormalities fell. Real but **very small-n** and a sleep-architecture surrogate, not a validated insomnia/clinical sleep endpoint. **Status: INVESTIGATIONAL.**

**Bodybuilding / athletic-performance efficacy — ABSENT.** No admissible randomized or controlled human trial demonstrating MK-677 improves athletic performance, strength, hypertrophy-for-performance, or recomposition in healthy/trained populations was located. The closest controlled data (Nass [5], Svensson [4]) measured body composition in older/obese non-athletes and showed **lean-mass gain WITHOUT strength or functional gain.** Performance/physique claims rest on vendor copy and anecdote, which are excluded as grounding (self-check 4). **Status: ABSENT.**

**Lineage shares (denominator = 8):**
- **Merck as sponsor/co-author appears on 6 of 8 primaries (75%)** — studies 1, 2, 4 (Merck co-authors), 6, 7 (Merck co-authors), 8 (Merck co-authors). Counting Merck-*sponsored/led* trials strictly (1, 2, 6) = 3/8 (37.5%).
- **Thorner/UVA group: 3 of 8 (37.5%)** — studies 3, 4, 5 (note study 4 is jointly UVA × Merck).
- **Single largest disease-program sponsor (Merck) by author footprint: 6/8 = 75%.**

**FLAG — concentration ≥70%.** Merck (the originator) is a co-author or sponsor on **75%** of the enumerated human primaries; no fully Merck-independent confirmatory disease-endpoint trial exists. The two largest **independent-of-the-physique-narrative** disease programs (AD, hip fracture) are also the two that **FAILED**. The remaining "positive" literature is dominated by sponsor-affiliated, surrogate-endpoint, short-duration studies in healthy/obese/elderly volunteers. **Interpretation:** MK-677's positive human evidence base is both (a) concentrated in a single commercial lineage and (b) restricted to biomarkers/body-composition surrogates; every time MK-677 was tested against a hard clinical disease endpoint it failed, and the one large independent functional readout (Nass) showed lean mass without function. Reader caution warranted: do not generalize sponsor-affiliated surrogate gains into clinical or performance benefit.
