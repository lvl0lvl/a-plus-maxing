# MK-677 (Ibutamoren) — Phase 2.5 Rubric + Section Plan (deep mode, efficient calibration)

## Mode
deep mode (25+ sources, ~10k words) under the **efficient calibration**: judge bar **92**;
genuine-defects-only; ONE remediation round per real defect.

## THE DEFINING FACTS (integrity axes)
1. **MK-677 (ibutamoren; Merck MK-0677; L-163,191) is a NON-PEPTIDIC, ORALLY-ACTIVE SMALL
   MOLECULE** that agonizes the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a). It is NOT a peptide and NOT a GHRH
   analogue — its oral activity + long half-life (~4-6 h, once-daily) is the key differentiator
   from the injectable peptide secretagogues (GHRPs/GHRH analogues). It indexes under the
   GH-secretagogue cluster but is chemically distinct. Be precise about "small molecule, not a peptide."
2. **It is the MOST-STUDIED GH secretagogue in LONG-TERM human RCTs** — more human evidence than
   the peptides. BUT the integrity story is: it durably raises **GH / IGF-1 / lean body mass**,
   yet **clinical/functional benefit was largely NOT demonstrated and its two major disease
   programs FAILED** (Alzheimer's Phase 3, Sevigny 2008; hip-fracture recovery). Biomarker/
   lean-mass ↑ ≠ functional benefit — make this the spine of the entry.
3. **Real safety signals from the RCTs:** increased appetite (ghrelin agonism), **fluid
   retention/edema**, **insulin resistance / raised fasting glucose + HbA1c**, modest cortisol
   rise, lethargy; a **congestive-heart-failure (CHF) signal** surfaced in the elderly/
   functional-decline program. State these honestly.
4. Merck MK-0677 — development discontinued, **never approved**; now sold gray-market as an
   oral "research chemical"/bodybuilding product. WADA prohibited (S2).

## Section plan (6 lean sections)
- **A — Identity, structure, mechanism.** Non-peptidic small molecule (spiroindane/spiropiperidine
  class); oral bioavailability + long t½; GHS-R1a agonism → GH (pulse-amplitude + basal) → IGF-1;
  contrast injectable peptide secretagogues; the oral-once-daily advantage.
- **B — Long-term human RCT efficacy.** Nass 2008 (Ann Intern Med, 2-yr healthy elderly: lean
  mass ↑, IGF-1 → young-adult range, but NO functional/strength benefit); Chapman/Murphy
  (older studies, GH/IGF-1, sleep, body comp); any obesity/catabolic/GHD data. INTEGRITY:
  biomarker/lean-mass ↑ vs functional benefit not shown; annotate population.
- **C — Failed clinical programs + other + concentration audit.** Alzheimer's Phase 3
  (Sevigny 2008 — FAILED); hip-fracture recovery (Adunsky/Bach — FAILED functional endpoint);
  catabolic/wasting; sleep/EEG. Label evidenced vs INVESTIGATIONAL vs FAILED vs ABSENT.
  Concentration audit — Merck + academic lineage; flag ≥70%.
- **D — Safety / adverse effects.** Appetite ↑; edema/fluid retention; **glucose/insulin —
  raised fasting glucose, reduced insulin sensitivity, HbA1c ↑** (the dominant safety issue);
  cortisol; lethargy; the **CHF signal**; contraindications (diabetes/glucose intolerance, CHF/
  cardiac, active malignancy, pregnancy); monitoring (fasting glucose/HbA1c + IGF-1).
- **E — Regulatory & sport.** Merck MK-0677 never approved (discontinued); gray-market oral
  "research chemical"; FDA actions on MK-677 in supplements/sports-nutrition (it is NOT a lawful
  dietary ingredient); §503A bulks list is for peptides — MK-677 (small molecule) likely not on
  it (verify; don't conflate with the Apr-2026 peptide action); WADA prohibited at all times
  **S2 (GH secretagogues)** — ibutamoren/MK-677 named (verify against the WADA 2026 primary).
- **F — Practitioner-practice + non-English.** Oral ~10-25 mg/day once daily (common bodybuilding
  convention); the glucose-monitoring caveat; whether compounded; Russian/Chinese/Japanese lit
  (Merck/academic English-published — distinguish).
