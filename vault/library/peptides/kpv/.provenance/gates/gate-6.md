# Phase 6 Critique — KPV

## Verdict
verdict: PASS (no CRITICAL gaps)

## Findings

The draft is unusually disciplined on the four highest-risk axes for this compound. The adversarial review found no CRITICAL gap that would force HALT. The zero-human-evidence reality, the Merlin single-lab concentration, the contested antimicrobial claim, and the KPV-vs-KdPT/(CKPV)₂ monomer-vs-analogue boundary are all carried consistently and fairly. Findings below are minor/major refinements compatible with PASS.

### Axis 1 — Missing perspectives / counter-evidence / bias

- category: balance-issue · severity: minor — **Zero-human-evidence reality is carried pervasively and well.** It appears in the frontmatter tier-C rationale, the "Read this first" block, the TL;DR, the §2 BLUF, §3 (the dedicated section), §6, §7 intro and §7.5, §8 headline, §9 critical framing, §10.1, and §11. This is not a gap; it is recorded as a positive confirmation. No fix required.

- category: balance-issue · severity: minor — **Merlin single-lab concentration is carried fairly.** §5 is first-class and up front, reports BOTH denominators (50% all-cause N=6, ~75% gut/IBD N=4) with the ≥70% flag, names the dominant cluster and its recurring co-authors, and §5.6 gives an honest "independent primaries reduce but do not eliminate" caveat. The ~75% figure is correctly scoped to the gut/IBD efficacy track (not laundered into an all-literature claim) and is cross-referenced in §6 and §7.1. Fair. No fix required.

- category: unexamined-counter-evidence · severity: minor — **Contested antimicrobial is balanced.** §2.7 and §7.3 both carry Cutuli (positive, originating lineage) AND Songok 2018 (independent null, capped Ac-KPV-NH₂) AND the originating group's own concession (Grieco/Catania: native melanocortins only "weak" in standard medium). It correctly lands at LOW confidence and assigns the robust signal to engineered/dimerized derivatives, not native KPV. Balanced.

- category: bias · severity: minor — **Monomer-vs-analogue discipline is consistent.** §1.8 is a dedicated non-conflation section; §3.3, §7.2, §7.4, and §11 all keep KdPT (acne, SZ95 sebocytes) and α-MSH+(CKPV)₂ (gout/MSU, Capsoni) and the (CKPV)₂ vaginitis result explicitly OFF the KPV-monomer ledger. No KdPT/(CKPV)₂ finding is laundered onto KPV. The marketing claims ("40% faster wound closure," NC/Nga IgE) are named and excluded as unsupported. Strong.

### Axis 2 — Logical consistency, citation completeness, balance, tone

- category: citation-incomplete · severity: major — **Dual numbering scheme is a real traceability friction.** Section bodies cite local per-section numbers (e.g. `[1, animal]`, `[3, in_vitro]`, `[4 in §7]`) that do NOT map to the unified Bibliography numbering (where Land 2012 = [3], the importin-α3 study; Bettenworth KdPT = [4]; etc.). A reader following `[3, in_vitro]` in §2.3 (intended: Land 2012) lands on a different paper in the bibliography. The bibliography header (line 432) discloses this ("the same paper appeared under different local section numbers"), and the upstream Phase-4.25 ID-reconcile is attested PASS, so the mapping is internally sound — but it is not reconstructable by the end reader from the published artifact alone. Recommended fix: add a per-section → bibliography crosswalk, or renumber inline citations to the unified scheme. Major (traceability), not critical (no claim is mis-grounded; the upstream reconcile attests the underlying mapping).

- category: logical-inconsistency · severity: minor — **§5.2 denominator classification is self-disclosed but slightly soft.** The "in-vivo efficacy primaries" table counts P6 Cutuli (antimicrobial) as in-vivo, while §7.3 and the bibliography tag it in-vitro-lead `in_vitro (+ animal)`; P5 Bonfiglio is corneal-wound (arguably efficacy but a different domain). §5.6 explicitly flags this denominator-sensitivity and notes it does not move the headline gut/IBD 75%. Self-corrected; minor.

- category: objectivity-issue · severity: minor — **Tone is appropriately non-promotional throughout.** "Mechanistically attractive but clinically unproven," repeated "none of it is human," and the §11 supported-vs-marketed split are even-handed. The one mechanistic enthusiasm point (the PepT1-KO "clean causal" result) is genuinely the strongest datum and is correctly bounded as preclinical. No fix.

### Axis 3 — Overstatement checks

- category: balance-issue · severity: minor — **All four overstatement traps are correctly avoided.** (a) The ~12,000× figure is explicitly framed as a single-group (Xiao/Merlin) intra-study nanoparticle-vs-free formulation comparison, NOT general KPV potency — flagged in §5.5, §7.1, §6.3, and §11. (b) Gut-IBD preclinical promise is not oversold as near-clinical: "preclinically promising, clinically unproven," and §7.1's honest "direction replicates / magnitude is single-lab / none of it is human" read. (c) Vendor LD50 / "≥100 mg/kg / no LD50" is marked UNVERIFIED and untraceable to a primary toxicology study (§8.2, §11). (d) FDA "removed from Cat-2" is repeatedly stated as NOT approval and NOT lawful compoundability (line 20, §4.1 ×3, §11). No fix.

### Axis 4 — Regulatory / WADA accuracy

- category: objectivity-issue · severity: minor — **Regulatory and WADA framing are accurate.** FDA: not approved; Cat-2 (2023) → removed ~15 Apr 2026 (nomination withdrawn, 1 of 12) → now unlisted pending PCAC 23–24 Jul 2026; removal ≠ approval; PCAC non-binding + rulemaking >1yr caveat. WADA: not individually named on the 2026 list, but prohibited at all times under S0 (catch-all, by mechanism not enumeration), contrasted correctly with BPC-157/S2 growth factors. The §4.4 coverage gap honestly concedes the FDA line-item and PCAC page were corroborated across four legal/industry sources rather than primary-quoted from fda.gov (404 at fetch). Accurate and appropriately caveated.

- category: missing-perspective · severity: minor — **One small balance addition would strengthen, not block.** The report could note explicitly that the ~Apr 2026 Cat-2 removal was driven by *nominator withdrawal* (an administrative/commercial act), which is arguably a *negative* signal for the evidence base, not a neutral one — the draft states the withdrawal fact (§4.1) but does not draw the inference that withdrawal ≠ vindication. Optional enhancement; the "removal ≠ approval" message already prevents the dangerous misread. Minor.

## Additional retrievals
None requested. Every counter-claim that could plausibly need testing (the antimicrobial null, the analogue attribution, the FDA-removal interpretation, the WADA S0 logic) is already represented in the corpus with its opposing evidence carried. No specific counter-claim genuinely requires new retrieval; PASS-with-minor/major-findings is the sound call.
