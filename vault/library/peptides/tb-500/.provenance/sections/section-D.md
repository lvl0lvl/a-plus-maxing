# Section D — Preclinical Efficacy: Cardiac/Neuro/Ophthalmic/Other (TB-500 / Tβ4)

> Scope: preclinical efficacy of Thymosin Beta-4 (Tβ4; the synthetic 43-aa peptide marketed
> in gray-market channels as "TB-500" — see scope note below) in cardiac, neurological,
> ophthalmic, and other-organ injury models. All animal claims carry species + n + route.
> No human efficacy claims are made in this section (those belong to clinical sections).
>
> SCOPE/NOMENCLATURE CAVEAT (important for downstream integrity): The published preclinical
> literature studies **full-length Tβ4** (and in some cardiac/eye work, recombinant human Tβ4
> or clinical-grade RGN-259). "TB-500" sold as a research chemical is frequently the full
> Tβ4 sequence, but vendor identity/purity is NOT verified in any peer-reviewed study cited
> here. Extrapolating these results to gray-market "TB-500" product is a route-tagged
> EXTRAPOLATION, not a demonstrated equivalence. Several vendor pages claim a "TB-500
> fragment (17-23 / LKKTETQ)" — the peptide literature below uses full-length Tβ4 unless noted.

## Claims

### 1. Cardiac repair / post-MI

1. In the foundational study, Tβ4 mobilized adult epicardium-derived progenitor cells and
   induced neovascularization, and was shown essential for embryonic coronary vessel
   development [1, animal][mechanism_review]. (Mouse; lineage-tracing + epicardial explant
   work; Smart/Riley group, UCL. n not enumerated in abstract.) [EXTRAPOLATION RISK: this is
   the origin of the "Tβ4 regrows heart muscle" narrative — see claim 4 for the failed
   replication of the cardiomyocyte-reprogramming part.]

2. Sustained intramyocardial delivery of Tβ4 (10 µg) from a self-assembling peptide scaffold
   significantly increased epicardial GFP+ EPDCs, raised microvessel density, reduced scar
   size, and improved ejection fraction/fractional shortening 4 weeks post-MI versus Tβ4
   alone and controls [2, animal]. (Transgenic mice Wt1CreERT2/+;Rosa26mTmG/+, male, 12 wk;
   n≥8/group; intramyocardial injection, peri-infarct, 4 sites.) [route=intramyocardial —
   not a systemic-injection model; effect depends on the delivery scaffold.]

3. Systemic AAV-delivered Tβ4 reduced post-MI cardiac oxidative stress and fibrosis and
   improved cardiac function: LVEF 74.3±3.2% (Tβ4) vs 66.8±5.4% (MI control) at day 42;
   hydroxyproline, MDA and MPO all significantly reduced; mortality 1/7 vs 3/7
   [3, animal]. (Male C57BL/6J mice; n=7/group; intraperitoneal AAV-Tβ4, 4×10¹⁰ vg,
   days 0 and 28.) [n=7 is small; AAV gene-delivery vector, not peptide injection — a
   route/modality extrapolation away from "TB-500 injection".]

4. NEGATIVE / CONTROVERSY — A genetic lineage-tracing replication found that Tβ4 given
   AFTER MI increased epicardial thickness and coronary capillary density but did **NOT**
   reprogram epicardium-derived cells into cardiomyocytes; treated cells became fibroblasts
   and smooth muscle, not myocytes [4, animal]. (Mice, Wt1CreERT2/+;Rosa26mTmG/+ lineage
   tracing; clinical-grade Tβ4 daily ×1 week post-MI.) This directly contradicts the
   cardiomyocyte-regeneration interpretation of [1]; the authors note Smart's reprogramming
   claim involved PRE-MI "priming," and post-MI dosing did not reproduce it. [Replication
   failure of the strongest mechanistic claim — load-bearing for honest assessment.]

### 2. Neurological models

5. Delayed Tβ4 improved functional recovery in embolic stroke and reduced infarct volume in
   AGED rats: infarct 12.77±3.61% (Tβ4) vs 26.04±4.29% (control), p<0.05 — BUT no
   improvement on any behavioral test at day 56 [5, animal]. (Aged male Wistar rats,
   18-21 mo; 23 randomized, 17 to endpoint [Tβ4 n=11, control n=12]; 12 mg/kg IP at 24 h
   post-stroke then every 3 days ×4 more; embolic MCAo; Chopp/Morris group, Henry Ford.)
   [Honest split result: anatomic benefit without functional benefit in aged animals.]

6. Tβ4 initiated 6 h after traumatic brain injury was neuroprotective and neurorestorative:
   higher-dose (30 mg/kg) reduced cortical lesion volume, Tβ4 reduced hippocampal
   (dentate/CA3) cell loss, lowered mNSS days 4-35, and improved Morris water maze spatial
   learning days 33-35 (all p<0.05) [6, animal]. (Young adult male Wistar rats; controlled
   cortical impact; saline n=7, Tβ4 6 mg/kg n=8, Tβ4 30 mg/kg n=8, sham n=6; IP at 6/24/48 h;
   Xiong/Chopp group, Henry Ford.)

7. In EAE (multiple sclerosis model), Tβ4 produced ~50% relative functional recovery vs
   controls (significant by day 11), reduced inflammatory infiltrates (3.6±0.3 vs 5±0.5
   per slide, p<0.05), and increased both oligodendrocyte progenitors (NG2+) and mature
   oligodendrocytes (CNPase+) with elevated BrdU+ proliferation, supporting remyelination
   [7, animal]. (SJL/J mice; Tβ4 n=10, saline n=11; 6 mg/kg every 3 days ×5; Zhang/Chopp
   group, Henry Ford.)

8. Mechanism (in vitro / EAE context): Tβ4 mediates oligodendrocyte differentiation by
   upregulating p38 MAPK [8, in_vitro][mechanism_review]. (Supports the remyelination
   claim in [7]; cell-level, not an efficacy endpoint.)

### 3. Ophthalmic (corneal/dry eye)

9. RGN-259 (clinical-grade topical Tβ4) matched or exceeded prescription dry-eye drugs in
   a desiccation-stress model: corneal fluorescein staining reduced 51.8% (4×/day) and
   33.6% (2×/day) vs 17.6% cyclosporine A / 15.1% diquafosol; tear production raised ~9×;
   goblet cells 2.0-2.4× and conjunctival mucin 2.4-2.6× (largest of all arms)
   [9, animal]. (NOD.B10-H2b mice; n=4-5/group, 8 groups; 5 µL/eye topical, 10 days.)
   [vendor-relevant: RGN-259 = pharmaceutical Tβ4, not gray-market TB-500.]

10. Recombinant human Tβ4 eye drops alleviated benzalkonium-chloride-induced dry eye:
    improved tear volume, reduced corneal staining, increased goblet-cell density (esp.
    0.1%), reduced CD4+ T cells and pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-4/6/17A, TNF-α, IFN-γ),
    decreased corneal/conjunctival apoptosis, via NF-κB inhibition and MMP modulation
    [10, animal]. (Female C57BL/6 mice, 8-10 wk; n=6/group; topical 0.05%/0.1%, 5 µL/eye
    4×/day, 7 days.)

### 4. Other organ systems

11. Tβ4 (1 mg/kg IP daily ×1 wk) prevented oxidative stress, inflammation and fibrosis in
    ethanol+LPS-induced liver injury: ROS down ~41-54%, GSH/Mn-SOD restored, NF-κB
    translocation blocked, TNF-α/IL-1β/IL-6 down, α-SMA/collagen-1/fibronectin reduced,
    Sirius Red staining down 80-88%, hydroxyproline down 28-50%, AST/ALT lowered
    [11, animal]. (Female C57BL/6 mice; n=4/group, 6 groups.) [n=4 is very small.]

12. Tβ4 and its degradation product Ac-SDKP are reparative in renal fibrosis (unilateral
    ureteral obstruction): Ac-SDKP consistently reduced collagen/fibronectin, myofibroblasts
    and macrophages in BOTH wild-type and PAI-1-knockout mice; Tβ4 ALONE reduced late
    fibrosis only in wild-type (no effect in PAI-1 KO), and Tβ4 + prolyl-oligopeptidase
    inhibitor (blocking Ac-SDKP generation) INCREASED fibrosis [12, animal]. (WT C57BL and
    PAI-1 KO mice; n=5-10/group; IP; UUO model; Fogo/Carretero groups.) [Important nuance:
    much of the renal benefit is attributable to the Ac-SDKP metabolite, and outcome is
    PAI-1-status-dependent — not a clean "Tβ4 is renoprotective" story.]

## Lab-provenance note (for downstream single-lab audit)

The Section-D evidence base is **dominated by two lab lineages**, which is the single biggest
provenance concern:

- **Chopp lab (Henry Ford Hospital, Detroit)** and close collaborators (Morris, Xiong, Zhang ZG,
  Zhang J) produce essentially ALL the neuro evidence — stroke [5], TBI [6], EAE [7], and the
  oligodendrocyte mechanism [8]. Carretero (renal [12]) is also Henry Ford. The neuro narrative
  is effectively SINGLE-GROUP; no fully independent lab replication of the neuro-functional
  endpoints was located. Treat neuro effect sizes as un-replicated-across-labs.
- **Riley/Smart lab (UCL/Oxford)** originated the cardiac-epicardium story [1]. Crucially, the
  most dramatic cardiac claim (cardiomyocyte reprogramming) FAILED independent replication by
  the **Pu/Zhou lab (Boston Children's)** [4] — a genuine cross-lab contradiction, the most
  important integrity signal in this section.
- Cardiac efficacy [2,3] and ophthalmic [10] come from independent (largely Chinese) groups;
  ophthalmic RGN-259 work [9] involves Sosne (Wayne State) with RegeneRx commercial affiliation.
- Liver [11] is a single VA/GWU group; renal [12] is Vanderbilt/Henry Ford.

Net: cardiac and ophthalmic have SOME cross-lab support; neuro is one-group; the strongest
single mechanistic claim (cardiomyocyte regeneration) is a documented replication failure.

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

1. **Single-lab dominance (neuro).** All neuro-functional efficacy traces to the Chopp group.
   No independent lab replication of stroke/TBI/EAE functional endpoints was found. This is a
   real un-replicated-across-labs flag, not a search artifact.
2. **The flagship cardiac claim is a replication failure.** "Tβ4 regrows heart muscle by
   reprogramming epicardium into cardiomyocytes" [1] was NOT confirmed when given post-MI [4].
   Surviving cardiac benefits are vascular/anti-fibrotic/anti-oxidant, not myocyte regeneration.
3. **Small n throughout.** Multiple key studies use n=4-8/group ([3] n=7, [6] n=7-8, [9] n=4-5,
   [11] n=4) — underpowered; effect-size precision is low.
4. **Route/modality mismatch with "TB-500".** Strong cardiac results used AAV gene delivery [3]
   or an intramyocardial peptide scaffold [2] — NOT the systemic injection route TB-500 users
   employ. Ophthalmic results are topical. Generalizing to subcutaneous/IP self-injection of
   gray-market TB-500 is an unproven extrapolation.
5. **Product-identity gap.** No cited study used a verified "TB-500" gray-market product; all
   used pharmaceutical/recombinant/clinical-grade Tβ4 (or RGN-259). The fragment vs full-length
   question (vendor "LKKTETQ fragment" vs research full-length Tβ4) is unresolved in this set.
6. **One citation is second-source-confirmed, not primary-fetched** ([1] Nature auth-wall) —
   flagged inline; PMID/DOI are real and the record exists, but field-level fetch was blocked.
   ([8] has now been fully primary-verified via PMC/PubMed — PMID 23073962, DOI 10.1002/glia.22400.) Lung-fibrosis (Ac-SDKP/bleomycin)
   and additional GI/lung studies exist (e.g., PMC5085123) but were NOT verified to citation
   depth and are deliberately EXCLUDED rather than cited thinly.
