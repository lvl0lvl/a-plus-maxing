# Section E — Human Clinical Evidence (TB-500 / Tβ4)

> Scope: ALL registered/published human clinical evidence for thymosin beta-4 (Tβ4, the full 43–44-aa peptide) and for the gray-market "TB-500" fragment. Decision-relevant bottom line is stated explicitly in the Coverage Gaps section.
>
> **Naming note (load-bearing):** Nearly all human clinical work uses *full-length* Tβ4 — the 43-aa endogenous peptide (RegeneRx programs RGN-259/RGN-352/RGN-137) or a 44-aa recombinant version (NL005, Beijing Northland). The gray-market athletic product marketed as **"TB-500" is the Tβ4 17-23 fragment**, NOT the full peptide. Until 2026 there were ZERO registered human trials of the "TB-500" fragment. The one fragment trial that now exists (NCT07487363) is cardiovascular, Phase 1/2, recruiting, with no results — see Claim 9. Do not conflate full-Tβ4 clinical data with "TB-500."

## Claims

**Claim 1 — There is NO completed human RCT with posted/published positive efficacy results that met its pre-specified primary endpoint for ANY Tβ4 indication.** Across ~19 registered studies, the strongest signals (dry eye, neurotrophic keratopathy, cardiac) are either small phase 2 trials, phase 3 trials that MISSED co-primary endpoints, or completed trials with results not yet posted. [1, regulatory][2, rct][6, rct]

**Claim 2 — Ophthalmic (RGN-259), dry eye: largest program; phase 3 co-primary endpoints NOT met.** ARISE-1 (NCT02597803, phase 2/3, n=317, completed 2016) and ARISE-2 (NCT02974907, phase 3, n=601, completed 2018) showed dose-dependent symptom signals; ARISE-3 (NCT03937882, phase 3, n=700, completed Oct 2021) **failed its pre-specified co-primary endpoints**, with only some secondary/subgroup signs (ocular grittiness, corneal staining in subgroups) reaching significance. Sponsor: ReGenTree (RegeneRx JV). No posted results in registry for ARISE-3. [2, rct][3, rct][4, rct]

**Claim 3 — Ophthalmic, neurotrophic keratopathy (NK): a small phase 3 (SEER-1) NARROWLY MISSED its primary endpoint.** SEER-1 (NCT02600429, phase 3, n=18 [10 drug / 8 placebo], terminated (business decision)) reported 60% complete corneal healing on 0.1% RGN-259 vs 12.5% placebo at Day 29, **p=0.0656 — not significant** on the pre-specified test (an alternative chi-square gave p=0.040). Published peer-reviewed. Confirmatory trials SEER-2 (NCT05555589, phase 3, n≈70, recruiting, est. completion 2026) and SEER-3 are ongoing. [5, rct][1, regulatory]

**Claim 4 — Ophthalmic, severe dry eye / GVHD: positive small phase 2.** A phase 2 randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled trial (n=9 patients, 12 eyes treated) of 0.1% Tβ4 drops showed significant improvement vs vehicle: 35.1% reduction in ocular discomfort (p=0.0141) and 59.1% reduction in corneal fluorescein staining (p=0.0108) by day 56. Tiny n; hypothesis-generating. **NCT linkage (honest):** the publication (PMID 25826322, Sosne 2015, severe dry eye / Sjögren / GVHD, n=9) does not itself cite an NCT number; its most plausible registry counterpart is NCT01393132 (Tβ4 drops, severe dry eye / Sjögren / GVHD, phase 2, n=9, completed 2012), and the table maps it there on that basis. NCT01387347 (RGN-259, dry eye, phase 2, n=72) is a separate, larger ReGenTree dry-eye study and is NOT the n=9 publication — it was previously listed alongside NCT01393132 here in a way that conflated the two. Treat the PMID 25826322 ↔ NCT01393132 link as a best-fit inference, not a registry-confirmed mapping. [6, rct]

**Claim 5 — Dermal (RGN-137), venous stasis ulcers: completed phase 2, NO peer-reviewed efficacy publication; results only via press release.** NCT00832091 (phase 2, n=72, completed Jan 2009): drug safe/well-tolerated (primary objective met); mid-dose "appeared most active" at initiating healing vs placebo per the sponsor press release. No registry results posted, no peer-reviewed trial paper for this specific NCT. [7, open_label][8, anecdote_aggregate]

**Claim 6 — Dermal, pressure ulcers: completed phase 2, safety met, efficacy only via press release.** NCT00382174 (phase 2, n=72, completed Dec 2008): safe/well-tolerated; mid-dose most active per sponsor press release. No registry results, no peer-reviewed trial paper. [9, open_label][10, anecdote_aggregate]

**Claim 7 — Dermal, epidermolysis bullosa (EB): phase 2 TERMINATED for lack of patients / drug expiry — no efficacy data.** NCT00311766 (phase 2, n=30 target, terminated 2012). Orphan-designated indication; trial did not produce usable efficacy results. [11, open_label]

**Claim 8 — Earlier European topical Tβ4 venous-ulcer work (Guarnera) is the strongest dermal evidence, but is dose-escalation/safety-focused, not a powered efficacy RCT.** An 8-site European (Italy/Poland) prospective randomized double-blind dose-escalation study of topical Tβ4 reported safety/tolerability and a healing-enhancement signal in venous ulcers. Two related publications (2007, 2010). Secondary efficacy only. [12, open_label][13, open_label]

**Claim 9 — THE "TB-500" FRAGMENT (Tβ4 17-23): exactly ONE registered human trial exists, and it is cardiovascular, not athletic.** NCT07487363 (Hudson Biotech), "Phase 1/2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, sequential dose-escalation study of TB-500 (Thymosin Beta 4 17-23 Fragment)" for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease / endothelial dysfunction. Phase 1/2, n=80 (estimated), **status: recruiting** (started 5 Feb 2026), primary endpoints are safety (TEAEs/SAEs); exploratory secondary = flow-mediated dilation, hs-CRP, NT-proBNP, vascular stiffness. **No results posted.** This is the first-ever registered human trial of the actual "TB-500" molecule — and it is NOT for muscle/tendon/athletic use. [14, rct]

**Claim 10 — Cardiac (RGN-352 / injectable Tβ4): NO completed phase 2 in the West; two registered RGN-352 records (one Phase 1, one Phase 2) were WITHDRAWN (n=0).** RegeneRx's injectable acute-MI program: NCT00743769 (phase 1, healthy volunteers) is listed **Withdrawn (n=0)**; NCT01311518 (phase 2, acute MI/STEMI) is **Withdrawn (n=0)**. The U.S. RGN-352 phase 2 was placed on FDA clinical hold. A separate phase 1 in healthy volunteers was completed/reported as safe (see Claim 11). [15, regulatory][16, regulatory]

**Claim 11 — Cardiac, China (Beijing Northland NL005, recombinant 44-aa Tβ4): a published phase 1 (safe) plus completed phase 2 trials in acute MI — but NO efficacy results posted yet.** First-in-human phase 1 (SAD n=54 / MAD n=30) published 2021: no SAEs/DLTs, dose-proportional PK (NCT04555824/NCT04555850). Phase 2/2b AMI trials NCT05485818 (n=62, completed 2021) and NCT05984134 (n=90, completed May 2023, primary endpoint = % change in infarct area by cardiac MRI) are **completed but have NO posted results (hasResults=false)**; further phase 2 NCT07586865 (n=189) not yet recruiting. [17, rct][18, rct][19, rct]

**Claim 12 — There is NO human efficacy evidence (no trial at all, completed or registered) for the athletic / musculoskeletal "TB-500" use case (muscle tear, tendon/ligament repair, sports recovery).** All musculoskeletal data are preclinical (rodent/rabbit). No published or registered human tendon/muscle/ligament trial of Tβ4 or the TB-500 fragment exists. Tβ4/TB-500 is not FDA-approved for any human therapeutic use. [20, mechanism_review][14, rct]

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

1. **NO completed human RCT has met its pre-specified primary efficacy endpoint with posted/published positive results, for ANY Tβ4 indication.** The flagship phase 3 (ARISE-3, dry eye, n=700) MISSED its co-primary endpoints. The phase 3 NK trial (SEER-1, n=18) NARROWLY MISSED its primary (p=0.0656). The positive trials are small phase 2s (n=9). This is the single most important honest statement.

2. **The Chinese phase 2/2b acute-MI trials (NL005, n=62 and n=90) are completed but have NO posted results** (registry hasResults=false). Until those efficacy data appear, the cardiac efficacy claim is unproven in humans.

3. **The Western injectable cardiac program (RGN-352) effectively never delivered controlled efficacy data** — both registered RGN-352 records (NCT00743769 Phase 1, NCT01311518 Phase 2) were withdrawn (n=0) and the US phase 2 was on FDA clinical hold. Only an uncontrolled/small phase 1 safety signal exists.

4. **Dermal (RGN-137) phase 2 efficacy claims rest on press releases, not peer-reviewed publications or posted registry results.** The EB trial was terminated. The strongest dermal datapoint (Guarnera EU venous-ulcer RCT) was a dose-escalation safety study with efficacy only as a secondary aim.

5. **ZERO human efficacy evidence exists for the athletic / musculoskeletal "TB-500" use case** (muscle tears, tendon/ligament repair, sports recovery). There is no completed trial, and the only registered trial of the actual TB-500 fragment (NCT07487363) is a CARDIOVASCULAR phase 1/2 safety study that is merely recruiting as of 2026 with no results. All musculoskeletal evidence is preclinical (animal). Tβ4/TB-500 is not FDA-approved for any human use.

6. **Critical conflation risk:** Buyers and vendors cite full-length Tβ4 clinical trials (RGN-259 dry eye, NL005 cardiac) as if they validate the injectable "TB-500" fragment. They do not — different molecule, different (non-athletic) indications, and even the full-peptide trials have not produced an endpoint-meeting, results-posted efficacy win.

7. **Publication/transparency gap:** several completed trials (ARISE-1/2/3, NL005 phase 2/2b, RGN-137 dermal) lack posted registry results, so outcome detail relies on sponsor statements or conference abstracts rather than full peer-reviewed data.
