# Section F — Safety, AEs, Regulatory & Anti-Doping (TB-500 / Tβ4)

> **Scope note.** "TB-500" is a synthetic actin-binding fragment marketed as / sold as Thymosin beta-4 (Tβ4). Most *human* clinical evidence is for full-length recombinant Tβ4 (RegeneRx programs RGN-259 [ophthalmic], RGN-352 [IV], RGN-137 [topical]) and one independent first-in-human IV Phase I (Wang 2021). No controlled human trial of the gray-market "TB-500" fragment itself exists; human safety is inferred from full-length Tβ4. This inference is itself a limitation and is flagged throughout.
>
> **AE existence vs. AE rate:** Numerical AE rates below are sourced only to Tier 1/2 human trials. Vendor/forum material is not used to ground any rate.

---

## Claims

1. In a first-in-human IV Phase I RCT of recombinant human Tβ4 (single ascending dose: 54 healthy adults, 0.05–25 µg/kg IV, 4:1 drug:placebo), **adverse events occurred in 25/44 (56.8%) Tβ4 subjects vs 7/10 (70%) placebo; all AEs were mild–moderate; there were no serious adverse events and no dose-limiting toxicities** up to 25 µg/kg. [1, rct] (AE rate — Tier 1)
2. In the multiple-ascending-dose arm of the same trial (30 healthy adults, 0.5/2.0/5.0 µg/kg IV daily ×10 days), **AEs occurred in 16/24 (66.7%) Tβ4 subjects vs 5/6 (83.3%) placebo; all mild–moderate, self-resolving; no SAEs, no dose-limiting toxicity.** [1, rct] (AE rate — Tier 1)
3. In a Phase III RCT of 0.1% RGN-259 (Tβ4) ophthalmic solution for neurotrophic keratopathy (n=18; 10 active, 8 placebo), **16 AEs occurred across 7 subjects; treatment-group AE burden (40% of subjects) was comparable to placebo (37.5%); only 1 AE was judged treatment-related; only 1 (non-ocular, unrelated) serious AE; all AEs resolved by study end.** [2, rct] (AE rate — Tier 1, but very small n)
4. Across the RegeneRx ophthalmic program (cited as ~425 subjects in 5 studies of 0.05%/0.1% RGN-259, including a 317-subject dry-eye trial), the drug has been **reported as safe and well-tolerated with no clinically significant safety-parameter changes**; reported AEs are predominantly mild, transient, local (ocular) events. [2, rct/regulatory; secondary aggregation] (AE existence + qualitative tolerability — Tier 2; the aggregate 425-subject figure is a sponsor-reported pooled statement, not an independent dataset)
5. In an early Phase I systemic study, **three uncommon, possibly-related events were reported — dizziness, headache, and pyrexia (1 subject each).** [4, regulatory/secondary] (AE existence — Tier 3 lead; corroborates the mild-systemic-AE pattern of [1])
6. **Tumor-promotion concern (PRO-tumor direction):** In a mouse model, Tβ4 over-expression (adenoviral) increased B16-F10 melanoma metastatic lung nodules to mean 46.7 (95% CI 35.0–57.7) vs 10.9 (6.2–15.6) in controls (P<.001); increased 20-day tumor size (21.7 mm vs 13.3 mm, P=.036); increased tumor cell migration 2.3-fold and intratumoral blood-vessel density 4.4-fold, associated with VEGF induction. [5, animal — mouse, C57BL/6, n≈20 IV-injection cohort; in vitro migration assays] (mechanistic/animal — Tier 2 for animals; NOT human evidence)
7. **Tumor direction is context-dependent — opposing evidence exists.** In multiple myeloma, Tβ4 behaves as a **tumor suppressor**: expression was significantly lower in myeloma vs normal plasma cells (P<0.001); Tβ4 over-expression *decreased* proliferation/migration and increased apoptosis sensitivity; below-median expression in 209 patients predicted shorter disease-free survival (26.2 vs 37.6 months, P<0.05); and Tβ4-over-expressing myeloma cells prolonged mouse survival (88.9 vs 65.9 days, P<0.05). [6, cohort + animal — mouse] (Tier 2 — establishes the bidirectional, cancer-type-dependent picture; net signal in *solid* tumors leans pro-tumor/pro-metastatic)
8. **Anti-fibrotic (a stated benefit, listed here because the same vascular/remodeling biology underlies both the benefit and the tumor concern):** Tβ4 reduced fibrosis in animal models of lung, kidney and heart injury, mechanistically via reduced macrophage infiltration, lower TGF-β/CTGF, and suppressed fibroblast→myofibroblast conversion. [7, mechanism_review/animal] (mechanism — Tier 2; not a safety AE, included for risk-balance context)
9. **Preclinical toxicology has not flagged organ toxicity.** Sponsor/program documentation describes ~23–24 completed nonclinical toxicology and safety-pharmacology studies (incl. rat and rabbit developmental toxicology with no maternal or embryo/fetal effects reported) as supporting Tβ4's intended clinical use; specific NOAEL values are redacted in public filings. [4/8, regulatory/secondary] (AE existence/absence at preclinical level — Tier 3 because the underlying study reports are not independently published/retrievable; treat as sponsor-reported)
10. **Regulatory:** Tβ4 is **not FDA-approved for any indication** (as of 2026). RGN-259 (ophthalmic) holds FDA orphan-drug designations (neurotrophic keratopathy; epidermolysis bullosa) and has completed Phase II/III ophthalmic trials without marketing approval. The systemic IV program for acute MI (RGN-352, NCT01311518, Phase 2) was **withdrawn / never initiated (enrollment 0)** — i.e., there is no completed controlled human trial of systemic Tβ4 for a systemic indication. [3, regulatory/clinicaltrials.gov; 2, regulatory] (regulatory fact)
11. **FDA compounding:** In April 2026 the FDA **REMOVED** thymosin beta-4 / TB-500 from the 503A interim **Category 2** bulk-substances list (notice published ~Apr 15–16, 2026; effective within 7 calendar days). TB-500 had been placed on Category 2 in September 2023 over significant-safety-risk concerns (immunogenicity, impurities, limited human clinical data); that listing is now rescinded. Removal from Category 2 is a procedural step and is **not** FDA approval and **not** an affirmative green-light for compounding — TB-500 remains non-compoundable pending rulemaking, because it is neither FDA-approved nor on the 503A Category-1 (permitted) list. A Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) meeting (July 23–24, 2026; Federal Register notice Apr 16, 2026) is scheduled to evaluate TB-500 (and BPC-157, KPV, MOTs-C) for potential **Category-1** listing; that decision had not yet occurred as of this writing. (This mirrors the parallel FDA action on BPC-157 in the same window.) [9, regulatory/secondary] (regulatory status — secondary source summarizing primary FDA action)
12. **Anti-doping:** Tβ4/TB-500 is **prohibited at all times (in- and out-of-competition)** on the WADA 2026 Prohibited List, class **S2.3 "Growth Factors and Growth Factor Modulators."** Verbatim list text: *"• Thymosin-ß4 and its derivatives e.g. TB-500."* The S2 class is designated **non-Specified Substances**; list valid 1 January 2026. [10, regulatory — WADA primary text] (regulatory fact — directly verified from the official list document)

## Risk-floor inputs (contraindications / monitoring / stopping considerations)

- **Active or history of malignancy / elevated cancer risk → avoid.** Tβ4 is pro-angiogenic (VEGF induction) and, in solid-tumor animal models, pro-migratory/pro-metastatic [5]. Although evidence is bidirectional and cancer-type-dependent [6,7], the prudent risk-floor position for a non-approved peptide is to avoid use in anyone with active or recent solid malignancy or undiagnosed masses. (cited: [5,6])
- **No established human dosing / no systemic safety regulatory file.** The only completed systemic human safety data is one Phase I in healthy volunteers up to 25 µg/kg IV [1]; the systemic therapeutic Phase 2 was withdrawn [3]. There is no long-term human safety data and no human data on the gray-market TB-500 fragment specifically → treat any systemic self-administration as unstudied. (cited: [1,3])
- **Immunogenicity / product-quality risk with compounded or research-chemical material.** The substantive concern stands on its own: a peptide carries inherent immunogenicity potential, and gray-market "research-only" vials are not quality-controlled (impurities, mislabeling, no sterility assurance). Note on regulatory framing: immunogenicity/impurities/limited-human-data were the **historical** rationale for FDA's Sept-2023 interim 503A **Category-2** listing of TB-500 — but that listing was **REMOVED in April 2026**, with a July 2026 PCAC review pending for possible Category-1 status [9]. The safety caveat is therefore anchored to the peptide/product biology, not to a current restriction. Monitor for hypersensitivity/injection-site reactions; stop on systemic allergic signs. (cited: [9])
- **Pregnancy/lactation → avoid.** Although animal developmental tox was reportedly unremarkable [9-context], there is no human reproductive safety data; default to avoidance. (cited: [3,4])
- **Monitoring if used despite the above (harm-reduction framing, not endorsement):** baseline and periodic clinical review for new/growing lesions given the angiogenesis signal; watch for systemic AEs reported in trials — dizziness, headache, pyrexia [5], and the mild–moderate self-limited AEs seen in [1]. No validated lab-monitoring panel exists. (cited: [1,5])
- **Athletes → categorical contraindication for anyone subject to anti-doping testing:** banned at all times, non-Specified; detection window reported in the ~30–45 day range (secondary). A positive test carries sanctions. (cited: [10])

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

- **Human safety data is thin and indirect.** The strongest systemic human safety source [1] is a single healthy-volunteer Phase I (n=84 total) with only 28-day follow-up; the only Phase III [2] is ophthalmic with n=18 in the pivotal report. There is **no long-term human safety data, no systemic therapeutic-population trial** (RGN-352 was withdrawn [3]), and **no human trial of the "TB-500" fragment itself** — all human inference rests on full-length recombinant Tβ4. State this plainly to readers.
- **Tumor risk is unresolved at the level that matters for humans.** Direction is genuinely bidirectional (pro-metastatic in solid-tumor animal models [5]; tumor-suppressive in myeloma [6]); there is **no human carcinogenicity/longitudinal cancer-incidence data** for exogenous Tβ4. The conservative read for a non-approved, self-administered, pro-angiogenic peptide is that the solid-tumor signal warrants treating malignancy/undiagnosed masses as a contraindication.
- **Preclinical tox is sponsor-asserted, not independently published.** Claim 9 rests on RegeneRx/ReGenTree program statements; the underlying NOAEL/study reports were not publicly retrievable. Carried as Tier 3.
- **Orphan-designation and EMA status** are cited via secondary/sponsor sources; the FDA Orphan Drug Product database entry and any EMA record were not independently fetched (recommend a follow-up primary-source confirmation if these become load-bearing).
- **Detection-window numbers (~30–45 days)** appear only in secondary anti-doping commentary and are not cited as fact here beyond a flagged secondary mention.
- **Immunogenicity:** an antibody-formation/neutralizing-antibody safety signal in *humans* was not located in published trials; it appears only as the *historical* rationale for FDA's now-rescinded interim Category-2 listing [9] and as a research tool (anti-Tβ4 antibodies) — i.e., a theoretical/regulatory concern, not a documented clinical AE rate.
