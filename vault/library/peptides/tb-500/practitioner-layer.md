# TB-500 / Thymosin Beta-4 — Prescribing-Practice Layer

> **Scope discipline.** This document records *how TB-500 is actually prescribed, dosed, cycled, and reconstituted in practice* — the dose/cycle/route/reconstitution conventions that circulate among named prescribers, peptide-medicine societies, and (where they exist) compounding pharmacies. It is **not** an efficacy document and must not be read as one. Per the source-tagging rule, `practitioner_protocol` (Tier 2.7), `compounding_data_sheet` (Tier 3), and `vendor_label` (Tier 4) sources are admissible **only** for dose/cycle/route/reconstitution conventions and (for vendor labels) purity/reconstitution math. **None** of these source classes grounds an efficacy, adverse-event-rate, or mechanism claim, and none appears here for that purpose. The efficacy state (no completed human RCT met its primary endpoint; **zero** human data for the athletic muscle/tendon use case; the marketed "TB-500" is the Ac-LKKTETQ heptapeptide fragment, NOT full-length Tβ4) lives in the research report and is unaffected by anything below.
>
> **Regulatory state, read first.** As of mid-2026 TB-500 / thymosin beta-4 is **not an FDA-approved drug for any indication** and is **not lawfully compoundable**. The FDA **removed TB-500 / thymosin beta-4 from the 503A interim Category-2 bulk-substances list in ~April 2026** (one of twelve peptides removed in a parallel FDA 503A interim-bulks action, reflected on FDA's Category-2 page), but removal from Category 2 is **not** placement on the permissive Category-1 list and confers **no FDA approval and no settled lawful compounding channel**; TB-500 is one of the seven peptides on the **Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) agenda for 23–24 July 2026** — the meeting scheduled by the primary Federal Register notice **FR Doc. 2026-07361** (which lists the seven agenda peptides, not the removal-of-12 tally). It is **WADA-prohibited at all times** (class S2.3, "Growth Factors and Growth Factor Modulators"). So the "prescribing practice" described here is a real but legally unsettled grey-market/telehealth practice, not an approved standard of care. (Regulatory specifics cross-referenced to the [research report](research-report.md) §4: the Cat-2 removal anchors to FDA's Category-2 page; FR Doc. 2026-07361 is the PCAC July-2026 meeting notice.)
>
> **Molecule caveat carried into every line below.** The retrievable *human* PK is for **IV full-length Tβ4**; the marketed injectable is the **7-residue Ac-LKKTETQ fragment** given SC/IM. Every dose/route convention below therefore crosses **both** the route boundary (SC/IM vs studied IV) and the molecule boundary (fragment vs full-length). These are conventions, not validated doses.

## Prescriber dose/cycle/route conventions (each [N, practitioner_protocol] with name+venue+date)

There is **no human dose-finding trial** for TB-500 (or for the marketed fragment), so every figure here is a *convention* extrapolated from preclinical (rodent) protocols and clinical anecdote, not an established therapeutic dose. Named practitioners and society materials state this caveat themselves.

**The modal practitioner convention (loading → maintenance, twice-weekly → weekly, SC/IM):**

- **Loading phase:** ~**2–5 mg twice weekly** (≈**4–10 mg/week**), most commonly cited as ~4–6 mg/week split into two injections, for **4–6 weeks** [1, practitioner_protocol][4, practitioner_protocol].
- **Maintenance phase:** ~**2 mg (≤2.5 mg) once weekly** (or once every 1–2 weeks) [1, practitioner_protocol][4, practitioner_protocol].
- **Total cycle:** commonly **8–12 weeks** (range 4–12), with a **4–8-week off-period** between cycles [4, practitioner_protocol].
- **Route:** **subcutaneous (SC)** is the modal route (abdominal SC fat, thigh, or upper arm); **intramuscular (IM)** appears as an alternative; intraperitoneal appears **only** in animal protocols and is not a human convention [1, practitioner_protocol][4, practitioner_protocol].
- **Universal caveat stated by every source:** because TB-500 is not FDA-approved, there is **no standardized/validated dose**; any protocol is "individualized and physician-supervised," and the dosing aggregators explicitly note their cycle planning is "community-derived and not validated by a published human RCT of the TB-500 fragment" [4, practitioner_protocol].

## Reconstitution / route notes

Reconstitution/route arithmetic is **admin bookkeeping only** (Tier-4 vendor / aggregator math; cannot ground efficacy or safety). The recurring convention:

- Injectable TB-500 is a **lyophilized powder**, reconstituted with **bacteriostatic water** (sterile water + 0.9% benzyl alcohol), refrigerated **2–8 °C** and used within ~**2–4 weeks** (some sources extend to 30 days); lyophilized powder stored long-term at −20 °C [4, vendor_label][6, vendor_label].

| Vial | + Bac. water | Concentration | 2.5 mg dose | On U-100 |
|------|--------------|---------------|-------------|----------|
| 10 mg | 2.0 mL | 5.0 mg/mL | 0.50 mL | 50 U [4, vendor_label] |
| 10 mg | 3.0 mL | ~3.33 mg/mL | 0.75 mL | ~75 U (≈100 U for 3.33 mg) [4, vendor_label] |

(The research report's §1.4/§9.3 carries the same convention: 10 mg + 2.0 mL → 5 mg/mL, or 3.0 mL → ~3.33 mg/mL, 2–8 °C, ~2–6 weeks — community convention, **not** an authenticated pharmacy data sheet.)

**Route-risk flag (carried, not efficacy):** the retrievable human PK is for **IV full-length Tβ4**; the modal practitioner route is **SC/IM of the fragment**. Parenteral routes also sit in FDA's *higher* immunogenicity-risk bucket than oral. Any SC/IM dose guidance crosses both route and molecule boundaries and is treated as a convention, not a validated parameter [research-report §1, route-extrapolation].
