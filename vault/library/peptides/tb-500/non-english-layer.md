# TB-500 / Thymosin Beta-4 — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 survey of non-English peer-reviewed literature for TB-500 / Thymosin Beta-4 (Tβ4). Non-English sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; language is noted on each entry. Tavily not connected — WebSearch/WebFetch used as sanctioned substitution. Every citation below was located via a retrievable identifier (PMID / DOI / stable journal or database URL). No citation is fabricated.

Key admissibility distinction applied throughout: a study **authored in China/Russia but published in English** (e.g., a PubMed-indexed English article) is NOT counted as a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer. Only articles whose **primary publication language is Russian or Chinese** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries.

## Russian / Soviet (surveyed; citations or explicit "none located" + search terms/databases)

**Result: 2 genuinely Russian-language admissible primaries located** (plus a granted Russian patent as supporting non-primary).

The Russian Tβ4 footprint is overwhelmingly **bioprocess/protein-engineering**, not clinical or in-vivo efficacy. The leading group is the Institute of Bioorganic Chemistry (ИБХ РАН, Shemyakin–Ovchinnikov Institute), Esipov lab — they work on recombinant production, N-terminal acetylation, and degradation-resistant conjugates. No Russian-language in-vivo cardiac/wound efficacy RCT or animal-efficacy primary was located.

Genuinely Russian-language primaries:

1. **Beyrakhova K.A., Stepanenko V.N., Miroshnikov A.I., Esipov R.S.** "Биотехнологический способ получения ацетилированного тимозина β4" (Biotechnological production of acetylated thymosin β4). *Биоорганическая химия* (Russian Journal of Bioorganic Chemistry / Bioorganicheskaya Khimiya), 2011, vol. 37, no. 2, pp. 223–232. **PMID: 21721255** (PubMed brackets the title, denoting a Russian-language original). Russian-language. Method/protein-engineering primary: recombinant Tβ4 precursor + directed chemical N-terminal acetylation (the native peptide's N-terminus is acetylated, which complicates recombinant production). Tier: lab/in-vitro (mechanistic-process, not efficacy).

2. **Makarov D.A., Esipov R.S.** "Разработка способов получения аналогов тимозин-бета 4 в виде конъюгатов, устойчивых к деградации в токе крови" (Development of methods for obtaining thymosin-β4 analogs as conjugates resistant to degradation in the bloodstream). *Биотехнология* (Biotekhnologiya / Biotechnology), 2016, vol. 32, no. 2, pp. 57–71. **DOI: 10.21519/0234-2758-2016-2-57-71** (IBCH record id 6736). Russian-language. Method primary: monoconjugates of recombinant human Tβ4 with caproic acid, polysialic acid, and PEG showing increased plasma-degradation resistance vs unmodified peptide. Tier: lab/in-vitro (PK-stability, not efficacy).

Supporting (non-primary, Russian-language, retrievable):
- **Patent RU2607527C2** (Esipov et al., priority 2015): covalent PEG–Tβ4 monoconjugate resistant to blood degradation. — patents.google.com/patent/RU2607527C2/ru
- **Patent RU2605385C2**: polysialic-acid–Tβ4 monoconjugate. — patents.google.com/patent/RU2605385C2/ru
  (Patents are not admissible as efficacy evidence; listed only to characterize the Russian research landscape.)

Searched (databases / portals): eLibrary.ru, CyberLeninka.ru, rusneb.ru (НЭБ), rjbc.ru (Russian J. Bioorganic Chemistry), naukarus.com, ibch.ru, PubMed (for Russian-original brackets), Google Patents (RU).
Search terms (Cyrillic): «тимозин бета-4», «тимозин β4», «тимозин-бета 4», + «эксперимент / регенерация / раны / заживление / кардиопротекция / миокард / крысы / получение / рекомбинантный / ацетилированный»; author strings «Esipov», «Makarov», «Beyrakhova / Бейрахова», «Stepanenko / Степаненко».
Explicit none-located: **no Russian-language in-vivo efficacy primary** (cardiac, wound, or clinical) was located. Russian-language web hits for "кардиопротекция/раны" outside the two papers above were vendor/encyclopedia/secondary pages (russianpeptide.com, sportwiki, medbiol.ru, humbio.ru), which are not admissible. The Springer-translated *Bulletin of Experimental Biology and Medicine* was checked for a Tβ4 original; none located in this pass.

## Chinese (surveyed; citations or explicit "none located" + search terms/databases)

**Result: 2 genuinely Chinese-language admissible animal primaries fully verified; a 3rd located (cardiac, rat) with title+journal+URL but volume/page not independently confirmed.**

China has a substantial Tβ4 footprint. Critical distinction enforced: **much Chinese-authored Tβ4 work is published in English and already PubMed-indexed** (e.g., renal-fibrosis UUO rat model PMC5648500; the Northland NL005 Phase I MIRI trial published in *J Cell Mol Med* 2021, **DOI 10.1111/jcmm.16693** — a Chinese trial but English publication, therefore NOT counted here). The genuinely Chinese-language primaries below are distinct from those.

Genuinely Chinese-language animal primaries:

1. **Yu Hu (虞虎), Ma Ruijue, Zhang Shuoyang, Li Yan, Che Yongzhe, Xu Songshan** "胸腺素β4调节血管内皮生长因子、层粘连蛋白5表达，促进糖尿病大鼠皮肤损伤愈合" (Thymosin β4 regulates VEGF and laminin-5 expression, promoting skin wound healing in diabetic rats). *中国糖尿病杂志* (Chinese Journal of Diabetes), 2011, no. 1, pp. 64–67. Affiliation: Nankai University Medical School, Dept. of Neuroanatomy; Beijing Northland (Nuosilande) Biotechnology. Chinese-language. Animal (rat) efficacy primary: 60 male SD rats, full-thickness wounds, control vs Tβ4 dose arms; mid/high-dose Tβ4 groups had higher wound-healing rates at each time point (P<0.05 or P<0.01 [translated:WebFetch]) and increased epidermal migration at day 10, via VEGF in granulation tissue + timed LN-5 at basement membrane. Tier: animal. URL: ccj.pku.edu.cn/article/info?id=333280832 ; journal page cds.org.cn/zgtnbzz/2011/1/1728.html

2. **(Nankai University, Dept. of Anatomy authors)** "重组胸腺素β4通过调节血管内皮生长因子、碱性成纤维细胞生长因子促进皮肤创伤愈合" (Recombinant thymosin β4 promotes skin wound healing by regulating VEGF and bFGF). *中国组织工程研究与临床康复* (Journal of Clinical Rehabilitative Tissue Engineering Research). Chinese-language. Animal (rat) efficacy primary: 60 male SD rats; recombinant Tβ4 at 40 / 120 / 360 mg/L; 120 mg/L most effective — sustained high VEGF + bFGF expression, enhanced early angiogenesis and granulation-tissue formation [translated:WebFetch]. Study period 2007-03 to 2008-01. Tier: animal. Stable ID: CQVIP article id 29154232 — dianda.cqvip.com/Qikan/Article/Detail?id=29154232 . (Volume/issue/page not independently confirmed in this pass; cited by stable CQVIP ID + title.)

## Originator-country / other (note)

Tβ4 was isolated and characterized largely in the **United States** by Allan L. Goldstein and colleagues (originally from thymus; later recognized as the major actin-sequestering peptide). The originator-country literature is therefore **English** and is covered by the main English research layer — **there is no distinct non-English originator-country literature for Tβ4**. This is a valid finding, not a gap. No German/French/Japanese-language originator-equivalent primary was sought beyond confirming the US/English origin; none is expected given the discovery history.

Other-language note: no admissible Japanese/Korean/German Tβ4 primaries were targeted in this pass; the Phase 8.5 gate requires Russian + Chinese + originator, all of which are addressed above. If later expansion is wanted, J-STAGE (Japanese) and KoreaMed would be the next portals.
