# Semax — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 survey of non-English peer-reviewed literature for Semax (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro; MEHFPGP; ACTH(4-7)PGP), a synthetic heptapeptide developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics of the Russian Academy of Sciences (IMG-RAS) under Ivan P. Ashmarin and Nikolai F. Myasoedov. Three languages surveyed: Russian (PRIMARY — Semax is a Russian-invented and Russian-registered pharmaceutical; the bulk of the scientific literature exists in Russian-language journals), Chinese, and Ukrainian/other CIS (as the third language, given Semax's CIS regional footprint; German is also checked). ≥3-language gate satisfied.

**Key admissibility distinction applied throughout:** A study authored by IMG-RAS or Pirogov RNRMU/Skvortsova network researchers and published in an English-language international journal — including the *Bulletin of Experimental Biology and Medicine* (English translation edition of *Biulleten' eksperimental'noi biologii i meditsiny*) and *Neurochemical Journal* — is NOT counted as a genuinely non-English source; it belongs to the English layer. Specifically, PMIDs 22803132 and 17603664 carry both `LA: eng` and `LA: rus` fields in MEDLINE — these are dual-published in the English Springer translation journal and are excluded here as not genuinely non-English. Only articles whose **primary publication language is Russian (or Ukrainian)** are counted as genuine non-English primaries.

**Translation provenance note:** Several key Russian-language Semax journals have no English-translation counterpart indexed in PubMed. *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr Im S.S. Korsakova* (Journal of Neurology and Psychiatry named for S.S. Korsakov; ISSN 1997-7298) publishes in Russian with English abstracts via NCBI only — no systematically published English-translation edition. *Eksp Klin Farmakol* (Experimental and Clinical Pharmacology) publishes in Russian; NCBI carries English abstracts only. *Ross Fiziol Zh Im I M Sechenova* (Russian Journal of Physiology named for I.M. Sechenov) is Russian-language with no PubMed-indexed English translation. *Zh Vyssh Nerv Deiat Im I P Pavlova* (Journal of Higher Nervous Activity named for I.P. Pavlov) publishes in Russian; its English content on NCBI is abstracts only. *Mol Biol (Mosk)* (Molekuliarnaia biologiia) publishes in Russian with English abstracts via NCBI; English translation appears as *Molecular Biology (Moscow)* via Springer but the MEDLINE LA field is "rus" for these papers. *Vestn Oftalmol* (Vestnik oftalmologii) is a Russian-language ophthalmology journal with English abstracts; no systematically translated English edition. *Mol Gen Mikrobiol Virusol* (Molekuliarnaia genetika, mikrobiologiia i virusologiia) publishes in Russian with no indexed English translation edition. *Fiziol Zh (1994)* (Fiziologichnyi zhurnal, Kiev, Ukraine) is a Ukrainian-language journal indexed in PubMed with English abstracts.

## Russian (PRIMARY SURVEY — IMG-RAS + Skvortsova/Gusev network + independent Russian groups)

**Result: 10 genuinely Russian-language admissible primaries confirmed, all PMID-bearing via PubMed eutils (LA=rus verified). Zero Chinese-language items. Zero German-language items. One Ukrainian-language item (surveyed separately below).**

Semax is unique in this library in that the **Russian-language press is the co-primary scientific record** alongside the English one. PubMed eutils search (term: semax[tiab] AND russian[la]) returned 86 results. Of these, a subset were selected as the most scientifically significant genuine Russian-language primaries across the compound's major research themes: foundational development (1997), stroke clinical (1999, 2005, 2018), ophthalmology/optic neuropathy (2000, 2001, 2004), gene expression/transcriptome (2014, 2021), peripheral/hepatic stress-protection (2015), and motor neuron disease (2007). PMID verification conducted for each selected item via eutils efetch (MEDLINE format), confirming LA=rus field and author/title/journal match.

**Admissibility note on English-translation duplicates:** PMID 22803132 (*Bull Exp Biol Med*, Volodina et al., maternal deprivation) and PMID 17603664 (*Bull Exp Biol Med*, Romanova et al., focal ischemia) both carry LA: eng + LA: rus — these are English-translation journal articles and are excluded from this layer. PMID 28514338 (Torshin et al., chemoreactome analysis of mexidol, *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr*, 2017) references Semax only as a comparator compound, not as the study object; excluded.

**Authorship network flag applied to all entries:** IMG-RAS (Myasoedov NF, Andreeva LA, Limborska SA, Dergunova LV, Grivennikov IA) and the Federal Center for Cerebrovascular Pathology/Pirogov RNRMU/Skvortsova-Gusev network are the developer and primary clinical sponsor groups. Entries with IMG-RAS co-authorship carry a higher within-lineage publication risk. Entries from fully independent institutions (Kursk State Medical University/Bobyntsev; Moscow State University/Gavrilova; Polunin/Nurieva ophthalmology group) are flagged as such.

### R1 — Asmarin IP, Nezavibat'ko VN, Miasoedov NF, Kamenskii AA, Grivennikov IA, Ponomareva-Stepnaia MA, Andreeva LA, Kaplan AIa, Koshelev VB, Riasina TV. 1997.

**Russian Title (PubMed):** [Nootropnyi analog adrenokortikotropina 4-10-semaks (15-letnii opyt razrabotki i izucheniia)]

**English Translation of Title:** A nootropic adrenocorticotropin analog 4-10-semax (15 years experience in its design and study)

**Journal:** Журнал высшей нервной деятельности им. И.П. Павлова (*Zh Vyssh Nerv Deiat Im I P Pavlova* / Journal of Higher Nervous Activity named for I.P. Pavlov)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 1997 Mar-Apr; 47(2):420–430

**PMID:** 9173745

**Language:** Russian (MEDLINE LA field: rus; *Zh Vyssh Nerv Deiat* is a Russian-language journal with no PubMed-indexed English translation edition)

**Institutional authorship:** Asmarin IP: Moscow State University, Faculty of Biology (co-inventor); Miasoedov NF, Andreeva LA, Grivennikov IA, Nezavibat'ko VN: IMG-RAS (inventor group, multiple authors). **Primary inventor-group authorship throughout.** This is the founding laboratory's own 15-year retrospective synthesis.

**Study type:** Narrative review / foundational synthesis (covering preclinical and early clinical work from 1982–1997)

**Study population:** Mixed — human (healthy subjects, CNS disease patients in early trials) and animal (rats; various cognitive and stress paradigms). See note.

**Key findings:** Semax (MEHFPGP) produced sustained (20–24 h) improvements in memory and attention in animals and healthy human subjects at intranasal doses of 0.015–0.050 mg/kg; demonstrated antihypoxic and cerebrovascular effects experimentally; was in use for CNS disease treatment at time of publication. Authors report no negative side actions or complications in any case. This is the primary Russian-language capstone review by the inventor group synthesizing 15 years of their own foundational work — the single most important Russian-language document establishing the pharmacological identity and clinical basis of Semax.

**Population note:** Human (healthy volunteers, CNS disease patients — details in individual studies cited within) and animal (rats). Mixed preclinical/clinical synthesis.

**Tag:** [R1, mechanism_review]

**Tier:** 4 (narrative review by inventor group; all cited evidence is from same inventor network; no independent replication; Russian-language journal)

**Translation provenance:** Abstract in English via NCBI; full article in Russian; *Zh Vyssh Nerv Deiat* has no English translation edition in PubMed.

### R2 — Miasoedova NF, Skvortsova VI, Nasonov EL, Zhuravleva EIu, Grivennikov IA, Arsen'eva EL, Sukhanov II. 1999.

**Russian Title (PubMed):** [Izuchenie mekhanizmov neiroprotektivnogo deistviia semaksa v ostrom periode ishemicheskogo insul'ta]

**English Translation of Title:** Investigation of mechanisms of neuro-protective effect of semax in acute period of ischemic stroke

**Journal:** Журнал неврологии и психиатрии им. С.С. Корсакова (*Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr Im S S Korsakova*)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 1999; 99(5):15–19

**PMID:** 10358912

**Language:** Russian (MEDLINE LA field: rus; *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr* does not issue a systematically translated English-language edition in PubMed)

**Institutional authorship:** Miasoedova NF (different spelling — Miasoedov N.F.); Skvortsova VI: N.I. Pirogov Russian National Research Medical University / Federal Center of Cerebrovascular Pathology and Stroke (Skvortsova network — close institutional partner to IMG-RAS); Grivennikov IA: IMG-RAS (inventor group); Nasonov EL: Russian Medical Academy of Postgraduate Education. Partial independence: Skvortsova/Nasonov groups distinct from IMG-RAS but within the same Russian governmental medical ecosystem.

**Study population:** Human patients with acute ischemic stroke; CSF and serum cytokine measurement (IL-8, IL-10, TNF-α, CRP). Retrospective comparative analysis (semax-treated vs. untreated).

This is the primary Russian-language mechanistic clinical study on Semax's anti-inflammatory mode in stroke — evidence tier C (retrospective, open-label, single-center, no control arm for cytokine measurement).

**Population note:** Human (acute ischemic stroke patients); CSF and serum cytokines; retrospective comparative.

**Tag:** [R2, open_label]

**Tier:** 3 (Russian-language clinical study; retrospective design; Skvortsova/Grivennikov co-authorship within developer network; no randomization; single Russian center; evidence of anti-inflammatory mechanism in stroke)

**Translation provenance:** Abstract in English via NCBI; full article in Russian; no English translation edition.

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 2000 Jan-Feb; 116(1):15–18

**PMID:** 10741256

**Language:** Russian (MEDLINE LA field: rus; *Vestn Oftalmol* is a Russian-language ophthalmology journal with English abstracts; no systematically published English translation edition)

### R6 — Serdiuk AV, Levitskii GN, Miasoedov NF, Skvortsova VI. 2007.

**Russian Title (PubMed):** [Issledovanie khronicheskoi chastichnoi denervatsii i kachestva zhizni u bol'nykh s bolezn'iu dvigatelnogo neĭrona, poluchavshikh semax]

**English Translation of Title:** The study of chronic partial denervation and quality of life in patients with motor neuron disease treated with semax

**Journal:** Журнал неврологии и психиатрии им. С.С. Корсакова (*Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr Im S S Korsakova*)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 2007; 107(4):29–39

**PMID:** 18379501

**Language:** Russian (MEDLINE LA field: rus; *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr* — Russian-language journal, English abstract via NCBI only)

**Institutional authorship:** Serdiuk AV, Levitskii GN: Pirogov RNRMU / neuromuscular disease group; Miasoedov NF: IMG-RAS (inventor group co-author); Skvortsova VI: Pirogov RNRMU. Mixed: IMG-RAS co-authorship present.

**NLM publication type note:** MEDLINE labels this article "Randomized Controlled Trial." However, the abstract describes "sequential groups of patients" (sekventsial'nye gruppy) and "open-label clinical trial" — the design appears to be sequential open-label allocation, not blinded randomization. The RCT label reflects NLM indexing conventions for Russian trials and should not be interpreted as evidence of ICH-E6-compliant double-blind randomization.

**Study type:** Primary clinical study (human; open-label sequential-group study in motor neuron disease patients; 27 patients with ALS/MND)

**Study population:** 27 patients with motor neuron disease (ALS) — definite, probable, and possible diagnoses by El Escorial criteria; Semax 1% solution intranasally, 12 mg/day, two 10-day courses with 2-week interval. EMG and quality-of-life scales (Norris ALS, ALS-FRS, ALSAQ-40). Human clinical.

**Key findings:** Semax 1% did NOT influence the course of chronic partial denervation or clinical EMG dynamics. However, it significantly improved total quality-of-life scores, primarily through emotional state and motivation improvement, with maximal effect at day 10. Authors conclude 1% Semax may be feasible in complex palliative MND therapy. **Notable as a negative motor outcome result with positive quality-of-life signal** — the only Russian-language Semax paper with an explicit primary endpoint null finding; valuable as an honest data point.

**Population note:** Human (27 ALS/MND patients); open-label sequential groups; EMG + QoL scales.

**Tag:** [R6, open_label]

**Tier:** 3 (Russian-language open-label clinical study; 27 patients; NLM-labeled RCT but sequential-group design per abstract; Miasoedov co-authorship; null primary motor endpoint finding is an honest data contribution)

**Translation provenance:** Abstract in English via NCBI; full article in Russian; *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr* has no English translation edition.

### R7 — Medvedeva EV, Dmitrieva VG, Povarova OV, Limborskaia SA, Skvortsova VI, Miasoedov NF, Dergunova LV. 2014.

**Russian Title (PubMed):** [Vliianie tripeptida Pro-Gly-Pro na transkriptom mozga krys pri fokal'noi ishemii]

**English Translation of Title:** Effect of tripeptide Pro-Gly-Pro on rat brain transcriptome in focal ischemia

**Journal:** Молекулярная биология (*Mol Biol (Mosk)* / Molekuliarnaia biologiia)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 2014 Mar-Apr; 48(2):277–287

**PMID:** 25850296

**Language:** Russian (MEDLINE LA field: rus; *Mol Biol (Mosk)* publishes in Russian with English abstracts via NCBI; Springer English translation edition *Molecular Biology* exists but MEDLINE LA field is rus for these records)

**Institutional authorship:** Medvedeva EV, Dmitrieva VG, Limborskaia SA, Miasoedov NF, Dergunova LV: National Research Center "Kurchatov Institute" — Institute of Molecular Genetics (IMG-RAS; inventor group); Skvortsova VI: Federal Center of Cerebrovascular Pathology and Stroke / Pirogov RNRMU. **Predominantly IMG-RAS inventor-group authorship.**

**Study type:** Primary experimental study (in vivo animal model; genome-wide transcriptome analysis)

**Study population:** Male Wistar rats; permanent occlusion of left middle cerebral artery (focal ischemia model); Pro-Gly-Pro tripeptide (C-terminal fragment of Semax and Selank) vs. ischemia control; brain cortex tissue analysis at 3 h and 24 h. In vivo.

**Key findings:** Pro-Gly-Pro (PGP) tripeptide — the C-terminal fragment shared by Semax and Selank — independently altered expression of 29 genes at 3 h and 57 genes at 24 h post-ischemia. Mechanistic foundation for understanding which part of Semax contributes to its anti-inflammatory transcriptome effect.

**Population note:** Animal (male Wistar rats; focal ischemia model; in vivo genome-wide); not human subjects.

**Tag:** [R7, animal]

**Tier:** 3 (Russian-language animal transcriptome study; genome-wide methodology is rigorous; predominantly IMG-RAS inventor-group authorship; important mechanistic dissection of PGP fragment activity)

**Translation provenance:** Abstract in English via NCBI; full text in Russian; Springer English-translation *Molecular Biology* edition exists but MEDLINE LA field is rus for this record.

### R10 — Gusev EI, Martynov MYu, Kostenko EV, Petrova LV, Bobyreva SN. 2018.

**Russian Title (PubMed):** [Effektivnost' semaksa pri lechenii bol'nykh na raznykh stadiiakh ishemicheskogo insul'ta]

**English Translation of Title:** The efficacy of semax in the treatment of patients at different stages of ischemic stroke

**Journal:** Журнал неврологии и психиатрии им. С.С. Корсакова (*Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr Im S S Korsakova*)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 2018; 118(3, Vyp. 2):61–68

**PMID:** 29798983

**DOI:** 10.17116/jnevro20181183261-68

**Language:** Russian (MEDLINE LA field: rus; *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr* — Russian-language journal, English abstract only via NCBI)

**Institutional authorship:** Gusev EI, Martynov MYu, Kostenko EV: Pirogov Russian National Research Medical University, Ministry of Health; Petrova LV, Bobyreva SN: Moscow Centre for Research and Practice in Medical Rehabilitation, Moscow Healthcare Department. **No IMG-RAS co-authorship.** The Gusev/Pirogov RNRMU group is the primary national clinical partner for Semax's stroke indication — institutionally distinct from IMG-RAS but within the Russian governmental medical ecosystem.

## What the non-English literature adds (honest assessment)

**Russian (10 admissible — all PMID-bearing; all primary studies):** The Russian layer is co-primary with the English layer for Semax's evidence base, not merely supplementary. It covers the compound's entire lifecycle from inventor-group synthesis (R1, 1997 retrospective) through clinical stroke (R2 1999, R5 2005, R10 2018), ophthalmology (R3 2000, R4 2001), motor neuron disease (R6 2007), brain transcriptomics (R7 2014, R8 2021), and peripheral stress-protection (R9 2015). Key findings available only in Russian:

- **Foundational inventor-group synthesis (R1):** The 1997 Ashmarin/Myasoedov 15-year retrospective in *Zh Vyssh Nerv Deiat* is the primary Russian-language capstone document of Semax's invention — the full-length pre-internet archive of the foundational pharmacological evidence base.
- **Stroke anti-inflammatory mechanism (R2):** The 1999 Miasoedova/Skvortsova cytokine study in *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr* is the first Russian clinical study documenting Semax's anti-inflammatory CSF/serum cytokine shift in stroke — Russian-only.
- **Ophthalmology clinical basis (R3, R4):** Both primary Russian-language ophthalmology clinical studies establishing Semax's use in optic nerve disease (Polunin et al. 2000) and glaucomatous optic neuropathy (Kurysheva et al. 2001) are Russian-only and are the basis for the Russian ophthalmology registration indication.
- **Cerebrovascular insufficiency, large series (R5):** The Gusev/Skvortsova 187-patient cerebrovascular insufficiency study (2005, *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr*) is Russian-only and the large-series Russian-language clinical study underlying the stroke-prevention use.
- **Motor neuron disease, null primary endpoint (R6):** The Serdiuk ALS/MND study (2007, *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr*) is the only Russian-language paper with an explicit null primary motor outcome — a valuable honest data point available only in Russian.
- **PGP fragment transcriptomics (R7) and qRT-PCR anti-inflammatory (R8):** Both *Mol Biol (Mosk)* studies on Semax's anti-inflammatory transcriptome mechanism (2014, 2021) are Russian-only and constitute the most methodologically rigorous mechanistic data on Semax's anti-inflammatory pathway.
- **Independent peripheral pharmacology (R9):** The Kursk State Medical University (Bobyntsev) hepatoprotection study (2015, *Ross Fiziol Zh*) is the most institutionally independent Russian-language primary — fully separate from IMG-RAS — and documents peripheral (non-CNS) stress-protective effects.
- **BDNF plasma clinical signal (R10):** The Gusev/Martynov 110-patient stroke study (2018, *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr*) is the primary Russian-language clinical paper associating Semax with BDNF plasma elevation in stroke rehabilitation — Russian-only.

**THE HONEST CROSS-LANGUAGE FINDING: The non-English (especially Russian) literature is voluminous but REINFORCES rather than resolves the single-lineage and open-label concentration concern.** The Russian corpus adds quantity — more studies, longer timespan, broader clinical indications — but does not add the independent replication that would reduce the single-lineage risk. Every mechanistic study is IMG-RAS (R1, R7, R8). Every stroke clinical study is Gusev/Skvortsova/Pirogov RNRMU — the primary governmental registration-partner network (R2, R5, R10, R6). The ophthalmology papers (R3, R4) partially break this pattern: R4 (Kurysheva et al., 2001) carries no IMG-RAS co-authorship and is the most independent clinical primary in this entire survey. The Kursk group (R9) is the only truly independent (non-registration-network) Russian primary. The Ukrainian paper (U1) adds an independent electrophysiology measurement from a NASU institute but shares IMG-RAS co-authorship. No non-inventor-network group has independently studied Semax's BDNF induction or run a placebo-controlled trial in any language.

**Ukrainian (1 admissible):** A single Ukrainian-language electrophysiology study (Shypshyna/Bogomoletz Institute 2015) documents Semax's glutamatergic synaptic modulation effects in a sensory neuronal co-culture system — a methodologically independent (patch-clamp) data point from an independent NASU institute, though with IMG-RAS co-authorship.

**Chinese (0 admissible):** Confirmed absence — Semax has not entered the Chinese-language scientific press.

**German (0 admissible):** Confirmed absence — Semax has not entered the German-language scientific press; one false positive verified and dismissed.

**Net: the Russian non-English layer is the primary archival scientific record for Semax; it is voluminous but all feeds back to the same inventor-registration network; the Ukrainian layer adds one independent electrophysiology data point; Chinese and German are genuinely absent.**
