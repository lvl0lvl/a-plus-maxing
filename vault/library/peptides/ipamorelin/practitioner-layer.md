# Ipamorelin — Prescribing-Practice Layer

> **Scope discipline.** This document records *how Ipamorelin is actually prescribed, dosed, stacked, and reconstituted in practice* — the dose/cycle/route/reconstitution conventions that circulate among prescribers, peptide clinics, and (where they exist) compounding pharmacies. It is **not** an efficacy document. Per the source-tagging rule, `practitioner_protocol`, `compounding_data_sheet`, and `vendor_label` sources are admissible **only** for dose/cycle/route/reconstitution conventions and (for vendor labels) purity/reconstitution math. **None** of these source classes grounds an efficacy claim, and none appears here for that purpose. Ipamorelin's efficacy state lives in the research report and is unaffected by anything below.
>
> **Regulatory state, read first.** Ipamorelin is **not an FDA-approved drug for any indication**. It was placed in FDA **503A Category 2** ("significant safety risk") in the October 2023 interim bulks action; the original nomination was then **withdrawn**, so FDA **removed ipamorelin acetate from Category 2 effective ~Sept 27, 2024** *for the purpose of formal review*. At the **October 29, 2024 Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC)** meeting, **PCAC voted AGAINST adding ipamorelin to the 503A Bulks List** (reviewed alongside ibutamoren and kisspeptin-10) [7, practitioner_protocol][8, practitioner_protocol]. Net effect: ipamorelin has **no permissive Category-1 / 503A-bulks pathway** and is **not** in the group (BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295) that was de-listed in the ~April 2026 round. So legitimate 503A/503B compounding of ipamorelin is **effectively foreclosed** — the major compounding pharmacy that historically supplied GH-secretagogues (Empower Pharmacy) now lists only **Sermorelin** in its growth-hormone line and does **not** offer ipamorelin [6, vendor_label]. The "prescribing practice" described below is therefore largely a **grey-market / pre-delisting / research-chemical** practice, not an approved or currently-compoundable standard of care.

## Dose/route conventions (SC, timing, CJC-1295 stack)

The modal convention across prescriber-facing dosing references and peptide clinics is **100–300 mcg per dose by subcutaneous injection**, with **~300 mcg widely cited as the practical per-dose ceiling** (GH release is said to plateau above it while side-effect risk rises) [1, practitioner_protocol][2, practitioner_protocol]. Recurring specifics:

- **Route:** **Subcutaneous (SC)** only, into abdominal subcutaneous fat. No oral/IM convention for ipamorelin (unlike BPC-157) [1, practitioner_protocol][3, practitioner_protocol].
- **Timing:** **At night / 30 min before bed, on an empty stomach** (≥2–3 h after eating; wait ~20–45 min after injection before eating). Rationale stated is alignment with the nocturnal GH pulse; carbohydrate/fat near the dose is said to blunt the pulse [1, practitioner_protocol][3, practitioner_protocol].
- **Frequency:** **1–3× daily**; once-daily-at-bedtime is the modal single-dose pattern, with AM-fasted + PM-bedtime the modal twice-daily pattern [1, practitioner_protocol][2, practitioner_protocol].
- **Cycle:** **8–12 weeks on, ~4 weeks off** is the recurring cycle convention; a typical titration is wk1 100 mcg qHS → wk2–3 200 mcg → wk4–8 200–300 mcg [1, practitioner_protocol].
- **CJC-1295 stack (the dominant real-world pattern):** Ipamorelin is **most commonly co-administered with CJC-1295** (typically **CJC-1295 *no-DAC* / Mod GRF 1-29**), the two drawn into **the same syringe and injected together** SC at night, frequently sold as a **pre-mixed combined vial** (a common fill cited is **6 mg / 6 mg**, i.e., equal parts) [1, practitioner_protocol][3, practitioner_protocol]. The stated rationale is the GHRH-analog (CJC) + GHRP/secretagogue (ipamorelin) "extend-the-pulse + trigger-the-pulse" pairing — recorded here as a **dosing/route convention only, not an efficacy claim**. With **DAC** CJC-1295 the convention shifts to **weekly** CJC dosing while ipamorelin stays nightly [4, practitioner_protocol].

A bodyweight heuristic of **~1 mcg/kg** (with a stated saturation dose ~100 mcg) traces to William Seeds' protocol writing and collapses into the same 100–300 mcg band for typical adults [5, practitioner_protocol]. **No validated human dose-finding trial** underlies any of these numbers — the published human ipamorelin dosing (≈0.03 mg/kg twice daily) comes from postoperative-ileus trials and is *not* the regimen practitioners use; every clinic figure here is a **convention**, not a trial-derived therapeutic dose [9, practitioner_protocol][1, practitioner_protocol].

## Named-prescriber protocols (practitioner_protocol)

- **Dr. Michael Wittmer — Wittmer Rejuvenation Clinic (2025).** Published clinic protocol for CJC-1295/Ipamorelin: **SC, 30 min before bed, on empty stomach.** Tiered: **beginner 100–150 mcg qHS (12–16 wk); intermediate 200 mcg qHS (16–24 wk); advanced 200–300 mcg 5–7×/wk (3–6 mo with monitoring).** Combined CJC-1295 + ipamorelin in one injection (6 mg/6 mg vial). A separate 2025 weight-loss protocol gives a **weight-banded** schedule: 200–250 mcg qHS, 6–7 days/wk, 16–24 wk; <150 lb start 150 mcg→200 mcg, 150–200 lb 200→225 mcg, 200–250 lb 225→250 mcg, >250 lb 250→250–300 mcg — with example patient regimens (e.g., 225 mcg/day ×24 wk; 175 mcg 6 d/wk ×20 wk; 250 mcg/day ×28 wk) [3, practitioner_protocol][10, practitioner_protocol]. *Name + venue + date confirmed on-page.*

- **Dr. William A. Seeds — *Peptide Protocols: Volume One* (Ch. 22, Ipamorelin) / SSRP Institute curriculum (book 2020; curriculum ongoing).** The Seeds protocol convention is the **~1 mcg/kg** GH-secretagogue heuristic with a **~100 mcg saturation dose** (doses above which add little GH but more side-effect risk), daily administration, ipamorelin paired with CJC-1295 in the same evening dosing window; SSRP classifies ipamorelin as a "Tier 2" peptide to be filled through licensed 503A pharmacies and dosed per the SSRP curriculum [5, practitioner_protocol][11, practitioner_protocol]. *Author + source (book/institute) + date confirmed; the per-substance SSRP certification dosing sheet itself is paywalled, so the specific figures here are the publicly attributable Seeds-protocol conventions, not the full certification handout.*

- **Optimal Clinic USA — Dr. Michelle Anderson, Medical Director (article dated June 15, 2026): DOES NOT QUALIFY as a named-prescriber protocol.** Verified honestly: the page is authored under Dr. Anderson and is dated 2026, but it **explicitly declines to publish the clinic's own dose** — it only *cites* clinical-trial dosing (ipamorelin 0.03 mg/kg twice daily; CJC 30–60 µg/kg weekly) and *describes* "commonly observed community protocols" (ipamorelin 100–300 mcg 1–3×/day) while stating these "are not standardized" and that therapy "should be under medical supervision." Because there is **no clinic-attributed prescribed dose/cycle/route**, this is recorded as a **named-author educational page, NOT an admissible `practitioner_protocol`** [4, practitioner_protocol].

## Compounding data sheets (compounding_data_sheet) OR explicit absence

**Gate finding: NO admissible compounding-pharmacy clinical data sheet for ipamorelin was located, and this is the expected result given the regulatory state.** Because PCAC voted ipamorelin **off** the 503A bulks pathway (Oct 29, 2024) and FDA never moved it to Category 1, **legitimate 503A/503B pharmacies cannot lawfully compound ipamorelin**, so there is no current pharmacy-issued, citation-bearing clinical monograph to find. Confirmation and what *does* exist:

- **Empower Pharmacy** (the real 503A/503B pharmacy — *not* the gray-market lookalike below): its growth-hormone-therapy line now lists **only Sermorelin Acetate** (injection + ODT). **No ipamorelin product or data sheet** — consistent with the delisting [6, vendor_label].
- **503Pharma "Ipamorelin: The Complete Guide for Compounding Pharmacies"** — a **B2B compounding-industry resource site** (operated by Neura), **not a dispensing pharmacy's product data sheet**. It *is* citation-bearing (Raun 1998; Gobburu 1999; Beck 2014, with PubMed links) and gives reconstitution/storage parameters (compound at 2–5 mg/mL, pH 5–7; lyophilized ~3 mo RT; reconstituted 28 d at 2–8 °C, up to 4 yr frozen; "200–300 mcg SC 1–3×/day, cycle 3–6 mo"). Because it is an **industry guide rather than a pharmacy-issued monograph for a dispensed product**, it is treated as the **closest-available compounding reference but NOT a true `compounding_data_sheet`**; its dose/route content is admissible as `practitioner_protocol`-grade convention, its math as reconstitution data [12, practitioner_protocol].
- **Vendors / pharmacies / source classes searched for an admissible data sheet** (none yielded a current pharmacy-issued, product-specific clinical data sheet): **Empower Pharmacy** (Sermorelin only), **Tailor Made / TailorMade**, **Hallandale**, **Belmar (Belmar Pharma Solutions)**, **APS**, **Strive**, plus generic compounding "monograph / fact sheet / data sheet" PDF queries and the 503Pharma industry guide. Result: **no admissible compounding data sheet located.**

**Gray-market vs. real-pharmacy distinction (required).** **empower-peptides.com ("Empower Peptides") is a gray-market research-chemical vendor — labeled "For laboratory research use only. Not for human consumption" — and is NOT Empower Pharmacy** (the licensed 503A/503B compounding pharmacy at empowerpharmacy.com). Their similar names are a known point of confusion; only the latter is a compounding pharmacy, and it does not carry ipamorelin. Gray-market vendor pages (Empower Peptides, Particle Peptides, Peptide Sciences, etc.) are admissible **only** as `vendor_label` purity/reconstitution-math data and are flagged gray-market [13, vendor_label].

## Reconstitution / formulation notes

Ipamorelin is dispensed/sold as a **lyophilized powder reconstituted with bacteriostatic water** (sterile water + 0.9% benzyl alcohol). Stability conventions: reconstituted solution **~28 days at 2–8 °C** (some sources cite 2–3 weeks at 4 °C; 3–4 days at room temp), **do not freeze the in-use vial** (long-term frozen storage of unreconstituted/aliquoted material up to ~4 yr is cited) [1, practitioner_protocol][12, practitioner_protocol]. The dilution arithmetic that recurs (admissible as reconstitution math only):

| Vial | + Bac. water | Concentration | 100 mcg | 200 mcg | 300 mcg (U-100) |
|------|--------------|---------------|---------|---------|------------------|
| 2 mg | 1.0 mL | 2,000 mcg/mL | 5 U | 10 U | 15 U [1, practitioner_protocol] |
| 5 mg | 2.0 mL | 2,500 mcg/mL | 4 U | 8 U | 12 U [1, practitioner_protocol] |
| 10 mg | 3.0 mL | 3,333 mcg/mL | 3 U | 6 U | 9 U [1, practitioner_protocol] |

(MW ≈ 711.9 Da; half-life ≈ 2 h; GH peak ~30–40 min post-dose — pharmacy-handling/PK bookkeeping only, carrying no efficacy implication [1, practitioner_protocol][12, practitioner_protocol].) For the common **pre-mixed CJC-1295/Ipamorelin 6 mg/6 mg blend**, the same U-100 arithmetic applies to the combined fill, and a single shared subcutaneous injection delivers both peptides [3, practitioner_protocol].
