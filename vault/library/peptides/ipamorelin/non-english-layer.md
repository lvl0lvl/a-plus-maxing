# Ipamorelin — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: survey of non-English peer-reviewed / academic literature on ipamorelin
(INN; NNC 26-0161; pentapeptide Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2; selective GH
secretagogue / ghrelin-receptor [GHS-R1a] agonist). Required survey languages for
the 8.5 gate: Russian, Chinese, originator-country (Denmark). Admissibility follows
the same tier rules as the English layer; non-English peer-reviewed records are
admissible with language noted. Machine-translated numerics are tagged
[translated:<tool>].

Search dates: 2026-06-19 / 2026-06-20. Tools: WebSearch + WebFetch (Tavily not
connected — sanctioned for this run).

---

## Russian / Soviet (citations or explicit none-located + search terms/databases)

RESULT: 2 genuine Russian-language academic sources located. Both are review/secondary
articles in which ipamorelin (ипаморелин) is discussed within the broader GH-secretagogue
/ ghrelin-mimetic class — there is **no located Russian-language primary trial or
original pharmacology study with ipamorelin as the principal compound**. No Soviet-era
literature located (ipamorelin was synthesized in the 1990s, post-USSR).

Verified sources:

1. **Брылёв М.И., Козин С.В., Королёв В.Л., Лоторев Д.С., Павлова Л.А., Алексеев А.А.
   (2013).** «Пептиды, повышающие секрецию соматотропного гормона, и их влияние на
   сердечно-сосудистую систему» [Peptides increasing somatotropin secretion and their
   effect on the cardiovascular system]. *Ученые записки. Электронный научный журнал
   Курского государственного университета*, 2(27), art. 3. (Russian; open access via
   CyberLeninka.) — Verified by WebFetch. States ipamorelin is a GHRP-class pentapeptide
   that fully mimics ghrelin, with greater GH-stimulating capacity than other GHRP
   variants, and does NOT cause strong hunger or elevated prolactin/cortisol even at doses
   >200-fold over therapeutic; notes synergy when combined with sermorelin and CJC-1295.
   Tier: secondary/review (narrative).

2. **Приходько В.А., Оковитый С.В. (2025).** «Препараты L-орнитина как модуляторы
   соматотропной оси для коррекции саркопении при хронических болезнях печени»
   [L-Ornithine salts as somatotropic-axis modulators for correction of sarcopenia in
   chronic liver disease]. *Медицинский совет* [Meditsinskiy Sovet], 19(8), 80–93.
   DOI: 10.21518/ms2025-223. (Russian, with English translation provided by journal.) —
   Verified by WebFetch. Mentions ipamorelin only in passing: «Разработка нескольких
   других пептидных миметиков грелина (эксаморелин, табиморелин, ипаморелин, реламорелин)
   была прекращена, во многих случаях из-за неудовлетворительного профиля безопасности»
   (development of several ghrelin-mimetic peptides incl. ipamorelin was discontinued,
   often due to unsatisfactory safety profile). Tier: secondary/review.

Databases / search terms used: CyberLeninka (cyberleninka.ru), eLibrary.ru. Terms:
"ипаморелин", "ipamorelin гормон роста секретагог", "грелин миметик / секреция
соматотропина", "ghrelin секретагоги соматотропин обзор саркопения". The eLibrary.ru
direct queries surfaced no additional admissible ipamorelin-centric record beyond what
CyberLeninka indexed. Numerous Russian results were commercial peptide-vendor / clinic
pages (refresh.doctor, russianpeptide.com, elixirpeptide.com, sportguardian.ru,
nanogens.ru, etc.) — EXCLUDED as non-peer-reviewed marketing.

## Chinese (citations or explicit none-located + search terms/databases)

RESULT: 1 genuine Chinese-LANGUAGE peer-reviewed journal article located in which
ipamorelin (伊帕瑞林) is named. No Chinese-language **primary** ipamorelin trial located;
the admissible record is a Chinese-language review discussing GH-releasing peptides
(GHRPs) in bone development, citing ipamorelin among them.

Verified source:

1. **叶楠, 黄健, 王丹 (Ye N., Huang J., Wang D.) (2020).** 《骨骼生长发育中生长激素释放肽的重要作用》
   [The important role of growth-hormone-releasing peptides in skeletal growth and
   development]. *中国组织工程研究* [Chinese Journal of Tissue Engineering Research],
   24(20), 3226–3233. DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.2095-4344.2540. (Chinese-language review.) —
   Citation confirmed via WebSearch of journal index + CrossRef DOI; abstract page
   (cjter.com/CN/abstract/abstract15668.shtml) returned HTTP 502 on direct fetch (server
   error, not paywall) so full-text content was NOT independently rendered. Search-engine
   indexed abstract content states GHRPs including hexarelin and ipamorelin (伊帕瑞林) can
   induce increases in cortical and total bone mass similar to GH, and that GHRPs regulate
   osteoblast differentiation, induce BMSC proliferation/differentiation, and coordinate
   bone metabolism with energy metabolism via leptin. CAVEAT: the ipamorelin-specific
   sentence is from machine-indexed/translated abstract snippets, not a verified rendering
   of the article body. Note: 中国组织工程研究 is a bilingual journal with an English
   companion edition; THIS record (Chinese title + Chinese authors-of-record + .../CN/...
   path) is the Chinese-language version. Tier: secondary/review.

Databases / search terms used: CNKI (cnki.net), Wanfang (wanfangdata.com.cn / d.wanfangdata),
publisher portals (pps.cpu.edu.cn = 药学进展/Prog Pharm Sci; cjter.com), Google Patents (zh).
Terms: "ipamorelin 生长激素 促分泌剂", "伊帕瑞林 生长激素 促泌素 研究", "伊帕瑞林 多肽 临床试验",
"伊帕瑞林 生长激素释放肽 综述", "ipamorelin 生长激素 site:d.wanfangdata.com.cn", "ipamorelin
临床 site:cnki.net OR site:wanfangdata.com.cn".

Confirmed Chinese name: 伊帕瑞林 (CAS 170851-70-4).

  (binary stream) — cannot confirm ipamorelin content; NOT cited.

## Danish / originator-country / other (note)

RESULT: none-located in Danish (valid "none"). Ipamorelin was discovered/developed by
**Novo Nordisk A/S, Bagsværd, Denmark** (1990s), but the originator's foundational
literature was published in ENGLISH in international peer-reviewed journals, not in
Danish. This is the expected outcome for a Danish-originated pharmaceutical and counts as
a valid "none located" for Danish-language literature.

Representative English-published originator work (covered in the main English layer, listed
here only to document the originator trail — NOT counted toward the non-English source tally):
- Raun K., et al. (1998). "Ipamorelin, the first selective growth hormone secretagogue."
  *Eur J Endocrinol* 139(5):552–561. PMID 9849822. (Novo Nordisk authors; English.)
- Johansen P.B., et al. (1999). "Ipamorelin, a new growth-hormone-releasing peptide,
  induces longitudinal bone growth in rats." *Growth Horm IGF Res* 9(2):106–113.
  PMID 10373343. (English.)
- Novo Nordisk clinical development under code NNC 26-0161 / later NNC126-0083; e.g.
  ClinicalTrials.gov NCT00715689 (GH-deficient adults) — English-registered.

Danish-language databases (e.g., den.dk / Danish university repositories) and the search
term "ipamorelin dansk væksthormon studie Novo Nordisk" surfaced only the English
international literature plus Norditropin (a different Novo product) pharmacy pages — no
Danish-language ipamorelin scientific article located. None-located is therefore valid.

No other-language (e.g., Japanese, German, Spanish) ipamorelin-centric peer-reviewed
record was located within this survey's scope.
