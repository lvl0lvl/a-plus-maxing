# Phase 6 Critique — Ipamorelin

## Verdict

verdict: PASS (no CRITICAL gaps)

The four load-bearing dimensions the brief demands — honesty on selectivity, plain statement of the Phase 2 ileus failure + discontinuation, "no human efficacy" clarity, and regulatory accuracy — are all handled correctly and, in several places, exemplarily. The report's substantive content is sound, non-promotional, and well balanced. The single real defect is a pervasive citation-numbering inconsistency between the inline body references and the "canonical" unified bibliography; it is *major* (it degrades direct traceability) but **not critical**, because (a) the crosswalk table makes every claim resolvable, (b) the unified bibliography itself is complete and Wikipedia-free, and (c) no honesty, balance, or factual claim is actually wrong. No additional retrievals required.

## Findings

1. **citation-incomplete / major** — The report declares the unified bibliography ([1]–[31], §"Bibliography") "the **canonical** numbering," but the detailed body sections (§1–§9) use **section-local** citation numbers that collide with the unified scheme, while §0 (Evidence Map) and parts of §4.5 use the **unified** numbers — the two schemes are mixed without warning at point of use. Concrete collisions: Gobburu 1999 (the keystone human PK study, unified [4]) is cited inline as `[2]` in §2.4/§2.5/§4.1 (lines 134, 138, 198) and as `[4]` in §1.3 — yet unified [2] is Ankersen 1998, a med-chem SAR paper. The "intermittent dosing did NOT raise IGF-1" rat finding (Johansen 1999, unified [10]) is cited as `[3]` in §2.5/§3.1/Bottom-Line (lines 138, 156, 374), but unified [3] is PubChem. The Svensson 2000 bone-BMC study (unified [11]) is cited as `[2]` in §3.2 and the Bottom Line (lines 162–164, 373). Beck 2014 (unified [15]) appears as `[1]/[3]` in §4.2/§7.1. The Oct-2024 PCAC vote (unified [22]) is cited `[4]` in §8; FR Doc 2026-07361 (unified [23]) is cited `[7]` in §8; WADA (unified [24]) is cited `[8]` in §8 — all colliding with unrelated unified entries. A reader following an inline marker to the canonical bibliography lands on the wrong paper. Required fix: renumber all inline references to the unified [1]–[31] scheme (or, at minimum, prominently state at first use that body sections retain section-local numbering decoded only by the §"Citation Numbering Note + Crosswalk" table).

2. **logical-inconsistency / minor** — Within a single paragraph (§4.5, line 221) the Phase 1 study is cited `[2]` (section-local D-column) while the Phase 2 RCT in the same sentence is cited `[15]` (unified). Mixing both schemes inside one sentence is the sharpest illustration of finding 1 and should be resolved when renumbering.

3. **citation-incomplete / minor** — §8 cites the PCAC rationale as `[10, regulatory (§F)]` and the net-status line as `[12, regulatory (§F)]` (lines 314, 316); unified [10] is Johansen 1999 (rat bone growth) and unified [12] is the glucocorticoid-bone 2001 rat study — neither is a regulatory source. These are section-F-local pointers that, read against the canonical list, point at animal papers. Same root cause as finding 1; flagged separately because the regulatory section is the highest-stakes place for a mis-resolving citation.

4. **balance-issue / minor** — The practitioner-convention dosing (§9.2) and the GH-axis class-risk reasoning (§7.2–7.6) are correctly type-tagged and hedged, but the report could state once, plainly, that because the only human safety data are 7-day IV inpatient, the *direction* of unknown risk for chronic SC use is asymmetric (the reassuring placebo-comparable AE rate cannot transfer). §7.7 nearly says this; a one-line elevation would close the only residual balance soft spot. Optional — not blocking.

## Notes on the dimensions that PASS cleanly (no findings)

- **Selectivity honesty:** correctly and repeatedly flagged as ANIMAL-supported / mechanistically plausible / NOT human-proven (read-first box, §0 table row, §2.3, §2.6, §6, Bottom Line — 7 locations). The 200×-ED50 margin is framed as an animal result, not a human one. Exemplary.
- **Phase 2 ileus failure + discontinuation:** stated plainly and never spun by the clean acute pharmacology — the §4.5 "biomarker moves, outcome does not" framing actively uses the failure against the marketed claims. The inferred (vs reported) negative read on NCT01280344 is transparently labeled.
- **"No human efficacy for muscle/fat/anti-aging":** unambiguous (§4.6, §11, read-first box).
- **Overstatement guards:** bone correctly framed as content/area with unchanged volumetric BMD (§3.2, explicit "Do not state 'increases bone density'"); IGF-1 rise not assumed and the contrary rat datum surfaced; human PK correctly IV-only with SC as flagged extrapolation (§1.3 ROUTE NOTE, §9.1).
- **Regulatory accuracy:** Cat-2 removal ~Sept 2024, PCAC vote AGAINST Oct 29 2024, explicit NOT-in-the-Apr-2026-FR-action (FR Doc 2026-07361), WADA S2.2 prohibited-at-all-times all correct; the MK-677-vs-PEG-MGF correction is internally consistent (MK-677 named only as a WADA GHS example, correctly excluded from the FR-Doc 12-peptide list).
- **No Wikipedia citations** anywhere (prior IC-12 HALT confirmed resolved).
