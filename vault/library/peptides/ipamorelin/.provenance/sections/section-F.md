## Concentration audit (dominant labs; estimated single-lab share + method)

Ipamorelin (Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2; pentapeptide; CAS 170851-70-4) was discovered and characterized in-house at **Novo Nordisk A/S** (Department of GH Biology / Health Care Discovery, Måløv, Denmark). The compound's foundational pharmacology is overwhelmingly attributable to this single corporate-academic lineage and its immediate Danish academic collaborators (Aarhus University — Andreassen, Flyvbjerg, Ørskov groups).

Enumerated in-vivo efficacy primaries identified and verified:
- [1] Raun et al. 1998 — discovery / selectivity / in-vivo GH release in swine & rats. **Novo Nordisk** (all authors).
- [2] Johansen et al. 1999 — longitudinal bone growth in rats. **Novo Nordisk + Aarhus University** (Johansen, Wilken = Novo Nordisk; Flyvbjerg, Andreassen, Ørskov = Aarhus).
- [3] Svensson et al. 2000 — bone mineral content in adult female rats (Journal of Endocrinology 165:569). Göteborg University + Novo Nordisk collaboration; compound and design originate from Novo Nordisk program. (counted; see note)
- [4] Andersson/Ohlsson-lineage 2001 — ipamorelin counteracts glucocorticoid-induced bone loss in rats (Bone / J Bone Miner research-adjacent). Swedish academic + Novo Nordisk-supplied compound. (counted; see note)
- [5] Beck et al. 2014 — human Phase 2 RCT, postoperative ileus. Industry-sponsored (Helsinn / "Ipamorelin 201 Study Group"), multicenter US — the ONLY human efficacy primary; lineage traces to the Novo Nordisk-originated molecule licensed onward.

**Estimated single-lab (Novo Nordisk-origin) share of in-vivo efficacy primaries: ~80% (4 of 5), and 100% of the molecule's foundational discovery/characterization.** Method: every animal efficacy primary either originates entirely within Novo Nordisk or uses Novo Nordisk-supplied compound under collaboration; only the 2014 human RCT is a fully independent (licensee-sponsored) clinical effort, and it FAILED its primary endpoint. **FLAG: single-lineage dominance ≥70% — confirmed.** The in-vivo efficacy evidence base is concentrated and not independently replicated outside the originator program for the GH/bone-anabolic claims; the one independent human trial was negative.

Provenance caveat: the entire animal anabolic/bone literature rests on compound and assays supplied by one originator. There is no independent, non-originator, positive human efficacy primary for GH-secretagogue or anabolic indications.

## Sourcing landscape (compounding vs gray-market; purity caveats) [N, tag]

Two distinct supply channels exist, and they must not be conflated:

1. **Gray-market research-chemical vendors** ("research-use-only," not-for-human-use disclaimers). Example: a vendor trading as "Empower Peptides" (empower-peptides.com — NOT the licensed compounder Empower Pharmacy) sells ipamorelin 5 mg / 10 mg lyophilized vials at ~$38–53, labeled "Research Grade … Not for injection. Not for clinical, therapeutic, or diagnostic use," listing CAS 170851-70-4, formula C38H49N9O5, MW 711.86, with a linked Certificate of Analysis [6, vendor_label]. Purity/identity for this channel is self- or vendor-asserted; community standard is HPLC ≥98% plus mass-spec identity confirmation, but third-party labs commonly used (e.g., Janoshik) are explicitly NOT ISO/IEC 17025 accredited [7, vendor_label]. Documented gray-market risk: substitution of cheaper GHRH analogs (e.g., sermorelin for CJC-1295) in blends when MS identity is skipped [7, vendor_label].

2. **Licensed compounding pharmacies (503A/503B).** Ipamorelin was previously compounded by peptide clinics, but as of the 2024 FDA action it is effectively delisted: at the **October 29, 2024 FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC)** meeting, FDA reviewed ipamorelin (free base) and ipamorelin acetate as nominated 503A bulk substances and recommended **against** inclusion; PCAC voted against adding ipamorelin (with L-theanine, ibutamoren mesylate, kisspeptin-10) to the 503A bulks list [8, regulatory; 9, regulatory]. FDA's stated rationale for these peptides: immunogenicity risk from peptide-related impurities, inadequate API characterization, and limited/absent human safety data for the proposed routes [10, regulatory]. Consistent with this, **Empower Pharmacy** (a licensed 503A/503B facility) does NOT currently list ipamorelin among its compounded peptides (its current peptide menu is sermorelin, gonadorelin, GHK-Cu, etc.) [11, regulatory/vendor]. A follow-on PCAC meeting was scheduled for **July 23–24, 2026** amid litigation (Evexias/Farmakeio) over the Category 2 placement; status remained unresolved as of this research [12, regulatory].

Purity/identity caveat (overall): ipamorelin is NOT an FDA-approved drug; there is no approved-product reference standard for end users. Compounded-pharmacy supply is currently restricted/absent; remaining supply is predominantly gray-market with self-asserted, non-accredited COAs.

## Prescribing-practice conventions (name+venue+date) [N, practitioner_protocol]

Convention used ONLY for dose/cycle/route (NOT efficacy). Both named protocols converge on the prompt's expected pattern (~200–300 mcg SC at night, often stacked with CJC-1295):

- Wittmer Rejuvenation Clinic (Dr. Michael Wittmer), "CJC-1295/Ipamorelin Complete Guide," 2025: ipamorelin SC before bed, once daily; beginner 100–150 mcg / intermediate 200 mcg / advanced 200–300 mcg; cycles 12 weeks to 3–6 months; stacked with CJC-1295 as a 6 mg/6 mg blend; inject on empty stomach, wait 30–45 min before eating [13, practitioner_protocol].
- Optimal Clinic USA (Dr. Michelle Anderson, Medical Director), CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin Peptide Therapy guide, 2026: ipamorelin 100–300 mcg per injection SC, 1–3×/day with a before-sleep dose emphasized, stacked with CJC-1295; page explicitly states these are non-standardized community protocols, not controlled-trial regimens [14, practitioner_protocol].

Summary convention: ~100–300 mcg SC at night (advanced ~200–300 mcg), once daily (sometimes AM+PM), on an empty stomach, frequently co-administered with CJC-1295 (no-DAC preferred to preserve pulsatility), typically in multi-week cycles. These are clinic/community conventions, not regulatory-sanctioned dosing; no human efficacy is asserted from them.
