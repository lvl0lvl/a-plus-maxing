# Section A — Identity, Chemistry, PK (Ipamorelin)

Target: **Ipamorelin** (NNC 26-0161). Pentapeptide GH-secretagogue / ghrelin-receptor (GHS-R1a) agonist.
Scope: sequence/structure, MW, CAS; ghrelin-mimetic identity; PK (half-life, absorption, clearance, excretion); formulation/stability.

## Claims (numbered; inline [N, tag]; species+n; route tags)

### Identity & chemistry

1. Ipamorelin is a synthetic **pentapeptide** with the sequence **Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2** (C-terminal amide), developed by Novo Nordisk under the code **NNC 26-0161** [1, rct/animal][5, mechanism_review]. It was designed within a GHRP-derived series lacking the central Ala-Trp dipeptide of GHRP-1 [1, animal].

2. Molecular formula **C38H49N9O5**; **average MW ≈ 711.9 g/mol**; **monoisotopic mass 711.3857**; IUPAC begins "(2S)-6-amino-2-[[(2R)-2-[[(2R)-2-[[(2S)-2-[(2-amino-2-methylpropanoyl)amino]…" [3, regulatory]. (PubChem CID 9831659.)

3. **CAS Registry Number: 170851-70-4** (free base / parent). Commonly supplied as the acetate salt [3, regulatory][6, vendor_label]. Physical form: white to off-white powder [6, vendor_label]. (Vendor cite supports physical description only; not used to ground any numeric pharmacology value.)

4. The N-terminal residue **Aib (α-aminoisobutyric acid / 2-aminoisobutyric acid)** is a non-proteinogenic, conformationally constraining amino acid; positions 3 and 4 are **D-amino acids (D-2-naphthylalanine, D-phenylalanine)**, conferring protease resistance relative to all-L peptides [1, animal][2, mechanism_review].

### Ghrelin-mimetic identity & selectivity

5. Ipamorelin is a selective agonist of the **growth-hormone-secretagogue receptor (GHS-R1a, the ghrelin receptor)**; it was characterized as **"the first selective GH secretagogue"** because it releases GH **without** significant concurrent release of ACTH, cortisol, prolactin, FSH, LH, or TSH — even at doses up to **200-fold** above the GH ED50 — distinguishing it from GHRP-2 and GHRP-6 [1, animal].

6. In vitro potency (primary rat pituitary cell assay): **EC50 = 1.3 ± 0.4 nmol/L**, Emax = 85 ± 5% (rat) [1, in_vitro].

7. In vivo GH-releasing potency: anesthetized **rats** ED50 = **80 ± 42 nmol/kg** (Emax 1545 ± 250 ng GH/mL); conscious **swine** ED50 = **2.3 ± 0.03 nmol/kg** (Emax 65 ± 0.2 ng GH/mL plasma) [1, animal; species=rat & domestic swine; route=IV] (n not specified in source abstract).

### Pharmacokinetics — human

8. **Human PK (n=40 healthy males, 8 per dose × 5 doses; 15-min IV infusion):** terminal **half-life ≈ 2 h**, plasma **clearance 0.078 L/h/kg**, **steady-state volume of distribution 0.22 L/kg**; PK was dose-proportional over 4.21–140.45 nmol/kg [4, open_label; species=human; route=IV infusion].

9. PK/PD link (same human study) used an **indirect (turnover) response model**: GH-stimulation potency **SC50 = 214 nmol/L**, maximal GH production rate ≈ 694 mIU/L/h [4, open_label; species=human; route=IV].

> ROUTE NOTE: The canonical "~2 h half-life" is established from **IV infusion in humans** [4]. No verified human **subcutaneous** PK (SC bioavailability/half-life) was located. SC is the predominant real-world/compounded route, so the SC PK profile is an **extrapolation from IV human + animal data**, not directly measured in the located Tier-1/2 literature (see gaps).

### Pharmacokinetics — animal (route/species annotated)

10. **Rat PK (male Sprague-Dawley):** ipamorelin's systemic **plasma clearance was ~5-fold lower** than GHRP-6; it was **excreted mainly in urine** (GHRP-6 predominantly biliary); 60–80% recovered as intact compound across excretion pathways, indicating metabolic stability [10, animal; species=rat; route=IV]. (Source: Johansen PB et al. 1998.)

11. **Intranasal bioavailability ≈ 20%** in rats (lower than NNC 26-0235 / NNC 26-0194 / GHRP-2 at ~50%) [10, animal; species=rat; route=intranasal]. (No SC bioavailability tested in this study.)

### Structure–activity / formulation context

12. The ipamorelin scaffold was the lead from which a peptidomimetic SAR series was built (size reduction + backbone N-methylation to pursue oral bioavailability); the derived tetrapeptide NNC 26-0235 reached ~10% oral bioavailability in dogs, whereas ipamorelin itself was developed as a parenteral peptide (no meaningful oral bioavailability claimed) [12, animal; species=dog for the derivative].

13. Stability/formulation specifics (lyophilized peptide; reconstitution, cold-chain, in-use stability) are **not** documented in the located Tier-1/2 primary literature; vendor labels assert lyophilized-powder presentation but are not admissible to ground stability numbers (see gaps).

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

- **No verified human subcutaneous PK.** The ~2 h half-life is from **IV infusion** in humans [4]. SC bioavailability/half-life/Tmax — the dominant real-world route — was not found in admissible literature; SC profile is currently an extrapolation. This is the single most important gap for a wiki ingest.
- **n for animal potency not stated** in the [1] abstract (rat/swine ED50 group sizes unverified at abstract level).
- **No SC bioavailability in any located study**; only intranasal (rat, ~20%) and IV are directly measured [10].
- **Formulation/stability** (reconstitution solvent, cold-chain, in-use and lyophilized shelf-life) absent from Tier-1/2 primary literature; only vendor assertions exist (not admissible for numbers).
- **No modern (post-2010) independent re-characterization** located; PK rests on 1998–1999 Novo Nordisk-era work.
- One **authorship discrepancy** resolved in favor of PubMed for [10] (Johansen PB, not Agersø).
- Receptor-binding affinity (Ki/IC50 at GHS-R1a) for ipamorelin specifically was not captured in this section's verified set; flagged for Section B (mechanism/PD) rather than guessed here.
