# Section E — Safety, Regulatory & Anti-Doping (Ipamorelin)

> Scope note: Ipamorelin is a selective, GH-releasing-peptide (GHRP)-class ghrelin/GHS-receptor agonist whose clinical development was discontinued. Human safety data are thin (one published Phase-2 RCT plus early pharmacology studies); GH-axis risk is therefore reasoned largely from the broader GH/IGF-1 literature and labelled as such. AE *existence* vs. quantitative AE *rate* is distinguished throughout, and rates are grounded only in primary human trials, never in vendor/anecdote sources.

## Claims (numbered; inline [N, tag]; species+n; AE rate vs existence distinguished)

1. In the only published Phase-2 human RCT (postoperative ileus after bowel resection; n=114 safety/mITT, ipamorelin 0.03 mg/kg IV BID up to 7 days), ipamorelin was **well tolerated with no serious compound-related adverse events** reported [1, rct]. **AE rate (quantitative):** overall treatment-emergent AE incidence was **87.5% ipamorelin vs 94.8% placebo** — i.e., not higher than placebo in this short-course IV setting [1, rct]. (This is a 7-day perioperative IV exposure and does NOT speak to chronic subcutaneous self-administration risk.)

2. Ipamorelin acutely and dose-dependently **raises serum GH** via ghrelin/GHS-receptor agonism; sustained GH elevation raises **IGF-1**, which is the proximate mediator of GH-axis benefit and of GH-excess risk [2, mechanism_review]. (Mechanism claim; ipamorelin-specific human IGF-1 dose-response over chronic dosing is not established in peer-reviewed trials — see gaps.)

3. **GH-axis adverse effects (existence, class-level, not ipamorelin-specific rates):** GH/secretagogue exposure in controlled adult studies produces **fluid-retention phenomena — peripheral edema, arthralgia, and carpal-tunnel-type symptoms — and impaired glucose tolerance / reduced insulin sensitivity** [3, mechanism_review]. Carpal tunnel in GH/IGF-1 excess is mechanistically attributed to median-nerve compression from GH-induced sodium/water retention and soft-tissue edema (the acromegaly model) [3, mechanism_review].

4. **Theoretical insulin-resistance / hyperglycemia signal is regulator-acknowledged for ipamorelin specifically:** at FDA's PCAC review, committee members voting against ipamorelin cited reported adverse effects including **fluid retention, hyperglycemia, and a congestive-heart-failure concern**, alongside insufficient efficacy/safety evidence [4, regulatory]. (Regulatory characterization of the evidence base, not a measured trial AE rate.)

5. **Cancer-promotion concern from sustained IGF-1 (class-level, epidemiologic):** higher circulating IGF-1 is **prospectively associated with increased risk of several cancers**. Pooled individual data from 17 prospective studies found breast-cancer **OR 1.28 (95% CI 1.14–1.44) highest vs lowest IGF-1**, concentrated in ER-positive tumors [5, cohort]. A prospective case-cohort (EPIC-Heidelberg, ~7,461 with IGF-1) found higher IGF-1 associated with **breast (HR 1.25) and prostate (HR 1.31)** cancer, with a U-shaped IGF-1–mortality relationship [6, cohort]. This is an association in endogenous-IGF-1 epidemiology — it has NOT been demonstrated that exogenous ipamorelin raises cancer risk, but it is the basis of the theoretical caution against chronically driving IGF-1 upward.

## Risk-floor inputs (contraindications / monitoring / stopping — each cited)

- **Contraindication / caution — active or history of malignancy:** the IGF-1–cancer prospective associations [5,6, cohort] make chronic GH-secretagogue-driven IGF-1 elevation a theoretical concern in anyone with active, prior, or high-risk cancer; the standard GH-therapy contraindication to active malignancy applies by extension [3, mechanism_review].
- **Caution — impaired glucose tolerance / diabetes:** GH-axis stimulation reduces insulin sensitivity and can raise glucose [3, mechanism_review]; hyperglycemia was a regulator-cited concern for ipamorelin [4, regulatory].
- **Caution — fluid-overload states (e.g., heart failure):** edema/fluid retention is a class effect [3, mechanism_review] and a congestive-heart-failure concern was raised at PCAC [4, regulatory].
- **Monitoring — named objective assays:**
  - **Serum IGF-1** (primary objective marker of GH-axis activity and the obvious ceiling marker; keep within age/sex reference range) — rationale from [2,3,5,6]. See [[biomarkers/igf-1]].
  - **Fasting glucose and HbA1c** (insulin-resistance / hyperglycemia surveillance) [3,4].
  - **Clinical surveillance for edema, arthralgia, and carpal-tunnel symptoms** as the bedside readout of fluid retention [3].
- **Stopping rules:** discontinue / re-evaluate on IGF-1 rising above the age-adjusted reference range, new or worsening glucose dysregulation, or onset of edema/arthralgia/carpal-tunnel symptoms — each maps to a cited risk above [3,4]; no validated ipamorelin-specific stopping threshold exists in the literature (gap).

## Regulatory & WADA status (cited)

- **FDA approval:** Ipamorelin has **never been FDA-approved**; clinical development was discontinued (no approved indication) [4, regulatory].
- **FDA 503A compounding status (CORRECTED vs prompt premise):** Ipamorelin was placed on the **interim 503A Category 2** list (significant safety concern). It was **removed from Category 2 effective ~September 27, 2024** after the nomination was withdrawn, and was referred to the **Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC)**. At the **October 29, 2024 PCAC meeting**, FDA recommended against inclusion and the **committee voted NOT to add ipamorelin (acetate and free base) to the 503A bulk drug substances list** [4, regulatory].
  - **The ~April 2026 Category-2 removal action (FR Doc 2026-07361, 91 FR 20465, published April 16, 2026) does NOT include ipamorelin.** That action removed a *different* set of 12 peptides (BPC-157, TB-500/thymosin β4 fragment, epitalon, GHK-Cu [injectable routes], MOTS-c, DSIP/emideltide, dihexa, **PEG-MGF (Mechano Growth Factor, pegylated)**, melanotan II, KPV, semax, LL-37/cathelicidin) and scheduled PCAC review meetings for **July 23–24, 2026** (with a further meeting before end of Feb 2027) — ipamorelin is not among the removed peptides nor on that PCAC review agenda [7, regulatory]. (Correction note: an earlier draft of this list erroneously named "MK-677/ibutamoren" as the 12th removed peptide; MK-677 is **not** in FR Doc 2026-07361 — the verified 12th substance is PEG-MGF. Verified via the Federal Register notice plus secondaries (Frier Levitt, Orrick, FDA Law Blog).) **Net status for ipamorelin: removed from Cat-2 (Sept 2024), PCAC-rejected (Oct 2024), NOT on the 503A bulks list, NOT part of the Apr-2026 action.**
- **WADA anti-doping (2026 Prohibited List):** Ipamorelin is **PROHIBITED AT ALL TIMES** (in- and out-of-competition) under **S2 → S2.2 (Growth Hormone (GH), its fragments and releasing factors)**, in the **growth-hormone-secretagogue (GHS) / GH-releasing-factor subsection**. The 2026 List names ipamorelin explicitly among GHS examples: "*Growth hormone secretagogues (GHS) and their mimetics, e.g. Anamorelin, Capromorelin, Ghrelin, Ibutamoren (MK-677), **Ipamorelin**, Lenomorelin (ghrelin)…*" [8, regulatory]. It is a non-specified substance (strictest sanction tier).

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

- **Single human safety RCT.** All quantitative ipamorelin AE-rate data come from one short-course (≤7-day) IV perioperative trial [1]; there is **no peer-reviewed safety dataset for chronic subcutaneous self-administration**, which is the real-world use pattern. Edema/glucose/IGF-1 risks under chronic dosing are inferred from the GH-axis class literature [3] and regulatory characterization [4], not from ipamorelin chronic trials.
- **No ipamorelin-specific human IGF-1 dose-response / ceiling data** in peer-reviewed sources; the IGF-1 monitoring rationale is mechanistic + class-epidemiologic, not ipamorelin-trial-derived.
- **Cancer link is associational and endogenous-IGF-1-based** [5,6]; no study shows exogenous ipamorelin alters cancer incidence. Stated as theoretical caution, not established causation.
- **WADA primary PDF did not machine-parse**; the verbatim ipamorelin entry was confirmed via an official national anti-doping authority mirror of the 2026 List [8] rather than the WADA-hosted PDF directly — content is consistent across both, but the WADA-hosted file itself was not text-extractable in this session.
- **Mortality nuance (anti-minimization):** [6] shows a *U-shaped* IGF-1–mortality curve — both very low and very high IGF-1 carry excess mortality. The monitoring goal is "keep IGF-1 in range," not "minimize IGF-1."
