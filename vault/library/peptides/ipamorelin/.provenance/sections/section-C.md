# Section C — Preclinical Efficacy (Ipamorelin)

> SCOPE NOTE: All evidence below is **preclinical (animal / in-vitro)**. None of these
> findings establish efficacy in humans. Ipamorelin (Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2) is a
> pentapeptide ghrelin-receptor (GHS-R1a) agonist / selective GH secretagogue. The compound
> was originally developed by Novo Nordisk and later explored clinically (Helsinn) for
> post-operative ileus; the ileus indication is the reason gut-motility models feature
> prominently in its preclinical record.

## Claims (numbered; inline [N, tag]; species+n; route tags)

### GH / IGF-1 axis

1. In primary **rat** pituitary cell culture, ipamorelin released GH with potency and efficacy
   similar to GHRP-6: EC50 = 1.3 ± 0.4 nmol/L, Emax = 85 ± 5% (GHRP-6 reference set to 100%,
   EC50 = 2.2 ± 0.3 nmol/L) [1, in_vitro].
2. In pentobarbital-anaesthetised **rats**, ipamorelin released GH with ED50 = 80 ± 42 nmol/kg
   and Emax = 1545 ± 250 ng GH/mL — comparable to GHRP-6 (ED50 = 115 ± 36 nmol/kg,
   Emax = 1167 ± 120 ng GH/mL) [1, animal]. (Route: i.v. bolus.)
3. In conscious **swine (pigs)**, ipamorelin released GH with ED50 = 2.3 ± 0.03 nmol/kg and
   Emax = 65 ± 0.2 ng GH/mL plasma [1, animal]. (Route: i.v.)
4. SELECTIVITY (efficacy-relevant): ipamorelin did NOT release ACTH or cortisol above
   GHRH-stimulated baseline even at doses up to ~200× the GH-releasing ED50; prolactin/FSH/LH/TSH
   also unaffected — distinguishing it from GHRP-6 and other early secretagogues [1, animal].
5. CONTRARY/NUANCE on IGF-1: in adult female **rats**, 15-day intermittent s.c. ipamorelin
   (3×/day) producing clear somatic growth did **not** raise total serum IGF-1, IGFBPs, or
   serum bone-turnover markers [3, animal]. Sustained IGF-1 elevation is therefore not a
   reliable feature of intermittent dosing in rodents; GH pulse amplitude is the proximate driver.

### Bone — BMC / BMD (a notable Ipamorelin preclinical area)

6. In 13-week-old female Sprague-Dawley **rats** (n = 7 ipamorelin), 0.5 mg/kg/day delivered
   **continuously** by s.c. osmotic minipump for 12 weeks increased total tibial and vertebral
   bone-mineral content (BMC, DXA in vivo) vs vehicle; GHRP-6 (n = 8, 0.5 mg/kg/day) and GH
   (n = 7, 3.5 mg/kg/day) did likewise [2, animal]. (Route: continuous s.c. infusion.)
7. The BMC gain tracked body-weight gain: **total** BMC corrected for body weight (BMC:BW ratio)
   was unaffected; **tibial** area BMD rose, but total and vertebral area BMDs were unchanged [2, animal].
8. By pQCT, the cortical BMC increase was driven by **increased cross-sectional bone area**,
   while cortical **volumetric** BMD was unchanged — i.e., bigger bones, not denser bone matrix [2, animal].

### Longitudinal growth

9. In adult female **rats**, s.c. ipamorelin 3×/day for 15 days dose-dependently increased
   longitudinal bone growth rate (tibial metaphysis, tetracycline-label method) from 42 µm/day
   (vehicle) to 44, 50, and 52 µm/day at 18, 90, and 450 µg/day respectively (P < 0.0001)
   [3, animal]. (Route: s.c., divided 3×/day.)
10. The same study showed a pronounced, dose-dependent **body-weight gain**, with no significant
    change in tartrate-resistant acid phosphatase-positive (osteoclast-marker) multinuclear cell
    counts in tibial metaphysis [3, animal].

### Body composition / lean mass

11. Across the bone/growth studies, ipamorelin's reproducible preclinical body-composition
    signal in **rats** is GH-mediated body-weight / lean somatic gain rather than a directly
    quantified fat-mass-vs-lean-mass DXA partition in the primary papers [2, animal][3, animal].
    NOTE: claims of selective fat-mass reduction with preserved lean mass in "diet-induced obese
    rats" appear in vendor/secondary write-ups but were NOT confirmed to a verifiable primary
    citation during this retrieval — see Coverage gaps. Do not ground a fat-loss number on those.

### Gut motility (post-op ileus — the development indication)

12. In a **rat** post-operative ileus model (laparotomy + intestinal manipulation), a single i.v.
    ipamorelin 1 mg/kg shortened time to first bowel movement vs vehicle; **repetitive** dosing
    (4×/day, 3-h intervals; 0.01–1 mg/kg range) significantly increased cumulative fecal pellet
    output, food intake, and 48-h body-weight gain [4, animal]. (Route: i.v.; fasted male rats.)
13. In a related **rat** gastric-dysmotility model, surgery delayed gastric emptying to 78 ± 5%
    of meal retained (vehicle); ipamorelin 0.014 µmol/kg i.v. accelerated emptying to 52 ± 11%
    retained, approaching the 44 ± 6% of nonsurgical controls [5, animal].
14. MECHANISM: in isolated gastric smooth-muscle preparations from operated **rats**, surgery
    blunted acetylcholine- and electrical-field-stimulation-evoked contractions; ipamorelin (and
    ghrelin) reversed this — i.e., gastroprokinesis via GHS-R1a-mediated activation of cholinergic
    excitatory neurons, not a direct GH effect [5, animal/ex_vivo].

## Lab-provenance note (for concentration audit)

- **Doses are species- and route-specific and NOT human-translatable.** GH-release ED50 values
  ([1]) are i.v. bolus in nmol/kg (rat ~80 nmol/kg; pig ~2.3 nmol/kg — note the ~35× interspecies
  potency gap, so any cross-species extrapolation is unsafe).
- Bone study [2] used **continuous** s.c. infusion at 0.5 mg/kg/day (osmotic minipump) — a very
  different pharmacology from the **pulsatile/intermittent** s.c. dosing in growth study [3]
  (18–450 µg/day split 3×/day). Continuous vs pulsatile matters mechanistically (GH-axis
  desensitisation) and the two cannot be pooled.
- Gut studies report doses in two unit systems: mg/kg ([4], 0.01–1 mg/kg) and µmol/kg ([5],
  0.014 µmol/kg ≈ 0.011 mg/kg using MW ≈ 711.85 g/mol for free base). Anyone tabulating these
  must normalise units and cannot assume the mg/kg and µmol/kg figures are independent dose tiers.
- Industry origin/co-authorship present in [1][2][3] (Novo Nordisk) and [5] (Tranzyme) — disclosed,
  does not disqualify, but flag for the concentration/effect-size auditor.

## Coverage gaps / honest limits

- **No orchidectomized / ovariectomized osteoporosis model** for ipamorelin was located. The
  bone evidence [2] is in intact young-adult female rats; it shows bigger bones (area-driven BMC)
  but **unchanged volumetric BMD** — so "increases bone density" is an overstatement of the
  primary data. State as "increased bone mineral *content* / bone size; volumetric BMD unchanged."
- **No verified primary body-composition (DXA fat-vs-lean partition) study** for ipamorelin was
  confirmed. The "diet-induced obese rat, fat down / lean preserved" claim circulating in
  secondary/vendor sources could NOT be tied to a resolvable citation here — treat as UNVERIFIED;
  do not ground a fat-loss effect size on it.
- **Species skew:** efficacy data are overwhelmingly rat; one pig GH-release data set [1]. No
  primate efficacy located.
- **IGF-1 conflict:** intermittent dosing did not raise total IGF-1 in rats [3]; vendor marketing
  often implies robust IGF-1 elevation. The preclinical record does not support a guaranteed
  IGF-1 rise, especially with pulsatile dosing.
- **Indication context:** the strongest *functional* (not just biomarker) preclinical efficacy is
  the gut-motility / post-op-ileus track ([4][5]) — consistent with its clinical development target.
- Sources deliberately capped at 5 strong, fully-verified primaries per the efficiency directive;
  no dredging of redundant or low-tier material.
