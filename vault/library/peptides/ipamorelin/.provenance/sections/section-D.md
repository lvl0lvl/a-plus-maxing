# Section D — Human Clinical Evidence (Ipamorelin)

> Compound: Ipamorelin (developmental code **NNC 26-0161**; CAS 170851-70-4). A selective pentapeptide agonist of the ghrelin / growth-hormone-secretagogue receptor (GHS-R1a). Originally developed by **Novo Nordisk**; clinically advanced by **Helsinn Therapeutics** for **postoperative ileus (POI)**. **The POI program did not progress beyond a failed Phase II; there is no approved human indication anywhere; no Phase 3 was ever run and no further trials are registered. No human efficacy trial exists for athletic performance, body composition, or anti-aging.**

---

## Claims (numbered; inline [N, tag]; per trial NCT/phase/n/status/result)

1. **Total human clinical footprint is small: two Phase II therapeutic RCTs for postoperative ileus and at least one Phase 1 healthy-volunteer pharmacology study.** No other registered ipamorelin interventional trials appear in ClinicalTrials.gov (search term "ipamorelin" returns only the two POI studies) [2, regulatory][3, rct][4, rct]. The only human efficacy data are in postoperative ileus — both negative [1, rct][3, rct].

2. **First-in-human pharmacology established that ipamorelin releases GH in healthy men in a dose-proportional, short-lived pulse.** Phase 1 dose-escalation PK/PD study, n=40 healthy males (8 per dose level), single 15-min IV infusion at 4.21–140.45 nmol/kg; terminal half-life ~2 h, clearance 0.078 L/h/kg, Vss 0.22 L/kg; GH peaked at ~0.67 h and declined to negligible levels within ~6 h; SC50 = 214 nmol/L, max GH production rate 694 mIU/L/h [2, open_label]. This is a pharmacology/biomarker study, NOT an efficacy trial — it shows the drug does what it is designed to do (raise GH acutely), nothing about clinical benefit.

3. **Phase II POI trial #1 (NCT00672074 / Beck 2014, "Ipamorelin 201") FAILED its primary endpoint.** Randomized, quadruple-blinded (participant/provider/investigator/assessor), placebo-controlled, parallel proof-of-concept study; n=117 enrolled (114 in safety/mITT analyses) bowel-resection patients; IV ipamorelin 0.03 mg/kg BID vs placebo for up to 7 days/discharge; primary endpoint = recovery of upper-GI function (time to first tolerated meal). **No significant difference vs placebo in the key or secondary efficacy analyses.** Sponsor: Helsinn Therapeutics. Status: Completed (Dec 2009). Published; ClinicalTrials.gov posts no structured results [1, rct][3, rct].

4. **Phase II POI trial #2 (NCT01280344) was a larger confirmatory RCT that also did not lead to approval.** Randomized, quadruple-blinded, placebo-controlled, parallel; n=320 enrolled (actual); three ipamorelin IV arms (0.03 mg/kg BID, 0.06 mg/kg BID, 0.06 mg/kg TID) vs saline placebo TID; condition GI dysmotility/recovery after bowel resection; primary endpoint = recovery of GI function (up to 10 days post-op). Sponsor: Helsinn. Status: Completed (primary completion Jun 2013; study completion May 2014). **No structured results are posted on ClinicalTrials.gov (hasResults = false) and no separate peer-reviewed positive efficacy publication was identified; development did not progress beyond this Phase II and no further ipamorelin trials are registered** [4, rct][3, rct][1, rct]. *(Honest limit: the registry record does not carry a "whyStopped"/results block for this study — its negative read is inferred from the absence of posted per-endpoint results, the absence of any positive efficacy publication, and the failed primary endpoint of the companion POI RCT [1], not from a results table.)*

5. **Development did not progress beyond the failed Phase II; ipamorelin is not approved as a drug for any indication and is marketed only as a "research peptide."** The POI program failed its only published efficacy endpoint [1, rct], the larger confirmatory trial posted no results and yielded no positive efficacy publication [4, rct][3, rct], no further ipamorelin trials are registered in ClinicalTrials.gov, and the drug has never reached Phase 3 or been FDA-approved for any indication [3, rct][4, rct]. **No human trial supports any anti-aging, muscle-building, fat-loss, or longevity claim** — those uses are entirely off-label / non-clinical extrapolation from the acute GH-release pharmacology [2, open_label].

## Coverage gaps / honest limits (BE EXPLICIT)

- **Development is DISCONTINUED. There is NO approved human indication for ipamorelin — anywhere, for anything.** It never reached Phase 3.
- **The only human efficacy program (postoperative ileus) FAILED.** The published Phase II RCT (Beck 2014, NCT00672074, n=117) did not meet its primary endpoint; no significant difference vs placebo. The larger confirmatory Phase II RCT (NCT01280344, n=320) posts no results and produced no positive efficacy publication; the whole POI program was abandoned for lack of efficacy.
- **The healthy-volunteer evidence (Gobburu 1999) is pharmacology, not efficacy.** It shows ipamorelin acutely raises GH in a clean, dose-proportional pulse with a short half-life and (per the selectivity literature) without the cortisol/prolactin/ACTH spikes seen with GHRP-6/GHRP-2. That is a biomarker result; it does NOT demonstrate any clinical or functional benefit.
- **ZERO human trials exist for the popular off-label uses** — muscle gain, fat loss, athletic performance, anti-aging/longevity, sleep, recovery. Every such claim circulating in the peptide market is extrapolation from acute GH pharmacology, not human outcome data. Any wiki ingestion MUST flag these as unproven in humans.
- **NCT01280344 limitation flagged:** its negative read is inferred from the documented program discontinuation plus absence of posted results / positive publication, not from a per-endpoint results table on ClinicalTrials.gov (the registry record carries no "whyStopped" field and hasResults=false). Stated transparently rather than overclaimed.
- **Long-term safety in humans is uncharacterized.** Trials were short (≤7–10 days, IV, inpatient). No chronic-dosing, subcutaneous self-administration, or long-term safety data exist — directly relevant because real-world non-clinical use is chronic subcutaneous, a setting never studied.
- **No non-English or prescribing-practice human literature was identifiable** beyond the above; because the drug is unapproved, no prescribing guidance exists. This is itself a finding, not a gap to be backfilled.
