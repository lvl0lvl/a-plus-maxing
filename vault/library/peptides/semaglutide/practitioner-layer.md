# Semaglutide — Prescribing-Practice Layer

`practitioner_protocol` cites may support dose/route/cycle conventions ONLY — never efficacy.

## On-label dosing (regulatory — the authoritative reference)
- **Wegovy (obesity), SC weekly:** titrate 0.25 → 0.5 → 1.0 → 1.7 → **2.4 mg**, ~4 wk per step (≥16 wk to target);
  slower titration permitted for GI intolerance `[regulatory: FDA Wegovy label]`.
- **Ozempic (T2D), SC weekly:** 0.25 (initiation, non-therapeutic) → 0.5 → 1.0 → max **2 mg** `[regulatory]`.
- **Rybelsus (oral T2D):** 3 mg/d (initiation) → 7 → **14 mg/d**, taken **fasting** with ≤4 oz water, wait ≥30 min
  before food/other meds (food destroys the ~1% bioavailability) `[regulatory]`.
- Slow titration exists to limit dose-dependent GI AEs.

## Off-label conventions
- **"Microdosing"** (sub-labeled doses / slower titration to a "minimal effective dose") is an off-label practice with
  **NO controlled-trial support** — `practitioner_protocol`/telehealth-tier only; dose/route framing, never efficacy.
  **NO ADMISSIBLE RCT FOUND** for any microdose value. Do not assign efficacy or PK numbers to it.
- **Compounded semaglutide:** the FDA flags that compounded **salt forms (semaglutide sodium/acetate) are NOT the
  approved active ingredient**; dosing errors/overdoses have been reported from self-measured multi-dose vials. With the
  shortage resolved (Feb 2025) and enforcement discretion ended (503A Apr 2025 / 503B May 2025), this channel is
  closing — branded product is the realistic, quality-assured route.

## Doctor-handout note
FDA-approved and prescribable — but **off-target for the operator's recovery/rebuilding goal**. Raise at the July-2026
visit only if fat loss is genuinely indicated; if used, pair with resistance training + adequate protein to defend lean
mass, and screen out personal/family MTC or MEN2 first.
