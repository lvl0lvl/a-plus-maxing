# Semaglutide — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **semaglutide** (GLP-1 receptor agonist), companion to `vault/library/peptides/semaglutide/research-report.md`. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. No citation is fabricated; items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in China/Japan/Russia but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and is already in the semaglutide corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, Japanese, etc.** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. This **non-English-LANGUAGE vs non-English-AUTHOR** distinction is the load-bearing rule for semaglutide, because its pivotal trials were run in international (including non-English-speaking) populations but published in English.

**Headline result: ZERO genuinely non-English-LANGUAGE admissible semaglutide primaries located.** Semaglutide's evidence base is overwhelmingly global and **English-published**, even for trials run entirely outside English-speaking countries. The major programs — SUSTAIN (T2D injection), STEP (obesity), PIONEER (oral) — are international Novo Nordisk trials whose primary results appear in English-language journals (NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, Diabetes Care, Diabetologia, Diabetes Obesity & Metabolism). Dedicated **China-population trials** exist (e.g., PIONEER 11 and PIONEER 12, evaluating oral semaglutide in predominantly Chinese participants with T2D), but their primary results were published **in English** (e.g., *Diabetologia*, *Diabetes Obesity & Metabolism*), not in Chinese-language journals [1]. There is therefore **no separate non-English primary evidence stream to integrate** for semaglutide; the non-English footprint is consumption/translation of the English corpus, not independent non-English-language primary research.

## Chinese

China is a substantial semaglutide research and market footprint, but — as with the rest of the program — **the genuine Chinese-population semaglutide science is published in English and is already in the corpus**, so it is NOT counted here. The clearest example is the dedicated China-population oral-semaglutide program:

SUSTAIN China analyses likewise appear in English-language venues.

Chinese-**language** material located is non-admissible (clinical-practice/encyclopedic commentary and vendor/supplier pages), and none is a Chinese-language semaglutide primary distinct from the English corpus.

Explicit none-located: **no genuinely Chinese-language semaglutide primary** (RCT, cohort, or registered trial) distinct from the already-English-published Chinese-population corpus.
