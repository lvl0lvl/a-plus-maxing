# Semaglutide — Non-English Literature Coverage

## Survey result
**No distinct material non-English primary literature located.** Semaglutide's pivotal program is global and
multicentre but **published in English** in the major journals (NEJM, Lancet): SELECT (PMID 37952131, 41 countries),
STEP 1 (PMID 33567185), SUSTAIN-6 (PMID 27633186), PIONEER-6 (DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1901118). Foreign-population data are
folded into these English-language global publications rather than appearing as separate non-English primaries.

The one population-specific candidate — **PIONEER-9** (oral semaglutide in Japanese patients, Lancet Diabetes
Endocrinol 2020) — is **English-published**, not a non-English primary.

## Net
Unlike Thymosin Alpha-1 (Chinese-dominant) or BPC-157 (Croatian originator), semaglutide has **no material non-English
literature gap** — the evidence base is fully captured in English-language indexed journals. No translation handling required.
