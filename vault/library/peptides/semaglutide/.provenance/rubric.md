# Semaglutide — Phase 2.5 Rubric + Section Plan (deep mode, efficient calibration)

## Mode
deep mode (25+ sources, ~10k words) under the **efficient calibration**: judge bar **92**;
genuine-defects-only; ONE remediation round per real defect. NOTE: semaglutide has a HUGE,
high-quality RCT base — prioritize the LANDMARK trials (SUSTAIN, STEP, SELECT, PIONEER, FLOW,
SUSTAIN-6) over exhaustive enumeration.

## THE DEFINING FACTS (integrity axes — different from the unapproved peptides)
1. **Semaglutide is a GENUINELY APPROVED, GENUINELY EFFICACIOUS GLP-1 receptor agonist** — this
   is the FIRST A-tier entry of the sweep. The integrity job is BALANCE: represent the **real,
   large benefits** accurately (don't under-sell a drug with landmark RCTs + CV/renal outcomes)
   AND the **real caveats/risks** (don't over-sell). It is an acylated long-acting GLP-1 analogue
   (Novo Nordisk); once-weekly SC (Ozempic/Wegovy) + once-daily oral (Rybelsus).
2. **The honest benefit picture (cite landmark RCTs):** glycemic (SUSTAIN, T2D HbA1c); weight
   (STEP program, ~15% mean weight loss at the 2.4 mg obesity dose); **cardiovascular outcomes**
   (SELECT — MACE reduction in obesity WITHOUT diabetes; SUSTAIN-6 in T2D); **renal** (FLOW).
   Emerging/investigational: MASH, OSA, possibly addiction — label INVESTIGATIONAL.
3. **The honest caveats/risks:** GI AEs (nausea/vomiting/diarrhea) are dominant + the main
   discontinuation driver; **weight REGAIN on discontinuation** (STEP-1 extension); **lean/muscle
   mass loss** accompanies weight loss; gallbladder disease; pancreatitis signal; **thyroid
   C-cell/medullary-carcinoma BOXED WARNING is RODENT-based and NOT demonstrated in humans —
   state this precisely**; gastroparesis/ileus + peri-operative aspiration concern; the
   SUSTAIN-6 diabetic-retinopathy signal; suicidality signal investigated and NOT confirmed by
   FDA/EMA. Cost + access.
4. Regulatory: FDA-approved (Ozempic 2017, Rybelsus 2019, Wegovy 2021) + the **compounded-
   semaglutide gray market** tied to the 2022-2024 shortage (503A/503B) and FDA's removal of
   semaglutide from the shortage list (compounding wind-down). WADA: GLP-1 agonists are **NOT
   on the WADA Prohibited List** (verify — do not assert prohibited).

## Section plan (6 lean sections)
- **A — Identity, structure, mechanism.** Acylated GLP-1(7-37) analogue (C18 fatty-diacid,
  albumin binding → ~1-week t½); GLP-1 receptor agonism → glucose-dependent insulin, glucagon
  suppression, slowed gastric emptying, central satiety; SC weekly + oral daily formulations.
- **B — Glycemic + weight efficacy (the STRONG section).** SUSTAIN (T2D HbA1c/weight); STEP
  1-8 (obesity, ~15% weight loss at 2.4 mg; STEP-2 in T2D); PIONEER (oral); head-to-head vs
  other GLP-1s. Magnitudes + populations annotated. Verify landmark PMIDs.
- **C — Cardiovascular / renal / other outcomes.** SELECT (Lincoff 2023, MACE −20% in obesity
  w/o diabetes); SUSTAIN-6 (Marso 2016, T2D MACE); FLOW (kidney outcomes); MASH/OSA/other =
  INVESTIGATIONAL/emerging. + concentration audit (Novo Nordisk sponsorship — expected for an
  approved drug; report honestly).
- **D — Safety / adverse effects.** GI (dominant, discontinuation driver); weight REGAIN on stop;
  lean-mass loss; gallbladder; pancreatitis; the **rodent thyroid C-cell boxed warning (not human-
  demonstrated)**; gastroparesis/ileus + peri-op aspiration; SUSTAIN-6 retinopathy; suicidality
  (investigated, not confirmed); contraindications (personal/family MTC or MEN2, pregnancy);
  monitoring.
- **E — Regulatory & sport.** FDA approvals (Ozempic 2017 / Rybelsus 2019 / Wegovy 2021) + exact
  indications; EMA; the **shortage + compounded-semaglutide** 503A/503B gray market + FDA's
  shortage-resolution/compounding wind-down + warnings on counterfeits/salt-forms; WADA: **NOT
  prohibited** (verify against the WADA 2026 list — do not assert it is banned); cost.
- **F — Practitioner-practice + non-English.** Titration schedule (0.25→escalate; the slow
  titration to mitigate GI); brand vs compounded; "microdosing" trend; lifestyle/muscle-
  preservation adjuncts; non-English literature (Novo/global mostly English-published — distinguish).

## Mandatory rubric dimensions (health)
- Concentration audit (Novo Nordisk sponsorship share — expected; report honestly, the caveat is
  independence of confirmatory data, e.g. SELECT was Novo-sponsored).
- Population annotation (T2D vs obesity-without-diabetes vs CKD vs adolescents — CRUCIAL: don't
  generalize a T2D finding to non-diabetic obesity or vice versa).
- Route fidelity (SC weekly vs oral daily — distinct bioavailability/dosing).
- Risk-floor readiness (contraindications + monitoring + stopping fillable).
