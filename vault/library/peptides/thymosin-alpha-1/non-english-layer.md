# Thymosin Alpha-1 — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 survey of non-English peer-reviewed literature for Thymosin Alpha-1 (Tα1; thymalfasin; Zadaxin; 28-amino-acid N-terminally acetylated thymic peptide). Non-English sources are admissible under the same tier rules as English; language is noted on each entry. Every citation below was located via a retrievable identifier (PMID / DOI / stable journal URL). No citation is fabricated.

Key admissibility distinction applied throughout: a study **authored by researchers at a Chinese, Italian, or Russian institution but published in English** (e.g., a PubMed-indexed English article in *Critical Care*, *Hepatology*, or an international society journal) is NOT counted as a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer. Only articles whose **primary publication language is Chinese, Italian, or Russian** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. This distinction matters sharply for Tα1: the Chinese clinical literature (ETASS, TESTS, CUHK meta-analysis) is extensively English-published; the Garaci/Tor Vergata Italian group and the Andreone/Bologna group published their primary work in English; Russian Zadaxin clinical registrational data likewise appears in English-indexed sources. The question for each language is whether any genuinely non-English-language work exists beyond what is already in the English layer.

---

## Chinese (surveyed; citations or explicit "none located" + search terms/databases)

**Result: 9 genuinely Chinese-language admissible primaries located** — 1 meta-analysis, 1 systematic review, 3 clinical primary studies, 2 in vitro primary studies, 1 animal study, and 1 biotechnology primary — all PubMed-indexed with confirmed "[Article in Chinese]" language tags. All are from Chinese medical universities or the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Tα1 has an unusually large Chinese-language publication record compared to most peptides in this library. This reflects that: (a) Zadaxin received Chinese regulatory approval in the 1990s and became one of the most widely used immunomodulatory pharmaceuticals in China; (b) Chinese academic medical journals (Zhonghua Yi Xue Za Zhi, Zhonghua Gan Zang Bing Za Zhi, Zhongguo Wei Zhong Bing Ji Jiu Yi Xue, Zhejiang Da Xue Xue Bao Yi Xue Ban, Sichuan Da Xue Xue Bao Yi Xue Ban, Sheng Wu Gong Cheng Xue Bao) regularly published Tα1 clinical and experimental work in Chinese. The Tα1 Chinese-language literature is therefore more clinically substantive than is typical for this library's peptide entries.

### C1 — Mao H, Shi T. 2011.

**Chinese Title (from PubMed):** Treatment with interferon and thymosin alpha-1 versus interferon monotherapy for HBeAg positive chronic hepatitis B: a meta-analysis

**Journal:** 中华肝脏病杂志 (*Zhonghua Gan Zang Bing Za Zhi* / Chinese Journal of Hepatology)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 2011 Jan; 19(1):29–33

**PMID:** 21272455 | **DOI:** 10.3760/cma.j.issn.1007-3418.2011.01.009

**Language:** Chinese (PubMed: "[Article in Chinese]")

**Affiliation:** Chongqing Medical University, Chongqing 400010, China

**Study type:** Meta-analysis (Chinese-language systematic review pooling RCT data)

**Key findings:** Combination therapy with interferon plus Tα1 was "remarkably more effective than monotherapy" for HBV-DNA negative rates, ALT normalization, HBeAg loss, and HBeAg seroconversion across treatment and follow-up periods, with comparable adverse effect profiles. **Population note:** Pooled RCT data in HBeAg-positive chronic hepatitis B patients; no animal or cell subjects directly in this paper.

**Translation provenance:** Title and abstract rendered in English by NCBI; main text confirmed as "[Article in Chinese]" by PubMed language indexing.

### Chinese landscape boundary note

The admissibility distinction matters especially sharply for Tα1. The large body of Chinese-institution Tα1 research is predominantly English-published: ETASS (Wu et al., *Critical Care* 2013), TESTS (Zhong et al., *BMJ* 2025), the CUHK meta-analyses, and most major trial data from Peking Union Medical College, Chinese PLA hospitals, and Shanghai clinical centers were published in English-indexed journals. Those belong to the English layer and are not double-counted here. ## Italian (surveyed; citations or explicit "none located" + search terms/databases)

**Result: 3 genuinely Italian-language admissible primaries located** — all primary experimental studies, all published in the *Bollettino della Società Italiana di Biologia Sperimentale* (Boll Soc Ital Biol Sper), all from a single Italian research group (Baldassarre / Mastino / Del Gobbo cluster), and all from the 1984–1988 window.

The Italian research landscape for Tα1 is dominated by the Garaci group at the University of Rome Tor Vergata and the Costantini/cancer groups, whose primary work was published entirely in English-language international journals (*Cancer*, *Journal of Biological Regulators and Homeostatic Agents*, *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences*, *Clinical and Experimental Immunology*). What exists in genuinely Italian-language publication is an early 1980s cluster from the Baldassarre group at what appears to be an Italian university, published in the Italian Society of Experimental Biology's Italian-language journal.

### I1 — Baldassarre AM, Mastino A, Del Gobbo V. 1984.

**Italian Title (from PubMed abstract):** Effects of thymosin alpha l on some time-dependent functions in thymectomized mice

**Journal:** *Bollettino della Società Italiana di Biologia Sperimentale* (Boll Soc Ital Biol Sper)

**Year / Volume / Issue / Pages:** 1984 Jan 30; 60(1):147–51

**PMID:** 6704239

**Language:** Italian (PubMed: "[Article in Italian]")

**Study type:** Primary experimental study (animal; in vivo)

**Study population:** Adult thymectomized mice. Tα1 administered in vivo; azathioprine-sensitivity of spleen cells and thymic-like activity of serum measured. Animal (thymectomized mice).

**Key findings:** Tα1 was able to "restore the lowered levels of these T-dependent functions" (azathioprine-sensitivity of spleen cells; thymic-like serum activity) after in vivo administration in thymectomized mice. An early in vivo demonstration of Tα1's capacity to reconstitute T-cell function in thymic-deficient mice, published in the Italian-language experimental biology press three years before Goldstein's GWU group published comparable data in English journals.

**Population note:** Animal (adult thymectomized mice; in vivo; group composition not recoverable from abstract).

**Translation provenance:** Abstract rendered in English by NCBI; main text confirmed as "[Article in Italian]" by PubMed language indexing and by journal identity (*Bollettino della Società Italiana di Biologia Sperimentale* is the flagship Italian-language journal of the Italian Society of Experimental Biology).

### Italian landscape boundary note

The three Italian-language articles above are from an early (1984–1988) research cluster in the *Bollettino della Società Italiana di Biologia Sperimentale*, the primary venue for Italian-language experimental biology at that time. All three are from a Baldassarre / Mastino / Stirparo / Del Gobbo group cluster. This group's work predates the Garaci/Tor Vergata program; the Garaci group's extensive Italian institutional contribution (cancer adjuvancy, antifungal, HIV) was published entirely in English-indexed international journals and belongs to the English layer.

The *Bollettino della Società Italiana di Biologia Sperimentale* ceased publication in its original form in the 1990s and is not digitally archived beyond PubMed indexing. The Italian Society of Experimental Biology's later journals shifted to English. No additional Italian-language Tα1 primary was located beyond these three PubMed-indexed items.

**Searched (databases / portals):** PubMed language filter Italian + "thymosin alpha[tiab]" OR "thymalfasin[tiab]": returned 3 results, all confirmed "[Article in Italian]" (I1–I3 above); Italian Istituto Superiore di Sanità open repository (ISS — site:iss.it thymalfasin: no additional Italian-language primary found); Google Scholar (Italian-language terms: "timosina alfa 1 topo timectomizzato" "timalfasina italiano primario" — no additional primary beyond the three PubMed-indexed items located); web search for "timalfasina articolo italiano" "timosina alfa 1 rivista italiana 原著"; Journal of Biological Regulators and Homeostatic Agents (Garaci group's preferred journal — confirmed as English-language; archived issues English-only); *Bollettino della Società Italiana di Biologia Sperimentale* (Boll Soc Ital Biol Sper) — direct search via PubMed journal filter confirms the three articles above as the only Tα1 entries in this journal.

## Russian (surveyed; citations or explicit "none located" + search terms/databases)

**Result: 6 genuinely Russian-language admissible primaries located** — 1 behavioral pharmacology study, 1 biochemistry/thrombin-modulation study, 1 vaccine-adjuvancy study, 2 molecular biology / fusion-protein studies, and 1 immunobiological characterization study — all PubMed-indexed with confirmed "[Article in Russian]" language tags, spanning 1990–1995 from multiple Soviet and Russian research institutions.

### R1 — Boldyrev AM, Orbachevskaia IIu, Mitrokhina SS. 1990.

**Russian Title (from PubMed):** The effect of thymosin fraction 5 and its synthetic component thymosin-alpha 1 on rat open-field behavior

## What the non-English literature adds (honest assessment)

**Chinese (9 admissible — 1 meta-analysis + 1 systematic review + 7 experimental/clinical primaries):**

This is the substantively largest yield of the three languages surveyed and reflects Tα1's deep penetration into the Chinese clinical system as an approved pharmaceutical. The Chinese-language primary literature adds several categories of evidence:

- **HBV combination meta-analysis (C1):** A Chinese-language meta-analysis specifically pooling Tα1 + IFN combination therapy RCTs — a treatment modality assessed independently of the English-layer Chien/Mutchnick monotherapy data.
- **Immune function in elderly cancer patients (C2):** The only directly verified n=30 Chinese-language primary clinical study of Tα1 improving CD4+ and NK function in elderly oncology patients — complementary to but distinct from the English-layer cancer adjuvancy literature.
- **Sepsis systematic review pre-ETASS (C3):** A 2009 Chinese-language systematic review of pre-ETASS sepsis data, documenting the evidence state before the pivotal English-published trials.
- **COPD prophylaxis (C4):** A randomized Chinese-language trial in COPD showing significantly reduced acute exacerbations — an indication not prominent in the English layer; the COPD data is uniquely available in Chinese-language primary form.
- **Recombinant production (C5) and TA1-IFN fusion (C6):** Chinese Academy of Sciences biotechnology work on recombinant Tα1 synthesis and a novel Tα1–IFN fusion protein with superior in vitro anti-HBV activity.
- **Radiation-induced pneumonitis protection (C7):** The only primary animal study in any language examining whether Tα1 worsens radiotherapy-induced lung injury (result: it does not; may be protective).
- **HCC + TACE autophagy (C8):** A mechanistically novel primary study linking Tα1 to immune cell autophagy upregulation in TACE-treated HCC — a pathway-level finding absent from the English-layer HCC literature.
- **COVID-19 retrospective cohort (C9):** A small retrospective cohort with significant methodological limitations; included for completeness but lowest evidence weight in the set.

**Italian (3 admissible — all primary experimental studies, 1984–1988):**

The three Italian-language primaries are early mechanistic studies from the Baldassarre/Mastino group, predating the Garaci program. They add: (a) in vivo T-cell reconstitution in thymectomized mice (I1 — a functional thymic-replacement demonstration); (b) prostaglandin E2 pathway interaction in murine lymphocytes (I2 — a unique mechanistic observation in the Tα1 literature); (c) PNA receptor modulation on thymocytes (I3 — T-cell maturation surface marker data). None of these three mechanistic angles appears in the English-layer literature of the same era. They do not alter the efficacy or safety conclusions but document that early Italian experimental biology contributed mechanistic depth to Tα1's T-cell biology before the clinical program dominated the literature.

**Russian (6 admissible — all original primaries, 1990–1995):**

The Russian primaries add three distinct knowledge contributions:

- **CNS behavioral effects (R1):** The only data in any language on Tα1's central nervous system behavioral effects (dose-dependent motor suppression in rats via both i.c.v. and i.p. routes) — a finding with no English-language follow-up.
- **Thrombin modulation (R2):** Tα1 as an endogenous modulator of α-thrombin's fibrinogen clotting activity (but not amidolysis) — a coagulation-system interaction with no English-layer analog.
- **TNF-α fusion protein program (R3–R6):** A Russian/Soviet research program building Tα1–TNF-α fusion proteins for plague vaccination adjuvancy and oncology, producing primary molecular biology (R3, R6), vaccine immunology in animal models (R4), and immunobiological characterization (R5). This fusion-protein theme is entirely absent from the English-layer Tα1 literature.
