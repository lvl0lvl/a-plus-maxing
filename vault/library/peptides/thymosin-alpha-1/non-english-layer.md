# Thymosin Alpha-1 — Non-English Literature Coverage

Unlike most peptides in this library, Tα1's clinical evidence base is **heavily Chinese-authored** — China
is the principal Zadaxin market and the major sepsis/COVID/hepatitis trials come from Chinese centres. This
layer documents the survey.

## What was surveyed and found
The strongest non-English-origin trials are published in **English-language international journals** by Chinese
investigators, so they are admissible and citable **without translation**:
- **TESTS** (the definitive negative sepsis RCT, 22 Chinese centres) — BMJ 2025, PMID 39814420 `[rct]`.
- **ETASS** (earlier sepsis RCT, 6 Chinese ICUs, Sun Yat-sen/Foshan) — Crit Care 2013, PMID 23327199 `[rct]`.
- **2025 sepsis meta-analysis** (Chinese-weighted trial base) — Front Cell Infect Microbiol, PMID 40969554 `[meta_analysis]`.
- **COVID (Wuhan/Chongqing)** — Clin Infect Dis 2020, PMID 32442287 `[cohort]`.
- **Chronic hepatitis B RCT (Kunming, Yunnan)** — J Chin Med Assoc 2005, PMID 15759817 `[rct]`.
- **Chinese Expert Consensus 2025** (protocol-tier) — Infect Microb Dis, DOI 10.1097/IM9.0000000000000176 `[practitioner_protocol]`.

## Translation notes & limits
- All items above carry English abstracts/full text → no machine translation required; numerical claims are
  taken from the English-language record.
- **CNKI / Wanfang / Chinese-only abstracts were NOT exhaustively mined** — those are lower-tier and could not
  be verified to the anti-fabrication standard in this pass. Flagged as `NOT-EXHAUSTIVELY-SEARCHED`; a deeper
  CNKI pass could add lower-tier coverage but is unlikely to change the headline (the definitive evidence is the
  English-published TESTS trial).
- Acta Pharmacologica Sinica / Chinese Medical Journal yielded no additional verified Tα1 RCT primary in this pass.

## Net
The non-English (Chinese) dimension is **material and was surveyed** — and it does not soften the headline: the
single best-quality trial (Chinese-run, English-published TESTS) is the one that turned the sepsis efficacy story null.
