# Retatrutide — Prescribing-Practice Layer

## There is no prescribing practice — it is investigational
Retatrutide is **not approved by any regulator** and has **no prescription or compounding pathway**. There is therefore
**no admissible `practitioner_protocol`**, no on-label dose, and no legitimate clinical prescribing convention. Any
dosing "protocol" circulating online is gray-market/research-chemical and inadmissible.

## Trial-derived dosing (reference only — NOT a prescribing recommendation)
For documentation: the Phase-2/3 trials used **SC once weekly, 1 / 4 / 8 / 12 mg**, with stepwise titration from a low
start dose (e.g. 2 mg) escalated over weeks to limit GI AEs `[rct: PMID 37366315]`. This is the trial regimen, not a
usable protocol — the drug cannot be legitimately or safely obtained outside a trial.

## Doctor-handout note
**Do not source or use retatrutide now.** The correct posture is to **watch the TRIUMPH peer-reviewed publications and
the FDA filing (NDA guided ~Q4 2026)**. If/when approved and fat loss is clinically indicated, re-evaluate then — as a
prescription, with the standard incretin caveats (lean-mass loss, MTC/MEN2 screen, heart-rate monitoring).
