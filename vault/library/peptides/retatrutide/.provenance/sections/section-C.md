## Section C — Beyond-weight + Phase 3 pipeline + concentration audit

> **DEFINING INTEGRITY FRAME.** Retatrutide (LY3437943) is an **INVESTIGATIONAL** once-weekly triple agonist (GIP / GLP-1 / glucagon receptors). It has **NO marketing approval** from FDA, EMA, or any regulator as of this writing (June 2026). Nothing in this section should be read as describing an approved indication. Every population is annotated; every efficacy claim is tagged to the trial and population in which it was generated.

### MASH / NAFLD — the strongest non-weight efficacy signal

The principal beyond-weight evidence is the dedicated hepatic-fat substudy of the Phase 2 obesity program, reported by **Sanyal AJ, Kaplan LM, Frias JP, et al., "Triple hormone receptor agonist retatrutide for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease: a randomized phase 2a trial," *Nature Medicine* 2024;30(7):2037–2048 (PMID 38858523; PMCID PMC11271400; DOI 10.1038/s41591-024-03018-2)** [1].

- **Design and population (annotated):** randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled **Phase 2a** trial in **98 adults** with obesity/overweight and **MASLD/MASH defined by ≥10% liver fat**, *explicitly excluding type 2 diabetes*. Once-weekly subcutaneous retatrutide at **1, 4, 8, or 12 mg vs placebo** for **48 weeks** (primary analysis at 24 weeks). Liver fat measured by MRI-PDFF (the imaging gold standard). `[rct]` [1]
- **Primary endpoint (relative liver-fat change, MRI-PDFF, 24 weeks):** −42.9% (1 mg), −57.0% (4 mg), −81.4% (8 mg), −82.4% (12 mg) vs **+0.3% placebo** (P < 0.001 for all active doses vs placebo). `[rct]` [1]
- **At 48 weeks** relative reductions deepened to approximately −51.3%, −59.0%, −81.7%, and **−86.0%** for the 1/4/8/12 mg arms respectively. `[rct]` [1]
- **Resolution of steatosis (<5% liver fat, 24 weeks):** 27% (1 mg), 52% (4 mg), 79% (8 mg), **86% (12 mg)** vs **0% placebo**. `[rct]` [1]
- **Interpretation discipline:** this is an **MRI-PDFF imaging surrogate** in a **Phase 2a** trial of only 98 participants without diabetes. It is a steatosis (liver-fat) endpoint, **not** a histological MASH-resolution-with-fibrosis endpoint and **not** a hard outcome (cirrhosis, decompensation). The magnitude is large, but the evidence tier remains early-phase and investigational. A registrational MASH program (see pipeline) is required before any liver claim could be made. `[rct]` [1]

### The Phase 3 program — TRIUMPH (enumerated, with verified registrations)

The registrational program is described in the design/rationale paper **Giblin K, Kaplan LM, Somers VK, et al., "Retatrutide for the treatment of obesity, obstructive sleep apnea and knee osteoarthritis: Rationale and design of the TRIUMPH registrational clinical trials," *Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism* 2026 (PMID 41090431; DOI 10.1111/dom.70209)** — a **design/rationale paper that reports NO efficacy outcomes** [2]. Trial-level facts below are taken from ClinicalTrials.gov primary records [3][4]. Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and knee osteoarthritis are studied as **nested sub-protocols within the master/basket trials**, not as separate standalone obesity trials, so the program does not map cleanly onto "one trial per complication."

**Enumerated core TRIUMPH trials (n = 4 master/registrational + 1 outcomes trial; all sponsor = Eli Lilly and Company):**

1. **TRIUMPH-1 — NCT05929066.** Phase 3 master protocol, adults **without T2D** with obesity/overweight + ≥1 weight-related complication (with nested OSA/knee-OA sub-protocols); ~2,339 randomized to retatrutide 4 / 9 / 12 mg vs placebo, 80 weeks (104-week extension for BMI ≥35). **Status: Completed** (primary completion April 2026). `[regulatory]` [3]
2. **TRIUMPH-2 — NCT05929079.** Phase 3 master protocol in adults **with type 2 diabetes** and obesity/overweight (nested OSA sub-protocol); ~1,000 participants; retatrutide 4 / 9 / 12 mg vs placebo. **Status: Active, not recruiting** (primary completion ~May 2026). `[regulatory]` [3]
3. **TRIUMPH-3 — NCT05882045.** Phase 3, adults with **severe obesity and established cardiovascular disease**; ~1,946 participants; retatrutide vs placebo. **Status: Active, not recruiting** (primary completion April 2026). `[regulatory]` [3]
4. **TRIUMPH-4 — NCT05931367.** Phase 3, adults with obesity/overweight **and knee osteoarthritis, without diabetes**; 445 participants randomized 1:1:1 to retatrutide 9 mg, 12 mg, or placebo, 68 weeks. **Status: Completed** (primary completion Nov 2025). `[regulatory]` [3]
5. **TRIUMPH-Outcomes (cardiovascular/kidney outcomes trial) — NCT06383390.** Phase 3, event-driven CVOT in adults with **BMI ≥27 plus atherosclerotic CVD and/or chronic kidney disease**; estimated **~10,000** participants; primary completion estimated **February 2029**. **Status: Active, not recruiting.** This is the trial carrying the **renal (MAKE) and hard cardiovascular (MACE) outcome** endpoints. `[regulatory]` [4]

> **NCT-resolution note (integrity).** Several secondary outlets mis-assigned NCTs across Lilly's metabolic portfolio. In particular **NCT05869903 is *ATTAIN-1*, a Phase 3 trial of orforglipron — a *different* Lilly drug — not a TRIUMPH/retatrutide trial** [3]; one TRIUMPH-4 press release reproduced that NCT in error. The registrations listed above were each opened on ClinicalTrials.gov and confirmed to name retatrutide/LY3437943.

#### Phase 3 efficacy status — what is and is NOT published

As of June 2026, **no TRIUMPH trial has a peer-reviewed primary-outcome publication.** Two readouts exist only as **sponsor topline/press-release announcements** (regulatory/corporate tier, *not* peer-reviewed, not yet presented in full at a scientific congress). Per Lilly, full data for both are slated for "a future medical meeting" (e.g., ADA Scientific Sessions / ObesityWeek) and subsequent peer-reviewed publication:

- **TRIUMPH-1 topline (Lilly press release, 21 May 2026):** at 80 weeks, mean weight reduction 19.0% (4 mg), 25.9% (9 mg), and **28.3% (12 mg)** vs 2.2% placebo; 45.3% of the 12 mg arm achieved ≥30% weight loss; an extension arm reported ~30.3% at 104 weeks. `[regulatory]` [5]
- **TRIUMPH-4 topline (Lilly press release, 11 Dec 2025):** at 68 weeks in obesity + knee-OA, weight reduction 26.4% (9 mg) and **28.7% (12 mg)** vs 2.1% placebo, with WOMAC pain improvements of ~75.8% / 74.3% vs 40.3% placebo. `[regulatory]` [6]

**These press-release numbers are reported here for pipeline-status transparency only and must not be treated as verified efficacy.** They are pre-publication, single-source (the sponsor), and have not undergone peer review or independent congress scrutiny. Common adverse events in both readouts were dose-dependent gastrointestinal effects (nausea, diarrhea, constipation), consistent with the incretin class. `[regulatory]` [5][6]

### Regulatory designation — FDA Fast Track

The brief assumed a possible **FDA Fast Track** designation claim. **This could not be verified.** No primary regulatory source or admissible Tier-1 source confirming an FDA Fast Track (or Breakthrough Therapy) designation for retatrutide was located. **It is therefore reported as UNVERIFIED and is not asserted here.** (For context only, the related Lilly agent tirzepatide received Fast Track for obesity in 2022 — that precedent does **not** establish any designation for retatrutide.) `[regulatory]`

### HFpEF / renal / other exploratory

- **Renal and cardiovascular hard outcomes** are not yet available; they are the dedicated endpoints of the ongoing **TRIUMPH-Outcomes** CVOT (NCT06383390), which does not complete until ~2029 [4]. No renal-outcome efficacy can be claimed today.
- A **dedicated HFpEF (heart-failure-with-preserved-ejection-fraction) registrational trial was not confirmed** in the primary records reviewed; the cardiometabolic risk reduction hypothesis is being tested via the TRIUMPH-Outcomes MACE/MAKE composite rather than a confirmed standalone HFpEF trial. Reported as **not verified**. `[regulatory]` [4]
- The strongest *organ-specific* beyond-weight signal remains the hepatic (MASLD/MASH) Phase 2a data above [1]; a registrational MASH histology trial would be the natural next step but was not confirmed as reporting in the records reviewed.

### Concentration / sponsor audit

This is the defining integrity feature of the retatrutide evidence base and must be stated plainly.

- **Sponsor concentration: 100% Eli Lilly and Company.** Every trial cited — the Phase 2a MASLD study [1], all five TRIUMPH-program registrations (NCT05929066, NCT05929079, NCT05882045, NCT05931367, NCT06383390) [3][4], the design/rationale paper [2], and both topline press releases [5][6] — is sponsored, designed, funded, and analyzed by a **single manufacturer**. There is **zero independent-sponsor replication.**
- **Author/funding concentration:** the Phase 2a MASLD paper [1] lists multiple **Eli Lilly employee co-authors**, and Lilly is credited with study design, execution, data analysis, and manuscript preparation. The TRIUMPH design paper [2] is likewise sponsor-authored. There is no investigator-initiated or government-funded efficacy dataset in the admissible set.
- **Evidence-maturity concentration:** the only large-magnitude organ outcome (liver fat) rests on **one 98-patient Phase 2a trial** with an **imaging surrogate**, and the headline Phase 3 weight results exist **only as sponsor press releases**, not peer-reviewed publications. The evidence base is therefore concentrated not just by sponsor but by **phase and publication tier** — it is early/pre-publication and investigational.
- **Net read:** retatrutide's beyond-weight and Phase 3 story is **promising but entirely single-sponsor, largely pre-publication, and investigational.** No claim of approved benefit, hard-outcome benefit (CV/renal), or histologic MASH benefit is supportable today. Independent replication and peer-reviewed Phase 3/outcome publication are the gating requirements before any of these signals should be elevated.
