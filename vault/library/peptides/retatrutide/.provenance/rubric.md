## THE DEFINING FACTS (integrity axes)
1. **Retatrutide (LY3437943; Eli Lilly) is a TRIPLE agonist — GIP + GLP-1 + GLUCAGON receptor.**
   The glucagon-receptor component (added vs tirzepatide's dual GIP/GLP-1) is the differentiator:
   it raises energy expenditure + has hepatic effects, but also brings glucagon-related safety
   wrinkles (heart-rate increase, hepatic glucose). Acylated, once-weekly SC.
2. **THE CENTRAL INTEGRITY FACT: it is INVESTIGATIONAL — NOT APPROVED anywhere.** The evidence is
   **Phase 2** (the striking ~24% mean weight loss at 48 wk / 12 mg in obesity, Jastreboff 2023
   NEJM — the largest weight-loss signal of any incretin agent yet; Rosenstock 2023 Lancet in
   T2D; a MASH Phase 2). **Phase 3 (TRIUMPH program) is ONGOING with NO published outcome
   results.** Do NOT present Phase-2 results as established/approved efficacy. The gray-market/
   "research-chemical" retatrutide is ESPECIALLY premature (no approved product, only Phase-2
   data, unknown long-term safety).
3. **Honest caveats:** GI AEs (dose-dependent, dominant); the **glucagon-component effects
   (heart-rate increase; transient hepatic/glucose/lipid)**; a transient dose-dependent
   eGFR/skin (cutaneous hyperesthesia) signal reported in Phase 2; NO long-term safety; NO CV
   outcome data; regain-on-discontinuation expected (class). evidence_tier B (one strong Phase 2,
   no approval/Phase 3) / risk_tier experimental (investigational, unapproved, long-term unknown).
