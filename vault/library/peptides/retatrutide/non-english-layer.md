# Retatrutide — Non-English Literature Coverage

## Survey result
**No distinct non-English primary literature located.** Retatrutide (LY3437943) is an Eli Lilly development compound;
its entire evidence base — Phase 1 (Cell Metabolism), Phase 2 (NEJM, Lancet, Nature Medicine), and Phase 3 TRIUMPH
(ClinicalTrials.gov + sponsor/conference reporting) — is **English-language**. Foreign-population participants are
folded into the global multicentre English publications.

## Net
Like the other Western-developed incretins (semaglutide, tirzepatide), retatrutide has **no material non-English
literature gap**. No translation handling required.
