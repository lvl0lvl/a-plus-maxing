# Retatrutide — Non-English Literature Coverage

Scope: Phase 8 non-English-literature survey for **retatrutide** (LY3437943; triple GIP + GLP-1 + glucagon receptor agonist), companion to `vault/library/peptides/retatrutide/research-report.md`. Non-English peer-reviewed sources are admissible under the SAME tier rules as English; primary publication language is noted on every entry. No citation is fabricated; items found only as secondhand mentions with no locatable record are reported as "none located," not cited.

**Critical admissibility distinction enforced throughout.** A study **authored in Japan/China/Russia but published in English** (PubMed/DOI-indexed English article) is NOT a "genuinely non-English source" — it is already reachable by the English layer and is already in the retatrutide corpus. Only works whose **primary publication language is Russian, Chinese, Japanese, etc.** are counted here as genuine non-English primaries. This **non-English-LANGUAGE vs non-English-AUTHOR** distinction is load-bearing for retatrutide, because the program runs international sites (including a dedicated Phase 1 study in Japanese adults to support PMDA submission) but publishes its results in English.

**Headline result: ZERO genuinely non-English-LANGUAGE admissible retatrutide primaries located.** Retatrutide's entire primary evidence base is **English-published and Eli Lilly-sponsored** (NEJM, Lancet, Nature Medicine, NCT registrations). The global Phase 3 **TRIUMPH** program runs international sites and includes a dedicated **Phase 1 study in Japanese adults** for PMDA support, but reports its results in English. There is therefore **no separate non-English primary evidence stream to integrate** for retatrutide; what exists in other languages is **derivative or machine-translated secondary content**, not independent non-English-language primary research.
