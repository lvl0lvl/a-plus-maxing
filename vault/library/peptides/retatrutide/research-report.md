# Retatrutide (triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon agonist; LY3437943; Eli Lilly — INVESTIGATIONAL)

## Metadata
- **Class:** peptide — single acylated peptide agonizing THREE receptors (GIP + GLP-1 + glucagon)
- **Evidence tier:** **B** — one strong Phase-2 RCT base (NEJM/Lancet/Nature Medicine) + Phase-3 TRIUMPH **topline only**; pivotal results not yet peer-reviewed.
- **Risk tier:** **moderate** — Phase-2 safety only (~24–48 wk); a distinctive dose-dependent heart-rate signal; no long-term data.
- **Binding constraint:** **INVESTIGATIONAL — not approved anywhere; no legitimate sourcing.** See §6.

## TL;DR

Retatrutide is the **highest-efficacy weight-loss agent yet reported** — a single peptide hitting GIP, GLP-1, **and
glucagon** receptors, where the added glucagon arm raises energy expenditure and drives hepatic fat oxidation on top of
incretin appetite suppression. Phase 2 gave **~24.2% weight loss at 48 weeks (12 mg) and the curve had not plateaued**;
liver fat fell **>80%**; Phase 3 TRIUMPH-1 topline reports ~28% at 80 weeks (press/conference-stage, **not yet
peer-reviewed**). **But the binding fact for the operator is sourcing: retatrutide is INVESTIGATIONAL — not approved by
any regulator, no prescription or compounding pathway exists, so any "retatrutide" for sale is gray-market/
research-chemical with no purity, dose, or pharmacovigilance assurance.** It also shares the incretin **goal mismatch**
(it *reduces* lean mass; no muscle-building/recovery RCT) and carries a characteristic **dose-dependent heart-rate
increase** (peaks ~+6.7 bpm at wk 24, then attenuates) plus thin long-term safety. The correct posture is **wait for
approval / the TRIUMPH peer-reviewed readout** — not acquisition.

## 1. Identity & Mechanism

- **What it is:** a single acylated 39-residue peptide agonizing **GIPR + GLP-1R + glucagon receptor (GCGR)**; potency
  rank is **GIP-dominant** (GIPR > GLP-1R > GCGR) `[in_vitro/mechanism_review: Coskun, Cell Metab 2022, PMID 35985340]`.
  Fatty-acid acylation → albumin binding → once-weekly dosing.
- **Mechanism — the glucagon arm is the differentiator:** GIP/GLP-1 suppress caloric intake and improve glycemia; the
  added **GCGR agonism raises energy expenditure and hepatic fat oxidation** — the "energy-out + energy-in suppression"
  combination thought to explain the class-leading weight loss `[animal: Coskun 2022; rct: Sanyal Nat Med 2024, PMID 38858523]`.
- **Caveat of the glucagon arm:** GCGR agonism intrinsically raises hepatic glucose output, so net glucose-lowering
  depends on the incretin arms outweighing it (they did, in trials) — but it is why glucose + heart-rate monitoring are flagged.

## 2. Human Clinical Evidence (all Phase 2 except Phase-3 topline)

### 2.1 Obesity (goal-relevant)
- **Jastreboff (NEJM 2023, PMID 37366315), Phase 2, n=338, 48 wk:** **−24.2% at 12 mg vs −2.1% placebo**; 83% reached
  ≥15% loss; **weight loss had NOT plateaued at 48 wk.** `[rct]`

### 2.2 Type 2 diabetes
- **Rosenstock (Lancet 2023, PMID 37385280), Phase 2, n=281:** HbA1c **−2.02%** (placebo-adjusted, 24 wk); weight
  **−16.94%** (36 wk); no severe hypoglycemia. `[rct]`
- **Body composition (Coskun, Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2025, DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(25)00092-0; DXA, n=189 enrolled /
  ~103 with paired scans):** total fat −26.1% (−10.9 kg), visceral −31.4% — **but lean mass also fell up to −6.5 kg**
  (the absolute lean loss is real; the lean-to-total-weight-loss ratio was comparable to tirzepatide — i.e. proportionate,
  but still **not muscle-building/sparing**). `[rct substudy]`

### 2.3 MASLD / hepatic fat
- **Sanyal (Nat Med 2024, PMID 38858523), Phase 2a, n=98:** hepatic fat **−81.4% (8 mg) / −82.4% (12 mg)** vs +0.3%
  placebo at 24 wk; >85% reached normal liver fat by 48 wk. `[rct]`

### 2.4 Phase 3 TRIUMPH — TOPLINE ONLY (not peer-reviewed)
- **TRIUMPH-1 (NCT05929066), n=2,339, 80 wk:** topline ~**−28.3% at 12 mg** vs −3.9% placebo (Lilly press / ADA 2026).
  **Pivotal full peer-reviewed publication NOT yet available** — treat as topline. `[regulatory/press-stage]`
- **TRIUMPH-Outcomes (NCT06383390):** ~5-yr CV/kidney outcomes trial — ongoing, **no efficacy readout.** `[regulatory]`

### 2.5 Lean-mass building / recovery (the operator's goal)
- **NO ADMISSIBLE PRIMARY FOUND.** No RCT for muscle building, recovery, or lean-mass gain; the only controlled
  body-comp data show retatrutide *causes* lean loss (~6.5 kg). Goal mismatch (verified absence).

## 3. Safety (Phase 2 horizon only — NO approved label, NO long-term data)

- **Common AEs:** dose-dependent **GI** (nausea up to ~60% at 12 mg, vomiting ~26%, diarrhea ~15%, constipation ~16%);
  mitigated by lower start dose `[rct: PMID 37366315]`.
- **Heart-rate signal (distinctive):** dose-dependent HR increase **peaking ~+6.7 bpm at week 24, then attenuating**
  toward baseline by wk 36–48 — partly glucagon-driven `[rct: PMID 37366315]`.
- **Cutaneous hyperesthesia ~7% vs ~1% placebo** — a retatrutide-specific skin-sensory AE not typical of single GLP-1 agents `[rct]`.
- **Serious AEs ~4%** (= placebo at Phase 2 horizon); MASLD substudy 1 cholecystitis `[rct]`.
- **Glucose:** net improvement despite glucagon agonism (incretin arms dominate); still warrants monitoring `[rct]`.
- **Thyroid C-cell:** only a **class/rodent** caution (MTC/MEN2 by extension); **no retatrutide-specific human data**, and
  no boxed warning exists yet (no label). `[mechanism_review — class-level]`
- **Long-term safety: NO ADMISSIBLE PRIMARY FOUND** beyond ~48 wk; CV/durability depends on TRIUMPH-Outcomes (years away).
- **Anti-doping:** retatrutide is **NOT separately listed** by WADA (only semaglutide/tirzepatide are on the 2026
  Monitoring Program; none prohibited). A "GLP-1s moved to S4 prohibition" rumor is **unsupported** by the official docs. `[regulatory]`

## 4. The Sourcing Caveat (FIRST-CLASS) + the goal mismatch

**This is the entry's defining caveat, and it overrides the impressive efficacy.** Retatrutide is **investigational —
not FDA/EMA-approved (NDA guided ~Q4 2026; review 2027+)**. There is **no legitimate prescription or compounding
pathway**: because it is unapproved it is not even on the FDA shortage/compounding list, so every "retatrutide" sold
online or by gray-market compounders/research-chemical vendors is **outside the regulated supply chain — unverified
identity/purity/dose, no GMP, no pharmacovigilance.** For an operator whose posture is legitimacy + safety, the answer
is **wait for approval / the TRIUMPH peer-reviewed readout**, not acquisition. Secondarily, it shares the incretin
**goal mismatch** (it reduces lean mass; no recovery/muscle RCT) — off-target for post-illness rebuilding regardless.

## 5. Pharmacokinetics & Dosing (trial-derived — NO approved label)

- **Dosing (trial):** SC once weekly, **1 / 4 / 8 / 12 mg** with stepwise titration (low start, e.g. 2 mg, escalated to
  limit GI AEs) `[rct: PMID 37366315]`.
- **PK:** terminal half-life **~6 days** (range ~5–7) → once-weekly; Tmax ~1–3 days; approximately dose-proportional
  `[rct: Urva, Lancet 2022, PMID 36354040; Coskun 2022]`.

---

## 6. Regulatory & Sourcing

- **INVESTIGATIONAL — not approved by FDA or EMA** (as of mid-2026); legally obtainable only via Lilly clinical trials.
  Phase 3 TRIUMPH reading out topline 2025–2026; NDA guided ~Q4 2026. `[regulatory]`
- **No legitimate sourcing** (see §4) — gray-market only, with all the attendant quality/safety unknowns; FDA enforcement
  against unapproved weight-loss marketers is active.
- **Non-English:** trials are Lilly-sponsored, English-published — **no distinct non-English primary located.**
- **Prescribing-practice:** no admissible practitioner protocol (it's investigational); trial dosing is the only reference.

---

## 7. Bottom line for the operator

The most powerful weight-loss molecule in development — but **not a candidate for use now**: investigational, no legal
or quality-assured supply, Phase-3 results still topline/unpublished, thin long-term safety, a notable heart-rate
signal, and the same lean-mass/goal mismatch as the approved incretins. **Posture: watch the TRIUMPH publications and
the FDA filing; do not source it.** If, after approval, fat loss is clinically indicated, re-evaluate then — as a
prescription, with resistance training + protein + MTC/MEN2 screen.
