## ADR-0034: Comprehensive Intake Content + De-Identification Mapping + Safety-Signal Routing

> **Y-Statement:** In the context of the live plan-generation engine whose de-identified `router.summarize` summary feeds the no-train planner ONLY closed-field-set tokens, where 5 of those tokens (`rx-interaction-classes`, `training-volume-band`, `dietary-pattern-class`, `supplement-stack-class`, `peptide-use-class`) fall to no-signal defaults because no live intake surface feeds their source — so the plan is generic — facing the tension between collecting a comprehensive, clinical-grade intake (which captures the most sensitive raw PII the system holds: medications, mental-health, substance use, race) and the crown-jewel boundary that lets ONLY de-identified tokens reach that planner. We decided to specify a field→destination CONTRACT under which every intake field lands in exactly one of (a) a de-identified planner token — WIRING the existing tokens, with NO `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` extension — (b) a safety-gate / medical-referral signal that is never a plan input, or (c) a raw-local-de-identified record, with raw medications captured RECORD-ONLY and curated into `rx-interaction-classes` by the care-agent (ADR-0035) rather than serve-auto-classified or user-self-classified, a NEW exercise-safety contraindication screen routing chest-pain/dizziness to a hard-training veto plus a medical-referral flag, and PHQ-2 / apnea routing to medical-referral, to achieve a non-generic, state-aware plan while the operator's IDENTITY and the name↔med linkage stay 100% local and safety signals route to the gates locally — raw PII held locally with ONE named, operator-signed-off carve-out: the ADR-0035 meds curation egresses DE-ASSOCIATED raw drug names (no identity attached) to the no-train model for interaction-class curation, on the ADR-0032 de-association principle — accepting a longer and more sensitive intake, a hard dependency on the ADR-0035 care-agent for medication-interaction awareness in the first plan (the same step where de-associated raw drug names leave the machine for class curation), and the keyword-bucket coarseness of mapping structured selections onto the existing derivers.

### Context

The plan-generation engine reasons only over a de-identified summary: `router.summarize` derives a name-addressable summary whose keys are drawn from the closed `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET`, and `dispatch` raises on any payload field outside that set before the model send — the load-bearing whitelist `set(payload) ⊆ SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` ([router.py:23-56](../../scripts/plan/router.py), [router.py:742-802](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]). The field-set carries 17 tokens [VERIFIED: import count]. Five of them — `rx-interaction-classes`, `training-volume-band`, `dietary-pattern-class`, `supplement-stack-class`, `peptide-use-class` ([router.py:40,47-49](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]) — are present in the summary but carry no live signal because no surface feeds their backing source. The four chat-fed tokens (`training-volume-band`, `dietary-pattern-class`, `supplement-stack-class`, `peptide-use-class`) fall to the ABSENT-source sentinel `not-discussed` (`_NOT_DISCUSSED`, [router.py:321](../../scripts/plan/router.py); returned at [router.py:452,477,501,533](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]), and `rx-interaction-classes` falls to the empty no-meds default `""` ([router.py:231](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]) until the care-agent curation runs. The present-but-unmatched derivation defaults — `general-diet` ([router.py:463](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]), supplement/peptide `none` ([router.py:480,486](../../scripts/plan/router.py) / [router.py:504,509](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]), and `moderate` ([router.py:547,552](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]) — apply only once a source IS captured but no keyword matches, so they are NOT the live current default. Their derivation plumbing already exists — `capture._CHAT_RAW_SOURCE_FIELDS` maps form fields to the named-excluded raw items `summarize` de-identifies ([capture.py:94-99](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]) — but the served surface submits no rich-domain field, and the chat domains for nutrition/supplements/peptides resolve to the empty mapped set so no raw-source readings exist ([chat.py:40-46](../../scripts/serve/chat.py) [VERIFIED]). The operator-observed effect (generic plans) is therefore real, but the precise framing is that these tokens fall to the absent-source `not-discussed` / empty `""` defaults, not that they are "missing from the field set" — the tokens are always present [VERIFIED].

Several forces pull against each other across more than one stakeholder. The operator needs a plan that reflects real diet, training volume, supplements, and medication-interaction surface rather than ignoring them — a comprehensive intake feeds that signal. The data subject's crown-jewel containment binds the second force in the opposite direction: a comprehensive clinical intake captures the most sensitive PII the system holds (raw medication names, the PHQ-2 mood screen, substance use, race/ethnicity), and ADR-0001's boundary admits ONLY de-identified tokens to the no-train planner, with raw PII held locally ([ADR-0001 Decision](ADR-0001-pii-trust-boundary-no-train-routing.md) [VERIFIED]) on a clonable PII-free trunk ([ADR-0005 Decision](ADR-0005-operator-agnostic-clonable-pii-free-trunk.md) [VERIFIED]). Growing the captured-field set must not grow what crosses the boundary. The maintainer's clinical-safety force binds a third: a raw drug name is not a token the serve layer can de-identify into an interaction class — there is no pharmacology lookup in serve-layer code by design, because an incomplete drug map would silently produce a false-clean ([router.py:144-153](../../scripts/plan/router.py), [capture.py:65-72](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]) — and a positive exercise-safety or mood-screen answer is a referral signal, not plan content. Because this sets the field→destination contract the boundary rests on (which fields are collected at all, which become de-identified tokens, which become safety signals, and which stay raw-local), it is a decision, not a markup detail.

### Decision

Adopt a field→destination contract in which every collected intake field routes to exactly one of three destinations: (a) a de-identified `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` planner token, by WIRING the existing tokens — no `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` extension is added; (b) a safety-gate or medical-referral signal that never enters a plan payload; or (c) a raw-local, de-identified-before-any-model-sees-it record. Activity (§8 days/minutes/type) feeds `training-volume-band`, dietary preferences/restrictions (§7) feed `dietary-pattern-class`, supplements (§14) feed `supplement-stack-class`, and peptides (added) feed `peptide-use-class` — each through its existing named-excluded raw-source item and `summarize` deriver. Food and drug allergies (§7/§14) route to `hard-limits`; demographics (sex, equipment, goals) ride the tokens already wired (ADR-0018), and DOB + bodyweight are AMENDED below. **[AMENDED 2026-06-30, operator-directed]:** the DOB and bodyweight mappings change from COARSE de-identified BANDS to ACTUAL VALUES — bands are too coarse to be useful (a weight RANGE cannot track weight-loss progress, and birth-YEAR alone is ±1yr on age, wrong for dosing). **Birthdate** → the ACTUAL full DOB is captured and stays 100% LOCAL (a full birthdate is a strong quasi-identifier; it never egresses); the planner receives the **EXACT AGE** computed from the DOB, DE-ASSOCIATED (a number, not the DOB) — superseding `training-age-band` (born-decade) for the age signal. **Bodyweight** → the ACTUAL weight is captured as a dated LOCAL TIME-SERIES (this enables weight-loss tracking — the dashboard charts the real trend); the planner receives the **actual current weight + trend direction**, DE-ASSOCIATED (no identity) — superseding `bodyweight-band` for the weight signal. Consistent with the de-association principle (the crown jewel protects IDENTITY + the identity↔fact linkage, not the precision of a de-associated health fact — the same call as the de-associated meds egress and the ADR-0032 de-associated variant queries). Whether `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` gains an actual-value age/weight token or the existing band tokens are repurposed/threaded is a build/spec detail (OQ-5), not specified here, and is DISTINCT from (does not relax) the five-default-fed-token no-extension guarantee. Raw medications (§14) are captured RECORD-ONLY and curated into `rx-interaction-classes` by the care-agent (ADR-0035), never serve-auto-classified and never user-self-classified. A NEW exercise-safety contraindication screen (§8 chest-pain/dizziness/shortness-of-breath during exercise), distinct from the existing `clearance_granted` load gate, vetoes hard-training content and raises a medical-referral flag on a positive answer. PHQ-2 (§11) routes to a mental-health referral flag and apnea (§10) to a medical-referral flag — never plan inputs. Sleep, stress, substance use, race/ethnicity, and occupation are raw-local de-identified records. Education, sexual orientation, marital status, environmental exposures, and health literacy are trimmed (not collected); race/ethnicity is kept (dosing + research population-matching). Editing a field in My-Info re-runs the same capture→de-identify path, so an edit cannot bypass the boundary. The new screens' pixel design is a separate downstream step, out of scope here.

### Rationale

The five default-fed tokens fail not because the field set lacks them but because no surface feeds their source ([chat.py:40-46](../../scripts/serve/chat.py) [VERIFIED]). Four — `dietary-pattern-class`, `supplement-stack-class`, `peptide-use-class`, `training-volume-band` — already have raw-source items and derivers in place ([capture.py:94-99](../../scripts/serve/capture.py), [router.py:116-125,560-568](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]); the structured intake simply submits those fields, so the contribution is WIRING, and `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` / the `dispatch` whitelist / `summarize` stay byte-unchanged (extend-not-rebuild — confirmed against the live field set, no ADR-0019-style token addition is needed) [VERIFIED]. This is the precedent ADR-0019 set when it fed its tokens ([ADR-0019 Decision](ADR-0019-summary-field-set-extension-chat-facts.md) [VERIFIED]).

The medication path is the one place the brief and the live architecture diverged, and the live architecture wins on clinical safety. The serve layer deliberately refuses to derive an interaction class from a raw drug name: `capture.py` routes the rx form field record-only because "a raw drug name + dose would route verbatim into the model-bound item, and the `pii_scan` value gate does not catch drug names" ([capture.py:65-72](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]), and `router.py` states "de-identification (drug name → interaction class) is an operator/liaison CURATION step at the store layer, NOT a code lookup: no pharmacology DB enters this module, and an incomplete drug map can never produce a false-clean" ([router.py:144-153](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]). The contract therefore routes raw meds to a record and assigns the curation to the care-agent (ADR-0035), which proposes class tokens and confirms with the operator when unsure — preserving the existing supplement↔Rx BPMH screen's consumer (`rx_interaction_class_set`, [router.py:180-197](../../scripts/plan/router.py); the reconciler's behavior-5 BPMH hold, [orchestrate.py reconcile](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]) without ever trusting serve-layer code to classify a drug.

The two safety screens follow the established asymmetric-downside gate pattern rather than inventing a new mechanism. The existing safety inputs are a STATE-driven veto (`red_s_lea_screen`, a whole-domain pre-translation short-circuit to clinical-care routing, [generate_plan.py:260-300](../../scripts/plan/generate_plan.py) [VERIFIED]) and a default-deny content gate (`clearance_granted`, which drops every `load` prescription until a real clinician clearance, [generate_plan.py:141-153](../../scripts/plan/generate_plan.py) [VERIFIED]). The exercise-safety contraindication screen is modeled on the `red_s_lea_screen` STATE veto — a positive chest-pain/dizziness answer is a far stronger signal than the default-deny clearance posture, so it both vetoes hard-training content and raises a medical-referral flag, which `clearance_granted` does not. PHQ-2 and apnea carry no plan-relevant content at all, so they route only to referral — the same honest-data principle that keeps the engine from fabricating a regimen ([generate_plan.py module docstring](../../scripts/plan/generate_plan.py) [VERIFIED]).

### Consequences

**Positive:**
- The five default-fed tokens get real signal: a filled intake feeds `dietary-pattern-class` / `supplement-stack-class` / `peptide-use-class` / `training-volume-band` from the structured rich-domain capture (via the existing raw-source items + derivers, [capture.py:94-99](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]) and `rx-interaction-classes` from the care-agent curation — so the plan reflects the operator's diet, training volume, stack, and medication-interaction surface instead of no-signal defaults.
- No `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` extension: the five tokens already exist ([router.py:40,47-49](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]), so this wires them and the frozen `dispatch` whitelist / `summarize` contract is unchanged — extend-not-rebuild verified against the live field set.
- The crown-jewel boundary holds on the identity + planner prongs while the captured-field set grows: the operator's IDENTITY (name, DOB) and the name↔med linkage stay 100% local, race / PHQ-2 / substance use stay 100% local, and only de-identified tokens cross to the no-train PLANNER — the `dispatch` whitelist plus the `summarize` 8j6 PII gate ([router.py:732-737](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]) remain the runtime backstops. The ONE carve-out is the ADR-0035 meds curation: DE-ASSOCIATED raw drug names (no identity, no name↔med linkage) egress to the no-train model for interaction-class curation as a named, operator-signed-off egress class ([ADR-0035 Decision](ADR-0035-care-agent-intake-review.md) [VERIFIED]), and no raw drug name reaches the PLANNER (only the curated `rx-interaction-classes` class token does). **[AMENDED 2026-06-30, operator-directed]:** the full DOB stays 100% local (a full birthdate is a classic quasi-identifier — it never egresses); the planner now receives the **EXACT AGE** (computed from the DOB, de-associated) and the **actual current weight + trend** (de-associated), superseding the former `training-age-band` born-decade and `bodyweight-band` coarse bands. Per the de-association principle the crown jewel protects identity + the identity↔fact linkage, not the precision of a de-associated health fact — so a more precise de-associated age/weight fact reaching the planner does NOT widen the identity boundary (the same call as the de-associated meds egress and the ADR-0032 de-associated variant queries).
- Safety signals are honestly separated from plan inputs: chest-pain/dizziness, PHQ-2, and apnea route to a contraindication veto / medical-referral, never into plan prose — the asymmetric-downside posture matching the existing `clearance_granted` / `red_s_lea_screen` gates.
- Editable My-Info cannot bypass the boundary: an edit re-runs the single `persist_capture` → `summarize` path ([capture.py:230-300](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]), re-de-identifying the edited field — there is no second write path that could skip the data-class routing.

**Negative:**
- A longer, higher-friction intake: the comprehensive clinical content is many more questions than ADR-0018's minimal demographics form, a completion-rate cost, and it re-balances the form/chat split the ADR-0018 set established (a standing topology-classification burden for every future field).
- A hard ordering dependency on the care-agent (ADR-0035) for medication awareness: `rx-interaction-classes` is NOT filled by intake, so the FIRST plan — generated before the care-agent review runs — ships with the empty no-Rx token and the supplement↔Rx BPMH screen is blind to the operator's medications until curation completes. A medication interaction the operator typed at intake is not screened against until ADR-0035 has run. Because `rx-interaction-classes` is filled ONLY post-unlock by the ADR-0035 review (which the unlock itself triggers), it is NOT a directly-captured intake token, so ADR-0033's first-run completeness gate MUST EXCLUDE it (and any other post-unlock-filled token) from the unlock-REQUIRED set — requiring it would deadlock the gate, since it is provably empty until after the save that unlocks ([ADR-0033 Decision / OQ-2](ADR-0033-first-run-intake-gate-and-shell.md) [VERIFIED]).
- A larger sensitive-data-capture surface even though local: capturing raw medications, PHQ-2 responses, substance use, and race creates a local store of highly sensitive PII whose only guards are the gitignored scaffold + `block-pii-commit` + the pre-push scan; a backup or clone of the operator's machine carries it. The boundary is unchanged, but the at-rest sensitive surface grows.
- Keyword-bucket coarseness on the wiring: the existing derivers (`_dietary_pattern_class` etc.) are keyword bucketers ([router.py:434-552](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]), so a structured bounded selection that does not hit a keyword falls to the no-signal default — the structured intake fields must be mapped to land on the buckets (OQ-1), a wiring-fidelity cost.

**Neutral:**
- The structured rich-domain capture writes the SAME named-excluded raw-source store items the chat path writes (`raw-nutrition-free-text`, etc., [capture.py:94-99](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]) — chat and form converge on one derivation, not two parallel sources.
- Race/ethnicity is kept as a raw-local record for dosing + research population-matching (the operator-profile Ancestry axis, [vault/meta/operator-profile.md](../../vault/meta/operator-profile.md) [VERIFIED]); it informs local population-matching, not a planner token — no `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` race token is added.

### Alternatives Considered

#### Alternative A: Serve-layer auto-derive raw drug name → interaction class
Add a pharmacology lookup in serve-layer code so an entered medication is automatically classified into `rx-interaction-classes` at capture time.
- **Supporting evidence:** Simplest operator experience — meds entered once and the planner's interaction surface is populated immediately, with no care-agent dependency; the first plan would be Rx-aware.
- **Trade-offs:** An incomplete or stale drug map silently produces a FALSE-CLEAN — a missed interaction reads as "no interaction," a clinical-safety failure the live architecture explicitly refuses ([router.py:144-153](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]); and the serve layer cannot de-identify a raw drug name (the `pii_scan` value gate does not catch drug names, [capture.py:65-72](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]), so a raw drug name could route verbatim into the model-bound token. Rejected on clinical safety and PII-boundary grounds.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** Only if a validated, complete, maintained pharmacology interaction database ran locally with a clinician-vetted update channel — then on-device classification could supersede the curation step.

#### Alternative B: User self-classifies medications into interaction classes
The operator selects the interaction class for each medication from a bounded list, keeping raw drug names local and feeding the planner a class token without a care-agent.
- **Supporting evidence:** Raw drug names stay 100% local; the planner gets a de-identified class token with no model curation; reuses the existing bounded-enum capture machinery.
- **Trade-offs:** Error-prone — a layperson cannot reliably map a drug to its interaction class, and a wrong or omitted class is the same false-clean clinical-safety risk as Alternative A, now sourced from operator guesswork. The curation requires clinical judgment. Rejected as the sole path.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** As an operator-confirmation step layered ON TOP of the care-agent's proposal (the ADR-0035 design — confirm-when-unsure), never as the originating classifier.

#### Alternative C: Record-only meds, no planner token at all
Capture raw medications to the local record and never derive `rx-interaction-classes` from them — the token stays at its empty no-Rx default permanently.
- **Supporting evidence:** Maximal privacy — raw meds stay 100% local with zero curation surface and zero new information flow; the empty token is already the safe default.
- **Trade-offs:** Defeats the purpose of capturing meds — the supplement↔Rx BPMH screen ([orchestrate.py reconcile behavior 5](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py), [router.py:180-197](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]) has no operator Rx surface to screen against, so supplement and peptide recommendations ship with no medication-interaction awareness. Rejected: the interaction screen is exactly why meds are collected.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** When the operator declines to share medications — then the empty `rx-interaction-classes` token IS the honest no-Rx state (already the default), and no curation is owed.

#### Alternative D: Raw-local record → care-agent curation (Chosen)
Capture raw meds record-only; the care-agent (ADR-0035) curates them into `rx-interaction-classes`, confirming with the operator when unsure.
- **Supporting evidence:** Keeps raw drug names out of serve-layer classification and out of the PLANNER payload, never trusts serve code to classify a drug, avoids the false-clean failure mode, and feeds the existing BPMH consumer a clinically-curated token. The only option satisfying clinical safety AND a populated interaction surface AND the identity/planner boundary — the curation it delegates to ADR-0035 egresses DE-ASSOCIATED raw drug names (no identity attached) to the no-train model as a named, operator-signed-off egress class, while the operator's identity and the name↔med linkage stay 100% local.
- **Trade-offs:** The first plan before curation has no Rx awareness (a hard ADR-0035 dependency, owned in Consequences), and the curation it delegates egresses de-associated raw drug names to the no-train model (the named, signed-off ADR-0035 egress class, no identity attached). Accepted because the false-clean risk of the alternatives is asymmetrically worse than a delayed-but-correct interaction surface, and the egress is de-associated and operator-signed-off.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** It is, for V1, while no validated local pharmacology database exists.

#### Dissent: The minimal-intake position (inherited from ADR-0018)
**Source:** ADR-0018's form/chat split, which moved the rich sections OUT of a rigid form into conversation precisely because a fixed form is lossy and burdensome ([ADR-0018 Decision](ADR-0018-form-chat-intake-split.md) [VERIFIED]). A minimalist holds that a comprehensive structured intake re-introduces the friction ADR-0018 removed and adds a sensitive-data-capture surface, and that pure-chat elicitation of the rich domains is the lower-burden path.
**Conditions under which this becomes the right choice:** If operator-use evidence shows the comprehensive intake's completion rate is low or its friction outweighs the plan-quality gain, the rich-domain structured capture should be retracted back to chat elicitation (the ADR-0018 direction), keeping only the safety screens and demographics in the structured form.

### Validation Approach

**Confirmation criteria:**
- A filled intake produces non-default values for the serve-fillable tokens: with the operator providing diet, training, supplement, and peptide data, `dietary-pattern-class` ≠ `general-diet`/`not-discussed`, `supplement-stack-class` ≠ `none`/`not-discussed`, `peptide-use-class` ≠ `none`/`not-discussed`, and `training-volume-band` ≠ `not-discussed` — expected **≥4 of 4** serve-fillable tokens non-default; `rx-interaction-classes` becomes non-empty only after the ADR-0035 care-agent curation, so **≥4 of 5** of the previously-default-fed tokens carry real signal after intake + care-agent review.
- Safety signals never reach the planner: scan the dispatched payload after a filled intake — expected **0** occurrences of the PHQ-2 response, the apnea answer, the chest-pain/dizziness answer, or any raw drug name as a `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` token or payload field; the `dispatch` whitelist ([router.py:777](../../scripts/plan/router.py) [VERIFIED]) rejects any out-of-set field.
- Editable re-de-identification: editing a field and re-saving re-runs `persist_capture`; an edited raw medication is re-routed record-only and never produces an `rx-interaction-classes` write from the serve path — expected the edited field re-derives through the same path with **0** new write surfaces.

**Falsification criteria (quantitative; the crown-jewel probe is load-bearing):**
- Crown-jewel probe: seed a known raw-PII token (legal name, DOB digits, a raw drug name, a PHQ-2 free-text answer, a race string) into a filled intake, build the summary, and inspect the `dispatch` payload. Threshold — **0 raw-PII tokens in the dispatched payload** and the whitelist rejects any out-of-set field; ≥1 raw field crossing reds the boundary → halt and repair before release. **[AMENDED 2026-06-30, operator-directed]:** the full DOB never appears in `router.summarize` output or any model request → **FAIL** if ≥1 full-DOB string crosses; the planner DOES receive the **EXACT AGE** (a de-associated number computed from the DOB) and the **actual current weight + trend** (de-associated) — those are EXPECTED de-associated facts, NOT a raw-PII crossing, so they do not red the probe (the probe reds only on the full DOB, a raw drug name, the PHQ-2 free-text answer, or a race string crossing).
- Serve-auto-classification probe: enter a raw medication name at intake and scan the store for an `rx-interaction-classes` write originating from the serve capture path. Threshold — **0 `rx-interaction-classes` store writes from `persist_capture`** (the raw drug routes record-only); ≥1 means serve auto-classified meds (the rejected Alternative A) → halt and restore the record-only routing.
- Generic-plan falsification: with a complete intake AND a completed care-agent review, count the five tokens still at their no-signal default for data the operator provided. Threshold — **0 of the 5 tokens stuck at a no-signal default when the operator supplied that domain's data**; ≥1 means the wiring did not connect → investigate the field→token path before release.
- Safety-bypass falsification: a positive exercise-safety, PHQ-2, or apnea answer that yields NO medical-referral flag, OR a positive exercise-safety answer that does not veto hard-training content, OR any of the three appearing as a plan input. Threshold — **0 safety-signal bypasses**; ≥1 reds the safety contract → halt.
- Time horizon: run the crown-jewel probe, the serve-auto-classification probe, the generic-plan check, and the safety-bypass check at the first build of the intake wiring, at the operator-present LIVE run, at every release thereafter, and before the July-2026 physician-visit deliverable.

**Review triggers:**
- A new intake field is added — classify its destination (de-identified token / safety signal / raw-local) per the ADR-0018 form/chat topology discipline before it is added.
- A change to `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET`, `router.summarize`, the `dispatch` whitelist, or `persist_capture` — re-confirm the field→destination contract and the extend-not-rebuild guarantee hold.
- The care-agent (ADR-0035) curation surface landing — confirm meds become `rx-interaction-classes` by curation, never by serve-auto-classification.
- A new safety-screen question — route it to a gate or referral flag, never a planner token.
