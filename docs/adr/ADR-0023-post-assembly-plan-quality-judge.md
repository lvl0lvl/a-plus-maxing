## ADR-0023: Adopt a Post-Assembly Plan-Quality Judge

> **Y-Statement:** In the context of the operator-confirmed S91 plan engine, whose inner pipeline assembles a multi-domain plan from roster specialists and runs every claim through attribution / sourcing / population-mismatch / fail-closed-HALT filters ([`assemble.py:278-360`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py)) before `run_generation` records and surfaces it ([`pipeline.py:28-65`](../../scripts/plan/pipeline.py)), facing the tension that plan QUALITY today rests only on each specialist's self-quality plus the cross-domain reconciliation pass — there is no independent gate that reads the WHOLE assembled plan for followability, coherence, internal consistency, and completeness before the operator sees it, and the build pipeline already proved an independent judge catches what a producer misses — we decided to adopt a post-assembly plan-quality judge that scores the assembled plan against a plan-quality rubric between assembly and surfacing and drives a bounded revise loop on a quality miss, mirroring the build pipeline's produce→judge→revise pattern and wrapping the inner engine unchanged, to achieve a verified whole-plan quality floor no specialist self-assessment can launder past, accepting added per-plan latency and dispatch cost, that the judge can reject a clinically-safe plan on quality grounds alone, and that the revise loop must be bounded (≤N then escalate) so a non-converging plan cannot loop forever.

### Context

The plan engine's inner pipeline is built and wired. `assemble(goal_set, summary, roster)` routes each in-scope goal-domain to its roster specialist and composes the outputs into one attributed document, running EVERY emitted claim through the same four filters over one canonical claim set — attribution, sourcing-completeness, population-mismatch, and the fail-closed class-aware HALT filter ([`assemble.py:278-360`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py) [VERIFIED]). `orchestrate.generate_plans` reconciles across domains (energy bounce, RED-S/LEA short-circuit, overlap/conflict, additive-AE, Rx-BPMH) and `run_generation` records the survivors plus the collated doctor-visit queue, then the plan is surfaced ([`orchestrate.py:1-55`](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py), [`pipeline.py:28-65`](../../scripts/plan/pipeline.py) [VERIFIED]). ADR-0022 supersedes the runtime that DRIVES this engine with a programmatic orchestrator ([ADR-0022, Decision](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]).

Two forces pull against each other. The merit of the status quo is that quality is owned where the evidence lives: each specialist self-asserts the depth and sourcing of its own section, and the reconciliation pass catches cross-domain incoherence (an un-fuelable load, an additive-AE stack) ([`orchestrate.py:11-55`](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]); `assemble`'s filters already strike incomplete or hard-limit-contradicting claims ([`assemble.py:116-124, 250-275`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py) [VERIFIED]). But that leaves a gap a reviewer will press: nothing reads the WHOLE assembled plan as one artifact against the physician-ready bar — sourced, decision-framed, honestly-uncertain, clinician-legible ([ADR-0006, Y-Statement](ADR-0006-multi-domain-plan-assembly-via-roster.md) [VERIFIED]). A plan can pass every per-claim filter and every cross-domain hold yet still be internally contradictory across sections, incoherent, or thin — the producer is judging its own output, the dominant failure mode independent judging exists to defeat ([judge-discipline.md §2, "rubber-stamping … LLM judges default to agreeable high scores"](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]). The autonomous-build pipeline answered this same force with an independent judge over the worker's deliverable: a fresh agent scores the artifact against a rubric and drives a bounded revise loop until every dimension clears the bar, with the judge isolated from the producer's self-assessment ([rubric SKILL.md, "produce → judge → iterate … bounded revise loop until every dimension clears the bar"](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/SKILL.md) [VERIFIED]). Because adding a verify gate with pass/revise authority over the assembled plan fixes a quality bar and a control-flow gate the orchestrator must honor — and is distinct from the existing per-finding safety adjudication — it is a decision, not a code change.

### Decision

Adopt a post-assembly plan-quality judge: an independent verify step that scores the ASSEMBLED plan against a plan-quality rubric (followability, coherence, internal consistency, completeness) AFTER `assemble`/`generate_plans` produce it and BEFORE it is surfaced, and that drives a bounded revise loop — a quality miss re-authors or revises the failing sections through the orchestrator, re-judged each pass, up to a finite iteration cap then escalate. The judge reuses the build pipeline's produce→judge→iterate pattern (a fresh agent isolated from the producer's self-assessment) and wraps the inner engine unchanged. This is the QUALITY gate, distinct from the clinical-SAFETY review (ADR-0024).

### Rationale

The plan-quality control was evaluated against the status quo (specialist self-quality plus reconciliation) and per-domain judges across three criteria the physician-ready bar makes load-bearing: **whole-plan quality coverage**, **judge independence**, and **revise-loop convergence**.

On **whole-plan quality coverage**, only a post-assembly judge reads the assembled plan as one artifact. Specialist self-quality covers each section in isolation and `assemble`'s filters strike per-claim defects (incomplete sourcing, hard-limit contradiction) over the single canonical claim set ([`assemble.py:116-124, 250-275`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py) [VERIFIED]) — but cross-section coherence and internal consistency (two sections setting contradictory targets, a plan that is not followable as a whole) are exactly what no per-claim or single-domain check sees. The reconciliation pass catches the cross-domain SAFETY interactions it was built for (energy/additive-AE/Rx-BPMH) ([`orchestrate.py:11-55`](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]), not whole-plan quality. On **judge independence**, the value of a judge is independent verification — it earns trust by checking the artifact, not by endorsing it, and the producer scoring its own work defaults to agreeable high scores ([judge-discipline.md §1-2](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]); a fresh judge isolated from the specialists' self-assessment is the only option that supplies that independence. On **revise-loop convergence**, a gate with no revise path is just a halt; folding a bounded revise loop (re-author the failing sections, re-judge, cap iterations then escalate) makes the gate productive, the same shape the rubric pipeline uses ([rubric SKILL.md](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/SKILL.md) [VERIFIED]).

Per-domain judges (one judge per section) were the strongest rejected option: they parallelize and localize a quality miss to its section, but a per-domain judge sees only its own section and cannot catch cross-section contradiction — the exact whole-plan defect class the gap exists for — so it reproduces the status-quo blind spot at finer granularity. Long-term, the judge does not bind the engine to one rubric: the plan-quality rubric is data the judge scores against, revisable as the physician-ready bar evolves, without re-deciding that a whole-plan judge gates the surface.

### Consequences

**Positive:**
- A verified whole-plan quality floor that no specialist self-assessment can launder past: the judge reads the assembled plan as one artifact against the rubric and a quality miss blocks surfacing until the revise loop clears it or escalates — coverage no per-claim filter or single-domain check provides ([`assemble.py:250-275`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py), [judge-discipline.md §1](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]).
- Zero engine rebuild: the judge WRAPS the built inner engine — it scores the `assemble`/`generate_plans` output and drives revision through the existing `reauthor` re-dispatch seam ([`pipeline.py:28-53`](../../scripts/plan/pipeline.py) [VERIFIED]), recording nothing the reconciler and safety gates did not already pass.
- The produce→judge→iterate pattern is a second instance of an already-validated control shape (the build pipeline's judge), not a novel mechanism the project must prove out ([rubric SKILL.md](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/SKILL.md) [VERIFIED]).

**Negative:**
- Added per-plan latency and dispatch cost: every plan now incurs at least one extra judge dispatch over the whole assembled plan, plus one re-author + re-judge cycle per revise iteration. Under ADR-0022's autonomous orchestrator this multiplies across re-runs as wearables/labs arrive — a recurring, not one-time, cost.
- The judge can REJECT a clinically-safe plan on quality grounds alone: a plan that passed every `assemble` filter and every cross-domain safety hold ([`orchestrate.py:62-69`](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]) can still be sent back for thin/incoherent/inconsistent content, withholding a safe plan from the operator until quality clears. Quality rejection is a distinct, sometimes-friction-causing gate from safety rejection.
- The revise loop must be bounded or it can loop forever on a plan that never converges to the quality bar (a thin-library domain that cannot be made deep, contradictory operator goals the specialists cannot reconcile). Policy: a finite iteration cap of N quality-revise passes (build-plan parameter, mirroring the rubric pipeline's bounded revise); on the (N+1)th miss the loop HALTS and escalates the residual quality deficit to the operator with the judge's deductions, rather than surfacing a sub-bar plan or looping indefinitely.

**Neutral:**
- The judge's role mirrors the build pipeline's execute-plan/rubric judge (independent agent, scores against a rubric, gates a bounded revise loop) — a second instance of the same orchestration pattern, not a new control shape.
- The plan-quality rubric becomes a maintained artifact the judge scores against; the specific dimensions (followability/coherence/consistency/completeness) are a spec-stage detail the rubric owns, not fixed by this ADR.

### Alternatives Considered

#### Alternative A: Post-assembly whole-plan quality judge (Chosen)
An independent judge scores the assembled plan against a plan-quality rubric between assembly and surfacing, driving a bounded revise loop on a quality miss, wrapping the inner engine.
- **Supporting evidence:** Only this option reads the whole assembled plan as one artifact for cross-section coherence and internal consistency — the defect class no per-claim filter ([`assemble.py:250-275`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py) [VERIFIED]) or single-domain check sees — and supplies producer-independent verification ([judge-discipline.md §1-2](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]); the build pipeline already runs this produce→judge→iterate shape successfully ([rubric SKILL.md](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/SKILL.md) [VERIFIED]). It reuses `generate_plans`/`run_generation` verbatim and drives revision through the existing `reauthor` seam ([`pipeline.py:28-53`](../../scripts/plan/pipeline.py) [VERIFIED]).
- **Trade-offs:** Adds per-plan judge latency and dispatch cost (Negative-1) and can reject a clinically-safe plan on quality grounds (Negative-2); requires a bounded-revise cap to avoid non-convergence (Negative-3).

#### Alternative B: No judge — rely on specialist self-quality plus reconciliation (status quo)
Keep quality ownership with each specialist's self-asserted section quality plus the cross-domain reconciliation pass; no independent whole-plan quality gate.
- **Supporting evidence:** It is the current, tested, shipped posture — `assemble`'s four filters already strike incomplete/contradicting claims and the reconciler already catches cross-domain safety incoherence ([`assemble.py:250-275`](../../scripts/plan/assemble.py), [`orchestrate.py:11-55`](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]); it adds zero latency and zero dispatch cost over the existing pipeline, and the specialist holds the domain's vetted evidence so it is the best-informed quality judge of its own section.
- **Trade-offs:** It leaves the producer judging its own output — the dominant rubber-stamping failure mode ([judge-discipline.md §2](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]) — and nothing reads the whole assembled plan for cross-section contradiction or whole-plan followability, so an internally inconsistent or incoherent plan that passes every per-claim filter still surfaces. The build pipeline's evidence is that an independent judge catches what the producer misses.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the per-plan judge latency/cost proves to outweigh the quality defects it catches in practice (the judge surfaces ~0 quality misses over a meaningful run of plans), the gate is not earning its cost and self-quality plus reconciliation suffices.

#### Alternative C: Per-domain judges instead of a whole-plan judge
Run one independent judge per plan section/domain rather than one judge over the assembled whole.
- **Supporting evidence:** Per-domain judging parallelizes (each section judged independently) and localizes a quality miss to the section that caused it, giving sharper revise targeting; it still supplies producer-independence at the section level.
- **Trade-offs:** A per-domain judge sees only its own section and structurally cannot catch cross-section contradiction or whole-plan incoherence — the precise defect class the quality gap exists for ([ADR-0006, Y-Statement, "single-coherent-attributable-document"](ADR-0006-multi-domain-plan-assembly-via-roster.md) [VERIFIED]) — so it reproduces the status-quo blind spot at finer granularity. Whole-plan quality is not the sum of per-section qualities.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If a future engine produces sections that are provably independent (no cross-section interaction the plan's coherence depends on), per-domain judging captures the localization benefit without the lost-coverage cost — but the current cross-domain reconciler exists precisely because sections DO interact ([`orchestrate.py:11-55`](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]).

### Validation Approach

**Confirmation criteria:**
- The judge runs over the assembled plan between assembly and surfacing: invoke the pipeline end-to-end on a synthetic PII-free fixture and assert the judge dispatch fires AFTER `assemble`/`generate_plans` produce the plan and BEFORE the plan is surfaced/recorded as final — a quality verdict (ACCEPT/REVISE) is produced for every plan, count of plans surfaced without a judge verdict = 0 ([`pipeline.py:54-65`](../../scripts/plan/pipeline.py) [VERIFIED]).
- The judge is producer-independent: assert the judge dispatch does not receive the specialists' self-assessments or the orchestrator's notes (judge-isolation), matching the judge-discipline contract ([judge-discipline.md §1](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]).
- The revise loop converges or escalates: on a seeded quality miss, assert the loop re-authors through the existing `reauthor` seam, re-judges, and either clears the bar within N passes or escalates the residual deficit — count of plans surfaced below the quality bar without escalation = 0 ([`pipeline.py:28-53`](../../scripts/plan/pipeline.py) [VERIFIED]).

**Falsification criteria:**
- **Seeded-quality-defect test (the load-bearing threshold):** construct an assembled plan with a SEEDED quality defect — internally contradictory cross-section targets, OR an empty/incoherent in-scope domain section — and run it through the judge. Threshold: **0 known-defective plans surfaced past the judge** — the judge must REJECT or trigger the revise loop on the seeded defect; if ≥1 seeded-defective plan reaches the surface with an ACCEPT verdict, the judge is rubber-stamping and the quality gate is not enforcing its floor — halt and re-verify the judge scores the artifact, not its impression ([judge-discipline.md §2](../../../skills_library/skills/rubric/references/judge-discipline.md) [VERIFIED]).
- **Bounded-revise test:** seed a non-convergent plan (a quality miss the revise loop cannot clear) and assert the loop HALTS at the iteration cap N and escalates — count of plans that loop past N revise passes = 0. If the loop runs unbounded, the bounded-revise policy has failed — halt and restore the cap before release.
- Time horizon: run the seeded-quality-defect test and the bounded-revise test at the first build of the judge and at every release thereafter, and before the July-2026 physician-visit deliverable ships.

**Review triggers:**
- ADR-0024 (the whole-plan safety review) reaches build — re-verify the quality-then-safety ordering and that the folded revise loop composes across both gates without double-revising or gap.
- The judge surfaces ~0 quality misses over a meaningful run of plans — re-open Alternative B (the gate is not earning its latency/cost).
- The per-plan judge dispatch cost under ADR-0022's autonomous re-run cadence exceeds the quality value it catches — re-open the cost trade-off and the iteration cap N.
