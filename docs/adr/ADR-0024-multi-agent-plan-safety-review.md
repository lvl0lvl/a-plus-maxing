## ADR-0024: Adopt a Multi-Agent Safety Review of the Assembled Plan

> **Y-Statement:** In the context of the S91 plan engine, whose only safety stage today is the inner medical-liaison `adjudicate` gate adjudicating individual held findings one at a time (supplement↔peptide additive-AE, cross-domain conflict, supplement↔Rx BPMH) and which the ADR-0022 autonomous orchestrator now drives with no per-dispatch human checkpoint, facing the tension that an assembled multi-domain plan can carry EMERGENT cross-domain safety issues no single held finding surfaces — cumulative load across domains, an interaction visible only when the four domains are read together — we decided to adopt a `/review-pr`-style multi-agent safety review over the WHOLE assembled plan whose findings drive the folded revise loop, WRAPPING (never replacing) the inner per-finding gate which survives as the per-finding layer, to achieve a standing whole-plan safety lens for the emergent class the per-finding gate cannot see, accepting added dispatch latency and cost, the risk of multiple safety lenses over-blocking or conflicting, and a compose-the-seam obligation to prevent double-gating or a gap against the inner gate.

### Context

The plan engine has exactly one safety stage in code, and it is per-finding. The reconciler (`scripts/plan/orchestrate.py`) HOLDS a domain when a cross-domain check fires — a supplement↔peptide additive-AE finding, an author-declared cross-domain conflict, or a supplement↔Rx BPMH match — and routes each held finding INDIVIDUALLY to the medical-liaison terminal gate `adjudicate(safety_finding, envelope)` ([orchestrate.py:196-308, 569-616](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]; [adjudicate.py:274-345](../../scripts/plan/adjudicate.py) [VERIFIED]). Each `safety_finding` is built from ONE finding by a dedicated builder — `_additive_ae_safety_finding` distills a pairwise supplement↔peptide screen, `_conflict_safety_finding` one declaring domain's conflicts, `_rx_bpmh_safety_finding` one compound's class-token intersection with the operator's present Rx classes ([orchestrate.py:196-225, 228-259, 283-308](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]). The screens are mechanical set/identity intersections over author-declared `ae_profile` tokens — `_normalized_ae_classes`, `_compound_identities`, `_declared_interaction_findings` ([orchestrate.py:72-193](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]). The gate is sound at what it does: a HIGH/MEDIUM finding clears only via a content-valid override record, a CRITICAL / H1-H2 finding is non-overridable, a malformed envelope leaves the block standing ([adjudicate.py:11-33, 129-148](../../scripts/plan/adjudicate.py) [VERIFIED]).

Two forces pull against each other. The per-finding gate's strength is its precision and its fail-closed default — each concern is adjudicated on its own merits and clears independently ([orchestrate.py:455-456, 618-635](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]). But by construction it never reads the assembled multi-domain plan as one object: it sees a pairwise compound interaction or a single class-token match, not the four domains composed together. An emergent safety issue — cumulative stimulant or hepatic load summed ACROSS workout, nutrition, supplements, and peptides; a contraindication that surfaces only when the training prescription is read against the supplement stack and the operator's state TOGETHER — produces no single held finding for any builder to route, so it passes the per-finding gate untouched. This was tolerable under the S68 interactive runtime, where a human reviewed every safety-relevant dispatch before the orchestrator proceeded ([generate-plan/SKILL.md L70-110](../../.claude/skills/generate-plan/SKILL.md) [VERIFIED]). ADR-0022 removed that per-dispatch human checkpoint and named this exact gap: "This shifts the safety burden ONTO the multi-agent whole-plan safety-review tier … that tier becomes load-bearing, not optional" ([ADR-0022, Consequences Negative-1](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]). Separately, the `/review-pr` pattern — six parallel lenses → synthesis → blind triage → verified resolution — is the project's demonstrated evidence that multi-lens review catches what a single pass misses (`~/.claude/skills/review-pr/SKILL.md` L66-89, the global review-pr skill [VERIFIED]).

### Decision

Adopt a `/review-pr`-style multi-agent safety review over the WHOLE assembled plan: multiple independent safety lenses (e.g. `medical-safety-reviewer`, `health-edge-case-reviewer`, the `medical-liaison` as a whole-plan reviewer) read the composed multi-domain plan as one system, their findings are synthesized and blind-triaged, and legitimate findings drive the folded post-review revise loop. This tier WRAPS the existing inner per-finding `adjudicate` gate, which survives unchanged as the per-finding safety layer — the new tier is additive and layered, not a replacement. It runs as a stage in the ADR-0022 orchestrator's loop, parallel to or after the ADR-0023 quality judge, before the plan is surfaced.

### Rationale

The whole-plan safety stage was evaluated against the status quo (the inner per-finding gate alone) and a single whole-plan safety reviewer, across three criteria the autonomous runtime makes load-bearing: **emergent-issue coverage**, **independence/false-negative resistance**, and **cost/latency**.

On **emergent-issue coverage**, only a whole-plan stage reaches the gap. The per-finding gate operates on pairwise screens and single class-token intersections ([orchestrate.py:168-193, 262-280](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]); a cumulative cross-domain load or a whole-plan contraindication produces NO held finding, so no `safety_finding` is ever built and the gate has nothing to adjudicate — the issue is invisible to it, not under-weighted by it. A reviewer reading the assembled four-domain document is the only stage positioned to see it. On **independence / false-negative resistance**, the `/review-pr` evidence is that multiple parallel lenses plus a profile-less blind triage catch what one pass misses, and that a fix is trusted only after blind verification — "a fix the author's own agent declares done is not verified" (`~/.claude/skills/review-pr/SKILL.md` L79-98 [VERIFIED]). For a health plan whose miss is a clinical harm, the multi-lens posture's lower false-negative rate is worth its cost over a single reviewer that can have one blind spot with no second lens to catch it. On **cost/latency**, the multi-agent tier is the most expensive option — N parallel safety dispatches plus triage plus a revise round per plan — but it runs on the ADR-0022 subscription runtime with no marginal per-call API charge ([ADR-0022, Rationale](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]), so the cost is wall-clock latency and dispatch volume, not recurring spend.

One honest limit on the `/review-pr` analogy: the build pipeline's evidence is that a judge/review catches what the producer misses, but the build pipeline's miss is a code defect (cost: a revise), whereas this pipeline's miss is potential clinical harm to the operator under NO human backstop — so the safety review's bar is set higher than the build judge's, not at parity, and the analogy justifies the SHAPE of the tier, not its acceptance threshold.

Long-term, the tier is load-bearing for a 1+ year structural reason, not a convenience: it is the standing safety lens a human formerly provided per-dispatch under the S68 runtime, now that ADR-0022 drives those dispatches autonomously ([ADR-0022, Consequences Negative-1](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]). It composes with — does not double-gate — the inner gate: the inner gate adjudicates the per-finding holds the reconciler raised; this tier reviews the assembled survivors for the emergent issues no hold represents. The two cover disjoint failure classes (per-finding vs whole-plan), so neither makes the other redundant.

### Consequences

**Positive:**
- The emergent cross-domain safety class — cumulative load summed across domains, a contraindication visible only in the composed plan — gets a standing review stage; the per-finding gate, operating on pairwise/class-token screens ([orchestrate.py:168-193](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]), structurally cannot surface it, so without this tier the class has no owner under the autonomous runtime.
- The inner gate is untouched and keeps its fail-closed precision: `adjudicate`'s HIGH/MEDIUM-clears-only-on-content-valid-override and CRITICAL/H1-H2-non-overridable behavior ([adjudicate.py:11-33](../../scripts/plan/adjudicate.py) [VERIFIED]) survives verbatim — this tier adds a layer, it does not redraw the reconciler or the per-finding gate.
- The multi-lens + blind-triage + verified-resolution posture lowers the whole-plan false-negative rate versus a single reviewer, the `/review-pr` independence property (`~/.claude/skills/review-pr/SKILL.md` L79-98 [VERIFIED]) — for a health plan, a missed whole-plan harm is the costliest failure.

**Negative:**
- Added latency and dispatch volume: every plan now incurs N parallel safety-lens dispatches plus a triage pass plus at least one revise round, multiplied by each re-run as wearables/labs arrive — a recurring cost the per-plan-plus-revise loop multiplies, bounded by the same subscription rate/concurrency ceiling ADR-0022 flagged ([ADR-0022, OQ-2](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]).
- Multiple safety lenses can over-block or conflict: two reviewers may flag overlapping concerns, or one may block what another clears, producing a non-converging revise loop or an over-conservative plan that holds content the inner gate would have released. The blind-triage + bounded-revise mechanism must dedupe and bound this, exactly as `/review-pr` bounds fix attempts (`~/.claude/skills/review-pr/SKILL.md` L86 [VERIFIED]) — left unbounded it is a standing over-block risk for the life of the autonomous runtime.
- The tier is now LOAD-BEARING, not optional: ADR-0022 made the autonomous orchestrator drive safety-relevant dispatches with no human gate, shifting the safety burden onto this tier ([ADR-0022, Consequences Negative-1](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]). If this tier is skipped, degraded, or its references are missing, the engine ships plans with no whole-plan safety lens AND no per-dispatch human — a 1+ year structural dependency, not a nicety.

**Neutral:**
- The tier mirrors the project's `/review-pr` topology (parallel lenses → synthesis → blind triage → verified resolution) — a second instance of the same review pattern, not a novel control shape.
- It composes with the ADR-0023 quality judge (complement): two distinct gates — one for quality, one for safety — over the same assembled plan, both feeding the same folded revise loop; their ordering (parallel vs sequential) and the one-combined-tier-vs-two-gates boundary is the compose-the-seam concern coordinated across ADR-0023 and ADR-0024.

### Alternatives Considered

#### Alternative A: Multi-agent whole-plan safety review (Chosen)
Multiple independent safety lenses read the assembled multi-domain plan as one system; findings are synthesized, blind-triaged, and drive the folded revise loop, wrapping the inner per-finding gate.
- **Supporting evidence:** Only a whole-plan stage reaches the emergent class the per-finding screens structurally cannot ([orchestrate.py:168-193, 262-280](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]); the `/review-pr` multi-lens + blind-triage + verified-resolution pattern is the project's evidence that multiple lenses catch what one pass misses (`~/.claude/skills/review-pr/SKILL.md` L66-89 [VERIFIED]); ADR-0022 explicitly designated this tier load-bearing once the per-dispatch human checkpoint was removed ([ADR-0022, Consequences Negative-1](ADR-0022-programmatic-subscription-orchestrator.md) [VERIFIED]).
- **Trade-offs:** Highest cost/latency of the three (Negative-1) and a real over-block / lens-conflict risk the blind-triage + bounded-revise must contain (Negative-2).
- **When this becomes the right choice:** It is, for V1, when the autonomous orchestrator produces assembled multi-domain plans with no human reviewing the composed whole and the emergent cross-domain class must have an owner.

#### Alternative B: Rely on the inner per-finding `adjudicate` gate only (status quo)
Keep the inner medical-liaison gate as the sole safety stage — the reconciler raises per-finding holds and `adjudicate` clears or blocks each one ([orchestrate.py:569-616](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py); [adjudicate.py:274-345](../../scripts/plan/adjudicate.py) [VERIFIED]).
- **Supporting evidence:** It is the current, built, tested stage with a strong fail-closed default ([adjudicate.py:11-33](../../scripts/plan/adjudicate.py) [VERIFIED]) and adds zero new dispatch cost. For the failure classes the reconciler DOES raise — pairwise additive-AE, declared conflict, Rx-BPMH — it is precise and per-concern independent ([orchestrate.py:455-456](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]).
- **Trade-offs:** It is per-finding by construction — each `safety_finding` is built from ONE finding ([orchestrate.py:196-225](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED]) — so an emergent issue that produces no single held finding (cumulative cross-domain load; a whole-plan contraindication) yields no `safety_finding` for any builder to route, and the gate never sees it. Under the S68 human-in-the-loop runtime a human covered that gap; ADR-0022 removed the human, so the status quo now leaves the emergent class with no owner at all.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the operator reinstates the S68 per-dispatch human checkpoint (rejecting ADR-0022's autonomy) so a human reads every composed plan, the per-finding gate plus that human suffices and this tier is rejected.

#### Alternative C: A single whole-plan safety reviewer (not multi-agent)
One safety reviewer reads the assembled plan as a whole and blocks/revises — the whole-plan coverage of Alternative A without the parallel-lens topology.
- **Supporting evidence:** It reaches the same emergent class a single lens can see, at roughly 1/N the dispatch cost and latency of the multi-agent tier, with no lens-conflict to dedupe — materially cheaper and simpler than Alternative A.
- **Trade-offs:** A single reviewer has one blind spot with no second lens to catch it; the `/review-pr` premise is precisely that multiple independent lenses plus a profile-less blind triage lower the false-negative rate a single pass leaves (`~/.claude/skills/review-pr/SKILL.md` L79-89 [VERIFIED]). For a health plan whose miss is a clinical harm under no human backstop, the single reviewer's higher false-negative rate is the dominating cost — the saved dispatch cost does not buy back a missed harm.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If measured false-negative rates show one reviewer catches the emergent class at parity with the multi-agent tier (the multi-lens redundancy buys nothing), OR the subscription dispatch ceiling (ADR-0022 OQ-2) makes N parallel safety lenses per plan infeasible — then collapse to a single whole-plan reviewer.

### Validation Approach

**Confirmation criteria:**
- The whole-plan safety review runs over a synthetic PII-free assembled plan as a stage in the autonomous orchestrator loop, dispatches ≥2 independent safety lenses over the COMPOSED multi-domain plan (not per-finding fragments), synthesizes + blind-triages their findings, and routes each legitimate finding into the folded revise loop — verified by invoking the orchestrator end-to-end and asserting the review stage produced ≥1 triaged finding set and ≥1 revise round fired for a seeded emergent issue.
- The inner per-finding gate is reused unchanged: assert the review tier adds NO override path of its own and does not touch `adjudicate` / the reconciler — the per-finding holds still clear/block via `adjudicate(safety_finding, envelope)` exactly as today ([adjudicate.py:274-345](../../scripts/plan/adjudicate.py) [VERIFIED]), and the tier neither double-adjudicates a per-finding hold nor leaves an inner-gate hold un-routed (no gap, no double-gate).

**Falsification criteria:**
- **Seeded-emergent-issue test (the load-bearing threshold):** craft an assembled plan carrying a KNOWN emergent cross-domain safety issue that the per-finding inner gate does NOT flag — e.g. cumulative stimulant/hepatic load summed across the workout + nutrition + supplement + peptide domains that no single pairwise additive-AE / Rx-BPMH / conflict screen raises (verify the inner gate raises 0 holds for it via `reconcile` over the candidates ([orchestrate.py:337-456](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED])). Threshold: **0 known-unsafe-emergent plans surfaced past the review** — the multi-agent review MUST catch the seeded issue and drive a revise (or block to the honest no-plan state). If ≥1 seeded emergent-unsafe plan reaches the operator-facing surface un-flagged, the tier has failed its sole reason to exist — halt and re-verify the lenses read the composed whole, before release.
- If the tier double-gates (≥1 per-finding hold the inner gate already adjudicated is re-adjudicated by the whole-plan tier and reaches a CONFLICTING disposition) OR leaves a gap (≥1 inner-gate hold reaches the surface because the tier assumed the inner gate handled it and the inner gate assumed the tier did), the compose-the-seam boundary is broken — halt and re-draw the per-finding-vs-whole-plan split.
- If the revise loop does not converge within its bound (the lenses keep producing conflicting blocks across > the bounded number of revise rounds), the multi-lens over-block risk has materialized — halt and bound/dedupe the lens findings before release.
- Time horizon: run the seeded-emergent-issue test and the double-gate/gap test at the first build of the tier and at every release thereafter, and before the July-2026 physician-visit deliverable ships.

**Review triggers:**
- ADR-0023 (the quality judge) reaches build — re-verify the quality and safety tiers compose over the same assembled plan and feed the same revise loop without double-gating (the complement seam).
- A subscription rate/concurrency ceiling (ADR-0022 OQ-2) blocks N parallel safety-lens dispatches per plan — re-open Alternative C (collapse to a single whole-plan reviewer) against the cost trade-off.
- A new plan domain is added to the roster (beyond workout/nutrition/supplements/peptides) — re-verify the safety lenses' whole-plan coverage spans the new domain's cumulative-load contribution.
- ≥1 missed emergent cross-domain harm is found in a shipped plan post-hoc — the tier's lens set or triage is under-covering; re-scope the lenses.
