## ADR-0018: Split Intake into an Objective Form (Demographics + Content Uploads) and a Conversational Agent for the Rich Sections

> **Y-Statement:** In the context of an intake wizard whose rigid Steps 3-5 are too lossy to encode the rich-section interplay a real plan needs — a session-87 audit found only 6 form signals reach the planner, the Step-1 demographics are dead placeholders, and four model-bound tokens are orphans — facing the tension between the objective layer (birth year, sex, bodyweight, equipment, file uploads) being genuinely structured and ill-suited to dialogue and the rich layer (goals, training, nutrition, supplements, peptides) needing back-and-forth to express the peptide⟷goal⟷recovery⟷bloodwork interplay, we decided to split intake into a structured form that keeps ONLY demographics + content uploads and a conversational agent that owns the rich sections, to achieve capture that actually feeds a usable plan, accepting that two intake surfaces now coexist (every new input must be classified form-vs-chat), the rich sections lose the form's bounded-enum safety, and the kept demographic layer must be built to actually work — its orphan tokens are dead today.

### Context

Intake today is one rigid 6-step wizard. A wiring audit during operator use found it captures far more than it feeds the plan: only **6 signals reach the planner** (goal-domains, goal-targets, goal-priority-order, hard-limits, recovery-status-band, and the train-around→active-issue-class derivation), while training specifics and all of nutrition route record-only and never reach it ([session-87.md L19](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]; the wired set is `WIRED_TOKENS` plus the raw-symptom item, [capture.py L50-56, L66-70](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]). Two specific defects make the rigid form's lossiness concrete. The Step-1 "About you" fields — birth year, sex, bodyweight, equipment — render as dead static placeholders: `_step1` emits each as `_field(..., True)`, a `class='box ph'` div holding a literal "—", and Step-1 is not part of the POSTing `capture_form` (only Steps 2-6 submit), so it captures nothing ([intake.py L258-266, L408-413](../../vault/design/templates/intake.py) [VERIFIED]). And four model-bound tokens the planner can consume — `sex-for-dosing`, `bodyweight-band`, `equipment-access-class`, `training-age-band` — are **orphans**: none appears in `WIRED_TOKENS` and none has a working input, so the planner asks for them and gets nothing ([capture.py L50-56](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]; [session-87.md L19](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]).

Several forces pull against each other across more than one perspective. From the operator's side, a rigid form cannot encode the rich-section interplay a real plan is built on: "workout" as a checkbox cannot distinguish cardio from strength from hypertrophy, and the plan's value is the back-and-forth between peptide, goal, recovery, bloodwork, and DNA — which needs dialogue, not fixed fields ([session-87.md L21](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]). Pulling the other way, the objective layer is genuinely structured and a poor fit for dialogue: a birth year is a number, sex/bodyweight/equipment are bounded selections, and DNA/HealthKit/labs are file uploads parsed locally — point-and-click is faster and lower-friction than a chat for all of these ([intake.py L11-13, L163-192](../../vault/design/templates/intake.py) [VERIFIED]). From the data-subject's side, the bounded-enum form gives a typed safety the open dialogue does not: `recovery-status-band` and `goal-domains` are validated against server-side enums before any write ([capture.py L80-85, L125-147](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]), whereas free dialogue has no such bounded shape. The decision is therefore which inputs are captured by a structured form and which by conversation — an input-surface topology, not a markup or chat-UI choice.

### Decision

Split intake into two surfaces by the nature of the input. The structured form keeps ONLY the objective layer — demographics (birth year, sex, bodyweight, equipment access) as a working point-and-click capture, plus content uploads (DNA, HealthKit/Apple-Health export, labs). All the rich sections — goals, training, nutrition, supplements, peptides — move to a conversational intake agent. The rich-section wizard steps the form previously rendered (Steps 3-5) are superseded by the chat; the Step-1 demographic layer is retained and activated.

### Rationale

The split was evaluated against two alternatives — keep the rigid form and just repair its dead Step-1 / orphan tokens (A), and move everything to chat with no form (B) — across three criteria the audit makes load-bearing: whether the captured input can express the rich-section interplay a usable plan needs, whether the objective layer is captured at its natural friction, and whether the bounded-enum safety of structured fields is preserved where it matters.

On **expressing the rich-section interplay**, dialogue wins decisively and the form cannot close the gap. The session-87 audit found the rigid form lossy precisely because checkboxes and single-line fields cannot encode cardio-vs-strength-vs-hypertrophy or the peptide⟷goal⟷recovery interplay; repairing the dead Step-1 fields (Alternative A) recovers the four demographic orphans but leaves the rich sections still record-only and still un-encodable as fixed fields, so the plan stays as thin as it is today ([session-87.md L19, L21](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]). A conversation can ask the targeted follow-ups a fixed field cannot. On **capturing the objective layer at its natural friction**, the form wins and the chat loses: a birth year is a number and sex/bodyweight/equipment are bounded selections — structured fields capture them faster and with less friction than a dialogue would, and DNA/HealthKit/labs are file uploads the existing `route_upload` seam ingests unchanged, not anything a conversation can carry ([route.py L58-93](../../scripts/serve/route.py) [VERIFIED]). Moving these to chat (Alternative B) would convert a one-click upload and a typed number into a multi-turn exchange for no gain. On **bounded-enum safety**, demographics stay structured so they keep the server-side enum validation the form already enforces ([capture.py L80-85](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]); the rich sections move to free dialogue and therefore give up that bounded shape — a cost the split accepts and routes to the extraction contract (the complementary decision) to enforce de-identification on.

The split is therefore not "patch the old rigid form." The rich sections (Steps 3-5) are superseded — they move to chat because they need back-and-forth. The demographic layer (Step-1) is retained and activated — demographics stay a structured form because they are objective and structured. Long-term, the split becomes a standing topology discipline: every future intake input must be classified objective-structured (→ form) or rich-interplay (→ chat) before it is added, and the demographic layer that is built here is what finally gives the four orphan tokens (`training-age-band`, `sex-for-dosing`, `bodyweight-band`, `equipment-access-class`) real inputs to derive from (the exact field→token wiring is a spec-stage concern, kept out of this ADR).

### Consequences

**Positive:**
- The plan's personalization inputs become followable rather than lossy: the rich sections that today route record-only and never reach the planner ([session-87.md L19](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]) move to a dialogue that can express the interplay a fixed field cannot, so an operator who completes intake produces a richer, dialogue-sourced input than the rigid form ever did — a richer feed into ADR-0006's roster.
- The objective layer is captured at its natural friction: demographics stay a fast point-and-click form, and content uploads keep the unchanged `route_upload` → `ingest.run`/`dna.land` seam ([route.py L58-93](../../scripts/serve/route.py) [VERIFIED]) — neither is converted into a multi-turn chat.
- Building the kept demographic layer to actually work activates the four orphan model-bound tokens (`sex-for-dosing`, `bodyweight-band`, `equipment-access-class`, `training-age-band`), which the planner asks for and gets nothing for today ([capture.py L50-56](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]; [session-87.md L19](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]).

**Negative:**
- Two intake surfaces now coexist (form + chat) where there was one wizard, so every new intake input carries a standing classification cost: it must be decided objective-structured (→ form) or rich-interplay (→ chat) before it is added, and a misclassified input lands on the wrong surface (a free-dialogue field that should have been a bounded form select, or vice versa) — a 1+ year topology-discipline burden the single wizard never imposed.
- The rich sections lose the form's bounded-enum safety: where `recovery-status-band` and `goal-domains` are validated against server-side enums before any write ([capture.py L80-85, L125-147](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]), free dialogue has no such bounded shape, so de-identification of the rich-section output becomes a harder surface than fixed form fields — it must be enforced by the conversation→store extraction contract (the complementary decision) rather than by an enum check, and an un-de-identified rich fact reaching the store is a model-boundary breach.
- The kept demographic layer is not a free win — it must be BUILT to work: the orphan tokens are dead today (the Step-1 fields capture nothing — `class='box ph'` placeholders outside the POSTing form), so the split's benefit is unrealized until the demographic form is wired to feed real inputs ([intake.py L258-266, L408-413](../../vault/design/templates/intake.py) [VERIFIED]); shipping the chat without activating Step-1 would leave the four orphans orphaned and the split half-delivered.

**Neutral:**
- The loopback intake server's surface becomes "form + chat" rather than "intake wizard": the chat is a new sibling surface on the same operator-started, ephemeral, loopback-bound server, and the form/upload transport posture is unchanged (the one outbound model call the chat makes is owned by the egress-relaxation decision, not a transport re-decision).
- Content-upload routing is byte-unchanged: DNA/HealthKit/labs continue through the existing `route_upload` content-branch and adapters, so the split touches the input-surface topology, not the ingestion seam ([route.py L1-18](../../scripts/serve/route.py) [VERIFIED]).

### Alternatives Considered

#### Alternative A: Keep the rigid form, just repair the dead Step-1 / orphan tokens
Wire Step-1's demographic fields into the POSTing capture form and derive the four orphan tokens (`training-age-band`, `sex-for-dosing`, `bodyweight-band`, `equipment-access-class`) from them, keeping the existing rigid wizard for the rich Steps 3-5 — the status quo the session-87 audit overturned.
- **Supporting evidence:** Smallest possible change — the form, the `WIRED_TOKENS` map, the by-data-class `persist_capture` routing, and the bounded-enum gates already exist ([capture.py L50-56, L189-244](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]); activating Step-1 is a localized wiring fix, and the four orphans would get working inputs with no new surface, no chat, and no egress relaxation. It keeps the bounded-enum safety across the whole form.
- **Trade-offs:** It does not address the load-bearing defect. The audit found the rigid form lossy for the rich sections themselves: "workout" cannot encode cardio-vs-strength-vs-hypertrophy and the plan's value is the peptide⟷goal⟷recovery interplay, which a checkbox or a single-line field cannot carry even with Step-1 repaired ([session-87.md L19, L21](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]). The rich sections would stay record-only and the plan would stay as thin as today. Rejected because it fixes the demographic orphans (which this decision also fixes, by retaining Step-1) while leaving the rich-section lossiness — the actual reason the pivot exists — untouched.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the rich sections turned out to be adequately expressible as bounded fields after all (no real interplay to capture, a plan that needs only checkbox-level inputs), repairing Step-1 in place would deliver a working form at far lower cost than standing up a second surface.

#### Alternative B: All-chat, no form
Move every intake input — demographics and content uploads included — into the conversational agent, so there is a single surface and no structured form at all.
- **Supporting evidence:** One surface, not two — it removes the form-vs-chat classification cost entirely and gives a uniform conversational intake. A chat can in principle ask for a birth year as easily as it asks about training.
- **Trade-offs:** DNA, HealthKit/Apple-Health exports, and labs are FILE UPLOADS parsed locally through the existing `route_upload` seam, not anything a dialogue can carry — a chat cannot receive a 23andMe zip or an Apple-Health export ([route.py L58-93](../../scripts/serve/route.py) [VERIFIED]). And demographics are structured and faster as fields: a birth year typed into a number field or a bounded select beats a multi-turn exchange, and it keeps the server-side enum safety the form enforces ([capture.py L80-85](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]). Rejected because it converts genuinely structured, upload-shaped inputs into dialogue for no gain and discards the bounded-enum safety where it is cheapest to keep.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the operator's documents stopped being file uploads (e.g. a future where DNA/labs/wearable data arrive through an authenticated API the chat can trigger rather than a file drop) and demographics were better captured conversationally, a single chat surface would remove the two-surface cost.

### Validation Approach

**Confirmation criteria:**
- The form captures only objective inputs: after the split, the structured form's submitting fields are demographics + content-upload affordances only, with 0 rich-section fields (no goals/training/nutrition/supplement/peptide form field) — verified by enumerating the form's POSTed field names against the demographic + upload set (expected: 0 rich-section fields remaining on the form).
- The rich sections route to chat: each rich-section input (goals, training, nutrition, supplements, peptides) is captured by the conversational agent, not a form field — verified by confirming the chat path produces the rich-section facts and no rich-section form field persists them (expected: 5 rich domains via chat, 0 via form).
- The kept demographic layer feeds working inputs: after the build, the four previously-orphan tokens (`training-age-band`, `sex-for-dosing`, `bodyweight-band`, `equipment-access-class`) each derive from a real demographic input — verified by checking each token has a non-dead source (expected: 0 orphan tokens, against today's baseline of 4) ([capture.py L50-56](../../scripts/serve/capture.py) [VERIFIED]; [session-87.md L19](../../vault/sessions/session-87.md) [VERIFIED]).

**Falsification criteria:**
- If a rich-section input (e.g. a peptide stack, a training split) is still captured by a structured form field after the build (≥1 rich-section form field persists a rich fact), the split has failed — the form is still owning a rich section; move that input to chat before release.
- If a demographic token is still orphaned after the build (≥1 of `training-age-band`/`sex-for-dosing`/`bodyweight-band`/`equipment-access-class` has no working input), the kept demographic layer was not activated — block the close until Step-1 feeds the orphan tokens, since shipping the chat with the demographics still dead leaves the split half-delivered ([intake.py L258-266](../../vault/design/templates/intake.py) [VERIFIED]).
- If a content upload (DNA/HealthKit/labs) is routed through the chat instead of the upload seam (≥1 file upload no longer reaching `route_upload`), the objective layer has been mis-split onto the conversational surface — return file uploads to the form/upload seam before release ([route.py L58-93](../../scripts/serve/route.py) [VERIFIED]).
- Time horizon: run the form-fields-only and orphan-token checks at the first build of the split and at every release thereafter; run the upload-routing check on every change to the intake surface.

**Review triggers:**
- A new intake input is proposed — classify it objective-structured (→ form) or rich-interplay (→ chat) before it is added; a new input that does not fit either cleanly re-opens the topology decision.
- The conversational agent's coverage of a rich domain changes (a domain added to or removed from chat) — re-confirm no form field re-captures it and no rich domain is left uncovered.
- ADR-0014's data-class-routing contract or the `SUMMARY_FIELD_SET` changes — re-confirm the form's surviving objective fields and the chat's extracted facts still route through the preserved PII contract.
- The North-Star transition is initiated — the loopback form+chat intake surface is superseded by the authenticated hosted intake surface, at which point the form-vs-chat split is re-decided under that trust model.
