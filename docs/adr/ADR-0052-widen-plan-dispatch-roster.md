## ADR-0052: Widen the Plan Dispatch Roster from Four Domains to the Full Deployed Care Team + Cross-Cutting Inputs

> **Y-Statement:** In the context of the `/generate-plan` front door — where the dispatch role map (`plan_driver._ROLE_OF_DOMAIN`) and `plan_schema.PLAN_DOMAINS` were ALREADY grown to all 13 card domains (ADR-0043-T3) and a progressive-activation gate (`activation.active_domains`, partitioning the 13 `CARD_DOMAINS` from the `{genetics, labs}` cross-cutting inputs) already exists, yet the plan the operator receives is still authored from at most the original four (workout / nutrition / supplements / peptides) because the front-door author loop narrows to `active ∩ plan_schema.RENDERABLE_DOMAINS` (the four thin-validator domains, [server.py:817](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED]) and the authoring coupling `client._contract_section` RAISES `ValueError` for any domain the four-entry `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` map does not carry ([client.py:538-544](../../scripts/model/client.py) [VERIFIED]), so the six always-on rich domains (§5–§10) structurally cannot be authored — facing the tension that a followable, physician-ready plan needs the full always-on care team reasoning over the operator's identity-stripped record, not four sections, we decided to widen the EFFECTIVE authoring roster from the floored four to the always-on TEN by mapping the six always-on rich domains into the single-source `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` (so `_contract_section` sources their §5–§10 contract without ValueError) and raising the activation FLOOR from the renderable core-four to the always-on ten, while keeping dermatology / GI / lymphatic (§11–§13) progressive-activation, genetics / labs (§14–§15) as cross-cutting INPUT layers that feed the assembler substrate before the domain authors run (not card authors), and medical-liaison / medical-safety-reviewer / care-assistant (§16–§18) excluded from plan authoring, to achieve a plan authored by the full always-on care team instead of four sections, accepting that the change widens render / synthesis / daily-execution are the separate downstream ADRs B–F), that per-generation author calls rise from an effective four to up to ten always-on (latency + spend), and that the always-on domain vocabulary this records is a moderate-reversibility commitment the downstream render / synthesis depend on — while this ADR touches NONE of the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core (the change to the already-grown `_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` / `PLAN_DOMAINS`.

### Context

The plan the `/generate-plan` front door produces is authored from at most the original four domains, and the reason is NOT where the accepted decision corpus (`vault/decisions/2026-07-19-plan-platform-full-roster-upgrade.md`) places it. That corpus states "Dispatch is 4 domains — `plan_driver._ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` / `plan_schema.PLAN_DOMAINS` = {workout, nutrition, supplements, peptides}." Grounded against the live tree on `main`, both of those registries are ALREADY the full 13: `_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` carries all §1–§13 card domains ([plan_driver.py:117-136](../../scripts/plan/plan_driver.py), comment "GROWN 4->§1-§13 (ADR-0043-T3)" at :117 [VERIFIED]) and `PLAN_DOMAINS = tuple(sorted(activation.CARD_DOMAINS))` derives the same 13 from a single source ([plan_schema.py:56](../../scripts/store/plan_schema.py) [VERIFIED]). A progressive-activation gate already exists too: `activation.py` holds the 13 `CARD_DOMAINS` (§1–§13), the disjoint `CROSS_CUTTING_INPUTS = {genetics, labs}` partition with an import-time tripwire, and `active_domains(operator_surface)` returning the touched-∩-card subset ([activation.py:26-89](../../scripts/plan/activation.py) [VERIFIED]). So the roster infrastructure the corpus asks to build is, in large part, already built.

The effective four-domain ceiling is enforced by two DIFFERENT, still-narrow levers. First, the front door narrows the author loop to `renderable_active = sorted(active & set(plan_schema.RENDERABLE_DOMAINS))` and authors only those ([server.py:817,832-838](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED]); `RENDERABLE_DOMAINS = tuple(_PLAN_VALIDATORS)` is exactly the original four (workout / nutrition / supplements / peptides — the only domains with a thin per-domain validator, [plan_schema.py:289](../../scripts/store/plan_schema.py); validators at [:185,210,241,259](../../scripts/store/plan_schema.py) [VERIFIED]), held at four on purpose (RT-009: rich domains record first-class via `plan_model`, never the thin path). Second — and this is the load-bearing coupling — every author call routes through `client.author` → `_author_system_prompt(domain)` → `_contract_section(domain)` ([client.py:845,573](../../scripts/model/client.py) [VERIFIED]), and `_contract_section` RAISES a domain-named `ValueError` for any domain absent from the four-entry `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` map ([client.py:513-518,538-544](../../scripts/model/client.py) [VERIFIED]). The front door's Leg-2 synthesize step already tries to author active rich domains via `_author_rich`, but that helper catches the exception and returns `None` ([server.py:865-872](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED]) — so the six always-on rich domains (§5–§10) are dispatch-eligible in the registries yet structurally UN-AUTHORABLE. That is why an all-signal operator surface still yields a plan expressible from four sections.

Three forces make the fix a decision rather than a code comment. The operator (and the physician who will read the plan) needs the full always-on care team authoring — the demonstrated S146 output is a multi-domain plan, not four sections. The maintainer must not disturb the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core (`store.py` / `keying.py` / `pipeline.py` / `adjudicate.py` / `adjust.py` / `router.py`, [tracker-ingestion-api-adapters.md:195](../design/tracker-ingestion-api-adapters.md) [VERIFIED]) — the byte-frozen SUBSET of ADR-0032's broader inner-engine glob (`scripts/plan/{orchestrate,pipeline,assemble,generate_plan,adjudicate,adjust,track}.py` + `scripts/store/*`, [ADR-0032:43](ADR-0032-dna-aware-planning.md) [VERIFIED]) that the ADR-0041/0043/0044 freeze-breaks never touched (AR-007). TODAY genetics reaches the planner de-id-by-construction through the frozen `router.summarize`: the raw rsID+allele genotype is NEVER read (the `dna-report` item is not on the assembler allowlist), genetics crosses only as the coarse `genetic-trait-classes` token ([context_assembler.py:28-31](../../scripts/plan/context_assembler.py) [VERIFIED]) — and ADR-0051 (`status: proposed`, UNBUILT) would reverse exactly this (Consequences, the forward-egress Negative). And the activation policy must stay honest: the contracts doc's own progressive-activation design note scopes empty-state activation to §11–§13 only ([specialist-plan-contracts.md:293](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]), so widening must not fabricate a dermatology / GI / lymphatic card for an operator with no skin / gut / fluid signal. This ADR decides WHICH specialists author a plan, in WHAT order (cross-cutting inputs first), and under WHAT activation policy — the interface every downstream ADR (B–F) builds on.

### Decision

Widen the EFFECTIVE authoring roster from the floored four to the always-on ten, classify the 18 contract sections into a durable four-bucket activation policy, and wire the two authoring couplings so the always-on ten can actually be authored. The registries (`_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN`, `PLAN_DOMAINS`) are already the full 13 and are NOT changed; the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core (and the broader inner-engine glob) is NOT touched (`orchestrate.generate_plans`, [orchestrate.py:756](../../scripts/plan/orchestrate.py) [VERIFIED], is CALLED not modified).

1. **The activation-policy classification (the core decision).** The 18 `specialist-plan-contracts.md` sections partition into four buckets, each justified by the contracts doc's own framing:
   - **(a) Always-on card-emitting (authoring) dispatch domains — the ten** (§1–§10): personal-trainer (workout), nutritionist (nutrition), peptide-specialist (peptides), supplement-specialist (supplements), endocrine-specialist (endocrine), cardiovascular-specialist (cardiovascular), recovery-specialist (recovery), sleep-coach (sleep), longevity-strategist (longevity), mental-performance-coach (mental-performance). These AUTHOR into the comprehensive `plan-model::` record on every generation — an authored domain, NOT a rendered card (the render stays four until ADR-E; "renderable" is reserved for the four `RENDERABLE_DOMAINS`, AR-003). Justification: the progressive-activation empty-state note is scoped to §11–§13 ONLY ([specialist-plan-contracts.md:293](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]); §1–§10 are the non-progressive, always-authoring set by exclusion. This always-on floor REFINES ADR-0046's decision, which gated ALL 13 card domains uniformly on progressive activation ("dispatched ONLY when the operator has a stated goal or data") and left the within-13 partition open (its OQ-3) — ADR-0052 promotes §1–§10 to an always-on floor, honest because §1–§10 are never empty-state, while keeping §11–§13 progressive (see the ADR-0046 row + Rationale, AR-002).
   - **(b) Cross-cutting INPUT layers — genetics + labs** (§14–§15): dispatched FIRST, feeding the domain authors, emitting NO card. Justification: the contracts doc calls genetics "a cross-cutting input layer (like labs), not a domain card" ([specialist-plan-contracts.md:365](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]) and labs "a cross-cutting interpretation layer (like genetics), not a domain card" ([:389](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]), and the Synthesis names "Two cross-cutting input layers (labs §15, genetics §14) feed every domain — not domain cards themselves" ([:489](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]). They are already `activation.CROSS_CUTTING_INPUTS`, disjoint from `CARD_DOMAINS` ([activation.py:45,51-53](../../scripts/plan/activation.py) [VERIFIED]).
   - **(c) Progressive-activation domains — dermatology / GI / lymphatic** (§11–§13): dispatched ONLY when the operator has a goal or data on file for that domain. Justification: the contracts doc flags them empty-state and "the domain card renders only when the operator surfaces a goal or data" ([:293](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]). The ACTIVATION mechanism already exists (`active_domains` returns them only when the surface touches them); the AUTHORING mechanism does NOT — §11–§13 have no `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` entry, so an activated progressive domain hits the same `_author_rich`→`None` silent-thinning this ADR fixes for §5–§10 ([server.py:865-872](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED]). Mapping §11–§13 into `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` is OUT of ADR-0052's scope (deferred to a later ADR, beaded `a-plus-maxing-00kh`); until then an activated progressive domain dispatches-but-authors-nothing (honest containment — no partial card is recorded; OQ-5 + Non-goals), not asserted-working (AR-005).
   - **(d) Excluded from plan authoring** (§16–§18): medical-liaison (the human-gate safety spine), medical-safety-reviewer (a deploy-time gate, "not a runtime plan contributor"), and care-assistant (the orchestrator, "no `.claude/agents/` profile — it's `scripts/serve/care_chat.py`"). Justification: their own contract sections ([:412,436,456](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]) place them as safety / escalation / orchestration, not as domain-card authors.

2. **The domain→role map + dispatch ORDER.** The always-on ten map to their deployed `.claude/agents/<slug>/` profiles, all VERIFIED present (personal-trainer, nutritionist, peptide-specialist, supplement-specialist, endocrine-specialist, cardiovascular-specialist, recovery-specialist, sleep-coach, longevity-strategist, mental-performance-coach — [`.claude/agents/`](../../.claude/agents) [VERIFIED]); `_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` already carries these ten mappings (plus the three progressive), so NO map edit is needed. The dispatch ORDER is cross-cutting inputs (genetics / labs) BEFORE the domain authors, so the genetics/labs substrate is present in the identity-stripped record the domain authors read — which the front door already realizes structurally: `assemble_context` computes the full record (genetics trait-classes + labs) at [server.py:823](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED] BEFORE the domain author loop at [server.py:832](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED].

3. **The `_contract_section` coupling — IN SCOPE for the always-on ten.** Map the six always-on rich domains (endocrine / cardiovascular / recovery / sleep / longevity / mental-performance) into the single-source `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` so `_contract_section` sources their §5–§10 sections from `specialist-plan-contracts.md` without ValueError. The contract file already carries §5–§10 in full, and `_contract_section`'s heading-slice mechanism is already generic (it slices `## <N>. <slug> —` to the next `## ` heading, [client.py:545-560](../../scripts/model/client.py) [VERIFIED]) — so the map entries plus the sourcing are ONE coherent change, not two. Raising the activation FLOOR from the renderable core-four to the always-on ten (`plan_loop.active_plan_domains` / `activation`) is the second half of the same change — and it must be an UNCONDITIONAL floor (the always-on ten unioned into the active set on EVERY surface: `active |= always_on_ten`), NOT the existing conditional idiom (`if not (active & RENDERABLE_DOMAINS): active |= …`, [plan_loop.py:233-235](../../scripts/serve/plan_loop.py) [VERIFIED]), so a PARTIAL-signal operator (who touches only, say, `workout`) still gets all ten rather than one (AR-006, the partial-signal floor probe).

4. **Synthetic / mock only; the live run is operator-gated.** Built and mock/fixture-tested at 0 live spend; the operator-present LIVE run (real spend, real data across ten always-on author calls) is operator-gated (OQ-4), as with ADR-0050 OQ-3 and ADR-0051 OQ-4.

**File manifest** (all outside the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core AND the broader inner-engine glob): `scripts/model/client.py` (add the six always-on rich domains to `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` so `_contract_section` sources §5–§10); `scripts/plan/activation.py` / `scripts/serve/plan_loop.py` (raise the activation floor from the renderable core-four to the always-on ten); `scripts/serve/server.py` (the author-loop narrowing at [:817,832](../../scripts/serve/server.py), repointed from `active ∩ RENDERABLE_DOMAINS` so the always-on ten reach `_author_rich` / synthesize). NOT touched: `scripts/plan/plan_driver.py` (`_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` already 13); `scripts/store/plan_schema.py`'s `RENDERABLE_DOMAINS` / `_PLAN_VALIDATORS` (stay at four, RT-009); and the frozen engine — `orchestrate`/`generate_plan`/`assemble` are out-of-scope-by-manifest (`orchestrate.generate_plans` is called-not-modified) and `scripts/plan/track.py` (a still-frozen ADR-0032 glob member outside the six) stays byte-frozen (AR-007). The always-on rich domains author and record into the disjoint `plan-model::` namespace via the Leg-2 `synthesize` path ([care_chat.py:584-585](../../scripts/serve/care_chat.py) [VERIFIED]), NOT the thin `plan::` validator path; `_plan_zone` does not read `plan-model::`, so the render is unchanged (the Consequences honest-sequencing note).

### Rationale

The choice was weighed against widening only the render (Alternative A), promoting all 18 sections to domains (Alternative B), shipping all six roster→render ADRs as one change (Alternative C), and making dermatology / GI / lymphatic always-on (Alternative D), across four criteria the plan goal makes load-bearing: **authoring reach** (how many specialists actually author the plan), **honesty of activation** (does a card ever get fabricated for an empty-state domain?), **blast radius** (does the change stay outside the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core and the broader inner-engine glob?), and **downstream sequencing** (is the roster decision reviewable independent of the render / synthesis?).

On **authoring reach**, only widening the authoring coupling removes the ceiling: the registries are already 13, so the binding constraints are `RENDERABLE_DOMAINS` (4) and the `_contract_section` ValueError. Mapping the six always-on rich domains into `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` is the minimum change that lets them author at all — the render (Alternative A) is downstream of authoring, so widening the render alone lays out nothing new because the sections were never authored. On **honesty of activation**, keeping §11–§13 progressive is the direct expression of the contracts doc's own empty-state note; making them always-on (Alternative D) would fabricate a dermatology / GI / lymphatic card for an operator with no skin / gut / fluid signal, which is the exact anti-generic failure the platform exists to avoid. The always-on-ten floor is a deliberate REFINEMENT of ADR-0046's uniform progressive-activation decision (which gated all 13 card domains on operator signal and left the within-13 partition to spec, its OQ-3): promoting §1–§10 to an always-on floor is honest because §1–§10 are never empty-state, whereas extending the floor to §11–§13 would re-instate the always-dispatch posture ADR-0046 rejected (its Alternative A) for exactly the empty-state domains (AR-002). On **blast radius**, the change lands in `client.py` / `activation.py` / `plan_loop.py` / `server.py`, none of which is one of the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core files nor the broader inner-engine glob; TODAY genetics still reaches the planner through the frozen `router.summarize` de-id seam (the derived `genetic-trait-classes` token, [context_assembler.py:28-31](../../scripts/plan/context_assembler.py) [VERIFIED]), so this ADR does NOT change the genetics mechanism (ADR-0051 owns any change to it), and `orchestrate.generate_plans` is called, not reopened. On **downstream sequencing**, deciding the roster and activation policy ALONE — before the rich render (ADR-E) or the synthesis layer (ADR-C) — keeps this ADR reviewable in isolation; folding the render / synthesis in (Alternative C) makes the change unreviewable and couples the roster vocabulary to layout decisions that are not yet made.

The cost the chosen path accepts is honestly a partial one: ADR-0052 widens what is AUTHORED (the always-on ten into the `plan-model::` comprehensive record) but NOT the demonstrated rich output. The render is still the thin `_plan_zone` collapse over the four `_PLAN_LABELS` / the thin `plan::` path ([app_shell.py:139-140,212](../../vault/design/templates/app_shell.py) [VERIFIED]) until ADR-E retires it; the cross-domain synthesis into ONE narrative is ADR-C; the daily-execution compile is ADR-D. So the operator still sees the same ≤4 thin cards, not ten sections and not the two-artifact S146 plan, after this ADR alone — the widened authoring is un-surfaced substrate until ADR-E. That is the deliberate first move of a beaded six-ADR sequence, not a free lunch. Long-term, the always-on domain vocabulary this records (`plan_model::<domain>` for the ten) is the substrate the rich render (ADR-E) must lay out and the synthesis (ADR-C) must reconcile — which is why a later NARROWING of the always-on set would strand recorded rich-domain entries and is a moderate-reversibility commitment, not a one-way door.

### Consequences

**Positive:**
- The plan is authored by the full always-on care team — up to ten specialists reasoning over the operator's identity-stripped record — instead of the effective four the `_contract_section` ValueError caps it at today ([client.py:538-544](../../scripts/model/client.py) [VERIFIED]).
- The activation policy is now an explicit, single-sourced four-bucket contract (always-on ten / cross-cutting inputs / progressive three / excluded three) grounded in the contracts doc's own framing, so the downstream ADRs (B–F) build against a stated interface rather than an implicit one.
- The change reuses the already-built roster infrastructure: `_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` / `PLAN_DOMAINS` (13) and `activation.CARD_DOMAINS` / `CROSS_CUTTING_INPUTS` / `active_domains` need no edit; only the authoring coupling and the activation floor move.
- The change lands entirely outside the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core (and the broader inner-engine glob) and does NOT alter how genetics reaches the assembler — TODAY the de-id `genetic-trait-classes` token via the frozen `router.summarize` ([context_assembler.py:28-31](../../scripts/plan/context_assembler.py) [VERIFIED]); ADR-0051 owns any change to that — so it neither reopens the frozen surface nor adds a NEW model-boundary egress class (it does scale the EXISTING one — see the forward-egress Negative).

**Negative:**
- **This ADR widens AUTHORING, NOT RENDERING — the honest sequencing cost (grounded, no free-lunch).** The always-on ten author into the rich comprehensive record: `care_chat.synthesize` folds each active domain beyond `RENDERABLE_DOMAINS` (via `author_rich`) into one composed version and records it under the disjoint `plan-model::` namespace ([care_chat.py:545,584-585](../../scripts/serve/care_chat.py); `_PREFIX_MODEL = "plan-model::"`, [plan_model.py:58](../../scripts/store/plan_model.py) [VERIFIED]). But the front-door RENDER does not change: `_plan_zone` iterates the four `_PLAN_LABELS` (workout / nutrition / supplements / peptides, [app_shell.py:139-140](../../vault/design/templates/app_shell.py) [VERIFIED]), reads only the thin `plan::<domain>` path ([app_shell.py:212](../../vault/design/templates/app_shell.py); `_PREFIX_PLAN = "plan::"`, [plan_schema.py:63](../../scripts/store/plan_schema.py) [VERIFIED]), and has ZERO references to `plan_model` (it never reads the comprehensive record). So after ADR-0052 alone the operator still SEES ≤4 thin cards; the widened always-on-ten authoring lands in the un-surfaced `plan-model::` record that ADR-E (rich render) retires `_plan_zone` to lay out. The cross-domain synthesis (ADR-C) and the daily-execution compile (ADR-D) are likewise separate downstream ADRs. ADR-0052 is the necessary substrate move — more domains AUTHORED — not the demonstrated rendered deliverable.
- **Per-generation author calls rise from an effective four to up to ten always-on (plus any active progressive domain) — a standing latency + spend cost.** Each always-on domain is one no-train author call over the full contract-grounded prompt; the cost scales with the roster and recurs on every generation, not once. The operator-present LIVE run now spends ~2.5× the author budget of the four-domain path.
- **Plan quality now depends on the §5–§10 contract content.** A thin or stale entry in `specialist-plan-contracts.md` yields a thin section for that always-on domain; six more contract sections become load-bearing quality inputs that must be maintained, extending the ADR-0050 "contract content is a quality input" consequence to the wider roster.
- **The always-on domain vocabulary is a moderate-reversibility commitment.** Once the always-on ten record `plan_model::<domain>` entries, a later narrowing (dropping a domain from always-on) strands those recorded entries and forces the render / synthesis to handle a vocabulary that shrank — reversible, but not free (the corpus flags this: widening/narrowing the domain map is data, but it establishes the vocabulary the render / store keys depend on).
- **The activation floor must be RAISED without zeroing an existing operator's plan, and UNCONDITIONALLY.** `active_plan_domains` currently floors at the renderable core-four so a signal-less surface still gets a baseline plan ([plan_loop.py:222+](../../scripts/serve/plan_loop.py) [VERIFIED]); raising the floor to the always-on ten must preserve that never-zeroes property AND be an unconditional union — the existing conditional idiom (`if not (active & …)`, [plan_loop.py:233-235](../../scripts/serve/plan_loop.py) [VERIFIED]) returns fewer than ten for a partial-signal operator (Validation, the partial-signal floor probe, AR-006). A floor that drops below ten is the silent regression.
- **The roster widening MULTIPLIES the genetics egress surface — a forward interaction with ADR-0051 the operator should weigh.** ADR-0052 does not change the genetics mechanism (ADR-0051 owns that decision, `status: proposed`, UNBUILT). But both author legs read the SAME `assemble_context` record ([server.py:823,832,865-872](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED]), so widening the authors from four to up to ten is a MULTIPLIER on whatever the assembler carries: TODAY it scales the de-id `genetic-trait-classes`-token exposure from four to up to ten no-train author calls; IF ADR-0051-T1 lands (identity-stripped RAW genotypes to the authors — which ADR-0051 calls "the highest-stakes and least-mitigable exposure in the system," inherently re-identifying and NOT anonymized by identity-stripping), this same widening scales that raw-genome egress from four to up to ten author calls. Within the operator's identity-stripped-raw standard, but a real forward privacy consequence ADR-0052 neither creates nor mitigates — it SCALES it (AR-001).

**Neutral:**
- `RENDERABLE_DOMAINS` and the thin `_PLAN_VALIDATORS` path stay at four (RT-009); the always-on rich domains record first-class into the disjoint `plan-model::` namespace via Leg-2 `synthesize` ([care_chat.py:584-585](../../scripts/serve/care_chat.py) [VERIFIED]), not the thin validator path, so no thin-path validator is added for §5–§10 and `_plan_zone`'s four-label render is untouched.
- Genetics and labs continue to reach the domain authors as the cross-cutting substrate they already are (genetics via the frozen `router.summarize` de-id trait-classes token TODAY — the raw genotype is never read, [context_assembler.py:28-31](../../scripts/plan/context_assembler.py) [VERIFIED]; labs via `assemble_context`); this ADR records them as INPUT layers, it does not change how they flow (ADR-0051 owns any future change to the genetics representation).
- `_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` and `PLAN_DOMAINS` are already the full 13; the corpus's "dispatch is 4 domains" framing is corrected here to "effective authoring is capped at four by the contract-section coupling + the renderable narrow," with no registry edit required.

### Alternatives Considered

#### Alternative A: Keep four dispatch domains and widen only the RENDER
Leave the author loop at the effective four and improve the plan by making the render lay out more per domain.
- **Supporting evidence:** Zero change to the authoring wiring or the `_contract_section` coupling; the render is where the operator actually sees the plan, so richness there is visible immediately.
- **Trade-offs:** The other domains never get AUTHORED — `_contract_section` still ValueErrors for §5–§10 ([client.py:538-544](../../scripts/model/client.py) [VERIFIED]) — so there is nothing new for the render to lay out. Render is downstream of authoring; widening it alone lays out the same four sections with more chrome. Rejected: it does not touch the binding constraint.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the always-on ten were already authoring and the only gap were layout — which is exactly ADR-E's scope, AFTER this ADR.

#### Alternative B: Promote all 18 contract sections to authoring "domains"
Dispatch every section — including medical-liaison, medical-safety-reviewer, and care-assistant — as a plan-authoring domain.
- **Supporting evidence:** Maximal roster; a single uniform rule ("every section is a domain") with no bucket classification to maintain.
- **Trade-offs:** §16–§18 are not domain-card authors: medical-liaison is the human-gate safety spine, medical-safety-reviewer is a deploy-time gate ("not a runtime plan contributor"), and care-assistant is the orchestrator with no agent profile ("it's `scripts/serve/care_chat.py`") ([specialist-plan-contracts.md:412,436,456](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]). Dispatching them as domains would either fabricate a "safety card" or invoke the orchestrator as one of its own briefed specialists — a category error. Rejected: it violates the contracts doc's own role placement.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** Never as authoring domains; medical-liaison's runtime role is the human-gate the synthesis (ADR-C) escalates to, not a card.

#### Alternative C: Big-bang all six roster→render ADRs (A–F) as one change
Ship the roster widening, onboarding capture, synthesis layer, daily-execution compile, rich render, and tweak loop together.
- **Supporting evidence:** The operator sees the full demonstrated output in one step; no intermediate "more cards but still thin" state.
- **Trade-offs:** The combined diff is unreviewable and couples the roster vocabulary to render / synthesis decisions not yet made; a regression anywhere in the six blocks all of them. The decision corpus deliberately beaded the sequence (B–F as `ckm1` / `noig` / `2drg` / `ahz9` / `nfdx`) so each is independently reviewable. Rejected: it trades reviewability for a shorter path and forfeits the frozen-surface isolation this ADR keeps.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the six changes were each trivial and tightly coupled — they are neither (each is a distinct architecture surface).

#### Alternative D: Make dermatology / GI / lymphatic always-on
Include §11–§13 in the always-on set so all 13 card domains author on every generation.
- **Supporting evidence:** A simpler policy (one "always-on 13" set, no progressive tier); every domain card is present for every operator.
- **Trade-offs:** §11–§13 are empty-state for an operator with no skin / gut / fluid goal or data — the contracts doc's own note says "the domain card renders only when the operator surfaces a goal or data" ([specialist-plan-contracts.md:293](../../design/specialist-plan-contracts.md) [VERIFIED]). Always-on would author a dermatology / GI / lymphatic card with nothing operator-specific to ground it, producing a fabricated or generic card — the exact anti-generic failure the platform exists to prevent. Rejected: it breaks the honesty-of-activation criterion.
- **When this becomes the right choice:** If the operator surfaced a persistent skin / gut / fluid concern, at which point `active_domains` already activates that specific domain — progressive activation IS the mechanism.

#### Dissent: "The widening is already done — this is wiring, not a decision"
**Source:** the grounded tree state — `_ROLE_OF_DOMAIN` and `PLAN_DOMAINS` are already 13, and `activation.active_domains` with the `CARD_DOMAINS` / `CROSS_CUTTING_INPUTS` partition already exists ([plan_driver.py:122-136](../../scripts/plan/plan_driver.py); [activation.py:26-89](../../scripts/plan/activation.py) [VERIFIED]). A reviewer can reasonably hold that the roster was widened by ADR-0043-T3 / ADR-0046, so ADR-0052 is merely a two-line `_AUTHOR_CONTRACT_SECTION` map edit plus an activation-floor bump — implementation, not architecture.
**Conditions under which this becomes the right choice:** If the ONLY remaining gap were the map entries, this would be a spec-level task, not an ADR. But the load-bearing decisions are the activation-policy classification (which of the 18 are always-on vs progressive vs cross-cutting-input vs excluded — a durable contract the render / synthesis depend on), the dispatch ordering (cross-cutting inputs before domain authors), and the moderate-reversibility domain-vocabulary commitment. Those are interface decisions the downstream ADRs (B–F) build on, so they belong in a record — and the grounding correction itself (the corpus's "dispatch is 4" is wrong about the mechanism) is exactly why an ADR, not a silent patch, is warranted.

### Validation Approach

**Confirmation criteria:**
- **Each always-on domain is authorable (executable):** call `_contract_section(domain)` for each of the always-on ten; assert each returns a non-empty section string and NONE raises `ValueError`. Threshold: 10/10 resolve non-empty; 0 ValueErrors. Today 6/10 raise (endocrine / cardiovascular / recovery / sleep / longevity / mental-performance), so this test REDs before the change and GREENs after — the non-tautological hook.
- **The activation floor is the always-on ten, UNCONDITIONALLY (executable):** run `plan_loop.active_plan_domains` over THREE surfaces — signal-less, PARTIAL-signal (touches only a subset of the ten, e.g. only `workout`), and all-signal — and assert each returns a SUPERSET of the always-on ten. Threshold: floored set ⊇ the always-on ten on ALL THREE. The partial-signal case is load-bearing: the existing floor is CONDITIONAL (`if not (active & RENDERABLE_DOMAINS): active |= …`, [plan_loop.py:233-235](../../scripts/serve/plan_loop.py) [VERIFIED]), and swapping the floored set in that same idiom passes the signal-less + all-signal endpoints while returning FEWER than ten for a partial-signal operator (touch only `workout` → `active & always_on_ten` non-empty → floor does not fire → one domain). The always-on ten MUST be an UNCONDITIONAL union, not the conditional baseline (AR-006).
- **The comprehensive record carries >4 authored domains (executable):** generate a plan over an all-signal fixture surface at 0 spend (fixture author envelopes); assert the `plan-model::` comprehensive version `synthesize` composes carries authored programs for ≥10 domains ([care_chat.py:584-585](../../scripts/serve/care_chat.py) [VERIFIED]). Threshold: ≥10 always-on programs in the composed version; the count the rich render (ADR-E) will lay out. NOTE: the thin `_plan_zone` render stays capped at the four `_PLAN_LABELS` reading the `plan::` path BY DESIGN (RT-009) — richness is ADR-E, not this ADR; the CONFIRMATION is the composed-version program COUNT, not the rendered card count (asserting the render shows ≥10 cards would FAIL and would be the wrong test).

**Falsification criteria:**
- **Frozen-surface probe → FAIL:** `git diff --numstat` on the ADR-0032 `<always-frozen>` core (`store.py` / `keying.py` / `pipeline.py` / `adjudicate.py` / `adjust.py` / `router.py`) AND the still-frozen glob members the six do not include (`orchestrate.py` / `generate_plan.py` / `assemble.py` / `track.py`) after the change. Threshold: **0 changed lines** in all six core files AND in `track.py` (an ADR-0032 glob member freed by no freeze-break); `orchestrate` / `generate_plan` / `assemble` are additionally kept out by the manifest (`orchestrate.generate_plans` is called-not-modified). ≥1 changed line in any means the change illegally touched a frozen surface — halt and re-scope to `client.py` / `activation.py` / `plan_loop.py` / `server.py` (AR-007: the six-only diff does NOT re-derive ADR-0032's wider engine freeze, so the probe covers `track.py` explicitly).
- **Unsourceable-domain probe → FAIL:** for each domain in the always-on ten, assert `_contract_section(domain)` does not raise. Threshold: 0 ValueErrors across the always-on ten; ≥1 means a dispatched domain has no contract section and will `_author_rich`→`None` silently at generation ([server.py:869-870](../../scripts/serve/server.py) [VERIFIED]) — the exact silent-thinning this ADR fixes; halt and add the missing section mapping. This probe is deliberately SCOPED to the always-on ten (not "every dispatch-eligible domain"): §11–§13 stay un-mapped by scope (OQ-5 / bead `a-plus-maxing-00kh`), and their containment is the separate progressive-containment probe; when a later ADR maps §11–§13, this probe extends to every dispatch-eligible domain (AR-005).
- **Floor-regression probe → FAIL:** assert `plan_loop.active_plan_domains` over a signal-less AND a PARTIAL-signal surface never returns fewer than the always-on ten. Threshold: floored set ⊇ the always-on ten on both; a partial-signal surface returning <10 means the floor was implemented as the conditional `if not (active & …)` idiom instead of an unconditional union (AR-006) — the silent regression a signal-less-only probe misses, and the RED-capable guard against a future change re-narrowing the floor.
- **Progressive-containment probe → FAIL:** activate a §11–§13 domain (surface a skin / gut / fluid goal) and assert NO partial or empty progressive card is RECORDED — the un-mapped `_contract_section` ValueError is swallowed by `_author_rich`→`None` and the domain is DROPPED, never shipped as a broken card ([server.py:865-872](../../scripts/serve/server.py); [care_chat.py:562-564](../../scripts/serve/care_chat.py) [VERIFIED]). Threshold: 0 recorded progressive cards while §11–§13 stay un-mapped (OQ-5 / bead `a-plus-maxing-00kh`) — the honest containment of the deferred authoring gap (AR-005).
- **Progressive-fabrication probe → FAIL:** generate a plan over a surface with NO dermatology / GI / lymphatic signal; assert NO §11–§13 card is authored. Threshold: 0 progressive-domain cards for an empty-state surface; ≥1 means progressive activation regressed to always-on and a card was fabricated (Alternative D's failure).
- **Time horizon:** run the frozen-surface, unsourceable-domain, floor-regression, and progressive-fabrication probes at the first build, at the operator-present LIVE run (OQ-4), and at every release thereafter, and before the July-2026 physician-visit deliverable.
