> It CREATES the progressive-activation gate `scripts/plan/activation.py` — `active_domains(operator_surface)`
> computes the active-domain set from the operator's surface (stated goals / tracked-stream data /
> care-conversation mentions / lab-or-genetic-trait touches) and partitions the §1-§13 CARD-EMITTING
> domain specialists from the §14-genetics / §15-labs CROSS-CUTTING INPUTS (`design/specialist-plan-contracts.md`,
> disposition #25) — and GROWS the three dispatch registries from the four hard-wired domains to the

> `_PLAN_TRANSLATORS` + `_DOMAIN_KIND` (`generate_plan.py:461-466,128-133`). The gate is at DISPATCH
> (disposition #24): don't brief an inactive specialist. **The gate is created + unit-tested here; its

> emits REAL specialist DOMAIN PROGRAMs whose `prescription` is the CANONICAL PERIODIZED shape (dated

> Option (B) (pinning the shape in `domain_program.validate`), because `domain_program.validate` is the
> frozen change-controlled seam and is NOT in this manifest. The recipe schedules a MANDATORY RED test with
> a canonical periodized-blocks prescription asserting: (1) the recorded plan carries NO `load` at ANY depth,
> (2) a blocks-only prescription does NOT crash plan generation (honest handling — projected renderable or
> honest no-plan, never a raised ValueError), (3) a roster specialist's periodized program round-trips
