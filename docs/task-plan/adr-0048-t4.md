credential step is APPENDED, not a 9-step rebuild (AC-1).** The mockup (Surface A) shows an 8-done + 1-
active 9-step stepper (Demographics / Goals / Training & activity / Diet / Supplements & peptides /
Medications / Health & lifestyle / Documents / Connect). But the LIVE `intake.py` `_STEPS` (`:46-49`) is
the 6-step "Build your plan" flow: `["Your info & documents", "Goals & priorities", "Training",

(`:180-195`). This task APPENDS ONE step — "Connect your data & keys" — to the live `_STEPS` (→ 7 steps,
rendered "Step 7 of 7"), reusing the live `.rail`/`.rstep`/`.panel`/`.seclab`/`.doc`/`.field` idiom. Do
NOT re-author the stepper to the mockup's 9-step content (the mockup's step LABELS are its own design-
exploration vocabulary; the reconciled contract is "append the credential step to the LIVE stepper").
The credential step's CONTENT (the cards + the D5 key choice) IS faithful to Surface A.
