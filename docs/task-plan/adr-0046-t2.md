> Wave-5 leaf task (build-plan Wave 5; Dependency Map: `ADR-0046-T1 --> ADR-0046-T2` [the `active_domains`
> gate whose renderable subset the fraction is computed against] + `ADR-0043-T3 --> ADR-0046-T2` [shared
> `plan_loop.py`; the front-door rewrite settles the file, the threshold re-base rebases on it]). Both
> predecessors are on `origin/main` (Wave 4 merged #336/#337, main @ `1cd134c2`). This task MODIFIES ONE
> region of `scripts/serve/plan_loop.py` — the large-change HOLD threshold — so the hold fires on a
> **strict majority of the active RENDERABLE set** (`|renderable|` = `len(active & RENDERABLE_DOMAINS)`,
> the `:273` `renderable` local), NOT the retired fixed count of `3` and NOT `len(active)`. On a closed
> 4-domain universe `3` was a 3-of-4 majority; on the grown §1-§13 roster (`PLAN_DOMAINS` = 13,

> protect. `len(renderable)` is COMMENSURABLE with the numerator (the ratio ∈[0,1], reaches 1.0) and
> reproduces the retired constant-3 for `|renderable|=4` (3-of-4 holds, 2-of-4 does not). The denominator
> is the `renderable` local (`:273`), already in scope at the `:304` check.
>
> **The change is one region:** the `_change_magnitude(...) >= LARGE_CHANGE_THRESHOLD_DOMAINS` comparison
> at `plan_loop.py:304` re-bases to a strict-majority-of-`len(renderable)` test, and the superseded
> `LARGE_CHANGE_THRESHOLD_DOMAINS = 3` constant (`:51`) + its closed-4-domain comment (`:45-50`) are
> removed (AC-3 "no fixed-3 residue"). DISJOINT from ADR-0043-T3's `regenerate` front-door body (which
> already landed the `active`/`renderable` locals + the Leg-1/Leg-2 seam) — this task touches ONLY the
> threshold comparison + constant inside `regenerate`, no other def. It READS 0043-T3's `renderable`
> local; it does not recompute or re-derive the surface.
>
> **PF-S130-01 (THE CRUX, MANDATORY):** the re-base is a SWAP of the threshold RHS, so it must be pinned
> by tests that RED on the wrong denominator in BOTH directions. Two load-bearing distinguishers, each
> through the REAL `plan_loop.regenerate` / ADR-0040 hold path (not a unit shim):
> - **Correct-direction (`len(renderable)` vs `len(active)`) — `test_renderable_swap_holds_even_with_rich_active`:**

>   `len(active)` impl (bar 6 > magnitude 3) fails to hold and REDs this test; the `len(renderable)` impl

> - **Constant-vs-fraction — `test_narrow_surface_2_of_2_holds`:** a narrowed 2-active-renderable surface

> AC-1's "parity with the retired constant" clause), NOT a distinguisher. Every fixture is SYNTHETIC

> **Numerator cap (GROUNDED — the reason the denominator is `|renderable|`):** `_change_magnitude` (`:386`)
> iterates `promoted = _promoted_domains(result)` (`:355`, the `recorded=True` domains) ⊆ `renderable =

> maxes at 4** — which is precisely why the denominator is `|renderable|` (≤ 4, commensurable), not

> former "6-of-10" AC-1 point DISSOLVES (a magnitude-6 swap is not real-path-reachable) and no
