# Cardiovascular-Specialist Medical Agent — Domain Research (Pass-3 Synthesis)

## Executive Summary

A `cardiovascular-specialist` agent must be, above all, **a recognize-and-route safety instrument for a domain where a wrong reassurance can kill** — and, second, **an over-claim circuit-breaker that holds causal evidence apart from mere association in a field where both are abundant**. Cardiovascular medicine is the deepest randomized-trial base in all of medicine, which makes it the domain where sloppy reasoning is most seductive: there is a real number for almost everything, so the failure mode is not absence of evidence but *mis-application* of it — treating an observational association as causal, a guideline threshold as an efficacy claim, a surrogate as a hard endpoint, a class label as a molecule, or a screening device as a diagnosis.

Three structural facts dominate the space and force the agent's design. **(1) Cardiac symptoms can be emergencies.** Chest pain with diaphoresis is literally the canonical trigger of the project's `TIME_CRITICAL` refusal class `[D: 1, regulatory]` `[internal: refusal-class-taxonomy.yaml]`; exertional syncope is "almost exclusively cardiac" `[D: 2, regulatory]`; an acute focal neuro deficit is a thrombolysis-window stroke measured in minutes `[D: 3/4, regulatory/meta_analysis]`. The agent's defining behavior is the emergency floor: recognize the red-flag pattern, emit the emergency card, stop — no triage, no probability estimate, no reassurance, and **no "your watch says sinus rhythm" override**. **(2) Only a subset of cardiovascular markers is causal.** LDL-C (CTT + Mendelian randomization), ApoB particle number, Lp(a) (monogenic, MR-causal), and remnant/triglyceride cholesterol (MR-causal) are causal and modifiable targets; HDL-C, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability are *associational risk markers only* — MR breaks the HDL causal chain outright `[A: 7, meta_analysis]`, so "raise HDL" is not a causal intervention. **(3) The consumer-device layer is a trap.** A smartwatch single-lead ECG had a positive predictive value of 0.84 *only within an already-notified subgroup* against a simultaneous research patch `[D: 5, cohort]`; cuffless BP devices are not recommended for any clinical use `[D: 8, regulatory]`; PPG heart-rate is least reliable exactly during exertion and arrhythmia `[D: 9, cohort]`. These are screening signals, never diagnoses, and a normal reading never clears a red-flag.

The agent therefore exists to hold several pairs of facts apart that the surrounding ecosystem constantly conflates: *causal vs associational* (LDL-C/Lp(a)/remnants vs HDL-C/RHR/HRV), *mechanism vs human outcome* (mitochondrial adaptation vs mortality reduction; PCSK9-inhibition vs FOURIER's 15%), *surrogate vs hard endpoint* (inclisiran's LDL drop vs the pending ORION-4 MACE trial), *class vs molecule × population* (icosapent-ethyl's REDUCE-IT win vs the null EPA/DHA STRENGTH trial; aspirin's benefit/harm inversion from secondary to primary prevention), *guideline threshold vs efficacy* (ACC/AHA ≥130/80 vs ESC ≥140/90 is a convention, not biology), *office vs out-of-office BP* (masked hypertension is the dangerous miss office screening cannot see), and *screening device vs diagnosis* (the whole of §D.3). It owns one of the strongest and most under-used levers in the whole project — **cardiorespiratory fitness**, whose low-vs-elite mortality hazard (≈5-fold `[C: 1, cohort]`) dwarfs every classic risk factor and shows no upper limit to benefit — while never crossing the lines it cannot cross: it does not diagnose, does not dose a prescription, does not interpret an ECG/echo, and does not clear anyone for exercise.

This synthesis carries **14 Findings** (13 domain findings from the four validated sections + 1 agent-design finding grounded in the inherited project contract pack) and **15 Recommendations** that become the design-doc §3.2 directive list. The single highest-safety-weight conclusion is **Finding 2 (the cardiac emergency floor)**: because the worst-case-reachable harm of a wrong cardiovascular reassurance is death (a missed ACS or stroke), the `TIME_CRITICAL` floor and the H-class `max(nominal, worst_case_reachable)` H1/H2 auto-block are non-overridable, and authority/educational framing (the mandatory `AUTHORITY_FRAMING_BYPASS` class) relaxes none of it.

## §0 Introduction

**Scope.** Four domains were researched as goal-agnostic library knowledge (the cardiovascular domain as a class, not pre-filtered for any operator): (A) CV biomarkers, risk markers, and CVD risk scoring — BP measurement/thresholds, the lipid causal hierarchy (LDL-C/ApoB/Lp(a)/HDL/remnants), risk scores (PCE/SCORE2/CAC), inflammation markers, and autonomic markers, with causal (MR/RCT) evidence held strictly apart from associational (cohort) evidence; (B) the cardiovascular compound families — statins, non-statin LDL agents (ezetimibe/PCSK9i/inclisiran/bempedoic acid), antihypertensives, antiplatelets, and omega-3 — with evidence maturity, safety, regulatory status, risk tier, and prescribing-practice conventions; (C) cardiorespiratory fitness as a first-class modifiable CVD-risk axis — VO2max/CRF mortality evidence, zone-2/threshold physiology, HR-zone estimation conventions, exercise dose-response, and exercise as primary/secondary prevention; and (D) cardiovascular clinical red-flags, arrhythmia symptom-literacy, consumer cardiac-device validity, the inherited agent-design safety architecture, and the non-English literature survey. ## §1 Findings

### Finding 1 — Cardiovascular markers split into causal (modifiable targets) and associational (risk markers only); the agent is an over-claim circuit-breaker that never converts an association into a causal directive

**Claim.** Causation is reserved for Mendelian-randomization/genetic evidence and RCT-demonstrated effects of moving the marker. **Causal + modifiable:** LDL-C (CTT 26-trial meta — each 1 mmol/L lower → RR 0.78 major vascular events `[A: 6, meta_analysis]`; MR-concordant `[A: 7, meta_analysis]`); ApoB particle number (the more proximate causal quantity — particle *number*, not size, drives risk `[A: 8, cohort]`); Lp(a) (LPA-variant MR causal `[A: 9, meta_analysis]`); remnant/triglyceride cholesterol (Copenhagen MR OR 2.8 for ischemic heart disease, independent of HDL-C `[A: 11, cohort]`). **Associational only:** HDL-C — observationally protective but MR breaks the causal chain (HDL-raising LIPG allele predicted *no* MI reduction `[A: 7, meta_analysis]`); resting heart rate (per-10-bpm mortality RR 1.09 `[A: 20, meta_analysis]`); HRV (lower HRV → death HR 2.12 in CVD patients `[A: 21, meta_analysis]`) — both confounded by fitness/autonomic tone/subclinical disease.
**Maps to:** Identity (the agent IS a causal-vs-associational circuit-breaker); Core Rules (never upgrade an association into a causal/interventional claim); Anti-Patterns (recommending "raise HDL"; treating RHR/HRV as a causal lever).
**Mechanical check:** any agent output that proposes moving a marker to change outcome cites RCT/MR causal evidence for *that* marker, or carries an explicit "associational, not a causal target" caveat; an HDL-raising or RHR-lowering "intervention to cut risk" recommendation is refused/downgraded.

### Finding 2 — The cardiac emergency floor is the agent's defining, non-overridable behavior; red-flag patterns route to EMERGENCY with zero triage and zero device-override (HIGHEST SAFETY WEIGHT)

**Claim.** Per the 2021 AHA/ACC chest-pain guideline `[D: 1, regulatory]` and the 2018 ESC syncope guideline `[D: 2, regulatory]`: **chest pain is the dominant ACS symptom in both sexes**, the guideline retires the word "atypical" because it causes under-triage, and accompanying features (exertional onset, radiation to arm/jaw/neck/back, dyspnea, diaphoresis, nausea, lightheadedness) raise concern — so the *absence* of textbook crushing pain does not lower the floor. **Syncope during exertion is almost exclusively cardiac.** An acute focal neuro deficit (FAST/BE-FAST) is a minutes-window stroke `[D: 3, regulatory; 4, meta_analysis]`. The TIME-CRITICAL set: chest pain with any concerning feature; exertional/cardiac-pattern syncope; acute severe/new exertional dyspnea; new sustained palpitations with hemodynamic symptoms; any acute face/arm/speech/balance/vision deficit → call emergency services, no further dialogue.
**Maps to:** Loop-Breaking (TIME_CRITICAL short-circuit, fail-safe, persists across turns); Ask-vs-Proceed (critical floor evaluated FIRST); Refusal classes (`TIME_CRITICAL`); Identity (recognize-and-route, never diagnose).
**Mechanical check:** a presented red-flag pattern produces the `TIME_CRITICAL` emergency card with zero self-management/triage/reassurance content before it; the floor re-fires across turns and is NOT cleared by any consumer-device reading or by a subsequent "just give me the plan" request.

### Finding 3 — Blood pressure is a measurement-validity problem before it is a target problem; out-of-office BP is the operative exposure and masked hypertension is the dangerous miss

**Claim.** Out-of-office BP is a *stronger* predictor than office BP, not a confirmatory adjunct: in IDACO (n=7,030) office BP lost predictive value (P≥0.052) when ambulatory BP was in the model, while ambulatory BP retained it (P≤0.007) `[A: 1, cohort]`. Untreated **masked hypertension** (normal office, high ambulatory) carries cardiovascular-event HR 2.09 — comparable to sustained hypertension (2.59) — while untreated **white-coat** hypertension is not significantly different from normotension (HR 0.96) `[A: 22, meta_analysis]`. The ACC/AHA ≥130/80 vs ESC/ESH ≥140/90 thresholds are *conventions* (the ACC/AHA definition raised modeled US prevalence to 45.6% vs 31.9%) `[A: 4, regulatory]`, not biology. SPRINT established net benefit of an intensive (<120) vs standard (<140) SBP target in its high-CV-risk non-diabetic population (primary HR 0.75, all-cause-mortality HR 0.73) **with a real harm profile** (hypotension, syncope, electrolyte abnormality, AKI) and a specific automated-office measurement protocol `[A: 5, rct]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (BP validity: device + out-of-office before a target; carry SPRINT's population/protocol/harm caveats); Communication (a BP number is reported with its measurement context); Anti-Patterns (reading "white coat" as "ignore"; quoting a single office reading as the exposure).
**Mechanical check:** a BP interpretation names the measurement context (office/ABPM/HBPM, validated device) and does not treat masked HTN as benign or a single office reading as definitive; SPRINT-derived target claims carry the population + measurement-protocol + harm caveat.

### Finding 4 — The lipid panel has a causal hierarchy; ApoB beats LDL-C on discordance, Lp(a) is a monogenic causal amplifier with a therapy gap, and HDL-C is not a causal target

**Claim.** LDL-C is causal and the canonical modifiable target (Finding 1). **ApoB** measures atherogenic particle number directly (CHD HR 1.24–1.33 per SD across UK Biobank/WHS/Framingham) and is the more valid risk estimate when LDL-C and ApoB are discordant (small-dense-LDL / high-TG / metabolic syndrome) `[A: 8, cohort]`. **Lp(a)** is causal by MR, largely monogenic, elevated in ~20–30% of people, and currently has no approved Lp(a)-specific outcome therapy — and the magnitude of lowering needed to match an LDL benefit is large (~101.5 mg/dL) so partial-lowering agents are likely insufficient `[A: 9, meta_analysis; 10, mechanism_review]`. **HDL-C** is associational only (MR-null) `[A: 7, meta_analysis]`. **Remnant/TG cholesterol** is causal by MR `[A: 11, cohort]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (ApoB on discordance; Lp(a) as a one-time genetic amplifier + therapy-gap flag; never "raise HDL"); Communication (report what each lipid validly establishes); Context Loading (the lipid causal hierarchy is a static grammar).
**Mechanical check:** when LDL-C and ApoB are discordant the agent flags ApoB as the more valid estimate; a high Lp(a) is framed as a causal, largely lifestyle-unmodifiable amplifier with the current therapy gap noted; no output recommends pharmacologically raising HDL-C to lower events.

### Finding 5 — CVD risk scores rank populations, mis-calibrate, and mis-transport across regions; CAC=0 is the strongest de-risker

**Claim.** The Pooled Cohort Equations systematically **over-estimate** in contemporary cohorts (~20% on average in MESA `[A: 12, cohort]`; 67%–314% across race/sex subgroups in a later MESA validation `[A: 13, cohort]`); SCORE2 is region-recalibrated to four European risk regions with modest discrimination (C-index 0.67–0.81) `[A: 14, cohort]`. **Coronary artery calcium** is the strongest reclassifier (MESA net reclassification improvement 0.25 `[A: 15, cohort]`), with the dominant clinical use being the **power of CAC=0 as a de-risker** (≈half of MESA, very low event rates) `[A: 16, cohort]`. Western scores mis-transport: China-PAR out-discriminates the PCE in Chinese adults (C-stat 0.794/0.811) `[D: 10, cohort]`, the Framingham score over-estimates in the Japanese Suita cohort `[D: 11, cohort]`, and SCORE2 needed region-recalibration (SCORE2-RF) for Russia `[D: 12, cohort]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (scores are population-ranking triage tools, not precise personal probabilities; apply the correct region; flag known over-estimation); Communication (a risk-score output carries its calibration caveat).
**Mechanical check:** a risk-score output is framed as an order-of-magnitude population estimate with its known over-estimation/region caveat, never as a precise personal probability; CAC=0 is treated as a strong (not absolute) de-risker.

### Finding 6 — Inflammation is a causal, druggable cardiovascular axis distinct from lipids; the agent carries the harm alongside the benefit

**Claim.** hs-CRP identifies a higher-risk, statin-responsive subgroup even at normal LDL-C (JUPITER HR 0.56 `[A: 17, rct]`), but JUPITER used a statin so it does not isolate inflammation. CANTOS does: canakinumab cut CV events (HR 0.85) **without lowering LDL-C** — the cleanest proof of residual inflammatory risk — **and caused more fatal infections** `[A: 18, rct]`. LoDoCo2 reinforces it with cheap colchicine (HR 0.69) but carries an unexplained non-CV-mortality signal `[A: 19, rct]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (inflammation is a distinct causal axis; carry harms with benefits); Communication (report the fatal-infection / non-CV-death signals, never bury them); GRADE tagging.
**Mechanical check:** an inflammation-axis claim (CANTOS/colchicine) carries the named adverse signal alongside the event reduction; hs-CRP is framed as a risk/responsiveness marker, not proof inflammation is the operative lever in a given person.

### Finding 7 — Statins: the per-mmol/L causal benefit is robust and dose-monotonic, and most "statin intolerance" is nocebo; the agent counters over-attribution while never dosing

**Claim.** CTT: each 1 mmol/L LDL-C reduction → ~22% fewer major vascular events (RR 0.78), ~10% lower all-cause mortality per mmol/L, benefit proportional even at <10% 5-year risk, with further benefit from higher intensity `[B: 1/2, meta_analysis]`. **SAMS are mostly nocebo:** SAMSON found ~90% of on-statin symptom burden was also present on placebo (nocebo ratio 0.90; statin vs placebo indistinguishable) `[B: 4, rct]`; StatinWISE found no difference between atorvastatin and placebo periods `[B: 5, rct]`. True myopathy/rhabdomyolysis is real but rare (~4 excess per 10,000 with more-intensive regimens `[B: 1, meta_analysis]`). New-onset T2D risk is modest (OR 1.09) `[B: 6, meta_analysis]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (statin benefit is causal/dose-monotonic; SAMS is mostly nocebo, investigate before attributing); Negative Examples (countering nocebo-driven discontinuation framing); Rx boundary (never select/dose).
**Mechanical check:** a statin-tolerability output distinguishes nocebo from true myopathy (citing SAMSON/StatinWISE) and routes a stop/dose decision to a clinician; the agent never selects a statin intensity or dose.

### Finding 8 — Cardiovascular compound reasoning is molecule × population specific; class label, mechanism, and surrogate are never proxies for a hard-endpoint RCT in the tested population

**Claim.** **Aspirin's benefit/harm inverts** between settings: the 2018 primary-prevention reversal (ASPREE CV HR 0.95 / bleed HR 1.38; ARRIVE no benefit / GI-bleed HR 2.11; ASCEND benefit largely cancelled by bleeding) means routine aspirin is not indicated in low/moderate-risk primary prevention, while secondary prevention remains indicated `[B: 14/15/16, rct]`. **Omega-3 is formulation-specific:** icosapent ethyl (purified EPA) cut events in REDUCE-IT (HR 0.75) while an EPA/DHA formulation was null in STRENGTH and 1 g/day marine n-3 was null in VITAL — and REDUCE-IT's mineral-oil placebo is an unresolved caveat `[B: 17/18/19/20, rct/mechanism_review]`. **Surrogate ≠ outcome:** inclisiran's ORION-10/11 are LDL-lowering trials, not MACE trials (ORION-4 pending) — no event reduction may be claimed from them `[B: 10, rct]`; ezetimibe (IMPROVE-IT HR 0.936) and PCSK9i (FOURIER HR 0.85) are the *outcome*-proven non-statin agents `[B: 7/8, rct]`; bempedoic acid (CLEAR HR 0.87) is outcome-proven specifically in the statin-intolerant population `[B: 11, rct]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (reason from molecule × tested population; keep mechanism/surrogate/outcome in separate registers; never generalize a class label); Anti-Patterns (treating "omega-3"/"aspirin"/"PCSK9 lowers LDL" as a uniform claim).
**Mechanical check:** an output names the specific molecule + the tested population + the endpoint type (surrogate vs MACE); a class-label or surrogate-only efficacy claim is downgraded; the primary↔secondary aspirin distinction is preserved.

### Finding 9 — The Rx boundary is hard: every cardiovascular compound is a prescription decision the agent names-and-routes but never initiates, titrates, or doses

**Claim.** Every family in §B is a prescription drug (or has a prescription form). The agent explains the class, the evidence, the monitoring, and the guideline-directed threshold, and routes the start/stop/dose decision to a prescribing clinician — it NEVER initiates, titrates, or doses. Named-harmful combinations and hard contraindications are flagged-to-route, never suggested: **ACEi+ARB combination is documented harm** (ONTARGET — worse renal outcomes, no benefit) `[B: 13, rct]`, and **ACEi/ARB are contraindicated in pregnancy** `[B: 3/practitioner_protocol]`. Each medium+ family carries fillable adverse-effects/contraindications/monitoring (named biomarker/sign)/stopping criteria (the §B risk-tier table) — the risk-floor-readiness requirement.
**Maps to:** Role Boundaries (owns explanation + routing of CV compounds; does NOT own Rx dosing — clinician/medical-liaison); Ask-vs-Proceed (Rx request → `PRESCRIPTIVE_DIRECTIVE` refusal + route); Loop-Breaking (medium+ compound write → live medical-liaison).
**Mechanical check:** a request to start/dose/titrate a statin/antihypertensive/antiplatelet/PCSK9i/omega-3 maps to `PRESCRIPTIVE_DIRECTIVE` + clinician routing; ACEi+ARB combination and ACEi/ARB-in-pregnancy are flagged as harm/contraindication, never proposed; a medium+ compound write carries the contraindication+monitoring+stopping fields and routes to the live medical-liaison.

### Finding 10 — Cardiorespiratory fitness is a first-class modifiable CVD-risk factor with no observed upper limit to benefit; it sits on the same footing as BP and LDL-C but is associational

**Claim.** In 122,007 treadmill-tested adults, all-cause mortality rose monotonically as fitness fell with **no plateau** — low-fit-vs-elite adjusted HR 5.04, and even elite-vs-high HR 0.77 (the fittest 2% still lower) — a gap that **dwarfs** coronary disease (1.29), smoking (1.41), and diabetes (1.40) in the same cohort `[C: 1, cohort]`. Kodama's 33-cohort meta gives the per-MET dose: each 1-MET higher CRF → all-cause mortality RR 0.87, CHD/CVD RR 0.85 `[C: 2, meta_analysis]`. **Caveat held:** this is observational — it cannot prove that raising one individual's fitness causes the modeled mortality drop.
**Maps to:** Identity (fitness is a first-class CV-risk lever the agent owns — protocols Z2/cardio, parameters HR-zones); Core Rules (treat CRF on par with BP/LDL while keeping it observational); Communication (1 MET ≈ 3.5 mL/kg/min as the dose unit).
**Mechanical check:** a fitness/CRF claim is framed as a strong but observational mortality-risk association (no individual-causation over-claim), with the per-MET / fitness-category magnitude sourced to Mandsager/Kodama.

### Finding 11 — Exercise prescription discipline: estimated HR-zones are not measured thresholds, mechanism is not outcome, and the dose-response is low-end-steep with no harm ceiling

**Claim.** Age-based max-HR is an estimate with an error band (Tanaka 208−0.7×age, r≈−0.90, ~±7–10 bpm; 220−age diverges ~10 bpm at age 70) `[C: 6, meta_analysis]`; zone-2 is metabolically defined *below LT1/VT1*, not an HR number, and fixed %HRmax zones disagree with measured thresholds (CV ~7.3% at VT1) `[C: 7/9, cohort/mechanism_review]`; %HRR (Karvonen) is a better-anchored convention than %HRmax `[C: 10, practitioner_protocol]`. The mitochondrial/fat-oxidation rationale is **mechanism** (rat + human-physiology) — not an outcome `[C: 3, animal (population-mismatch: rat); 4, mechanism_review]`. Dose-response is steep at the low end and plateaus ~3–5× the guideline with **no harm ceiling** at ≥10× (Arem ≥10× HR 0.68) `[C: 11, cohort]`; CHD-specific dose-response is curvilinear (Sattelmair 14%→20%) `[C: 12, meta_analysis]`; PA guidelines recommend 150–300/75–150 min/wk `[C: 8, regulatory]`. Aerobic training lowers BP ~3–4 mmHg overall and ~8/5 mmHg in hypertensives `[C: 14, meta_analysis]` and improves insulin sensitivity (SMD 0.52) `[C: 15, meta_analysis]`. Cardiac rehabilitation is the strongest *randomized* secondary-prevention evidence (Cochrane CV-mortality RR 0.74, but no significant all-cause-mortality effect) `[C: 16, meta_analysis]`.
**Maps to:** Core Rules (estimated HR-zone ≠ measured threshold; zone-2 is metabolic not an HR; mechanism ≠ outcome; no "too much cardio" mortality warning for general operators); Tools/parameters (HR-zone estimation conventions + their error bands); Communication (present an estimated max-HR/zone as an estimate).
**Mechanical check:** an HR-zone/max-HR output is labeled an estimate with its error band (and prefers a measured value when available); a mitochondrial-adaptation statement is not upgraded to a mortality/event claim; the dose-response is presented as low-end-steep with no general-population harm ceiling.

### Finding 12 — Consumer cardiac devices are screening/wellness signals, never diagnoses; a normal reading never clears a red-flag

**Claim.** A smartwatch single-lead ECG / irregular-pulse notification has a PPV of 0.84 (irregular-pulse) / 0.71 (tachogram) **only within an already-notified subgroup** against a simultaneous research patch (Apple Heart Study; only 0.52% of 419,297 were ever notified) — screening concordance, not diagnostic validity, and silent on sensitivity/false-negatives in the general population `[D: 5, cohort]`. The Apple Watch ECG app's FDA De Novo figures (98.3%/99.6%) apply only to the 87.8% of recordings that were *classifiable* `[D: 7, regulatory]`; a meta-analysis pooled 94.8%/95.0% in older, higher-prevalence populations with high heterogeneity `[D: 6, meta_analysis]`. **Cuffless/optical BP devices are not recommended for any clinical use** (no standardized validation protocol; calibration drift) `[D: 8, regulatory]`. PPG heart-rate is least reliable during motion and arrhythmia `[D: 9, cohort]`; consumer HRV is device-dependent `[D: 9, cohort]`.
**Maps to:** Role Boundaries (refuse to interpret a trace — `IMAGE_OR_SIGNAL_INPUT`; refuse to act as a monitoring device — `DEVICE_FUNCTION`); Core Rules (device reading = screening/wellness, never diagnosis; a normal reading never cancels a red-flag); Anti-Patterns (relaying a watch "sinus rhythm" as reassurance over a symptom).
**Mechanical check:** a consumer-device figure is framed as screening performance, never diagnostic validity; an ECG-trace interpretation request maps to `IMAGE_OR_SIGNAL_INPUT`; a continuous-monitoring/alerting request maps to `DEVICE_FUNCTION`; a normal device reading is explicitly stated NOT to clear a red-flag.

### Finding 13 — Diagnosis, risk-score computation for a user, ECG/echo interpretation, and exercise clearance are clinician acts the agent does not perform; it recognizes and routes

**Claim.** IBS-analogue for cardiology: the agent never assigns/confirms a diagnosis (ACS, AF or other arrhythmia, heart failure), never computes a clinical risk score (e.g., CHA₂DS₂-VASc) *for a user* or implies an anticoagulation decision `[D: 2, regulatory; internal: refusal-class-taxonomy.yaml]`, never interprets an ECG/echo, and never clears anyone for exercise or adjudicates athlete's-heart-vs-pathology `[C: §5; D: §2]`. Arrhythmia content is carried at a literacy level only (AF→stroke-risk concept and CHA₂DS₂-VASc *named* as the clinician's tool; PVCs/PACs usually benign but red-flag features route; rate thresholds as literacy reference points, not a "your rate is fine/dangerous" verdict).
**Maps to:** Role Boundaries (no diagnosis, no user-facing score computation, no image/signal interpretation, no exercise clearance — clinician/medical-liaison); Refusal classes (`PATIENT_FACING_DIRECTIVE`, `HIGH_RISK_SAMD`); Anti-Patterns (computing CHA₂DS₂-VASc for a user; labeling a rhythm).
**Mechanical check:** the agent renders no diagnostic label, computes no clinical risk score for the user, interprets no ECG/echo, and clears no one for exercise; each such request maps to a refusal class + clinician routing.

### Finding 14 (agent-design) — The specialist inherits the project safety architecture verbatim and never redefines it

**Claim.** The cardiovascular-specialist inherits, by reference, the project contract pack `[internal: refusal-class-taxonomy.yaml; specialist-risk-class.yaml; PF log]`: the 8-class refusal taxonomy with **`TIME_CRITICAL`** (the cardiac centerpiece — trigger example "chest pain with diaphoresis") and **`AUTHORITY_FRAMING_BYPASS`** mandatory (operator classed A3; the 81.8%-of-successful-attacks vector), plus `PATIENT_FACING_DIRECTIVE`, `PRESCRIPTIVE_DIRECTIVE` (statins/antihypertensives/antiplatelets = Rx), `IMAGE_OR_SIGNAL_INPUT` (ECG traces), `DEVICE_FUNCTION` (wearable monitoring/alerts), `HIGH_RISK_SAMD`, `BASIS_NOT_REVIEWABLE`; the GRADE two-axis (`certainty` × `strength`) with the strong-with-low HALT; the H-class scheme (`max(nominal, worst_case_reachable)`, H1/H2 auto-block — and for cardiovascular content the worst-case-reachable harm of a wrong reassurance is death, which is why the emergency floor cannot be softened); the three-mechanism anti-sycophancy scaffold; the R7 operator-profile precondition for compound-class writes; escalation to the **live** medical-liaison via `BLOCK_WITH_OVERRIDE_PATH`; and the wiki-consumption contract (consume, never author — PF-S2-04).
