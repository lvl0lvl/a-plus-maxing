# Rubric — cortisol (biomarker, standard mode)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology & The HPA Axis:** cortisol = the primary glucocorticoid, synthesized in the adrenal **zona fasciculata** under pituitary **ACTH** (itself driven by hypothalamic CRH + AVP) — the **HPA axis**, with cortisol negative-feedback on hypothalamus/pituitary; the strong **circadian rhythm** (peak ~early morning/waking, the **cortisol awakening response CAR**, nadir ~midnight) — why TIMED sampling is essential; the **ultradian pulsatility**; binding — **~90% bound to corticosteroid-binding globulin (CBG/transcortin) + albumin; ~5-10% free** (the free fraction is active, per the free-hormone hypothesis); cortisol's physiological roles (gluconeogenesis/metabolism, anti-inflammatory/immune, vascular tone, stress response); the cortisol→cortisone interconversion (11β-HSD1/2) + tissue-level regulation.
- **B — Reference Ranges, Units & Dynamic Testing (load-bearing):** the morning (8 AM) serum cortisol reference (~**~5–25 µg/dL** = ~**~138–690 nmol/L**), late-afternoon lower, midnight low; units µg/dL ↔ nmol/L (×27.6); **a single random cortisol is rarely diagnostic — DYNAMIC + timed tests dominate:** the **1 mg overnight dexamethasone suppression test** (DST; screening for Cushing's — fail to suppress <1.8 µg/dL), **late-night salivary cortisol** (loss of nadir = Cushing's), **24-h urinary free cortisol (UFC)**, the **ACTH stimulation (cosyntropin) test** for adrenal insufficiency (peak cortisol cutoff), the morning cortisol thresholds for ruling in/out AI; salivary + urinary free-cortisol as free-fraction measures; thresholds are assay-dependent.
- **C — Measurement & Interferences:** serum cortisol by immunoassay vs **LC-MS/MS** (immunoassays cross-react with other steroids + synthetic glucocorticoids — e.g. prednisolone cross-reactivity; LC-MS/MS more specific + needed for low/dynamic-test accuracy); the **CBG problem** — total cortisol misleads when CBG is altered (estrogen/OCP/pregnancy RAISE CBG → high total but normal free; critical illness/nephrotic LOWER CBG → low total but normal free) → salivary/free cortisol or LC-MS/MS; salivary cortisol assays; 24-h UFC method; biotin + heterophile interferences; the standardization status; pre-analytics — exact sampling TIME, stress/venipuncture artifact, recent glucocorticoid use (topical/inhaled/exogenous), shift work.
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** **HIGH cortisol / Cushing's syndrome** (ACTH-dependent: pituitary [Cushing disease] / ectopic ACTH; ACTH-independent: adrenal adenoma/carcinoma; exogenous glucocorticoids the commonest cause) — features + the screening tests; **pseudo-Cushing's** (obesity, depression, alcohol, stress); **LOW cortisol / adrenal insufficiency** (primary = Addison's [adrenal, high ACTH]; secondary = pituitary/hypothalamic [low ACTH]; tertiary = exogenous-steroid-withdrawal HPA suppression — the commonest) — the adrenal-crisis risk; determinants that RAISE cortisol (acute + chronic stress, Cushing's, exogenous glucocorticoids [measured as cross-reactivity], estrogen↑CBG↑total, depression, alcohol, critical illness, sleep loss, intense exercise acutely) / LOWER (adrenal insufficiency, suppressive exogenous steroids [HPA suppression], hypopituitarism); the **"cortisol and chronic stress / adrenal fatigue" framing — address honestly: "adrenal fatigue" is NOT an endocrine-society-recognized diagnosis** (the evidence does not support it); associations of dysregulated cortisol with metabolic/CV/mood outcomes (cite cautiously); limitations — extreme time-dependence, the single-value uninterpretability, CBG confounding, the dynamic-test dependence.
