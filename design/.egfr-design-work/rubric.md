# Rubric — egfr (biomarker, standard mode; covers serum creatinine + the calculated eGFR)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology: Creatinine & What eGFR Estimates:** **creatinine** = a waste product of muscle creatine/phosphocreatine breakdown, produced at a rate proportional to **muscle mass**, freely filtered by the glomerulus + a small tubular secretion → serum creatinine inversely reflects glomerular filtration rate (GFR); **GFR** = the volume of plasma filtered by the glomeruli per unit time, the best overall index of kidney FUNCTION; **eGFR = GFR ESTIMATED from serum creatinine** (+ age, sex) via an equation — because measured GFR (inulin/iohexol clearance) is impractical; the non-linear creatinine↔GFR relationship (creatinine stays "normal" until GFR drops ~50% — the "creatinine-blind range", a key caveat); the muscle-mass + diet (cooked meat) + secretion determinants that make creatinine an imperfect filtration marker; cystatin C as a muscle-independent alternative filtration marker (introduce).
- **B — The eGFR Equations, Units & CKD Staging (load-bearing):** the **estimating equations** — MDRD (older), and the **CKD-EPI creatinine equation**; **THE LOAD-BEARING DEVELOPMENT: the 2021 CKD-EPI race-free refit (Inker et al. 2021 NEJM)** that removed the Black-race coefficient (the prior 2009 equation included a race multiplier now recognized as inappropriate) — explain the change + why; the **CKD-EPI creatinine-cystatin C (2021)** equation as most accurate; units — eGFR in **mL/min/1.73 m²** (body-surface-area normalized); serum creatinine in mg/dL (↔ µmol/L ×88.4); the **KDIGO CKD staging** — G1 (≥90), G2 (60-89), G3a (45-59), G3b (30-44), G4 (15-29), G5 (<15) mL/min/1.73m², combined with albuminuria (the GFR+ACR "heat map"); the eGFR cutoffs for CKD (<60 for ≥3 months) + the caveats (a single eGFR isn't CKD; age-related decline; the equations' bias/imprecision at higher GFR).
- **C — Measurement & Standardization:** **serum creatinine assays** — Jaffe (alkaline picrate, subject to non-creatinine chromogen interferences) vs enzymatic (more specific); **IDMS-traceable standardization (the NKDEP/IFCC creatinine standardization)** — critical because the eGFR equations are calibrated to IDMS-traceable creatinine; cystatin C measurement + its standardization (the ERM-DA471 reference material); interferences (Jaffe: bilirubin, glucose, ketones, cephalosporins, hemolysis; enzymatic: fewer); the eGFR is only as good as the creatinine input + the equation's population fit; non-GFR determinants of creatinine confound eGFR (muscle, diet, drugs blocking tubular secretion — cimetidine/trimethoprim raise creatinine without changing GFR); when measured GFR (iohexol/inulin) or cystatin-C-based eGFR is preferred.
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** **LOW eGFR / elevated creatinine** — acute kidney injury (pre-renal/intrinsic/post-renal) vs chronic kidney disease; the determinants: true GFR loss (CKD from diabetes [leading], hypertension, glomerulonephritis, PKD, aging) vs the non-GFR creatinine confounders (high muscle mass / bodybuilders + creatine supplementation RAISE creatinine → falsely LOW eGFR without true kidney disease; the cooked-meat effect; drugs blocking secretion); **HIGH eGFR / low creatinine** — low muscle mass / sarcopenia / cachexia / amputation / cirrhosis (falsely HIGH eGFR — a dangerous over-estimate in the frail), pregnancy (true hyperfiltration); the prognostic significance — **low eGFR + albuminuria independently predict cardiovascular disease, ESKD, and all-cause mortality** (cite the CKD Prognosis Consortium / named meta-analyses with effect sizes); CKD management thresholds (nephrology referral, drug dosing, contrast/nephrotoxin caution); limitations — eGFR is an ESTIMATE (the creatinine-blind range, muscle/diet/secretion confounding, equation imprecision at high GFR, not validated in extremes of body size / acute changing GFR); the role of cystatin C + measured GFR when creatinine-eGFR is unreliable.
