# Rubric — total-testosterone (biomarker, standard mode)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology & What It Measures:** testosterone synthesis (Leydig cells under LH; the HPG axis — GnRH→LH/FSH→testosterone, negative feedback); **total testosterone = the sum of SHBG-bound (~44%, tightly bound, NOT bioavailable) + albumin-bound (~50%, loosely bound, bioavailable) + free (~2%, the active fraction)**; the free-hormone hypothesis; diurnal rhythm (peak ~AM, ~morning sampling essential); T→DHT (5α-reductase) + T→estradiol (aromatase) conversions; women's much lower levels (ovary + adrenal + peripheral); age decline (~1%/yr after ~30-40 in men).
- **B — Reference Ranges, Units & Thresholds:** the HARMONIZED reference range — the CDC HoSt / Endocrine Society / Travison 2017 JCEM harmonized non-obese-young-men range (~**264–916 ng/dL** = ~**9.2–31.8 nmol/L**); the ng/dL↔nmol/L conversion (×0.0347); the diagnostic threshold for hypogonadism (~300 ng/dL, with the caveat that it's a continuum + needs ≥2 fasting AM samples + symptoms); female ranges (~15–70 ng/dL); age/assay/lab variation; why a single low value isn't diagnostic.
- **C — Measurement & Standardization:** the load-bearing standardization story — immunoassays (esp. older/direct/analog) are UNRELIABLE at low concentrations (women, children, hypogonadal men); **LC-MS/MS is the reference method**; the **CDC Hormone Standardization (HoSt) Program** for testosterone; assay bias/harmonization; interferences (biotin, heterophile antibodies, cross-reactivity with other steroids in immunoassays); sample timing (AM, fasting) + the effect of acute illness/glucose; SHBG-driven discordance (when total T misleads → measure free T or calculate).
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** what low total T means (hypogonadism — primary [testicular, high LH] vs secondary [pituitary/hypothalamic, low/normal LH]; functional/age-related); symptoms + the threshold controversy; what RAISES (TRT/exogenous androgens, hCG, weight loss in obese men, recovery; in women PCOS/adrenal/ovarian tumors) / LOWERS (aging, obesity/metabolic syndrome [via ↓SHBG + central], opioids, glucocorticoids, chronic illness, sleep deprivation, alcohol, marijuana, anabolic-steroid-induced suppression, prior TRT); associations (low T with metabolic syndrome, mortality — cohort, note reverse-causation/confounding); the TRT cardiovascular-safety question (TRAVERSE 2023); SHBG's role in interpreting total T; limitations — diurnal/biological variability, assay dependence, SHBG confounding, single-sample unreliability.
