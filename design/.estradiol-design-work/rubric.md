# Rubric — estradiol (biomarker, standard mode)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology & What It Measures:** estradiol (E2, 17β-estradiol) = the most potent natural estrogen; women — ovarian (granulosa cell, FSH-driven aromatization of androgens) the dominant premenopausal source, fluctuating across the menstrual cycle (follicular rise → preovulatory peak → luteal); men + postmenopausal women — **peripheral aromatization of testosterone/androstenedione by aromatase (CYP19A1)** in adipose/bone/brain is the dominant source; the HPG-axis feedback role (E2 feeds back on hypothalamus/pituitary, in men too); binding (SHBG + albumin); the estrogen receptors (ERα/ERβ); E2's roles in BOTH sexes (bone, the male role — libido/bone/fat/sperm); distinguish estradiol (E2) from estrone (E1, dominant postmenopausal) and estriol (E3, pregnancy).
- **B — Reference Ranges, Units & Thresholds:** the strongly SEX- + CYCLE- + AGE-dependent ranges — premenopausal women by phase (follicular ~**~12–166 pg/mL**, mid-cycle peak ~**~85–500+ pg/mL**, luteal ~**~40–260 pg/mL**), postmenopausal women LOW (~**<10–30 pg/mL**), men LOW (~**~10–40 pg/mL**); units pg/mL ↔ pmol/L (×3.671); the diagnostic uses (female: ovarian function/menopause/IVF monitoring/precocious puberty; male: gynecomastia, aromatase-excess, the T:E2 balance); no single cutpoint; pregnancy very high.
- **C — Measurement & Standardization (the load-bearing section):** the assay problem — **immunoassays are INACCURATE at LOW estradiol concentrations** (men, postmenopausal women, children) — they overestimate + lack precision below ~20–50 pg/mL; **LC-MS/MS (or GC-MS) is the reference method**, essential for low-level E2; the CDC Hormone Standardization (HoSt) Program for estradiol; cross-reactivity (other estrogens/steroids, conjugates); biotin + heterophile interferences; why a sensitive assay matters in men + postmenopausal women + pediatric/aromatase-inhibitor monitoring; sample timing (cycle phase in women).
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** **female** — menstrual-cycle assessment, menopause, ovarian failure/POI, IVF/ovulation-induction monitoring, precocious/delayed puberty, estrogen-secreting tumors; HRT monitoring; **male** — gynecomastia, the testosterone→estradiol aromatization balance (obesity raises aromatase → higher E2), aromatase excess/deficiency, E2's role in male bone health + libido (E2 deficiency from over-aromatase-inhibition is harmful — the role of E2 in men is REAL, not just "estrogen is bad"); determinants that RAISE E2 (obesity/↑aromatase, exogenous estrogen, ovarian/testicular/adrenal estrogen-secreting tumors, hCG, cirrhosis, hyperthyroidism, aromatase-overactivity, pregnancy) / LOWER (menopause/ovarian failure, aromatase inhibitors, GnRH agonists, anorexia/low energy availability, hypogonadism); the bone + cardiovascular + breast-cancer-risk associations (cite cohorts cautiously); limitations — extreme sex/cycle dependence, immunoassay inaccuracy at low levels, single time point in a cycling hormone.
