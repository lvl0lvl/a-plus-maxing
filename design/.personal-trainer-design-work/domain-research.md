## Findings

### Finding 1 — the personal-trainer is an inform-class coach, not a diagnostician, prescriber, or clearance authority

The domain divides into a coachable layer (load/volume/intensity programming, exercise selection, progressive-overload and deload planning, autoregulation, conservative re-entry programming) and a clinical layer the agent must recognize-and-route (diagnosing injury/disease, prescribing rehab for a *diagnosed* condition, medical clearance after a cardiac/serious-illness event, ED/RED-S treatment). Section C8 + D7 establish three qualitatively different route types the agent must keep separate because they have different correct routes: **(1) STOP-and-escalate** (exertional cardiac red flags, rhabdomyolysis cluster, cauda equina) → out of the coaching loop entirely; **(2) refer-then-defer** (acute myocarditis, high-risk-site BSI, suspected fracture, progressive neuro deficit, malignancy/infection cluster, final RTS clearance for a managed serious injury) → surface the concern, hand the return decision to a clinician; **(3) coach-with-constraints** (criteria-gated progressive loading, graded symptom-gated post-illness return, load progression respecting healing ranges) → the agent's actual operating zone, always with the two prior gates upstream. The posture mirrors the deployed `labs-specialist`/`sleep-coach` (inform-class, basis-reviewable, escalation-over-interpretation).
**Informs:** Identity, Role Boundaries, Loop-Breaking, Anti-Patterns.

### Finding 2 — established-vs-provisional is the agent's central epistemic discipline; training is a field where coaching narrative routinely outruns the evidence

Across all four sections the sharpest design constraint is separating ESTABLISHED (replicated RCT / meta-analysis / strong consensus) from what coaching culture asserts as proven but the literature holds PROVISIONAL/CONTESTED. The domain's contested-claim catalog the agent must flag rather than assert: the acute:chronic workload ratio as injury prevention (C, largely repudiated), superiority of any one periodization model (B), velocity-loss-threshold numbers (B), routine prophylactic deloads (B), muscle-damage/soreness as a *driver* of hypertrophy (A), MEV/MAV/MRV "volume landmarks" (A), HRV-guided training benefit (D), isometric analgesia for tendinopathy (C), and myonuclear-permanence "muscle memory" (B, rodent). The agent must preserve the established/provisional boundary in every recommendation and tag certainty.
**Informs:** Core Rules, Anti-Patterns, Negative Examples.

### Finding 3 — resistance-training dose-response is graded and lever-ordered, with load specific to the goal

Volume is the primary hypertrophy lever: weekly sets → hypertrophy is graded and positive across the studied low-to-moderate range (Schoenfeld/Ogborn/Krieger meta-analysis, A[2], meta_analysis). Load is *specific to strength*: heavy > light for 1RM, but hypertrophy is largely load-agnostic when sets are taken near failure (A[4], meta_analysis) — the SAID/load-specificity principle. Frequency, volume-equated, does not independently drive hypertrophy (A[8], meta_analysis) — it is a way to *distribute* a target weekly volume. Concurrent-training interference is real but moderate and modality-/limb-specific (running impairs lower-body strength more than cycling; A[16], meta_analysis). Absolute failure is not required; sufficient proximity-to-failure matters more (A[5], meta_analysis).
**Informs:** Core Rules, Communication.

### Finding 4 — individual response is large and partly heritable, so any prescription is a hypothesis to revise against measured response

Same-program response varies enormously: unilateral RT in 585 subjects produced muscle-CSA change −2% to +59% and 1RM 0% to +250% (A[3], rct); the HERITAGE Family Study found ~47% heritability of the VO2max training response with non-responders existing (A[7], cohort). Population-mean dose-response curves describe the *expected* response; an individual prescription is never a guarantee and must be revised against the operator's measured response.
**Informs:** Core Rules, Modes (the empty-data and the revise-against-response paths).

### Finding 5 — "volume landmarks" and "chase the damage/soreness" are coaching heuristics/myths the agent must not assert as fact

MEV/MAV/MRV (minimum-effective / maximum-adaptive / maximum-recoverable volume) are a popular practitioner framework with NO validated per-muscle numeric thresholds in the peer-reviewed literature — useful vocabulary, but their attached numbers are practitioner-derived (`anecdote_aggregate`), never to be stated as established (A2, A[anecdote]). Direct human time-course evidence dissociates muscle damage and the early protein-synthesis spike from eventual hypertrophy; growth tracks synthesis only *after* damage attenuates (A[6], rct). Post-2016 the field views damage as a *byproduct*, not a necessary cause — the "you must get sore / chase damage to grow" narrative is unsupported and must be flagged as a myth.
**Informs:** Core Rules, Anti-Patterns, Negative Examples.

### Finding 6 — periodization confers a modest strength edge but no specific model is proven superior, and it does not boost hypertrophy volume-equated

Periodized training beats non-periodized for 1RM strength (ES 0.43, B[2], meta_analysis), but that meta-analysis did NOT equate volume. When volume IS equated, periodization keeps a strength edge but does NOT affect hypertrophy (B[8], meta_analysis). Linear vs undulating vs block vs conjugate: no specific model is demonstrated superior to another when volume is equated (B[3], B[4], meta_analysis); progress is attributed to overload + variation/novelty, not a magic scheme. The agent presents periodization's value as strength-expression/skill, not as a hypertrophy multiplier, and does not claim model superiority.
**Informs:** Core Rules.

### Finding 7 — autoregulation is a valid readiness tool but not an established superiority; tapering is established, routine deloads are not

RIR-based RPE is a valid proximity-to-failure readout in trained lifters on compound lifts (strong inverse RPE↔velocity correlation, B[9], cohort), degrading in novices and at high reps. Velocity-loss thresholds are directional (low VL for power, high VL for hypertrophy, VL-agnostic for strength) but the specific threshold numbers are PROVISIONAL (B[11], meta_analysis). Autoregulation is equal-or-slightly-better than fixed progression, not an established superiority (B[12], meta_analysis). Tapering for peaking is ESTABLISHED (~2 wk, volume cut 41–60%, intensity maintained; B[13], meta_analysis), but routine prophylactic deloads in general trainees are thinly studied — one RCT shows a mid-cycle deload *cost* lower-body strength with no upside (B[15], rct). The agent must not present "deload every 4th week" as evidence-based.
**Informs:** Core Rules.

### Finding 8 — detraining/retraining and "muscle memory": strength is durable, behavioral retraining is real, the myonuclear mechanism is rodent-and-contested

Strength is relatively durable (~3–4 wk maintained, gradual decline after); aerobic fitness declines faster (early plasma-volume-driven loss). Faster *behavioral* retraining in previously-trained humans is well-supported (B[14], B[7]). Maintenance is achievable at sharply reduced volume *if load/effort is preserved* (A[17][18]). Mechanism caution: myonuclear-permanence muscle memory is a **mouse** finding (Bruusgaard, B[16], animal — species-flagged, NOT directly human-transferable) and is CONTESTED in humans (B[17]); human epigenetic memory (B[7]) is the more defensible human candidate but rests on small-n single-lineage work — PROVISIONAL.
**Informs:** Core Rules, Modes.

### Finding 9 — return-to-training carries real safety weight: healing timelines are RANGES, time is a floor not a clearance trigger, and RTS is criteria-based + clinician-shared

Tissue-healing timelines (muscle BAMIC grade, tendon collagen-turnover, bone-stress-injury MRI grade ~6–14 wk, post-ACL ~9-month floor) CONSTRAIN loading but are RANGES, not guarantees, and are mostly elite-athlete-derived (population-mismatch flag for recreational trainees) (C1, C[1][3][4][5]). The single load-bearing cross-cutting rule: **time-since-injury is a FLOOR, not a clearance trigger** — criteria (pain-free loading, strength symmetry, functional tests, symptom-free progression) drive return (C[5], cohort; C4 Bern/StARRT consensus, C[11], regulatory). Final RTS/RTP clearance for a managed serious injury is a shared clinician decision, not an autonomous coaching call. Progressive loading (not rest) is the established core of tendinopathy management; eccentric vs HSR are broadly equivalent (C[6], rct); isometric analgesia is CONTESTED (did not replicate beyond the n=6 origin; C[7] vs C[8]).
**Informs:** Core Rules, Loop-Breaking, Edge Cases.

### Finding 10 — the acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) is methodologically repudiated and must never be presented as settled injury-prevention science

Gabbett's ACWR "sweet spot" (0.8–1.3; ≥1.5 spike → 2–4× risk) drove near-universal adoption (C[9], mechanism_review), but Impellizzeri/Lolli et al. showed the association is substantially a statistical artifact (ratio rescaling inflates apparent effect; contrived/random denominators reproduce the odds ratios; predictive c-statistic ≈0.57, barely above chance) and explicitly concluded "dismiss ACWR and its underlying theory" (C[10], meta_analysis). **Designated HALT-risk:** the agent reports BOTH the original hypothesis and the repudiation; the *general* principle that abrupt large load spikes after a layoff are risky is plausible (and consistent with deconditioning/rhabdo risk), but the agent must NOT present the ACWR number as an evidence-based safety device.
**Informs:** Core Rules, Anti-Patterns, Negative Examples.

### Finding 11 — exertional red flags are STOP-and-escalate, never a programming adjustment; the rhabdomyolysis cluster is in the agent's most-served population

Established sports-cardiology and clinical-guideline structure: exertional chest pain/pressure, syncope/near-syncope on exertion, disproportionate dyspnea, and sustained/irregular palpitations are STOP-exercise + urgent medical evaluation signals (C6, C[13][14], regulatory) — not deload/substitute/"monitor next session." Exertional rhabdomyolysis (ER) is the highest-yield item because it occurs in exactly the population the agent most serves — deconditioned individuals doing unaccustomed, high-volume eccentric work: severe pain beyond DOMS + marked swelling + dark/tea-coloured urine (myoglobinuria) + disproportionate weakness → STOP + urgent/ED evaluation (CK ≥~5× ULN, AKI risk; C[16] Sayers & Clarkson + StatPearls, mechanism_review). Cauda-equina features (saddle anaesthesia, new bladder/bowel dysfunction, bilateral leg weakness) are EMERGENCY referral (C[12], regulatory). Misclassifying any of these as a programming problem is the most dangerous failure mode in the domain.
**Informs:** Role Boundaries (TIME_CRITICAL), Loop-Breaking (fail-safe escalation floor), Negative Examples.

### Finding 12 — return-to-exercise after illness: acute myocarditis is an exercise contraindication, and post-illness return is graded, symptom-gated, and clinician-cleared

Guideline-level consensus: acute myocarditis (any active myocardial involvement, regardless of LV function) is a contraindication to exercise — abstain during the acute phase (~3–6 months) and return only after cardiology clearance (normalised function + biomarkers + no arrhythmia on Holter/exercise testing) (C7, C[13][14][15], regulatory). The post-illness graded-return template generalises: rest → asymptomatic baseline → stepwise reintroduction → drop back a stage on symptom recurrence → escalate to cardiac evaluation on any red-flag symptom during progression (C[15], regulatory; links to F11). The agent never overrides an exercise contraindication or post-event clearance with a training goal. (Goal-agnostic: the agent reads `operator-profile.md` January-2026-issue + contraindication fields at dispatch and binds whatever is present — it does not hardcode operator state.)
**Informs:** Role Boundaries, Loop-Breaking, Edge Cases, Context Loading.

### Finding 13 — overtraining is a diagnosis of exclusion and low-energy-availability is recognize-and-route, not the agent's to diagnose or treat

The FOR→NFOR→OTS continuum is real as vocabulary, but OTS is a diagnosis of exclusion with "very difficult" NFOR/OTS distinction and NO confirmatory biomarker (D[1], regulatory) — the agent describes observable signs (unexplained performance decrement, persistent fatigue, mood/sleep disturbance), recommends load reduction, and routes the exclusion work (organic disease, infection, iron deficiency, low energy availability) to a clinician; it never labels a trainee "overtrained." RED-S/Relative-Energy-Deficiency: low energy availability is the established root cause, the male triad exists (not female-only), and REDs overlaps OTS (D[2][3][4], regulatory). The agent recognizes the LEA/disordered-eating signal and routes to the nutritionist and/or clinician; it does not program an energy deficit into that picture or own ED/RED-S management.
**Informs:** Core Rules, Role Boundaries, Anti-Patterns.

### Finding 14 — monitoring claims must be validity-tiered, and the empty-wearable-state is today's default

sRPE (session-RPE × duration) is a validated internal-load tool (D[5], mechanism_review). HRV-guided training is modest and CONTESTED — a meta-analytic medium effect on submaximal markers but small, non-significant effects on performance and VO2peak (D[6], meta_analysis); present it as plausibly-helpful-but-modest, mostly studied in trained endurance athletes (population-mismatch flag for novices). Subjective wellness questionnaires are trend signals, not diagnostic instruments. Wearable "readiness/recovery" composite scores (Oura/WHOOP/Garmin/etc.) are non-validated, undisclosed black boxes — only the raw inputs (RHR, HRV) are evidence-supported (D[8], mechanism_review). Per `current-state.md` no wearable exists today (Oura pending), so the default is the **empty-wearable-state**: coach from established science + self-report, fabricate no HRV/readiness/recovery metric; the validation-tiering discipline binds the moment data appears (mirrors the deployed sleep-coach).
**Informs:** Core Rules, Tools, Modes, Communication.

### Finding 15 — pre-participation screening is the formal coaching↔clinical hinge; masters physiology shifts protein/recovery (coordinate with nutritionist)

ACSM 2015 (Riebe) and AHA/ACC deliberately moved AWAY from routine medical clearance for everyone (it became a barrier to activity), gating instead on three variables: current activity level, known cardiovascular/metabolic/renal disease (CMRD) and/or signs/symptoms, and desired intensity (D6, D[15][16], regulatory). Medical evaluation before vigorous exercise is triggered by known CMRD or suggestive signs/symptoms; PAR-Q+ is a validated self-screen whose positive flag is a route signal, not a clearance (D[17], regulatory). When criteria warrant, the agent defers programming until clearance rather than coaching through an un-evaluated risk. Masters/older-adult physiology: anabolic resistance raises the protein need (~1.0–1.2, up to 1.5 g/kg/d) with resistance training the primary countermeasure (D[12][13], regulatory; the dose detail is the nutritionist's owned write — coordinate, don't prescribe); the masters recovery-deficit magnitude is inconsistent/uncertain (D[14]).
**Informs:** Core Rules, Context Loading, Edge Cases, Role Boundaries.

### Finding 16 — AUTHORITY_FRAMING_BYPASS is mandatory and the three-mechanism anti-sycophancy scaffold is inherited verbatim

The operator is the single user and is classed A3 (operator-self-harm via own-agent) — the 81.8%-of-successful-attacks vector per Role 1 §2.2. AUTHORITY_FRAMING_BYPASS is mandatory for every specialist: educational/credential ("as a PT/physio/coach"), hypothetical, third-party ("for a friend / my client"), and "skip the disclaimer" framings never relax a directive gate OR a red-flag/contraindication gate (a returning-from-myocarditis or exertional-chest-pain stimulus stays gated regardless of framing). Inherit the three-mechanism anti-sycophancy block from Role 1 verbatim (A: multi-agent silent agreement → Role 4 Council-Mode; B: user-acquiescence → maintain-position-without-new-evidence; C: RLHF drift → Negative Examples). Never self-attest an `aplus-research` gate verdict — it is dispatched-judge-produced (PF-S2-01/PF-S3-01).
**Informs:** Identity/IDENTICAL anti-sycophancy block, Core Rules, Role Boundaries, Anti-Patterns.
