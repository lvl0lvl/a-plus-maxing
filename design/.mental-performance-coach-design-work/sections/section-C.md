# Section C — Mental-Health Safety Boundary + Consumer-Product Validity + Non-English Survey

Goal-agnostic LIBRARY survey of the single most load-bearing fact in this build: the boundary between PERFORMANCE coaching and mental-health CARE, and the suicidality-detection / escalation discipline that boundary requires. Type-tagged per claim. Also surveys consumer cognitive-product validity (brain-training, wearable stress scores) and folds the mandatory non-English layer (§C-5, Russian nootropic peptides). The escalation behaviors are grounded directly in the instrument-validation and crisis-guidance primaries; the agent-design conclusions in the relevance notes are derived from those sources, not invented.

## Findings

### The performance-coach vs mental-health-provider boundary

**1. Professional coaching is defined by scope as NOT diagnosis or treatment of mental disorders; the ethically mandated response to depression, anxiety, substance abuse, PTSD, eating disorders, or suicidal ideation is REFERRAL, not coaching.** The International Coaching Federation's published referral guidance frames coaching as visioning/present/future-oriented partnership that "assumes the client is resourceful and whole," where the coach "does not diagnose problems or prescribe solutions," and explicitly enumerates anxiety, depression, eating disorders, PTSD, substance abuse, suicidal ideation, and thought disorders as conditions that warrant referral to a mental-health professional [1, regulatory]. The threshold is functional: when a problem "gets in the way of everyday functioning (professionally, personally, or socially)," it exceeds coaching scope [1, regulatory]. **Agent relevance — the central design fact:** a mental-performance-coach operates in the focus / stress-resilience / motivation-as-performance lane. The moment a signal crosses into diagnosis or treatment of a mental disorder, the coach has NO scope authority — it escalates to the live medical-liaison; it never presents as a substitute for mental-health care.

**2. Software that delivers recommendations a clinician cannot independently scrutinize, or that is intended to be relied on for diagnosis/treatment, falls under medical-device regulation — the regulatory line that separates a non-device coach from an unauthorized clinical tool.** The FDA's final Clinical Decision Support Software guidance (28 Sep 2022) holds that software escapes device classification only when, among four criteria, it "enabl[es] the HCP to independently review the basis for the recommendations… so that it is not the intent that the HCP rely primarily on" the software for a clinical diagnosis or treatment decision [2, regulatory]. **Agent relevance:** an LLM performance coach must NOT position itself as the basis for a mental-health diagnosis or treatment decision. It detects signal and routes to a licensed human; it does not produce clinical determinations a user would rely on in place of care. This is both an ethical and a regulatory boundary.

### Depression / anxiety / burnout screening — detect-and-escalate, never diagnose

**3. The PHQ-9 is a validated 9-item depression severity measure (sensitivity 88% / specificity 88% at the ≥10 cutoff for major depression), with scores of 5/10/15/20 marking mild/moderate/moderately-severe/severe.** Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams (2001) validated the PHQ-9 against an independent structured mental-health interview in a 580-patient subsample drawn from ~6,000 primary-care and ob-gyn patients [3, cohort]. **Agent relevance:** the PHQ-9 is a screening/severity instrument administered and interpreted by clinicians. A performance coach may recognize the *signal pattern* (persistent low mood, anhedonia, hopelessness) and escalate — it does NOT administer, score, or interpret the PHQ-9 as a diagnosis. Note that PHQ-9 item 9 itself screens for thoughts of being "better off dead or of hurting yourself," which is a suicidality signal that triggers Finding 7's escalation, not a coaching topic.

**4. The GAD-7 is a validated 7-item generalized-anxiety severity measure (sensitivity 89% / specificity 82% at the ≥10 cutoff).** Spitzer, Kroenke, Williams & Löwe (2006) developed and validated the GAD-7 against DSM-IV GAD using the 6-month duration criterion as the criterion standard [4, cohort]. **Agent relevance:** same discipline as PHQ-9 — the coach detects the anxiety-signal pattern and escalates; it does not screen-and-diagnose. Performance-relevant "stress" and a clinical anxiety disorder are different objects; conflating them is the boundary failure.

**5. Burnout is, by WHO's ICD-11, an OCCUPATIONAL PHENOMENON and explicitly "not classified as a medical condition" — three dimensions (exhaustion, mental distance/cynicism, reduced professional efficacy), confined to the occupational context.** WHO's 28 May 2019 statement places burn-out (ICD-11 code QD85) in the chapter "Factors influencing health status or contact with health services," not among diseases, and states it "should not be applied to describe experiences in other areas of life" [5, regulatory]. The construct's measurement instrument is the Maslach Burnout Inventory (Maslach & Jackson 1981), whose three validated subscales — emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, reduced personal accomplishment — map onto the WHO dimensions [6, mechanism_review]. **Agent relevance:** burnout-as-occupational-phenomenon is plausibly within performance-coaching scope (workload, recovery, demand management) — BUT the coach must not treat "burnout" as a license to manage what is actually a depressive or anxiety disorder. Burnout that co-presents with PHQ-9/GAD-7-pattern signal, functional collapse, or any suicidality crosses the boundary and escalates.

### Suicidality — the highest-stakes item

**6. The Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) stratifies ideation along a passive→active continuum, and the highest-severity ideation bands (active ideation with intent, or with plan) carry materially higher odds of subsequent attempt — passive ideation is a real signal, not a null one.** Posner et al. (2011) validated the C-SSRS across three multisite studies (N=673 across adolescents and adults), reporting good convergent/divergent validity and high sensitivity/specificity for behavior classification; worst-point lifetime ideation predicted future attempts, and "participants with the two highest levels of ideation severity (intent, or intent with plan) at baseline had higher odds for attempting suicide" [7, cohort]. The C-SSRS ideation subscale runs from (1) wish to be dead → (2) nonspecific active thoughts → (3) ideation with methods → (4) ideation with some intent → (5) ideation with specific plan and intent. **Agent relevance — the band that governs escalation:** active ideation with intent and/or plan (bands 4–5) maps to EMERGENCY; passive ideation / wish-to-be-dead / depressive hopelessness (bands 1–2) maps to URGENT in-person care. The C-SSRS provides the severity vocabulary the coach uses to set the escalation tier — it is NOT a scale the coach administers.

**7. Both passive and active suicidal ideation are associated with elevated attempt risk; the two are highly correlated latent factors, and co-occurring passive+active ideation carries the greatest risk — so detected passive SI must NOT be treated as benign.** Wastler et al. (2023), modeling 8 suicidal-thought items across two large U.S. samples (n=6,200; n=10,625), found a two-factor (passive/active) structure with a very high inter-factor correlation (β=0.867 lifetime), meaning the constructs are distinct but tightly linked; hopelessness is the strongest affective correlate of passive SI and the greatest attempt risk is among those reporting BOTH [8, cohort]. **Agent relevance — the load-bearing escalation contract (mirrors and intensifies the deployed sleep-coach SI handling):**
- **Active SI / plan / intent → EMERGENCY.** Surface 988 (call or text) and direct to the emergency department / 911. This is the TIME_CRITICAL floor.
- **Passive SI / "better off not waking up" / "no point to any of it" / "want it all to stop" / depressive hopelessness → URGENT in-person care** (same-day/next-day clinician), with 988 offered.
- **Oblique / masked ideation counts.** Detection must cover passive, oblique, and masked phrasings — not only explicit "I want to kill myself." Bands 1–2 of the C-SSRS [7] and the passive-SI factor [8] are exactly these.
- **A benign trailing request never cancels a detected SI signal.** If a message contains an SI signal and then a normal-sounding question, the SI signal governs the response; the trailing request does not downgrade or reset it.
- **Operator minimization never downgrades.** "I'm fine, forget I said it" after a detected signal does not lower the tier. The detected band sets the floor.

**8. Asking directly about suicide does NOT induce or increase suicidal ideation; the evidence supports asking, and acknowledging suicide may reduce ideation.** Dazzi et al. (2014), reviewing studies across community and at-risk samples, found no study reporting a statistically significant increase in suicidal ideation from being asked, and concluded that asking may in fact reduce ideation and improve mental-health outcomes in treatment-seeking groups [9, mechanism_review]. **Agent relevance:** this removes the most common rationalization for evasion. A coach that detects an SI signal must NOT avoid naming it for fear of "planting the idea." It can and should acknowledge the signal directly and route to 988 — the "don't ask" instinct is empirically unfounded.

**9. The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is the US standing escalation target: free, confidential, 24/7, SAMHSA-funded, live since 16 July 2022, serving anyone in suicidal crisis or emotional distress via call/text/chat.** Per SAMHSA/Vibrant Emotional Health, 988 is a national network of 200+ local crisis centers providing "free and confidential emotional support to people in suicidal crisis or emotional distress 24 hours a day, 7 days a week," administered under SAMHSA funding [10, regulatory]; Congress designated the 3-digit code via the 2020 National Suicide Hotline Designation Act with go-live on 16 July 2022 [11, regulatory]. **Agent relevance:** 988 is the concrete, always-correct US referral the coach surfaces for any detected SI signal (active → alongside ED/911; passive → alongside urgent in-person care). It is jurisdiction-specific (US); a goal-agnostic build must treat the *line* as a parameterized escalation target, not hardcode 988 as universal.

### Stimulant / nootropic misuse → mental-health risk

**10. Prescription stimulant use carries a real, dose- and drug-dependent psychosis risk — amphetamine roughly DOUBLES the risk of new-onset psychosis versus methylphenidate in a large cohort — which is the bidirectional hazard: cognitive-enhancement seekers may have, or may precipitate, a mental-health condition.** Moran et al. (2019, NEJM), in 221,846 matched ADHD patients aged 13–25, found a psychotic-episode incidence of 0.10% with methylphenidate vs 0.21% with amphetamine (about double), supported by negative-control analyses [12, cohort]. Beyond psychosis, stimulants and nootropic misuse associate with anxiety, dependence, and a withdrawal syndrome (see Section B caffeine findings for the withdrawal model). **Agent relevance:** a query about pushing stimulants/nootropics for cognitive enhancement is not only a Section-B PRESCRIPTIVE_DIRECTIVE (route to prescriber) — it is ALSO a mental-health screen. Anxiety, agitation, paranoia, or psychotic-spectrum signal in a stimulant/nootropic context escalates to medical-liaison; the coach never characterizes such symptoms as a tolerable side effect of a "stack."

### Consumer cognitive-product validity

**11. Commercial "brain-training" efficacy claims were found DECEPTIVE by the FTC: Lumosity (Lumos Labs) paid $2 million under a $50M suspended judgment for claiming its games improve real-world/work/school performance and delay age-related decline without "competent and reliable scientific evidence."** FTC matter 132-3212 (civil action 3:16-cv-00001, N.D. Cal.); the Stipulated Final Judgment and Order was entered 8 Jan 2016, imposing a $50,000,000 judgment suspended after a $2,000,000 payment, and barring future cognitive-benefit claims absent competent scientific substantiation [13, regulatory]. **Agent relevance:** a coach must NOT relay brain-training-app marketing as efficacy. "Lumosity-type" cognitive-game claims are the canonical regulator-sanctioned over-claim — present them as not substantiated.

**12. The scientific picture behind brain-training is near-transfer-not-far-transfer: people get better at the trained task with little credible evidence of generalization to untrained real-world cognition.** Simons et al. (2016), the Psychological Science in the Public Interest consensus review, found "little evidence that brain-training games yield real-world benefits," distinguishing near transfer (similar tasks, often present) from far transfer (real-world generalization, largely absent) [14, mechanism_review]. This itself followed a 2014 Stanford Center on Longevity / Max Planck Institute consensus statement from 70+ scientists holding there is no compelling evidence brain training reverses cognitive decline and that claims are "frequently exaggerated and at times misleading" [15, mechanism_review]. **Balance (the rebuttal exists):** an open letter posted by ~133 scientists/practitioners via "Cognitive Training Data" contested that a true "consensus" existed and argued the literature shows benefits of some training regimens — but this was a non-peer-reviewed open-letter rebuttal, not new pooled evidence [16, anecdote_aggregate]. **Agent relevance:** the honest framing is "you'll improve at the game; real-world far-transfer is largely unproven." Present both the dominant skeptical synthesis [14,15] and the existence of dissent [16] without treating the rebuttal as efficacy data.

**13. Consumer wearable "stress / readiness / focus" scores rest on PPG-derived HRV that is accurate mainly at rest and degrades badly with movement, and the proprietary scores themselves are largely unvalidated black boxes.** Sinichi et al. (2025, Psychophysiology), validating four heart-rate wearables against ECG, found devices captured HR more accurately than HRV and that "PPG devices, even those advertised and designed for research purposes, may pose validity concerns for HRV measurement in conditions other than those similar to resting states" [17, cohort]. Reviews note proprietary Recovery/Readiness/stress scores combine inputs in a "black-box" manner and "are not yet validated in the scientific literature and have not been shown to reliably predict performance" [18, mechanism_review]. **Agent relevance:** a coach must NOT treat a wearable "stress score" or "readiness score" as a measured physiological truth. These are directional, motion-sensitive, proprietary estimates — usable as a soft trend signal at most, never as a diagnostic readout of stress or recovery, and never as grounds to dismiss a reported symptom ("your readiness is green, so you're fine" is a boundary failure).

## §C-5 Non-English (Russian nootropic) survey

Mandatory folded non-English layer. The Russian-language literature on regulatory-peptide nootropics — Semax, Selank, Noopept — is real and includes human trials, but is dominated by a small number of Moscow institutes, mostly published in Russian, with small samples and limited independent Western replication. Survey what exists, then the evidence-quality and concentration caveats.

**C5-1. Semax** — a synthetic heptapeptide analog of ACTH(4-10) (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro), developed at the **Institute of Molecular Genetics, Russian Academy of Sciences**, approved in Russia/CIS as an intranasal nootropic/neuroprotective for stroke recovery and cognitive impairment, unapproved elsewhere. Human signal: Kaplan et al. (1996) reported intranasal Semax (0.25–1.0 mg, ~4–16 µg/kg) improved operator work-efficiency over 20–24 h in volunteers vs placebo [19, open_label] [route-extrapolation: intranasal]. The mechanistic backbone is rodent: Eremin et al. (2005) showed Semax raises striatal 5-HIAA (+25%) and potentiates amphetamine-driven dopamine release/locomotion in mice [population-mismatch: mouse] [20, animal]. The widely cited BDNF/NGF/TrkB-upregulation mechanism is also predominantly rodent hippocampal data — do not upgrade to human-outcome certainty.

**C5-2. Selank** — a synthetic heptapeptide derivative of the immunomodulatory tetrapeptide tuftsin, developed by the Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS with the **V.V. Zakusov Institute of Pharmacology**, registered in Russia as an intranasal anxiolytic. Human signal: Zozulia et al. (2008) compared Selank (n=30) vs medazepam (n=32) in 62 patients with generalized anxiety disorder and neurasthenia, reporting comparable anxiolytic effect plus antiasthenic/psychostimulant effects, with correlated changes in serum enkephalin activity [21, open_label] [route-extrapolation: intranasal]. **Boundary note:** that Selank is studied *for GAD* underscores Finding 4 — these are mental-health-disorder treatments in their home jurisdiction, squarely outside performance-coach scope.

**C5-3. Noopept (GVS-111 / omberacetam)** — an N-phenylacetyl-L-prolylglycine ethyl ester dipeptide designed as a piracetam analog at the **Zakusov Research Institute of Pharmacology** (Gudasheva and colleagues, mid-1990s). Human signal: Neznamov & Teleshova (2008 Russian original; English translation 2009) compared Noopept 10 mg BID vs piracetam in patients with mild cognitive disorders of vascular/post-traumatic origin over 56 days, reporting cognitive and affective improvement [22, open_label] (Russian-language; in a clinical, not healthy-enhancement, population). Animal backbone: Ostrovskaya et al. (2001) showed oral GVS-111 retained antiamnestic activity in rats at 0.5–10 mg/kg [population-mismatch: rat] [23, animal].

**Evidence-quality + concentration caveat (CANONICAL ≥70% watch item).** Across C5-1 through C5-3, the originating institutions reduce to two Moscow centers — the **Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS** (Semax, Selank) and the **V.V. Zakusov Institute of Pharmacology** (Selank, Noopept) — i.e., effectively a single national research cluster authors the foundational and most of the clinical literature, comfortably exceeding the 70% single-cluster threshold. The human trials are small (tens of patients), open-label or active-comparator (not placebo-RCT), mostly Russian-language, single-region, and lacking independent Western replication; the strongest mechanistic claims are rodent. **Do NOT over-claim.** These compounds are NOT established human cognitive enhancers by Western evidentiary standards; their regulatory approval is jurisdiction-specific (Russia/CIS) and does not transfer. For a goal-agnostic library, the correct framing is: "real but concentrated, low-replication, mostly-non-English, mostly-clinical-population, route-specific (intranasal) evidence — flagged for source concentration." Any agent surfacing these routes the compound class to the supplement-specialist and the human-use question to a prescriber; it never presents them as coach-directed.

## Concentration / population notes

- **Russian nootropic peptides [19–23] — concentration flag (≥70%):** the literature concentrates in two Moscow institutes (Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS; V.V. Zakusov Institute of Pharmacology). Treat the body as single-cluster-dominated, low-replication, and mostly Russian-language. This is the section's canonical concentration-audit item.
- **Population-mismatch flags:** Eremin 2005 (Semax serotonergic/dopaminergic) is mouse [20]; Ostrovskaya 2001 (oral GVS-111 antiamnestic) is rat [23]. Semax/Selank BDNF-upregulation mechanism is predominantly rodent hippocampal data — not upgraded to human certainty. Human Russian-peptide signals [19,21,22] are open-label/active-comparator, not placebo-RCT, and the cognitive-impairment trials are *clinical* populations, not healthy enhancement.
- **Route-fidelity flags:** Semax [19] and Selank [21] human data are intranasal — `[route-extrapolation: intranasal]` applied; no oral-equivalence claim is made.
- **Instrument primaries [3,4,7] are validation cohorts** (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS) — tagged `cohort`; they ground the *signal-detection* vocabulary, NOT a coach-administered diagnostic.
- **Regulatory primaries [1,2,5,10,11,13]** (ICF scope, FDA CDS, WHO ICD-11 burnout, SAMHSA 988, FTC Lumosity) are tagged `regulatory` and carry the scope/boundary conclusions.
- **Brain-training rebuttal [16]** is an open letter, not pooled evidence → tagged `anecdote_aggregate`; it never grounds an efficacy claim, only documents that dissent exists.
- **No sponsorship conflicts surfaced** in the safety/instrument primaries; the brain-training and wearable findings inherently involve commercial products, which is precisely why the regulator/independent-review sources [13,14,15,17,18] anchor those claims rather than vendor material.
