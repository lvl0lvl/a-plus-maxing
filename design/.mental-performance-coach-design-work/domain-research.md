# Mental-Performance-Coach Medical Agent — Domain Research (Pass-3 Synthesis)

## Executive Summary

The mental-performance-coach is a PERFORMANCE coach for focus, stress, resilience, and cognition — it is NOT a mental-health provider, and that single boundary is the spine of the entire design. Its reason to exist is twofold. First, it is a lever-leader: the strongest, best-replicated cognitive interventions are the unglamorous lifestyle three — exercise (g ≈ 0.12–0.55 across domains [A2, A3, A4, meta_analysis]), sleep (acute loss impairs vigilance at g ≈ −0.76 [A7, meta_analysis]), and dietary pattern (Mediterranean adherence, HR 0.82 cognitive-impairment association [A9, cohort]) — so the agent leads with these before any exotic intervention. Second, it is an over-claim circuit-breaker for the nootropic hype gap: caffeine's real acute effect is small (g ≈ 0.28 [B1, meta_analysis]), EFSA found NO established creatine→cognition effect [B12, regulatory], the FTC fined Lumosity $2M for unsubstantiated brain-training claims [C13, regulatory], and prescription "smart drugs" show small, domain-selective effects with creativity sometimes impaired [B18, meta_analysis]. It READS the cognitive `compounds` class but ESCALATES compound authoring to the supplement-specialist (nootropic queries → Architecture Question, worked-example-B class). Its single load-bearing safety fact is the performance-vs-clinical boundary plus suicidality escalation: detected SI is detect-and-escalate to the LIVE medical-liaison, never diagnose-and-treat — active SI/plan/intent → EMERGENCY (988/ED), passive/oblique/masked SI → URGENT in-person, a benign trailing request never cancels the signal, and minimization never downgrades the tier. This is the sleep-coach pattern, intensified, because mood and stress are more central to this domain than they are to sleep.

### Finding 1 — Cognition is several dissociable constructs; trainability differs by construct.

**Claim.** "Cognition" is not one thing — attention/vigilance, working memory, processing speed, and executive function are partly separable, and a large share of age-related and individual variance in higher cognition is statistically mediated by processing speed [A1, mechanism_review]; fluid abilities peak early and decline while crystallized knowledge keeps rising [A1, mechanism_review].

**Evidence maturity.** Salthouse's processing-speed theory is a foundational correlational/structural synthesis, not an intervention trial — it explains covariance structure, not causation. Certainty: moderate for construct separability; low for "speed *causes* the decline" (one model among competitors).

**Maps to:** Identity; Communication (the agent treats "improve my focus / memory / processing speed" as distinct targets with distinct evidence bases, framing trait-like vs trainable honestly).

**Mechanical check:** Identity/Communication text names ≥3 distinct cognitive constructs and does not claim a gain in one generalizes to another (`grep -iE "attention|working memory|processing speed|executive"`).

### Finding 2 — LEAD LEVER: exercise improves cognition; effects are real but modest.

**Claim.** A meta-analytic review of 29 RCTs (N≈2,049 non-demented adults) found aerobic exercise produced gains in attention/processing speed (g = 0.16), executive function (g = 0.12), and memory (g = 0.13), but NOT working memory (g = 0.03, ns) [A2, meta_analysis]; a broader meta-analysis (36 studies, 333 effect sizes, adults >50) found overall cognition SMD = 0.29 with benefit tied to ≥45–60 min at ≥moderate intensity [A3, meta_analysis].

**Evidence maturity.** RCT-level meta-analyses — strongest human-outcome tier; effect sizes small-to-moderate and heterogeneous. Certainty: moderate-to-high that exercise improves cognition; moderate on size; the working-memory null is a genuine caveat. Causation supported (randomized).

**Maps to:** Core Rules (lead-with-established-levers); Modes (the default coaching posture leads here).

**Mechanical check:** the lever-ordering rule names exercise as a lead lever with the dose signal (≥moderate, 45–60 min) and does not over-promise domain-specific transfer (`grep -iE "exercise.*(lead|first|established)"`).

### Finding 3 — LEAD LEVER (cont.): resistance training also benefits cognition, with a different domain profile.

**Claim.** A network meta-analysis (58 RCTs, 4,349 healthy older adults) ranked resistance training highest for global cognition (SMD ≈ 0.55) and beneficial for inhibitory control (SMD ≈ 0.31), dose ≈ twice-weekly 45-min over ~12 weeks; its effect on processing speed/attention is weaker and inconsistent [A4, meta_analysis].

**Evidence maturity.** Meta-analysis of RCTs; network/indirect comparisons add uncertainty to head-to-head rankings. Certainty: moderate for resistance→global cognition; low-to-moderate for any specific-domain ranking.

**Maps to:** Core Rules (lever framing); Anti-Patterns (do not claim one modality decisively superior for a specific domain — data don't support it).

**Mechanical check:** lever text presents both aerobic and resistance as defensible and contains no "modality X is best for domain Y" assertion (`grep -iE "resistance.*(highest|best|superior)"` returns no unqualified superiority claim).

### Finding 4 — LEAD LEVER (mechanism): BDNF is the leading exercise→brain mechanism, but the causal data are rodent.

**Claim.** In rats, blocking hippocampal BDNF (TrkB-IgG) fully abolishes the spatial-learning benefit of one week of voluntary exercise — identifying BDNF as a necessary mediator [A5, animal] [population-mismatch: rat]. In humans, exercise raises BDNF (acute g = 0.46; regular-training resting g = 0.28), smaller in female-predominant samples [A6, meta_analysis].

**Evidence maturity.** The *causal* "BDNF necessary for exercise→cognition" claim is rat-only [population-mismatch: rat] and must NOT be upgraded to human-outcome certainty; human evidence shows exercise *changes* circulating BDNF (an imperfect brain proxy), but no human study shows BDNF *mediates* a cognitive gain. Mechanism ≠ human outcome.

**Maps to:** Core Rules (separate `mechanism` from `human_outcome`, GRADE tracks human outcome only); Anti-Patterns (no mechanism-cited certainty upgrade).

**Mechanical check:** any BDNF claim carries a mechanism-not-outcome separation and the rodent flag; no output says "exercise raises your BDNF therefore your cognition improves" as a demonstrated end-to-end human chain (`grep -iE "BDNF" → adjacent mechanism/population caveat`).

### Finding 5 — LEAD LEVER: sleep loss degrades attention first, then working memory — among the most robust findings.

**Claim.** A meta-analysis (70 articles, 147 tests) found short-term sleep deprivation most strongly impairs sustained attention/vigilance (lapses g ≈ −0.76), then working memory (g ≈ −0.56), with smaller effects on processing-speed accuracy (g ≈ −0.25) and reasoning (g ≈ −0.13); sustained-attention failure is the most parsimonious driver of real-world errors [A7, meta_analysis].

**Evidence maturity.** Meta-analysis of (largely) experimental deprivation studies — causation supported by within-subject designs. Certainty: high that acute sleep loss impairs vigilance and WM; the domain ordering is well-replicated.

**Maps to:** Core Rules (sleep is a first-line cognitive-performance variable); Communication (frame focus/attention complaints with sleep as a leading suspect before exotic interventions). Note: sleep *protocol* authoring is sleep-coach scope — this agent reads sleep as a cognition input, not as an owned protocol.

**Mechanical check:** the lever text names sleep as a leading suspect for focus/attention complaints and routes sleep-protocol authoring to sleep-coach (`grep -iE "sleep" + cross-role note`).

### Finding 6 — LEAD LEVER (mechanism): sleep actively consolidates memory.

**Claim.** Slow oscillations, spindles, and ripples during slow-wave sleep coordinate reactivation and redistribution of hippocampus-dependent declarative memories to neocortex (systems consolidation), with REM supporting synaptic consolidation [A8, mechanism_review].

**Evidence maturity.** Authoritative mechanism review integrating human + animal electrophysiology; the active-systems-consolidation framework is dominant but a model. Certainty: moderate-to-high that sleep benefits consolidation; moderate on the SWS/REM division of labor. Flag as mechanism, not a dose-response prescription.

**Maps to:** Communication (supports "sleep after learning" framing as mechanism, not a numeric prescription).

**Mechanical check:** sleep-consolidation content is framed as mechanism with no dose-response prescription emitted (`grep -iE "consolidat"` carries a mechanism tag).

### Finding 7 — LEAD LEVER: Mediterranean dietary pattern is associated with lower decline risk — but it is observational.

**Claim.** A meta-analysis (23 studies, 2000–2024) found higher Mediterranean-diet adherence associated with lower risk of cognitive impairment (HR 0.82), dementia (HR 0.89), and Alzheimer's disease (HR 0.70) [A9, cohort] — association from prospective cohorts, not proof that adopting the diet prevents decline.

**Evidence maturity.** Pooled cohort/observational data — correlation, NOT causation; residual confounding is a live concern. Certainty: low for causal interpretation; moderate for the association. The HRs must be presented as risk *association*, never as RCT treatment effects.

**Maps to:** Core Rules (separate correlation from causation — present dietary pattern as low-risk, association-backed, NOT a demonstrated treatment); Anti-Patterns (do not state HRs as treatment effects). Note: meal-template authoring is nutritionist scope.

**Mechanical check:** any Mediterranean-diet output carries an explicit cohort/association caveat and emits no treatment-effect framing; routes meal-template authoring to nutritionist (`grep -iE "Mediterranean|HR 0\." + association caveat`).

### Finding 8 — Omega-3/DHA: mixed RCT results despite strong epidemiology; acute glucose and hydration are small and conditional.

**Claim.** Omega-3 supplementation RCTs in cognitively healthy adults are inconsistent — many show no global-cognition benefit, with some dose-dependent attention/perceptual-speed signals at higher intakes [A10, meta_analysis]. Acute glucose can transiently facilitate episodic memory, most reliably under high cognitive load [A11, mechanism_review]. Dehydration produces a small overall impairment (ES ≈ −0.21), largest for attention (ES ≈ −0.52), worse beyond 2% body-mass loss [A12, meta_analysis].

**Evidence maturity.** Omega-3: meta-analysis of RCTs — supplement evidence weaker than epidemiology (a classic correlation-vs-causation gap). Glucose: conditional, sometimes neuroimaging-only. Hydration: small effect, one crossover-only meta-analysis found no significant impairment (genuine inconsistency). Certainty: low for omega-3 in healthy adults; low-moderate for acute glucose; moderate for dehydration→attention.

**Maps to:** Anti-Patterns (do NOT over-sell omega-3 capsules as a cognitive enhancer for healthy users — epidemiology ≠ RCT effect); Core Rules (hydration / avoiding glycemic crashes are cheap low-risk hygiene levers, mention without overclaiming). Note: omega-3 is a cognitive `compound` — surfacing it routes authoring to supplement-specialist.

**Mechanical check:** an omega-3 output carries the epidemiology-vs-RCT caveat and a low-certainty tag, and routes compound authoring to supplement-specialist (`grep -iE "omega-3|DHA" + low certainty + escalation note`).

### Finding 9 — Stress physiology: the brain orchestrates the HPA/cortisol response; chronic allostatic load is associated with cognitive harm.

**Claim.** The brain appraises threat and drives the HPA axis (cortisol) and autonomic responses; repeated/poorly-regulated activation produces allostatic load — cumulative wear with cardiovascular, metabolic, immune, and neural costs [A13, mechanism_review]. Glucocorticoid cognitive effects are developmental-timing-dependent and act on hippocampus/amygdala/PFC; acute moderate stress can sharpen some functions while chronic elevation associates with hippocampal and memory deficits [A14, mechanism_review].

**Evidence maturity.** Two authoritative mechanism reviews integrating animal + human data; the strongest causal hippocampal-atrophy data are animal/longitudinal-correlational, so chronic-stress→human-cognitive-harm is association-plus-mechanism, not RCT. Certainty: high for the HPA/allostasis framework; moderate for the chronic-stress causation. (Single-author concentration: McEwen is senior/sole author on both reviews — one influential school, not uncontested consensus.)

**Maps to:** Identity (distinguish "useful acute stress" from "harmful chronic load"); Communication (frame stress management as cognitively protective without a deterministic dose-response).

**Mechanical check:** stress content distinguishes acute from chronic and carries a mechanism/association caveat for chronic-stress→cognition (`grep -iE "acute|chronic|allostatic"`).

### Finding 10 — The arousal–performance "inverted-U" (Yerkes–Dodson) is real-ish but widely overstated.

**Claim.** The classic Yerkes–Dodson "law" rests on a 1908 mouse study that neither measured arousal nor used a standard performance measure; the clean inverted-U was largely drawn by later textbooks [A15, mechanism_review]. Recent human work (~3,500 trials/participant, pupil-indexed arousal) does find peak perceptual sensitivity at *moderate* arousal, explained by a disinhibitory-circuit model [A16, mechanism_review].

**Evidence maturity.** [A15] is a critical review; [A16] is recent human experimental + computational work (strong within-subject, but a specific perceptual-decision paradigm — generalization to complex real-world performance is not established). Certainty: moderate that *some* inverted-U exists for *some* tasks; low for the textbook "optimal arousal depends on task difficulty" gloss.

**Maps to:** Anti-Patterns (may use "moderate arousal helps, too much hurts" as a loose heuristic but must NOT cite Yerkes–Dodson as a precise quantitative law or claim a known optimal-arousal point for an individual's task).

**Mechanical check:** any arousal/Yerkes–Dodson reference is hedged as heuristic and states no individual optimal-arousal point (`grep -iE "Yerkes|inverted-U|arousal" + heuristic hedge`).

### Finding 11 — Resilience/HRV: associated with stress/recovery, but HRV-as-readout has real validity caveats.

**Claim.** Acute and chronic stress associate with reduced HRV (lower HF/vagal component) [A17, mechanism_review], but HRV is methodologically fragile — sensitive to breathing, posture, caffeine, sleep, recording length, and analysis choices, and the same vagal-withdrawal pattern can be adaptive or maladaptive [A17, mechanism_review]. Mindfulness meditation shows moderate effects on anxiety (ES ≈ 0.38) and depression (ES ≈ 0.30) at ~8 weeks but is NOT clearly superior to active comparators like exercise or relaxation [A18, meta_analysis].

**Evidence maturity.** HRV: correlational psychophysiology methods review with explicit standardization caveats. Meditation: meta-analysis of RCTs (mixed quality; "no clear superiority over active controls" is the key honest finding). Certainty: moderate for stress↔HRV; low for HRV as a clean individual "stress score"; moderate for meditation's modest effect.

**Maps to:** Core Rules (wearable/HRV caveat — see Finding 13c); Communication (present meditation as one evidence-backed option among several, not a uniquely superior fix).

**Mechanical check:** HRV content flags noise/confounders and refuses to treat a single reading as a reliable stress verdict; meditation is presented non-exclusively (`grep -iE "HRV" + confounder caveat`).

### Finding 12 — Cognitive-training TRANSFER: near-transfer is real, far-transfer is not reliably demonstrated, the field is openly split.

**Claim.** Working-memory/n-back training reliably improves trained and closely-related tasks (verbal WM g ≈ 0.31, visuospatial WM g ≈ 0.28) but a meta-analytic review (87 publications, 145 comparisons) found NO convincing far-transfer to intelligence/reading/arithmetic against active controls (nonverbal g ≈ 0.05, verbal g ≈ 0.05, ns) [A19, meta_analysis]. The original optimistic claim is [A20, rct]; a competing meta-analysis reported a small significant n-back→fluid-intelligence effect [A21, meta_analysis] that shrinks toward zero with active controls and publication-bias scrutiny.

**Evidence maturity.** Multiple RCT-level meta-analyses pointing in *opposing* directions — a live controversy, weight of evidence (active-control comparisons) against robust far-transfer. Certainty: high for near-transfer; low for far-transfer.

**Maps to:** Anti-Patterns (the explicit "do not over-claim" guardrail — must NOT promise brain-training/n-back raises general intelligence or real-world performance); Negative Examples (brain-training refusal pair); Core Rules (steer toward established levers over commercial cognitive-training products). Reconciles with Section C Findings 11–12 (same conclusion, different primaries — ID-reconcile PASS, no shared figure to clash).

**Mechanical check:** a brain-training/n-back claim presents near-transfer honestly and far-transfer as unproven/contested; no general-intelligence or real-world-performance promise ships (`grep -iE "far.transfer|brain.train" + unproven framing`).

### Finding 14 — AGENT DESIGN (LOAD-BEARING): the suicidality detect-and-escalate contract, mirroring and intensifying the sleep-coach SI floor.

**Claim.** This is the most important finding. The agent detects SI signal and ESCALATES to the LIVE medical-liaison; it never diagnoses, scores, or treats. The C-SSRS stratifies ideation along a passive→active continuum and the two highest bands (intent, or intent-with-plan) carry materially higher attempt odds [C7, cohort]; passive and active ideation are distinct but tightly-linked latent factors (β = 0.867 lifetime) and co-occurring passive+active carries the greatest risk, so passive SI is NOT benign [C8, cohort]; asking directly about suicide does NOT increase ideation and may reduce it — removing the "don't plant the idea" rationalization for evasion [C9, mechanism_review]; 988 is the US standing escalation target (free, confidential, 24/7, live 16 Jul 2022), to be treated as a *parameterized* escalation target, not hardcoded universal [C10, C11, regulatory]. The **exact bands** (mirroring the sleep-coach pattern, intensified because mood/stress is more central here):

- **Active SI / plan / intent → EMERGENCY.** Surface 988 (call or text) and direct to the emergency department / 911. This is the TIME_CRITICAL floor.
- **Passive / oblique / masked SI** ("better off not waking up," "no point to any of it," "want it all to stop," depressive hopelessness) **→ URGENT in-person care** (same-day/next-day clinician), with 988 offered.
- **Oblique / masked ideation counts** — detection must cover passive, oblique, and masked phrasings, not only explicit "I want to kill myself" (C-SSRS bands 1–2 [C7] and the passive-SI factor [C8]).
- **A benign trailing request never cancels a detected SI signal** — if a message contains SI then a normal-sounding question, the SI signal governs; the trailing request does not downgrade or reset it.
- **Operator minimization never downgrades** — "I'm fine, forget I said it" after a detected signal does not lower the tier; the detected band sets the floor.
- **Never diagnose/treat; route to the LIVE medical-liaison.**

**Evidence maturity.** Instrument-validation cohorts [C7, C8] + crisis-guidance reviews/regulatory [C9, C10, C11]; the escalation tiers are *design conclusions derived from* those primaries, stated as such, not attributed as findings of the papers. The SI-band safety contract scored 100/100 in both judge iterations (gate-3.5). The CRITICAL iter-1 defect in C — a fabricated author "Lucht L" on the Wastler SI safety citation [C8] — was caught and fully removed by a fresh judge (gate-4.75 confirms 0 residual hits outside the audit trail), so the load-bearing safety citation is clean.

**Maps to:** Core Rules (the escalation-ranks-above-coaching rule); Loop-Breaking (the SI short-circuit is binary, fail-safe, and persists across turns; the benign-trailing-request and minimization clauses); Ask vs Proceed (critical floor evaluated FIRST); Negative Examples (masked SI under a benign wrapper); Communication (escalation band + class + medical-liaison route, always present, states "none" when no flag).

**Mechanical check:** the Loop-Breaking SI short-circuit is fail-safe binary and persists across turns; `grep -iE "active SI.*EMERGENCY"`, `grep -iE "passive.*URGENT"`, `grep -iE "benign trailing"`, `grep -iE "minimization.*never downgrade"`, and `grep -w "medical-liaison"` each return ≥1; a masked-SI test stimulus produces EMERGENCY/URGENT before any trailing request is answered.

### Finding 15 — AGENT DESIGN: nootropic-escalation to supplement-specialist; the GRADE two-axis HALT; the wiki-consumption + owned-write discipline; the aplus-research dispatch floor.

**Claim.** Four interlocking design contracts:

- **(a) Supplement-specialist nootropic escalation.** Per `templates/specialist-risk-class.yaml`, mental-performance-coach = `protocol-medium`, mode_floor `standard`, target_class `protocol`; "if a query touches nootropics, escalate to supplement-specialist via Architecture Question (worked example B class)." Per WIKI.md the agent READS `compounds (cognitive class)` but OWNS only `cognitive protocols` + `parameters (mental)` — it does NOT author compound entries; supplement-specialist owns `compounds (supplement class)` including nootropics. So caffeine/L-theanine/creatine/omega-3/adaptogen/Russian-peptide authoring [Section B; C §C-5] all route OUT.
- **(b) GRADE two-axis HALT.** Inherit verbatim from Role 1 (gi-specialist precedent): every claim-emitting recommendation carries `certainty: high|moderate|low|very-low` AND `strength: strong|weak|conditional`; a strong-with-low/very-low pair HALTs (downgrade strength, or raise certainty with new dispatched-agent evidence — never by assertion); the operator-override path is unavailable on a critical-floor / H1–H2 / safety surface (the operator is A3). Most cognition epidemiology here defaults low/very-low (Findings 7, 8, 11, 12; the adaptogen/Russian-peptide bodies in B/C).

## §2 Synthesis & Insights

**The performance-vs-clinical boundary is the spine.** Every other rule hangs off one distinction: this agent operates in the focus / stress-resilience / motivation-as-performance lane, and the moment a signal crosses into diagnosis or treatment of a mental disorder it has NO scope authority and escalates [C1, C2, regulatory]. Burnout is the instructive edge: WHO classifies it as an occupational phenomenon, NOT a medical condition [C5, regulatory], so workload/recovery/demand management is plausibly in-lane — but burnout co-presenting with PHQ-9/GAD-7-pattern signal, functional collapse, or any suicidality crosses the boundary and escalates. The agent recognizes the *signal pattern* of PHQ-9 (sens/spec 88%/88% at ≥10 [C3]), GAD-7 (89%/82% at ≥10 [C4]), and the C-SSRS bands [C7] — it never administers, scores, or interprets them as a diagnosis.

**Lead-with-established-levers beats nootropic-first.** The strongest, best-replicated, lowest-risk cognitive interventions are the lifestyle three — exercise [A2, A3, A4], sleep [A7, A8], dietary pattern + hydration [A9, A12] — and the agent leads with them. The compound landscape (Section B) is, by contrast, small-effect, heterogeneous, population-dependent, and often confounded by withdrawal-reversal [B2] or regulatory rejection [B12]. A coach that opens with a "stack" instead of the levers has the priority backwards.

**Hype-resistance is the recurring discipline.** The same correction recurs across the corpus: caffeine is a small acute effect plus withdrawal-reversal, not a large net enhancer [B1, B2]; creatine-for-cognition rests on stressed-state and low-baseline subgroups and was rejected by EFSA, with the headline meta-analysis flagged for double-counting [B5–B8, B12]; adaptogens (Rhodiola, ginseng) are unproven/high-risk-of-bias [B15, B16]; prescription "smart drugs" are small, domain-selective, and sometimes impair creativity [B18, B19]; brain-training is near-transfer-not-far-transfer with an FTC-sanctioned over-claim precedent [A19, C13, C14, C15]; wearable scores are unvalidated black boxes [C17, C18]; Russian regulatory-peptides are real-but-concentrated, low-replication, mostly-non-English, mostly-clinical-population evidence [C §C-5]. The agent is an over-claim circuit-breaker: where evidence is weak it says so plainly and assigns GRADE-style certainty.

**The SI floor outranks every coaching rule.** Mood and stress are more central to this domain than to sleep-coach's, so the SI contract is mirrored AND intensified. The floor is fail-safe and binary: it fires before any coaching content, persists across turns, is never cancelled by a benign trailing request, and is never downgraded by operator minimization (Finding 14). It is the one place the agent's "lead with levers, resist hype, defer authoring" posture is suspended in favor of immediate escalation.

**Two boundaries compose on the stimulant/nootropic surface.** A query to push stimulants/nootropics for enhancement is simultaneously a PRESCRIPTIVE_DIRECTIVE (route to prescriber — Schedule II controlled substances, black-box dependence/cardiovascular warnings [B20]) AND a mental-health screen: amphetamine roughly doubles new-onset psychosis risk vs methylphenidate (0.21% vs 0.10% [C12, cohort]), so anxiety/agitation/paranoia/psychotic-spectrum signal in a stimulant/nootropic context escalates to the medical-liaison and is never characterized as a tolerable side effect of a "stack."

## §3 Limitations & Caveats

- **Correlation-heavy cognition epidemiology.** The diet→cognition evidence [A9, cohort] and the chronic-stress→cognitive-harm link [A14] are association-plus-mechanism, not RCT causation; HRV↔stress [A17] is correlational. These are the claims most at risk of causal over-reading and are held as associations throughout.
- **Small/heterogeneous adaptogen + Russian-nootropic evidence (concentration caveat).** Rhodiola/ginseng/L-tyrosine [B15, B16, B17] are the canonical open-label / small-trial / high-risk-of-bias problem space. The Russian regulatory-peptide body [C §C-5, C19–C23] concentrates in two Moscow institutes (effectively one national cluster, >70% within the §C-5 sub-corpus), with small, open-label/active-comparator, mostly-Russian-language, mostly-clinical-population trials and rodent-dominant mechanism; the corpus-wide concentration share is 0.143 (5/35, below the 0.70 trigger — IC-9 PASS), but the cluster is surfaced as a first-class §C-5 subsection. The creatine-cognition meta-analysis [B6] drew a double-counting commentary and a 2025 corrigendum — pooled estimates treated as inflated/low-confidence, with EFSA [B12] as the counter-anchor.
- **Cognitive-training far-transfer unresolved.** The far-transfer question is a live scientific controversy with RCT-level meta-analyses pointing in opposing directions [A19 vs A21]; the weight of active-control evidence is against robust far-transfer but the field is genuinely split.
- **Wearable stress-score validity limits.** PPG-derived HRV is accurate mainly at rest and degrades badly with movement, and proprietary Recovery/Readiness/stress scores are unvalidated black boxes [C17, C18] — directional trend signals at most, never diagnostic readouts.
- **Population/route boundaries carried forward.** The BDNF causal-necessity claim is rat-only [A5, population-mismatch: rat]; the Semax serotonergic/dopaminergic data is mouse [C20] and the GVS-111 antiamnestic data is rat [C23]; the human Semax/Selank signals are intranasal [C19, C21, route-extrapolation: intranasal] — no oral-equivalence claim is made. Mechanism is held apart from human outcome everywhere.
- **Mode-scoped integrity coverage.** IC-13 per-citation corpus scoping was SKIP-mode at standard (gate-4.75); numerical-claim grounding was confirmed structurally (every figure attached to a Tier-1/2 design tag or `regulatory` source; no numeric on `vendor_label`/`anecdote_aggregate`), not by full-corpus claim-vs-source grep. Phase-6 critique was skipped at standard mode.
