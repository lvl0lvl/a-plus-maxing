# Rubric — igf-1 (biomarker, standard mode)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology & The GH/IGF-1 Axis:** IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1, somatomedin C) = a peptide hormone produced mainly by the **LIVER** under **growth hormone (GH)** stimulation (also local autocrine/paracrine production in many tissues); the **GH→IGF-1 axis** (hypothalamic GHRH/somatostatin → pituitary GH → hepatic IGF-1 → negative feedback on GH/GHRH); why IGF-1 is the **stable, integrated read-out of GH status** — GH is pulsatile + has a short half-life so a random GH is uninterpretable, whereas IGF-1 is stable across the day (a key advantage); the **IGFBP system** — IGF-1 circulates almost entirely bound to IGF-binding proteins (esp. **IGFBP-3**) + the acid-labile subunit (ALS) in a ternary complex (<1% free); IGF-1 actions (anabolic/growth-promoting, mitogenic, metabolic — insulin-like; mediates most of GH's growth effects, esp. longitudinal bone growth via the growth plate); the strong age dependence (peaks in puberty, declines with age — "somatopause").
- **B — Reference Ranges, Units & Thresholds (load-bearing — age-banding):** IGF-1 is **STRONGLY age- (and somewhat sex-/puberty-) dependent** — the reference interval MUST be age-matched (a value is uninterpretable without age); express as **age-specific reference intervals or SD scores (z-scores)** — the preferred reporting is the SDS/z-score relative to age; representative adult ranges in ng/mL (µg/L) — high in puberty, declining through adulthood; units ng/mL = µg/L (1:1); diagnostic uses — **acromegaly/GH excess** (high IGF-1, the key screening test — more reliable than random GH), **GH deficiency** (low IGF-1, supportive but not definitive — needs dynamic GH stimulation testing), monitoring GH-replacement + acromegaly treatment; the no-universal-cutpoint / age-SDS framing.
- **C — Measurement & Standardization:** the assay challenge — IGF-1 immunoassays historically had POOR standardization + IGFBP interference (must dissociate IGF-1 from its binding proteins before assay); the **IFCC/consensus harmonization effort + the WHO IS 02/254 reference standard**; LC-MS/MS emerging; **inter-assay/inter-method variation remains significant** → results + reference intervals are method-specific, and the age-normalized SDS depends on the assay's own reference population; interferences (IGFBP carryover, heterophile, biotin); the importance of using the SAME assay + its matched age-reference for serial monitoring; pre-analytics (IGF-1 is stable, not diurnal — an advantage; nutritional status matters — see determinants).
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** **HIGH IGF-1:** acromegaly / GH-secreting pituitary adenoma (the primary clinical use — IGF-1 is THE screening + monitoring marker), exogenous GH; **LOW IGF-1:** GH deficiency (childhood + adult-onset), hypopituitarism, **malnutrition / fasting / catabolic states / poorly-controlled diabetes / liver disease** (IGF-1 is nutritionally regulated + hepatically made, so low IGF-1 is NOT specific to GH deficiency — a key caveat), hypothyroidism, aging (somatopause); the **GH/IGF-1 and longevity paradox — address honestly:** higher IGF-1 drives growth but lower IGF-1 / reduced GH-IGF signaling is associated with LONGEVITY in model organisms + some human data (Laron syndrome — GH-receptor deficiency, very low IGF-1 — protective against cancer + diabetes); the **U-shaped / cancer associations** — both high AND low IGF-1 associate with adverse outcomes in cohorts (high IGF-1 with some cancers [breast/prostate/colorectal], low with CV/frailty) — cite NAMED cohorts/meta cautiously, note it's associational; determinants raising (GH excess/therapy, puberty, good nutrition) / lowering (GHD, malnutrition/anorexia, liver disease, uncontrolled diabetes, hypothyroidism, estrogen [oral], aging); the GH-doping context (IGF-1 as an anti-doping marker); limitations — extreme age dependence (MUST age-SDS), assay standardization, nutritional confounding, single value needs clinical + dynamic-test context.
