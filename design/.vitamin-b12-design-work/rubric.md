# Rubric — vitamin-b12 (serum vitamin B12 / cobalamin, biomarker, standard mode)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology & What It Measures:** **vitamin B12 (cobalamin)** = a cobalt-containing corrinoid; an essential cofactor for TWO human enzymes — **methionine synthase** (cytosolic; methylcobalamin; homocysteine + 5-methyl-THF → methionine + THF — links to folate + methylation) and **methylmalonyl-CoA mutase** (mitochondrial; adenosylcobalamin; methylmalonyl-CoA → succinyl-CoA — the basis of the MMA marker). Source: ANIMAL foods only (synthesized by bacteria/archaea); large hepatic stores (~2-5 mg → years-long reserve). The ABSORPTION cascade (load-bearing for the deficiency causes): gastric **acid + pepsin** release food-B12 → **R-binder (haptocorrin)** in saliva/stomach → pancreatic proteases free it in the duodenum → **intrinsic factor (IF, from gastric parietal cells)** binds B12 → the **IF-B12 complex absorbed in the terminal ileum (cubam/cubilin-amnionless receptor)**. Transport: in blood ~**80% bound to haptocorrin (metabolically INERT) + only ~20% bound to TRANSCOBALAMIN = "holotranscobalamin / holoTC / active-B12" (the fraction delivered to cells)** — THE KEY measurement concept: total serum B12 includes the inert haptocorrin pool, so it is an imperfect status marker (vs holoTC + the functional markers).
- **B — Reference Ranges, Tests & The "Unreliable Test" Problem (load-bearing):** units **pg/mL (ng/L) ↔ pmol/L (×0.738)**; total serum B12 reference **~200-900 pg/mL** (lab-dependent); **THE LOAD-BEARING PROBLEM: serum total B12 has POOR sensitivity + specificity for true tissue deficiency** — a large indeterminate "grey zone" (~200-400 pg/mL / 148-300 pmol/L) where deficiency cannot be confirmed or excluded by total B12 alone. **The better/functional tests:** (1) **holotranscobalamin (holoTC / active-B12)** — the metabolically active fraction, earlier marker of depleting stores; (2) **methylmalonic acid (MMA)** — rises in true B12 deficiency (mutase substrate accumulates), the most specific functional marker (but also rises in renal impairment); (3) **homocysteine (tHcy)** — rises in B12 OR folate deficiency (less specific). The **diagnostic algorithm**: borderline total B12 → confirm with MMA ± holoTC; B12 deficiency is a CLINICAL + biochemical diagnosis, not a single number. The folate-masking/interaction note.
- **C — Measurement & Standardization:** the **competitive-binding chemiluminescent immunoassays** for total B12; the **INTRINSIC-FACTOR-ANTIBODY interference pitfall (load-bearing): in patients with pernicious anemia, anti-IF antibodies can interfere with the IF-based competitive assay → FALSELY NORMAL or HIGH B12 despite true deficiency** (a dangerous analytical false-negative); the holoTC assay (active-B12); MMA by LC-MS/MS/GC-MS (+ the renal-impairment confound); homocysteine assay (+ pre-analytic: must be processed quickly/chilled or RBC release falsely raises it); standardization status (the WHO IS for B12; harmonization is imperfect — like ferritin/vitamin D); heterophile interference; pre-analytic. The assay-method limitations compound the biology's "grey zone."
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** **DEFICIENCY causes (mapped to the absorption cascade):** **autoimmune — pernicious anemia (anti-IF + anti-parietal-cell antibodies → IF loss; the classic cause, associated with atrophic gastritis)**; **malabsorption** — atrophic gastritis/aging (food-bound-B12 malabsorption), **gastrectomy / bariatric surgery**, terminal ileal disease/resection (Crohn's), pancreatic insufficiency, fish tapeworm, bacterial overgrowth; **drugs — METFORMIN (a common, under-recognized cause — reduces ileal absorption), PPIs / H2-blockers (acid suppression)**; **dietary — strict VEGAN / vegetarian (no animal source)**, and in infants of B12-deficient mothers; **aging**. **CONSEQUENCES (load-bearing):** **megaloblastic (macrocytic) anemia** (impaired DNA synthesis — but anemia may be ABSENT); **NEUROLOGICAL — subacute combined degeneration of the spinal cord (dorsal + lateral columns) — paresthesias, ataxia, proprioceptive loss; peripheral neuropathy; cognitive/psychiatric changes — and CRITICALLY the NEURO disease can occur WITHOUT anemia + can be IRREVERSIBLE if untreated (the reason not to wait for anemia)**; glossitis. **THE FOLATE-MASKING TRAP (load-bearing safety point): high folate intake (folic-acid fortification/supplements) can correct/mask the megaloblastic ANEMIA of B12 deficiency while the NEURological damage PROGRESSES** — never treat presumed-folate-deficiency macrocytosis without checking B12. **HIGH B12:** usually exogenous (supplements/injections) or a marker of liver disease / myeloproliferative disorders / malignancy (elevated haptocorrin) — a high B12 can be a paraneoplastic red flag. **Limitations:** total B12 is an unreliable single test (the grey zone + the IF-antibody false-normal in PA); confirm with MMA/holoTC; the neuro-without-anemia + folate-masking pitfalls; interpret with the clinical picture + the functional markers.
