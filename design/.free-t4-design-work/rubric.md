# Rubric — free-t4 (biomarker, standard mode)

**Judge threshold:** 92/100. route_fidelity + risk_floor_readiness = null (biomarker).

## Section plan (4 sections, paired retrieval+judge)
- **A — Physiology & What It Measures:** thyroxine (T4) as the main thyroid secretory product (~80-100 µg/day, the prohormone); >99.95% protein-bound (TBG, transthyretin, albumin) → the **free fraction (~0.02-0.03%)** is the metabolically active, homeostatically defended pool; T4→T3 peripheral conversion by deiodinases (D1/D2) as the activation step (T4 is the reservoir, T3 the active hormone); why FREE T4 (not total T4) is measured — it bypasses binding-protein variation (pregnancy/estrogen ↑TBG, nephrotic/illness ↓); fT4's role in the HPT feedback (the dominant feedback signal setting TSH).
- **B — Reference Ranges, Units & Thresholds:** typical adult free-T4 reference interval (~**0.8-1.8 ng/dL** or **~10-23 pmol/L**, assay-dependent) — give the ng/dL↔pmol/L conversion (×12.87); method-dependence is load-bearing (free-T4 estimates differ by assay/platform — no single transferable interval); trimester-specific pregnancy ranges (fT4 falls across gestation; method-specific); diagnostic patterns with TSH (high TSH+low fT4 = overt hypo; low TSH+high fT4 = overt hyper; the TSH-fT4 grid); the fT4-index (FTI/T7) historical alternative.
- **C — Measurement & Interferences:** the measurement challenge — true free T4 by **equilibrium dialysis / ultrafiltration + LC-MS/MS** (reference method) vs the routine **analog/automated immunoassays** (estimates, susceptible to binding-protein + albumin effects); assay standardization/harmonization status (IFCC C-STFT free-T4 standardization); **interferences** — biotin (assay-specific, can falsely raise OR lower depending on assay design), heterophile/anti-streptavidin antibodies, anti-T4 autoantibodies, abnormal binding proteins (familial dysalbuminemic hyperthyroxinemia FDH), drugs displacing T4 (heparin → spurious ↑free T4 via in-vitro lipolysis; furosemide, salicylates, phenytoin); non-thyroidal illness (low T4 syndrome); pregnancy assay pitfalls.
- **D — Determinants & Clinical Significance:** fT4 as the reflex/confirmatory test after abnormal TSH — distinguishes overt vs subclinical + grades severity; **central (secondary) hypothyroidism** — the key scenario where TSH is unreliable and fT4 is essential (low fT4 + inappropriately normal/low TSH); monitoring levothyroxine + (esp.) central-hypothyroid + thyroid-cancer suppression therapy; what raises fT4 (hyperthyroidism — Graves'/toxic nodule, exogenous/factitious thyroxine, thyroiditis release phase, FDH artifact, amiodarone, heparin artifact) / lowers (hypothyroidism, central hypothyroidism, non-thyroidal illness, certain drugs); limitations — assay-method dependence, the free-hormone-measurement controversy in pregnancy + severe illness (when total T4 or fT4-index may be preferred).
